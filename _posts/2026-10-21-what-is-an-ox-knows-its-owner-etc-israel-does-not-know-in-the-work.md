### Opening

An ox knows who feeds it. It acts by its nature, without mistakes and without choice. So what makes a person more than a "bigger ox"? Rav Laitman's answer is that a person becomes **Adam** (man) only by building a connection to the Creator above everything he knows and is forced to do by nature. The material for that connection is the disturbances the Creator sends. The lesson then turns practical: how to treat the same disturbance in two ways at once, corporeally like an ox and spiritually like a man.

## Part 1 — The Degree of the Ox

#### Acting from nature

- A person who has not yet acquired the degree of man works like the still, vegetative and animate. They act from qualities given by nature and have "no deviation from nature."
- Without deviation there is no free choice: "Everything is according to the program." So there is no reward or punishment, no wickedness or righteousness.
- This is why such a person does not yet exist in spirituality. Spirituality is the area where the created being is free, has a personality and choice, either as a shell or in holiness.
- Everything in nature is the Creator's domain, made by His laws. The still, vegetative, animate, and the person who is still on those degrees, have only a "simulated or false existence," felt in our vessels but not in the true, eternal vessels.

#### What the ox knows

- The ox knows its owner from its own nature: how it is built, what qualities and desires it has, and from that what the Creator demands of it. This is **knowing** at the beastly degree.
- Rav Laitman applied the same to people: "Who created me in my shape and form, the way I am, with my qualities, desires, impulses." My knowledge of myself and my environment lets me draw conclusions about the Creator, "and no more."

## Part 2 — Faith Builds the Created Being

#### The one thing above matter

- Above the matter, only one thing can exist from the side of the created being: **his address to the Creator**, the awakening and rising toward Him.
- This attraction toward the Creator is called **faith**, and its magnitude determines the created being's spiritual height.
- A person developing the man in him doesn't look at the events that happen to him or at what nature forces him to do. All that matters is how, above these actions and above his life, he builds a connection to the Creator.

> The connection built over the disruptions "is called Adam, the human, the created being."

#### Why the Creator is concealed

- Everything that happens in and around a person is accepted only as the conditions above which he must build his faith.
- This is why "Israel does not know": the Creator is "concealed deliberately," to allow the created being to build that connection and so build himself.
- Above the degree of the beast there is an empty space, which the created being has to fill with his attitude toward the Creator.

#### From faith back to knowing

- After a person builds faith, he comes again to knowing, "from Your actions we shall know You." The disruptions are also the Creator's program, and when he builds a connection above them, he begins to know Him through them.
- The Creator sends human desires and disruptions, "for me to forget, to get confused, to make mistakes, to not want it." Building a connection above them builds "my image, my body, which is called Adam."
- This knowing is **knowledge above reason**, because it was built above the beastly reason, "above the knowledge of the ox."

#### Complete faith

- **Complete faith** means the attitude toward the Creator (faith) is filled with knowledge of Him.
- In terms of the **Partzuf**: the inclination to bestow is like the **Guf** filled with the light of **Hochma**. "The light of **Hassadim** with the illumination of Hochma is called complete faith."
- Later he restated it: when I become like Him in action, "I'm filled with the light of Hochma. He fills me."

## Part 3 — Concealment and the Disturbance

- **Q: What does it mean that the Creator is not revealed?** Ask on the street and some will say He is everywhere, others that He doesn't exist. Concealment is measured from my own desire: if I want to connect to Him and He sends a disruption against it, the measure of that disruption is the measure of concealment.
- Rav Laitman's example: I want to think about Him, and instead I'm consumed by fear of the police. The size of that fear, which disconnects me from Him, is the size of the concealment.
- Our work is that of a child who is frightened and runs to his mother to adhere to her. The task is to discover that the Creator is in the disruption. "And then the disruption itself becomes revelation."
- A person who can't attribute what happens to the Creator doesn't even feel he is in concealment. Once he feels it, "he has an opening for revelation." Concealment is the back side of revelation: "Night, day, night, day."

#### The path of truth

- **Q: The article says man, unlike animals, must follow the truth. What does that mean?** The path of truth means that all actions, thoughts and desires in the created being happen only according to the Creator's will.
- Truth is not a starting point but "an outcome of the entire work." Rav Laitman mentioned that the "four angels," four forces within us, need a separate study.
- **Q: Where do we take the force to work above reason, by coercion?** If you had knowledge of the Creator, "instead of an ox, you'd be just a bigger ox." Man is not an ox with more knowledge. The spiritual degree means working not by our senses but by our connection with the Creator. How to do it is internal: through **the light that reforms**.

## Part 4 — The Exercise: Two Levels at Once

#### Body like an ox, soul like a man

- Rav Laitman described an exercise he had given the day before in a virtual session. Since "there is nothing besides Him," every disruption must now be related to the Creator, and above it we must make a real connection with Him.
- At the same time, on the corporeal level, we deal with it "just like everyone else": "If I'm not for myself, then who?"
- "To my body I have to relate in a bodily way, because it belongs to the degree of the ox. And to my soul I need to relate as man, because it belongs to the degree of man." This is called **beginning to acquire the degree of man**.

#### An example from home

- Your wife yells at you for waking in the middle of the night to study. Corporeally, do what needs to be done at home, "as if there's nothing else."
- Spiritually, understand it as a disturbance from the Creator, either because you aren't aiming strongly enough toward Him or because He wants you to grow in connection with Him. Try each time to hold on to Him despite it.
- New disturbances will keep coming, until you use them all to connect, "until you adhere to him all day and night, and then this iron gateway will open up."

| Level | Degree | How to respond |
|---|---|---|
| Corporeal | Ox (beast) | Solve it normally: the doctor, work, the neighbor, the laws of the state |
| Spiritual | Man (Adam) | Attribute it to the Creator and build a connection to Him above it |

#### No miracles

- The Creator is the system of laws we feel in our body and our society. On the degree of the ox we must keep them: see a doctor, eat healthy food, work for a living.
- "There are no miracles, don't rely on miracles... It's just laws." A higher realm is only your connection with the Creator.
- Without disturbances, "you'll have nothing with which to build connection with Him" and would "remain as a beast." The more a person can advance, the greater the disturbances he is sent.

#### How the two levels connect

- **Q: How do these two connect?** "You don't need to connect them together. They'll connect together afterwards." Solving them on two levels already begins the connection. Trying to connect them now, without a vessel for it, is artificial and distorted.
- Working on both levels is called **tying together HaVaYaH and Elokim**: one is in nature, the other above nature.
- Using disturbances to connect with the Creator is called **making unifications**, "but that's only on the human level."

## Part 5 — Small Disturbances, Great Connection

- **Q: How can I make the connection more real?** It depends on how a person relates to what happens. A small fear can send me to the friends to be calmed, or to be awakened, "so that by this little disturbance I can make a great connection."
- If the Creator is great in a person's eyes, even the smallest disturbance holds many discernments, and over it he can build a rich, many-sided attitude toward the Creator. This is "the Creator is precise with the righteous by a hair's breadth."
- The entrance to spirituality is "a very small hole," like the eye of a needle. Through it a great pipeline opens, up to **Ein Sof**, until you are enveloped in connection with the Creator from all directions. This happens only through disturbances, the pieces of the **shattering**.
- **Q: Why not shut off fear, honor and the rest?** You can't. They are the fuel on which you build your attitude to the Creator: "This fear, it's very good that I had it because on top of it I made contact with You." Such a blow reveals itself as "a hint of love."
- Like a child afraid of a "bear outside," these are imaginary things, "but without them we can't move." Don't dwell on the issues themselves; use them as quickly as possible to connect.

## Part 6 — Disturbances Inside the Group

- In the group, disturbances are closer to spirituality and must be solved almost entirely on the spiritual level.
- Rav Laitman's example: being told to work in the kitchen when you feel you can't get up. You can solve it "on the ox level" (it's a good society, I'll be thrown out, the Rav is yelling). But eventually you need the greatness of the Creator, and for that you turn to the society, which supplies it.
- "So the kitchen becomes a kind of a sign" of how much I connected with the Creator and how great He is in my eyes.
- **Q: How do we move to the spiritual plane?** "This is man's inner work... You have to push yourself." The fuel should come from the Creator, though we cannot simply switch off corporeal fuel.
- On publishing and distribution work: "The action is an action. The relationship is up to you." Perhaps a spiritual committee is needed to remind people during work time: where are you taking your fuel? Are you coming to work the way you come to a lesson?

## Part 7 — The Creator, Faith and the AHP of the Upper One

#### What "Creator" means

- **Boreh** (Creator) is read as *Bo u'Re'eh*, "come and see": the attitude of the upper degree toward us, as we feel, understand and sense it in our vessels.
- "If you're asking who is the Creator outside of me, there is no such thing." Likewise "abstract light" is not something we can ever feel. When we say the light of Ein Sof fills all of reality, that is not abstract.
- To believe means the degree of connection with Him according to my equivalence to Him, my desire to bestow.

#### Love covers all transgressions

- The Creator seemingly lowers His **AHP** to me. His qualities differ from mine, and I feel the gap as a blow.
- If I still want to connect to Him above the blow, like a mother who loves her child no matter what he does, "love covers all transgressions." Then the blow begins to feel like part of me. I justify it and come to love it, and acquire the next degree.
- The AHP of the upper one connects to my **Galgalta veEinayim**, and I enter the upper one: first as an embryo (**Ibur**), then nursing (**Yenika**), then adulthood (**Mochin**), until the upper one's AHP becomes my vessels of reception.

#### The passage on choice

- Rav Laitman had a passage brought ("the association of the measure of mercy with judgment," no. 106). Its point: the main work is choice. "You chose life in spite of the disturbances." With revealed providence there is no choice: "You work like an ox."
- So the upper one raised his **Malchut** to the eyes and concealed himself. The lower one then feels no **Gadlut** in the upper one, only blows. There is room for choice: to say the upper one restricted himself for my sake.
- This is "when Israel are exiled, the **Shechina** is with them": the exile itself awakens us to connection.
- But a person must say what he actually tastes. Calling the blows wonderful is "a lie." If he overcomes and says the bitter taste is because his vessels are not yet for bestowal, he raises **MAN**, and the upper one begins to be revealed.
- "There are people here sitting five years and more, and they might be sitting here another twenty and thirty years. If they don't persist on this point, that's how time will pass."

#### Sparks in the shells

- **Q: What is the connection between corporeality and the shells?** Corporeality means solving things corporeally, by the laws of the place you live, because the Creator arranges those laws too. Spirituality is "the attitude to the Creator. It's not the angels in the heavens, not miracles."
- As the **Ari** said, corporeal pleasures hold only holy sparks that fell into the **Klipot**, a thin light. In each of our desires there are sparks of bestowal, and only through them do we respond at all to what the Kabbalists write, "not in understanding, but in the working."

## Part 8 — Sensitivity, Investment and Reward

- **Q: How can I grow my sensitivity to the disturbances?** Through exertion, like any expert. Rav Laitman's example was his daughter's PhD on one quality of a virus: hours of work on something tiny, and for her it is a whole world. "It's sensitivity through exertion."
- "Our problem is that we invest a lot, but we don't connect it with... the object of our work. We forget why we're doing what we're doing."
- "We don't ask for the reward where we have to ask for it." Our investment comes out in quantity, not quality. A qualitative investment is when I demand a specific payment: **connection with Him**. With that, he said, it takes "maximum three years. I think even less."
- **Q: The researcher knows her reward. How can I ask for a reward I can't picture?** Scrutinize more and more what the reward is. "The society has to provide you with these ideas." The reward of a commandment is to know the One who commanded it: adhesion.
- If disturbances feel like crushing blows, it is because you are ready for them, but also because you lack connection with the sources of strength: books, authors, group, Rav, friends. Every state is more advanced than the previous one, unless you don't use what you were given.
- After the Torah was given, Job's question no longer stands: there is a force to turn to in every state. Job is like "the left line disconnected from the right."

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **The ox acts from nature; man builds above it.** Without deviation from nature there is no choice and no spiritual existence. A person becomes Adam only through the connection he builds to the Creator above his nature.
2. **Faith is the created being's measure.** The attraction toward the Creator, built above the disruptions, is called faith, and its size is the person's spiritual height.
3. **Concealment is measured by the disturbance.** The Creator hides deliberately. When I find Him in the disturbance, the disturbance becomes revelation.
4. **Work on two levels.** Solve every problem corporeally like an ox, by the laws of nature and the state, with no miracles, and at the same time spiritually, by attributing it to the Creator and connecting above it.
5. **Disturbances are the only material.** Fear, honor, blows, even small ones, are the fuel for connection. The greater the Creator in a person's eyes, the more he can build on the smallest thing.
6. **Complete faith is faith filled with knowledge.** Hassadim with the illumination of Hochma: knowing the Creator above reason, built on top of the ox's knowledge.
7. **Ask for the right reward.** Invest for quality, not quantity, by demanding connection with the Creator in return, and draw strength and the idea of the reward from the group, the books and the Rav.
