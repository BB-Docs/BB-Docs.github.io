### Opening

Repentance means returning the **Hey** to the **Vav**: bringing Malchut, the vessel of the souls, back into equivalence of form with the Giver. But who is the one who returns? Rav Laitman says the will to receive is not the created being; the Creator made it, and it is entirely under His control. The **created being** is only what is outside His control: the intention a person forms toward the "host," and above all the ability to decide how much of the Creator will be revealed and how much concealed. Repentance, the screen and the spiritual degree all depend on that independence.

## Part 1 — Who Is the Created Being?

#### The guest at the table

- The guest sitting at the table, seeing the dishes and wanting them: all of that the Creator created. The desire, the situation and the food "is not called the created being."
- "The created being could be called only someone who is outside of the Creator, outside of His will, His control." So the will to receive "can never be free and it is not called created being."
- What is new is what is born in the guest from **the revelation of the host**: his responses and his relation to the host, wanting either to delight the host (in order to bestow) or to receive from him (in order to receive). This is **the root of the created beings**.

> The created being is an intention. In order to receive or in order to bestow, that does not matter... this is already something that doesn't come directly from the Creator.

#### Independence: deciding the measure of revelation

- The intention follows from how much the host is revealed: when He is concealed, we want to enjoy from Him; when His qualities are revealed, an awakening to bestow upon Him arises.
- So the truly independent thing is "to establish the revelation of the host or his concealment." One who can "stand against the revelation of Godliness to conceal Him, or to reveal Him to the extent that he wants" is independent, and in that measure he is called a created being.
- Therefore "the root of the created beings is the restriction and the concealment," and the created being is called **the screen**, by which he can fully conceal the Creator and reveal Him whenever and however he decides.
- On this depends **repentance**: returning the Hey to the Vav, to the source. The measure of repentance a person can do "is the measure of his independence, the measure of the created being in him."
- So the obligation of correction falls "not on the desire, but rather on the intentions." One who cries out that he is in darkness and the Creator isn't revealed cries "from his will to receive... like that with every beast." The true created being is concerned about the manner of the Creator's revelation, so that it builds screens in him and doesn't violate his independence.

## Part 2 — Q&A: How Can Anything Not Come from the Creator?

- **Q: How can any intention come from a source other than the Creator?** The will to receive comes from the Creator; the will to bestow results from His revelation within the vessel. Before He is revealed, no right intention can exist in us. But the Creator also lets us form an intention that is not simply a result of His revelation.
- **Through the group.** "More than what He gave you, you can acquire through exertion in the group with the friends. You invest in them, you acquire in them an additional desire, which He did not give you." It is like a child playing, in the time of preparation, "but certainly in that there is truly our exertion."

#### The first screen: the barrier

- When a person is given the possibility to discover the Creator, he is given screens. The first screen is the **barrier**: the Creator is revealed to a certain extent ("I and not a messenger"), and a screen forms against Him.
- The person doesn't want the revelation for filling his vessels, "but rather he wants to discover the Creator, first of all, for the correction of the vessels." The power to stand against his own will in this way "is called independence. This is called a created being."

#### Like a seed that becomes a person

- "The fact that the Creator is the root of everything is certain." But a seed, until it develops, is attributed to its parents; later we attribute it to the embryo, the child, the adult. From a certain point the Creator "hides Himself and does not control him."
- Independence is relative to the created being, not to the Creator. Can the Creator still govern him? "Yes He can, but He doesn't do it... He wants to let the person be as himself."
- It is "a true feeling": through the screen a person distinguishes between his own desires and the pleasures in the Creator, and seemingly breaks the law the Creator set. "The Creator determined that there is a will to receive and He's gonna fill it, and the created being says stop... I'm going to place an iron partition."
- The power for this comes from the revelation of **the importance of the Giver**. With two forces outside him, the pleasure and the Giver's importance, he chooses the importance first, "so that I will determine how I receive."
- In spirituality we immediately feel "two different colors": forces under the Creator's control and desires, thoughts and intentions under the created being's control.

## Part 3 — The Partition Is Never Removed

- The created being reaches a stage where he wants the concealment: "He determines the concealment," and to that extent the Creator is revealed. This stage is **the barrier**. From there, each degree measures how much the Creator is revealed and how much the created being conceals Him.
- "The first restriction is not cancelled." Throughout the ladder there is always a 100% partition between the Creator and the created being. What changes is who holds it.

| Position on the ladder | Who holds the restriction |
|---|---|
| **Bottom (before the barrier)** | 100% the Creator, who conceals Himself |
| **Degree 20 of 100** | The created being holds the restriction at 20%; the Creator is revealed at 20% and concealed at 80% |
| **Top** | The created being keeps the entire restriction himself: "I don't need Your pleasures. I only need Your revelation in order to resemble You" |

- Rav Laitman pictures it as two triangles, an upper and a lower one. "This determines the height of the degree."

#### Who chooses the path?

- **Q: The Creator decides the created being will become like Him, but who chooses the path?** The created being, once he can separate himself, determines that the Creator's revelation should serve to build the screen, "and accordingly he already determines the entire process... Nothing is done except through the exertion of the created being."
- **The path of Torah and the path of suffering** exist only until a person discovers this one point: that he must demand the revelation of God so it will correct him. After that decision there is one path: correction through the revelation of the host, so that "I'll be ashamed, I will want to be like him."
- The nature of the degrees, the structure of the soul and the form of His revelation are not ours to decide. "Our freedom is only to reach the decision" to want the restriction and His revelation for building screens, and even that decision comes from the **surrounding light**, which I awaken through my actions, "especially through the society, dissemination and such things."

## Part 4 — The Hole in the Ear

- If I don't want an action, I won't feel it; it doesn't correct me. "I need to come to a lack. If I have a lack for some action, for some correction, and it happens, by this I correct my lack. Otherwise the action passes me by."
- "You sit, you hear things for maybe a year, two years, it passes a centimeter past your ear. Suddenly... boom. You hear it." Before, you hadn't prepared an **inner vessel**; now one has formed, "a hole is made where that thing can enter."
- This recalls the hole bored in the ear of the slave who wants to stay with his master: without making that hole, "nothing will happen."

#### Greater revelation, greater screen

- **Q: The weak annul before the strong. If the Creator seems greater, won't I just feel more like a puppet?** If He were revealed 100% at once, "I won't have the ability to be independent. These degrees determine the extent to which I can maintain my independence opposite Him."
- That is what the screen says when it reflects the light: "Don't control me. You created in me desires to receive... They're addicted to Your pleasures. I don't want them to come into contact with the pleasure. Otherwise it's over."
- "The more you advance, the more you'll need to demand the revelation of the Creator so as to have more power to receive in order to bestow upon Him... you will have a greater screen."

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **The will to receive isn't the created being.** The Creator made it and controls it; the created being is the intention toward the host.
2. **Independence is deciding the revelation.** The screen lets a person conceal or reveal the Creator by his own decision, and that measure is the created being in him.
3. **Repentance falls on the intention.** Returning the Hey to the Vav depends on the measure of a person's independence, not on changing the desire.
4. **The group adds a desire that wasn't given.** Investing in the friends acquires a new, truly earned desire even in the time of preparation.
5. **The restriction is never cancelled.** On every degree the partition is complete; the height of the degree is how much of it the created being holds himself.
6. **One decision, then one path.** Torah or suffering bring a person to demand the Creator's revelation for correction; after that, correction comes only through the revelation of the host.
7. **Build the inner vessel.** Without a lack, a "hole in the ear," the words and the lights pass by unfelt.

## Key Terms & Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Created being (Nivra)** | Not the will to receive but the independent intention toward the host, especially the power to decide how much the Creator is revealed. |
| **Guest and host** | The parable of the will to receive (the guest at the table) and the relation born from the host's revelation. |
| **Root of the created beings** | The restriction and concealment, held by the created being himself. |
| **Screen (Masach)** | The created being's ability to conceal the Creator and accept His revelation only for correction. |
| **Repentance (Teshuva)** | Returning the Hey (Malchut) to the Vav, the Giver, in the measure of one's independence. |
| **Barrier (Machsom)** | The first screen: wanting the Creator's revelation for correction rather than filling. |
| **First restriction** | The partition between Creator and created being, never cancelled on any degree. |
| **Importance of the Giver** | The second force received from the Creator, used against the pleasure to decide how to receive. |
| **Path of Torah / path of suffering** | The two ways to the decision to demand the Creator's revelation for correction. |
| **Surrounding light** | The light a person awakens through the society and dissemination, which brings the decision. |
| **Hole in the ear** | An inner vessel, a lack, prepared so that what one hears can finally enter. |
