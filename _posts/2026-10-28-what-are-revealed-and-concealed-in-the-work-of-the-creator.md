### Opening

How does a person move from concealment to revelation, from keeping the commandments out of fear, coercion or a wish for reward to "walking humbly with your God," working for the Creator without knowing whether He even receives it? Rav Laitman explains how the name **HaVaYaH** is acquired from below upward, why fear is the first degree beyond the barrier, what "walking humbly" means in spirituality, and what a person can actually do inside his will to receive: want only spirituality, increase that wanting through the society and prayer during the study, and let the light do the rest.

## Part 1 — Acquiring the Name HaVaYaH from Below

#### From commandment to adhesion

- Adhesion is attained through a **commandment** (Mitzva): a **coupling by striking**, where the reflected light clothes the direct light and becomes **inner light** in the vessel. Then "the dweller dwells in the soul and fills it."
- The work begins in the smallest coarseness, with **fear**, and goes on to **love**. Both are degrees of revelation **beyond the barrier**.

#### The stages of the path

| Stage | Where | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Double concealment** | Before the barrier | Sins |
| **Single concealment** | Before the barrier | Mistakes |
| **Reward and punishment (fear)** | Beyond the barrier | Fear for the sake of bestowal; the reward is the ability to bestow. The **Yud** of HaVaYaH |
| **Love** | Beyond the barrier | Receiving in order to bestow. The first **Hey** |
| **Vav** | | The action itself: what happens in the vessels as one receives in order to bestow |
| **Last Hey (Mitzva)** | | The final result of these actions: adhesion. The full name |

- This "fear" is not the fear of this world described in the *Introduction to the Book of Zohar*, but fear in order to bestow.

#### Two directions

- There is HaVaYaH in **direct light**, from above downward (Keter as the tip of the Yud, then Hochma, Bina, ZA, Malchut), and HaVaYaH in **reflected light**, from below upward.
- In attainment we don't go **Ta'amim, Nekudot, Tagin, Otiot** but the reverse: a person gathers the "letters" of this world, copies them into spirituality, and rises from the records of the vessels to the records of the lights, through the deficiencies (why the light departed and shattered) until "he's rewarded in the Ta'amim, the tastes of the Torah."
- This resolves the puzzle in the article: Malchut is called fear of the Creator, yet fear is Hochma, because Malchut that corrects herself fills with the light of Hochma. "Two forms of revelation that come one towards the other."

## Part 2 — What Is Fear?

- **Beastly fear:** the will to receive moves only by calculating whether it is filled or empty: "the fear to be empty, to lose the fulfillment."
- Fear expands with development: for myself, my family, friends, city, country, the whole world. But it is still only the recognition that "my fulfillment depends on them."
- **Fear toward the Creator** begins with faith that He controls everything: I depend on Him, so I'll do His will because He pays. Even someone who gives up this world for the next is "a very big will to receive." Rav Laitman jokes that when you get old and can't eat sweets or fatty food, "you only have your future to be worried about, and then you're called a righteous."
- **True fear** comes only after the restriction: what does the Creator get from me, with nothing coming back to me? "I don't see my results... I don't know if He feels that He's getting from me." Where do I take fuel for that? Here we demand that the Creator be revealed, only to have the opportunity to bestow.

## Part 3 — Walking Humbly

> This is called walking humbly: where a person doesn't know who he's working for and what exactly it is he's doing, what bestowal is coming from him, and if the one receiving it feels that he's receiving that bestowal from him.

- Planning such bestowal is impossible for us, let alone carrying it out. For mind, heart, planning and execution "we need a force from above," but first "a necessity for that thing."
- Crossing the barrier means emerging into a state completely independent of anything coming back to me: "the Creator doesn't know me... and I don't see Him, and I don't see that I'm doing anything for Him."
- **Q: How can the Creator not know?** "Of course He knows." What is missing is the revelation that I can act so that He doesn't know it comes from me, and agree to it, so I have no way of claiming any profit. "I truly ask to have the quality of Bina in a complete, perfect, pure way."

## Part 4 — What Can a Person Do Inside the Will to Receive?

#### Increasing the desire

- You can only increase what already exists. From **Lo Lishma**, there is a little relation to the Creator; by yearning for it "like chasing after a great lust," salvation comes from above: a different desire, a detachment from the self, "a completely different creation."
- **Q: How do I look at myself from the side?** "You need the upper force to do this for you... The light reforms. It brings me out of the will to receive and positions me off to the side."
- The demand forms within the will to receive through the study. "This is the maximum that you can do": want only what seems to you to be spirituality, until you reach the maximal will to receive, like one **lovesick**, for whom "that goal does not move from his eyes day or night."
- We have examples of love in this world precisely so we can gather "the letters" to imagine spirituality. That's why it is called Lo Lishma.
- To magnify the will: "That needs to come to you from the society. First of all, they need to tell you that this is the only thing that's important," and pray during the study "that you want only that."

#### Hastening

- Hastening means helping the will grow, be scrutinized and be used up as fast as possible. This happens mainly "through the lights, when you study, pray," praying that the light comes only for this purpose, like someone with one disease who thinks of nothing else.

#### Restriction

- **Q: Is restriction not wanting to use the desires, or not wanting the pleasure?** "It's the same thing. The desires remain." You can't change the desire, only its use. In our world holding back means I want something else; in spirituality I stay within the desire. "It's all about the intention... What I feel within my desires, that is called this world. What I feel in the intention is called that world."

## Part 5 — Pharaoh, Moses and the Exodus Within

- **Q: We should advance from greatness, but Pharaoh responds only to blows.** The picture is incomplete. The Creator is revealed to **Moses and Aaron**, the only corrected qualities in a person, but they can't leave alone. The rest of a person's desires, the Jews and the Egyptians, are still under Pharaoh.
- The **plagues** come to show the inferiority of the state and to separate the desires that tie you to this world: through them the "Jews" in a person discover for the first time that they are not Pharaoh's servants. "Without the punishments, how is that possible?"
- **Pharaoh** is the **heart of stone**, will to receive in phase four of four, which can't perceive bestowal. "There's nothing you can do with him... You simply flee from him." He doesn't die; those discernments stay in Egypt until the end of correction, which is why one may never return to Egypt.
- **Where is the way out?** Only through a desire given from above, the **point in the heart**, which is Moses. I must magnify it "to the size of the entire world," and join to it the **600,000** souls around it. "Moses cannot leave alone. He wanted to flee... but he was brought back."
- Moses is from the next degree: he grew up in Pharaoh's house, adopted by Pharaoh's daughter. These discernments are discovered "only when it is revealed, slowly, slowly."
- We want to drive the soul like a car, in control of every desire. Instead, "you want Him to do it," agreeing in advance with all things even when they seem the opposite. "There is no coercion in spirituality": first the desires change, then comes the execution.

## Part 6 — Faith, Not Intellect; Honor from the Society

- **Q: How do I know I'm studying for faith and not intellect?** By what you demand from the study. If your true prayer is to think about the Creator always, "not to forget Him for even a moment," even in order to receive, "that is sufficient. Later, that light will come and reform me."
- **Q: Should I take honor from the society, or only the greatness of the goal?** "We mustn't shut ourselves off from receiving pleasant influences." They magnify the will to receive, and "a person can't live off of negation... only positivity," like children who grow by being fed. Customs of honoring those who contribute were established by Kabbalists.
- Ultimately the Creator must fill all my desires, thoughts and intentions: "I'm full of the Creator." Whether that is in order to bestow or receive is the question. "What is the soul? Desires, in order to bestow, that is called the soul. Simple."

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **HaVaYaH is attained from below upward.** From the commandment through Torah and love to fear, the reverse of how it descends from above.
2. **True fear begins after the restriction:** fear of not bringing contentment to the Creator, with nothing coming back to me.
3. **Walking humbly** means bestowing without knowing whether He receives it or even knows it came from me: the pure quality of Bina.
4. **Want only spirituality.** Inside the will to receive, the maximum is to yearn like one lovesick; the society and prayer during the study magnify that wanting.
5. **The light does the correcting.** It takes a person out of the will to receive and sets him to the side.
6. **The point in the heart leads the exodus.** Moses must gather the other desires around him; Pharaoh, the heart of stone, is fled from, not corrected.
7. **Positive fuel is allowed.** Pleasant influences and honor from the society help growth; the question is always the intention.

## Key Terms & Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **HaVaYaH (Yud-Hey-Vav-Hey)** | The full name: here, fear (Yud), love (first Hey), Torah/action (Vav), and commandment (last Hey), acquired from below upward. |
| **Ta'amim, Nekudot, Tagin, Otiot** | The four parts of the light's descent; in attainment a person goes in reverse, from the letters up to the tastes. |
| **Walking humbly** | Bestowing without knowing whom one works for or whether the bestowal is received. |
| **Reward and punishment** | The first degree beyond the barrier, where the reward is the ability to bestow; the start of fear. |
| **Double / single concealment** | The states before the barrier: sins and mistakes. |
| **Coupling by striking** | The screen's reflected light clothing the direct light so it enters as inner light. |
| **Lovesick** | The maximal will to receive directed only at spirituality, the most a person can do himself. |
| **Restriction** | Not changing the desire but holding back its use; spirituality is felt in the intention. |
| **Pharaoh / heart of stone** | The will to receive of phase four, which cannot perceive bestowal and is fled rather than corrected. |
| **Moses / point in the heart** | The desire given from above that must gather the 600,000 souls to leave Egypt. |
| **Lo Lishma** | Wanting the Creator still for oneself; the starting point from which the light brings Lishma. |
