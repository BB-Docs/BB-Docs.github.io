### Opening

Our world is a projection of the spiritual one, so the relationship between the Creator and the created being can be told as a story of a **groom** and a **bride**. The bride is the corrected vessel, the vessel of bestowal, and when a person looks at her honestly she does not look beautiful at all: bestowal seems far away, impossible and unattractive. How, then, does one come to see her as "a beautiful and graceful bride," reach the wedding canopy, and enjoy the groom's meal without "breaching in five voices"? And what does a person do when he falls so low that he cannot even pray? Rav Laitman's answer keeps returning to one point: the force has to come from outside, from the greatness of the Creator that the society supplies, and it has to be prepared in advance.

## Part 1 — Branch and Root: The Groom and the Bride

#### From Ein Sof down to this world

- In **Ein Sof** the Creator created the created being and filled it with light. This is still "a system," a state, not yet a created being who feels, has free choice and stands under his own authority.
- The created being moves away from the Creator and in doing so builds the **worlds** (*Olamot*, from *He'elem*, concealment): **Adam Kadmon, Atzilut, Beria, Yetzira, Assiya**. Each one diminishes the light more, like five filters.
- Below them is the **barrier** (Machsom). It is not a real curtain; it is simply "the end of the light," the place where the light has been reduced to zero. This is our world.
- Here a person prepares, then begins to rise, each time receiving more light. His degree is measured by what he receives in that place.

#### Why we can speak about spirituality at all

- Our world has "the same relations between its parts as in the spiritual world." A tree here, a tree there; a table here, a table there. The concepts differ, but there is a match between the **branch and the root**, even if we don't know what that connection is.
- This is why spiritual states can be told through events of this world, such as the relation between the Creator and the created being as **groom and bride**. The Zohar's article **Leil Kallah** ("the night of the bride"), read on Shavuot, explains it beautifully.

## Part 2 — How Does One Dance Before the Bride?

#### The house of Shammai: "the bride as she is"

- The bride is a person's **corrected vessel**, the vessel in which he receives the revelation of the Creator.
- Judging by reason, by what he sees and by his own strength, a person says: this state of bestowal is above reason, I'm not capable of it, "it's not very convenient and good," and he truly rejects it.
- Rav Laitman compares this to the couples who come to him almost daily asking, "Are we suitable? Are we a match?" In spirituality too, the bride must match the groom: the vessel is suitable for the Creator only if it works **in order to bestow**, as He bestows. Only then can there be a **Chuppah** (wedding canopy).

> The groom is fine. I'm willing to receive him, full of pleasures, eternity... But to be the bride, that seems impossible to me.

#### The house of Hillel: "a beautiful and graceful bride"

- "Even though the bride does not seem beautiful, we need to say that she's a beautiful and graceful bride." This means going **above reason**: taking that state upon myself as my task even though it doesn't attract me.
- Rabash says this can be achieved "only through prayer," and through **exertion**.

#### Dancing: ascents and descents

- Dancing means jumping up and down, from one state to another. We dance **before the bride**, not before the groom, because the dancing is how we acquire her.
- "Bit by bit, through these jumps, through these ascents and descents, I acquire her corrections."
- The bride's beauty is measured by how much **intention to bestow** she has, and she can be corrected only through the **greatness of the Creator**. To the extent a person wants to correct himself, the Creator opens up to him, until he sees that the bride, which under concealment seemed unattractive and uninteresting, "is beautiful."

#### The order of the wedding

| Stage | Meaning in the work |
|---|---|
| **The bride as she is** | My honest, within-reason view of bestowal: unattractive, impossible |
| **Dancing before the bride** | Ascents and descents through which I acquire her qualities |
| **Beautiful and graceful bride** | The vessel corrected in order to bestow, seen as beautiful |
| **Chuppah and Ketubah** | Connection and adhesion, with "many details" of the conditions |
| **The groom's meal** | The Creator fills the soul: revelation of Divinity, eternity, wholeness |

- Why is it called the **groom's** meal? "Because the Creator fills the soul," so the banquet is named after Him, while the bride's vessels receive all the filling.

#### Breaching in five voices

- "One who enjoys the groom's meal and does not delight him breaches in five voices." The **five voices** are the five limitations, the five screens or discernments in us, as with the five afflictions of Yom Kippur.
- To delight the groom (or the bride) means to connect with them, to acquire their qualities. To enjoy the meal without delighting the groom means wanting all the revelation of Divinity **in order to receive**.
- "There's nothing more here than Creator and created being... and each one of us who corrects himself becomes that bride."

## Part 3 — Complete Descent: When One Cannot Even Pray

- A friend reads item 27: the advice for leaving a descent is prayer, and if he cannot pray he should pray for the power to pray. But sometimes even that is beyond him. This is called **complete descent**.
- Rav Laitman: if you are still asking how to pray, it is not yet a complete descent. In a complete descent "he cannot bring out any cause, any power" from within himself.

#### The only way out: an external cause

- What can bring him out? "Either the Creator or the environment. Who else do you have?" The environment includes everything around a person that can remind him, awaken him, shake him.
- The Creator may do it "a thousand years in the future." The environment depends on what connection you built with it. So both must be taken care of **in advance**, before the descent.

#### Savings for the time of weakness

- Everyone who works sets something aside for a pension and social insurance, so that if he falls he won't become a burden to others. Saying "the Creator is great, someone will take care of me" is "an even bigger will to receive."
- The same in spirituality: without preparation a descent "can last for half a year, a year... There's nothing that can be done. Because the society also can't help you more than what you invested into it before." The bank won't give you money you didn't deposit.
- There is a **general law** by which each person advances along his own lane according to his Reshimot. "There's no such thing as, I want to relate to that person in this way." The only choice is to **accelerate**.

> Instead of sitting for half a year, a year, maybe ten years, I sit precisely half a second and I come out of it. How to build such a mechanism? That depends only on you.

- Don't wait for a complete descent. "Even if I descend by ten percent, that's enough." Like a generator that switches on the moment the electricity fails, so that "you don't even feel there was a break," the society should take over as soon as I weaken.

## Part 4 — Suffering Behind, Pleasure Ahead

- The push toward spirituality comes from only two things: **suffering** behind me that I run away from, or **pleasure** ahead of me that I run toward "like someone crazy," until my present state seems like death compared to the next one.
- Suffering is "a long path"; it takes a long time until it teaches us that it's worthwhile to advance. The pleasure ahead is **the greatness of the Creator**, the importance of spirituality, and this is what the society must supply.
- Society is "what each of us puts into it. No more than that." Rav Laitman says that each time he visits a group abroad he absorbs their awakening and comes back able to explain better.

#### Advertising the Creator to ourselves

- "We're a commercial company. We want to sell the Creator to the whole world." Through books, tapes and lectures we sell Him to everyone except ourselves. "That's a problem."
- Learn from advertising: a TV or radio commercial you keep hearing becomes important, "and at some moment it's gonna jump up in you." What is in Coca-Cola besides sugar? Yet it sells "the taste of life."
- Every advertisement wears off and starts to feel artificial, "but that's the only exertion you can give."
- This is the **freedom of choice** article. Otherwise we advance under **general providence**, the light gradually working on the shattered Reshimot over thousands of years. **Israel** (*Yashar-El*, "straight to the Creator") is one who yearns for the Creator and hastens the time, and he can yearn only if he feels the Creator is great.

## Part 5 — Q&A: Inspiration and Coarseness

#### Two works with the friends

1. **Giving greatness:** project my inspiration and elation toward the friend, even if I don't have it, "act before him." For this I don't need to incorporate with him.
2. **Connecting:** take his desires and deficiencies as if they were my own, in order to **enlarge my vessel**. This is how I annul myself.

- If I run only because the Creator is great, the running is "without coarseness, without the will to receive... like I'll be blown up in the air." I don't participate in it. "So who am I here?"
- I discover my **coarseness** only by coming close to a friend: then "I discover his deficiencies and my deficiencies." Advancement needs the full measure of **clothing and coarseness**, a large amplitude between ascent and descent, between **Keter and Malchut**. "The problem is the pace."
- Look at humanity: "everything's moving because of envy." My coarseness has to come from society.

#### Starting from "beastly closeness"

- We begin by simply helping each other in corporeal matters. Why does it help? It shows me that "I'm not in this," a **point of truth**: I see for certain that the bride isn't beautiful and I have nothing with her. Then I must turn to the society for greatness of the Creator.
- Without incorporating in the society "you're gonna float around like an angel," staying in a small coarseness all the time.

#### Feedback from the Creator

- **Q: You said I'd get feedback from the Creator, and I don't.** Working with the friends brings an uplifting and then a descent, and "he runs away from you faster." But the ascents are bigger and the pace rises, and life feels fuller.
- An ascent that lasts too long fades: once the lights of **NRNHY** fill the vessels without renewal, "the feeling disappears," and a person goes on "like a beast." Imagine how long the pauses are between our efforts toward one another.
- Scrutinies are good only if they bring you to **deeds**, and the deed is bestowal or whatever brings me to it.

#### The common vessel

- When ten friends incorporate, each one has "ten in the power of ten," growing infinitely as they keep incorporating. Beyond that, as in Gematria where there is always "plus one," there is the **inclusive** one: the Creator revealed in that general vessel, drawing all of us ahead.

## Part 6 — Quantity and Quality: The Instructions Are There

#### "Why isn't there an instruction booklet?"

- Every machine comes with instructions. Here we get the Zohar, tests and books we can't understand. Rav Laitman: "The instructions are there. Just we want to see them according to our brain," our "beastly brain": tell me one, two, three, and I'll do it.
- Instructions written within reason could be followed within reason; these are above reason, and "it's the best possible way" to go from this state to a spiritual one. All we do is invite **the light that reforms**.

#### Introduction to TES, item 18

- If one engaged in Torah and didn't get rid of the evil inclination, he either neglected the **exertion in quantity** or neglected the **quality**.
- **Quantity:** getting up and coming to the lesson from 3 to 5:30 every morning. Even one who sleeps through it still advances by incorporating in the society, "so instead of five years it'll take twenty-five years."
- **Quality:** what are you thinking about while studying? Not "how Galgalta goes into AB-SAG," but extending the light that reforms so that it corrects me. Otherwise it is like rubbing the medicine on the body instead of swallowing it.
- The need to take the medicine depends on how ill I feel. People with cancer come to him looking "like a dog at his landlord. Please help me." "Why aren't we like that?" From myself I won't hear that I'm ill; I'll hear it from the friends, the way people who join a dieting or vegetarian society quickly become like it.
- You don't tell a friend he is lazy or a liar. "You show him that there's greatness of the Creator," as an example through your work and awakening, "not the words that will go into the ear." "The prisoner does not free himself from prison."

#### You are not asked to bestow

- **Q: I don't have the strength to do even that.** "You think as a deed that you have to do above reason... They don't tell you to jump above your head." Trying to perform an act of bestowal as if you had a **screen** is wasted effort. "It's none of your business."
- "The result doesn't depend on you, but the exertion you have to give." What is asked is only to be in constant **yearning**, like an ill person or a man chasing his love (Letter 70), and that yearning can be increased only by the external cause, the society.
- The longing the Creator gives you from above "is not your longing"; it is His action, not your addition. "You will run if you bestow it to society, and from them you'll get a result of your exertion in them." That running is yours.

> In the friends you have that amplifier, only in them.

- Rav Laitman tells a student that his daughter found the student's talk on free choice far more interesting than the Rav's: "You know how to work on an audience... learn from this." Bestow to the society and you can hear the same thing back from it, "and that's already an additional force."
- Be greater in giving to the society and smaller in receiving, lowering yourself rather than keeping "your nose up in the air." What you invested in the society is what you can get back: "You really create your own force. Not that you got it from above." Then you can come and demand correction.

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **The bride is the corrected vessel.** Seen through reason ("the bride as she is") bestowal is unattractive; going above reason, we say she is "beautiful and graceful" and dance through ascents and descents until we truly see it.
2. **The groom's meal is the Creator's filling.** To enjoy it without delighting the groom, receiving for oneself, "breaches in five voices," the five screens.
3. **Prepare for descent in advance.** In a complete descent nothing comes from within; only the Creator or the environment can lift you, and the society gives back only what you invested in it.
4. **Pleasure ahead beats suffering behind.** The society must keep "advertising" the greatness of the Creator to us, the way we try to sell Him to the world.
5. **Inspiration and coarseness together.** Running on greatness alone floats like an angel; connecting to the friends' deficiencies supplies the coarseness and widens the amplitude of the work.
6. **Quantity and quality.** Showing up is quantity; during the study, aim at the light that reforms, like an ill person who needs the cure.
7. **Not the deed, the yearning.** We aren't asked to perform bestowal; the light does that. Our part is to raise the need for it through the friends.

|---|---|
| **Groom (Chatan) / Bride (Kallah)** | The Creator and the created being; the bride is the corrected vessel that receives the revelation of the Creator. |
| **Beit Shammai / Beit Hillel** | "The bride as she is" (judging by reason) vs. "a beautiful and graceful bride" (going above reason). |
| **Dancing before the bride** | The ascents and descents through which a person acquires the corrections of the vessel of bestowal. |
| **Chuppah / Ketubah** | The wedding canopy and contract: connection and adhesion with the Creator and its detailed conditions. |
| **Groom's meal (Seudat Chatan)** | The Creator's filling of the soul: revelation of Divinity, eternity and wholeness. |
| **Breaching in five voices** | Enjoying the meal for oneself, against the five limitations or screens in a person. |
| **Leil Kallah** | "The night of the bride," a Zohar article read on Shavuot about preparing the bride. |
| **Complete descent** | A state in which a person cannot even pray for the strength to pray and can be lifted only by an external cause. |
| **Environment / Society** | The external cause, prepared in advance, that supplies the greatness of the Creator in a time of weakness. |
| **Clothing and coarseness** | Inspiration from the greatness of the Creator together with the will to receive revealed through the friends. |
| **Exertion in quantity / quality** | Coming and studying vs. aiming the study at the light that reforms (Introduction to TES, item 18). |
| **General providence / Hastening** | Advancing slowly under the general law vs. accelerating through the society; "Israel" is one who yearns straight to the Creator. |
| **The inclusive one** | The "plus one" beyond the friends' mutual incorporation: the Creator revealed in the common vessel. |
