### Opening

A friend asked Rabash: if the Creator wanted to delight His creatures with holiness and Dvekut, why all the mundane things, the labor and the pain, the whole system of Klipot? Rabash answers through The Zohar: without vessels, nothing can be perceived. The pleasure in obtaining something depends on the pain of not having it, as water tastes according to the thirst. Yet if you advised a person to eat salty food and not drink for half a day so he'd enjoy the water more, he would say "neither they nor their reward." Rav Laitman takes up exactly this: we can't build the vessel directly through suffering, so it is built indirectly, through the society and the reforming light. He goes on to the grace of holiness, the point in the heart, how completely we depend on the society, and the stages of the love of friends.

## Part 1 — The Vessel Can't Be Built Directly

- "Receiving the reward actually depends on the vessels." One who drinks water not to quench his thirst "is not considered that he is drinking; it's considered that he is receiving." Drinking is according to the deficiency, "like cold water to a weary soul."
- But who would deliberately go to prison, be sick or be humiliated so that afterward he'd feel the pleasure? "A person, since he was created from the will to receive, cannot bring himself into suffering." Even those who lived on bread with salt did so out of the will to receive.
- Building the smallest vessel for the smallest light above the barrier directly would take months or years "in a state that we are incapable of enduring even for a moment," and we would naturally flee. "Here we need to devise some other solution, and that is called the reforming light."
- Not beating myself all day, and not soothing myself that I'm progressing, but working on the will to receive "without giving it any kind of compensation, so that the deficiency remains a deficiency." With compensation it becomes bestowing in order to receive, increasing the vessels of this world, not the yearning for spirituality. Many who get up at night to study do receive payment, "it simply reaches them in a concealed way."

## Part 2 — Through the Society and the Light

- "The light builds the vessels through the recognition of His greatness." From the society a person receives recognition of the greatness of the Creator and the goal, and in contrast the baseness of his present state. "What is created within the person from these two factors is a deficiency. And this deficiency is called a vessel."
- This brings spiritual suffering, not bodily, and yearning for the Creator's greatness doesn't feel bad, "because we actually feel ourselves as though we belong to someone great." Only the light that comes afterward awakens the gap.
- The group eases it: "a trouble shared is a trouble halved. And the joy of the public also multiplies the importance and greatness of the goal."
- "All of our actions are indirect actions." First through the society, "something supernatural," which gives increased desire and direction; then through the study we draw the lights, also indirectly; and the lights gradually give us "the grace of Kedusha, the grace of holiness," so that we invert our intention to bestowal.

## Part 3 — Grace of Holiness and the Point in the Heart

- **Q: What is the grace of holiness?** When a person, instead of feeling spirituality can bring him pleasure, "begins to feel that spirituality, meaning the quality of bestowal, is good in itself." As in the four phases of direct light, phase one turns into a desire to bestow: "it remains a desire to receive, but it wants to have the form of bestowal."
- **Q: Isn't that the point in the heart?** The point in the heart is "a desire for something that exists beyond the pleasures of this world," beyond the animate (food, sex, home) and the human (money, honor, knowledge). Asking the meaning of life from a lack in those desires is different from asking from the point in the heart, "because I lack the revelation of spirituality."
- That is why many come and many leave. "The wisdom of Kabbalah, in our time, doesn't need any screening of students. Let everyone come." Those who ask from the point will have no choice but to stay.

#### Asking above time and space

- **Q: Can someone without the point receive it from the society?** One who annuls himself before the society can be so inspired that, if he is close to the point, the society accelerates him "within six months" instead of 100 or 200 years. "But that's something very, very rare."
- **Q: How can a person know what he's really asking?** He can't. "They simply come with their mouths open, ready to swallow," and the light in the study and discussions scrutinizes it in them.
- The deepest question is "where do I come from... for what purpose am I actually living," above space (not caring what happens here) and above time (what was before birth and comes after death). Asked seriously, "that's already fine."
- "A thousand enter a room and one emerges to teach," but the 999 also accelerate and stay connected to the system. "I would gladly open a beginner's course, knowing with absolute certainty that none of them would remain." Like a cell connected to the light, "the system has already begun to operate."

## Part 4 — A Deficiency Without a Deficiency

- Building a spiritual vessel is supernatural: "a deficiency for something that's totally opposed to me. There's simply nothing worse... than spirituality if I view it from corporeality."
- We don't feel how terrible it is "because we don't admit to ourselves that we'll actually have to go through death in the middle, the death of the intention to receive." The inversion happens by itself, from above, "when our attention is elsewhere," just as in the four phases the light turns phase one into phase two. "We should be so grateful that we have this means, the reforming light."
- The path of suffering also exists, but "this is terrible suffering." A man in a Russian camp came, through great suffering, to write poems in the language of Psalms without having known Hebrew; Rabash said his sensation of spirituality was true, though he couldn't measure his level. "Such paths do exist, but it's very bad."

## Part 5 — The Quality of the Effort

- **Q: Does a group cross the barrier together?** On the path of the group everything takes the form of correction. "I don't need to suffer from anything if I use the force of the society," which gives me the greatness of the Creator instead of the suffering of this world. I move from yearning for nonsense to yearning for what they advertise, go to the book, and the light corrects me.
- But the society must also explain that the light works depending on my effort: "I need to yearn for some change," though it isn't yet real in me, like a baby who learns by doing. "You nonetheless have to think about the quality of the exertion, so the light that comes during the study will change you... Without this work on the intention towards the light, you will not find success."
- **Q: If the appeal is artificial?** You want the light to give qualities "that allow you to love the society, because that is spirituality, in essence." The friends can drip into your ear how important it is. "Instead of my desire, I will use their desire... That's education in a nutshell. If we build a proper society and everyone participates in that, success is then guaranteed."

#### Two "lies" on the path

- **Q: Brainwashing with words or thoughts?** Words, thoughts, actions, every means. Even a place you don't understand, like the marble mountains of Tuscany, changes your whole interior: "that is the power of the group. We want to build such a power source." "We have no thoughts of our own"; a person cut off in space or a forest needs to be rehabilitated.
- So two things work on us: the society and the reforming light. "It's called a lack without a lack": from the society I receive a lack for something I have no lack for, the change from Lo Lishma to Lishma, "and the light then does the work."

## Part 6 — The Umbilical Cord to the Society

- **Q: Can I examine whether I'm fully dependent on the society?** It's a simple feeling. If I sink a little into life as something serious "and not as just a kind of an envelope," I must immediately feel "that I have disconnected from the society." A person with this sensitivity "just has luck from the heavens."
- "He alone can never connect... by his own power. He's completely dependent on this umbilical cord to the society." One who leaves it has no inner mechanism to remind him to return: either the Creator, through the society, pulls him back, "or nothing happens with him."
- **Q: How do I gain confidence in the society?** "You don't need to attain anything that doesn't come from the society. If you lack confidence, get it from the society." "I'm a cell in the body, fed by the entire body."

#### Problems and free choice

- **Q: Do corporeal disturbances mean I disconnected?** "It doesn't matter what kinds of problems come to the person. They're all an outcome of him having disconnected from the society." It isn't seen directly at first, but later it becomes very clear: me and the Creator, with the society as the secondary force that connects me.
- **Q: If everything is predetermined, how can my choice change what happens?** We connect to the Creator through yearning, which comes from the society; the more I'm connected to it, "to that extent I can change my feeling in the world." "Only the sensation. There's nothing else... I get up today, and the world is horrific. I get up a different day, it's a beautiful world. What changed? It's all inside."
- Why are labor and suffering called "mundane" (Chulin)? Because "the vessel is called mundane," as in the tractate Chulin: all the disturbances are the way to build the vessel.

## Part 7 — The Stages of Love of Friends

- **Q: Does love of friends go through four stages?** Yes, though we can't measure them. First we work on it without desire, then recognize it may be necessary, until we feel the necessity. At first, "I give to the society to receive from it a hundredfold the power to connect to the Creator": the society is an amplifier.
- Then "my appeal to the society is actually like the appeal to the Creator Himself, in order to bestow." Later "I discover in it the Creator... because the Creator is not elsewhere other than the society. He exists in the society as the spirit that fills it."
- "I feel that the society is like my first nine Sefirot, and I am Malchut... Then it's He and His name are one."
- "All the changes, only by the light." A person reaches the clarity that he can't change anything in himself directly: if he wants change, "he needs to make an effort so the change doesn't come from him, but rather from above."

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **Pleasure follows the vessel.** As water tastes by the thirst, spiritual fulfillment needs a deficiency, but we cannot build it directly through suffering.
2. **The vessel is built indirectly.** The society gives the greatness of the goal and the baseness of my state; the gap between them is the vessel, and the light completes it.
3. **Grace of holiness is bestowal good in itself.** Not good because it fills me, but because the quality itself is good.
4. **Let everyone come.** The point in the heart sorts students by itself; even those who leave stay connected and accelerate.
5. **A lack without a lack.** From the society I receive a desire for the change from Lo Lishma to Lishma, and the light does the work, if I attend to the quality of my effort.
6. **The society is the umbilical cord.** Every fall is a sign of disconnecting from it; whatever I lack, I take from the society.
7. **The Creator is found in the society.** Love of friends grows from an amplifier to an appeal to Him, until the society is my first nine and He is revealed in it.

## Key Terms & Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Vessel (Kli)** | A deficiency; the pleasure received is measured by it, as water by the thirst. |
| **Mundane (Chulin)** | The labor and suffering of this world; called mundane because the vessel is called so, the way to build it. |
| **Reforming light** | The light that builds the vessel and inverts the intention, the only workable means instead of direct suffering. |
| **Grace of holiness** | Feeling that the quality of bestowal is good in itself; phase one acquiring the form of phase two. |
| **Point in the heart** | A desire for something beyond animate and human pleasures, from which the real question of life's meaning arises. |
| **A lack without a lack** | A desire for spirituality received from the society for something one does not naturally lack. |
| **Quality of exertion** | Directing one's intention toward the light during study so that it will change him. |
| **Path of suffering** | Coming to bestowal through great suffering; possible but terrible, as against the path of the group. |
| **Umbilical cord to the society** | A person's total dependence on the society for every change and for his connection to the Creator. |
| **Society as the first nine** | The stage where the friends are felt as one's Sefirot above Malchut and the Creator is revealed in them. |
