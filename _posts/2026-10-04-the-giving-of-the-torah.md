### Opening

The Congress is ending, and the group has just passed through its first shared cycle: a common ascent, common hopes, and the first revelation of a **common ego**. Now comes the descent. Rav Laitman's question is what desire we actually need in order to cross the **Machsom**. His answer: the light that pulls us through, the light of **AB-SAG**, doesn't change. It acts on us only to the extent our desire resembles it, and that desire can only be a desire for connection between us. The rest of the lesson is practical: how to study, how to attend lessons, dissemination, Maaser, the structure of the world group, and finally how to leave the Congress without looking back.

## Part 1 — The First Phase Is Complete

- We went through a common ascent and common hopes. We drew the upper light, and it created something that "never happened before": a **common Kli**, a **common Hisaron**, many people around the world with one aspiration at the same time.
- This created a plus and a minus. Our effort and the light we drew awakened "the elimination of our ego," and that ego was already a common one.
- Work from below upward follows the same system as from above downward. The Creator built one general Kli; it broke, the light left, the pieces separated, and the **Machsom** appeared between them. Now we begin to sense the difference between the light and the Kli, specifically in the Kli we are trying to create.
- Before this, each one strove toward the Creator "in a chaotic way," on his own. Now connections are forming, whether we feel them or not. To the extent the bond between us matches the upper Kli, a "miniature, but similar" Kli forms below the Machsom, whether it is 200 people or 2,000.

> If we don't receive a constant illumination from above that clarifies everything within us, it's because our Kli is not yet ready.

- That sense of unreadiness, the despair that "there's not enough connection between us," has to pass through us. That is the next phase.

## Part 2 — What the Light of AB-SAG Is

- **AB-SAG** is the light that pulls us up through the Machsom. In itself it "doesn't have any colors," not Hassadim or Hochma, mercy or wisdom. "Light is just light," revealed in whatever quality the Kli desires.
- So the problem is not what the light carries, but what we want from it. If each of us wants it for his own advancement, we won't recognize it. "There is no individual advancement," only advancement within the whole squad.
- **The sleeping cat.** We live inside infinite light and don't sense it, like a cat sleeping calmly while birds and beetles move around it, untouched because none of it concerns it. We don't sense the upper light because we have fallen out of the desire for it.
- Each "dot" on its own senses nothing. The Creator is sensed only on the **common aspirations between us**, so that desire must be "very conscious, very focused."

#### What it corrects, and how to resemble it

- The light of AB-SAG can correct any egoism except **Lev HaEven** (the stony heart). Lev HaEven is corrected only through **Rav Pealim Mekabtziel**, when all the other desires are corrected and make a common Zivug, and from that the light reaches Lev HaEven.
- Every other correction of our **613 desires** is by the light of AB-SAG. It corrects our small, earthly egoism below the Machsom and the greater egoism we'll later receive from the spiritual worlds.
- **AB-SAG is the quality of Bina (SAG):** complete renunciation of my "I," and the use of all my egoism for the good of the group and the friends.
- I can't be in that state yet. I can only "play, act, artificially become similar to it." Then the light acts on us and pulls us into it.

## Part 3 — What It Means to Pass the Machsom

- "I came here to pass the Machsom" has to mean: I came to connect with everyone who aspires to exit into the upper world, and build one common Kli with them.
- The **revelation of evil** here is specific: realizing I don't correctly imagine what the spiritual world, a Kli, or a common Kli is.

> The Kli is not that I want something greater or better in my own egoism. The Kli is each one exiting himself into the connection between us.

- **Why are we scattered?** Mississippi, the Volga, the land of the kangaroo: the distances are "programmed from above." We need such yearning to be together that we build the common Kli regardless of distance. The internet was created for exactly this.
- **Why virtual?** It is purposely virtual because that state is closer to the spiritual and trains us to distance ourselves from the ego. Don't fall into small, desperate states; the more you imagine the common Kli, the closer you are.

## Part 4 — Building the Connections

- The work has two layers. First, administrative connections, "tubes and pipes," economic support, the utilitarian structure. On top of that, the spiritual work.
- No action done for the group counts unless a person sets his **intention**: why he does it, and what result he wants.
- Organize common boards and forums with moderators from each group. But protect each other from hundreds of messages, leaving time for inner work. The goal is "the most optimum and effective field of love," one that doesn't distract a person but gently arouses him.
- Disturbances arising in different countries and groups are the negative ego. Overcoming and connecting them is exactly what draws the light of AB-SAG, which "will immediately pull us through the Machsom."
- **Does the common desire have a name?** It's called the common Kli, the aspiration toward the Creator, and also **Nachshon**, after the man who was first to jump into the Red Sea to break out of Egypt.
- **How do we evoke AB-SAG?** Remind one another constantly in the virtual lessons, ask the right questions, and strive for spiritual connection with each other. Groups that cut themselves off, considering themselves self-sufficient, were "playing around like little children in their corner"; we'll try to bring them back to what Baal HaSulam writes in "The Giving of the Torah" and "The Arvut" about the common Kli and critical mass.
- Any lecture or public work done without intention and without connection to the common group "is completely devoid of spiritual aspiration," however many students or flyers. "The final spiritual effect depends only on our intention."

## Part 5 — What and How to Study

#### Sources

- Study only: Sefer Yetzira, the Zohar, Talmud Eser Sefirot, the works of the Ari, Baal HaSulam, Rabash, and Rav Laitman's books. No New Age, psychological training, or "spiritual gimmicks."
- He doesn't want people held to his books. He sees himself as "a bridge" between the modern person and the heavy original sources. Read an abridged version first, then the full translation that matches the original, then Hebrew if you can. "The closer the text is to the original, the stronger spiritually it is," and his force can't compare with Baal HaSulam's.
- Even native Hebrew speakers can't read these texts properly at first; the language isn't used in ordinary life.
- **Text that comes alive.** In the originals, additional words begin to "show through," not as miracles but because your inner Kli illuminates another layer, like the moving pictures in Harry Potter books. The letters dissolve into the nodes of a machine: forces, thoughts, laws, actions.
- **Ramchal and HaGra.** HaGra (the Gaon of Vilna) is extremely difficult; don't start. Ramchal is valuable not for understanding: he studied **Adir BaMarom** with his Rav and understood nothing. Some books are read "the way Psalms are read," to stand under "a shower of light."

| Kind of reading | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Most Kabbalistic texts | To understand, remember, and connect the worlds, Sefirot, and Partzufim mentally |
| Ramchal and similar | For a general impression, so the text acts on you |

- Reading silently or aloud makes no difference. He reads tiny scraps of Baal HaSulam's notes, two or three words; "the main thing is what it awakens in me."

#### The virtual lesson

- Attendance is essential, not for the knowledge but because thousands around the world are laboring over the same material at the same time, which creates the conditions for one common Kli.
- The best way: sit together, arms around each other, watching on a large screen in a small company, knowing others are connected at the same time. Alone at home is worse; a big auditorium is fine only if it still feels like a group lesson and not a screening.
- No distractions during the lesson, such as games on the screen. Even necessary technical duties must rotate, "because our egoism constantly offers us all kinds of tricks to get busy with something else besides the lesson."
- Broadcasts should be educational, not "Hollywood style," and should show the group asking questions. He accepted feedback that showing only himself was a mistake.

## Part 6 — Dissemination and Maaser

- "There's nothing more unifying, more connecting between us and connecting us to the Creator than the dissemination of His knowledge." The Creator's one goal is to fulfill all creatures, which means preparing the whole world Kli.
- Every member must have a task: if you can't write, read for others; if you can't stand at the metro, hand things out; if not that, make posters or set chairs. Editing correct Kabbalistic texts is more effective than writing your own.
- He was the only one in Rabash's group who disseminated, published his first small book in '83 and lectured around Israel, and he believes "this really forms a person." Not because explaining helps you understand, but because it evokes the **surrounding light**, and if you intend to raise the whole world group, "a billion times greater."
- **Maaser**: every group pays monthly, and it goes to dissemination. "Not even a discussion" if you want to participate in spiritual work. One dollar out of ten has "the same participation in the spiritual world" as a large donation.
- **Why a tenth?** You give back to the Creator the tenth that He corrects in you before the Machsom. After the Machsom, material values lose their meaning except as a means to move forward.
- The aim is a transparent, common budget for the world group, "one family that lives out of one pocket."
- **Organization.** Regional groups report weekly to the world organization, and nothing printed goes out, even internally, without approval from the center. He wants to deal only with the spiritual side, not business matters, and asks for care in building the authentic image of Kabbalah with state and religious bodies.

## Part 7 — Groups, Students, and Contests

- **Women** also need group work, mutual obligations, and connections. In spirituality, aspiration toward the Creator is "neither female nor male"; **Zachar and Nukva** are not what gender is in our world. Women's groups are separate only because of our current uncorrectedness.
- **Different groups for different people.** Israelis, Russian speakers, even VIPs who can't walk into a factory to watch a Kabbalah film: create whatever conditions make the path into Kabbalah easiest. Separate groups are welcome.
- **Merging groups?** One big group isn't better than two. Let them strive to be as one without physically becoming one; then there's something to work on. Gathering into a *Hamula* (clan) brings nothing good.
- **Contests.** Games are fine if the contest is "who loves the other more, not who's smarter." Real success is like a father letting his son win in order to teach him.
- **Who is a student?** Not appointed or elected: "the one that fulfills all the conditions necessary for spiritual development."
- **Can those who strayed return?** Yes. Often "we were to blame": a friend was overloaded with work and received too little inspiration. Once Rav Laitman realized this, he sent people to bring him back.
- **The name Bnei Baruch.** Other names may come if they take root. But Baruch is the name of Rabash, the last in the chain of Kabbalists, the name of the Creator (the Blessed One), and the name Baal HaSulam gave his firstborn: "So, let us be the firstborn."
- **Kabbalistic music** helps if it helps you, mostly in private moments, or at a gathering to match the group's state.

## Part 8 — Shame Without the Left Line

- Shame is "a very good state" to build on, and a constant one, expressed as **responsibility before the whole group**.
- If my thoughts or actions don't connect me with my friends around the world, "I'm in one boat with them, I am rowing in the opposite direction. I'm drilling a hole."
- "Any interruption, even for one minute of our joint effort and aspiration, that is betrayal." Rabash's example: a husband who spends five minutes a week elsewhere isn't with his wife the rest of the time either. "In adhesion, there cannot be a break."
- Every moment is optimal. There's no need to look for better circumstances or a better inner state; the Creator gives the best conditions for each of us and all of us at every moment.

## Part 9 — No Tears, No Looking Back

- **Glimpses of openness** come and go quickly. They will stay only when we form the authentic desire for the correcting light of AB-SAG and know what it should correct in us.
- **No farewells.** When he came to Rabash, he was shocked by how calmly the students met a friend's death and their mother's Shiva. Later he understood: we're always moving to a better state. Even the worst state is the revelation of the Kli of a higher state.
- **Hamtaka (sweetening).** Bring the Creator into the worst state, understand it comes only from Him, and the state softens and becomes sweet. When He pushes the ego down while you aspire higher, the Partzuf grows.

> Remove these bodies, and you will see this one common aspiration upward, and live in it.

- **Lot's wife** looked back and became a pillar of salt. Old states don't exist; "there is no past."
- "There's no coercion in the spiritual world." The light won't correct me by force; I must desire the correction. That depends only on us.
- He was surprised that three or four days were enough to go through the entire cycle, and calls the world group the foundation "from which the last generation will be created."

## Part 10 — Crossing the Machsom and the Holidays

- The crossing is the next state, our common Kli. For the first time we received a common ego, made connections, and will now feel a rupture between us. If we aspire to remove it, we evoke the surrounding light as **Ohr AB-SAG** and are corrected.
- It doesn't have to happen on Pesach or Sukkot. Wait for it every second, as a person must await the **Mashiach**: "this very Ohr AB-SAG, it's called Mashiach."
- The order of the path: Egypt, receiving the Torah, building and destroying the idol (the connections being destroyed, which we'll feel now), then the desert and building the Temple, when Malchut and Bina join.

| Stage | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Six days of Sukkot | Hesed, Gevura, Tifferet, Netzach, Hod, Yesod; the surrounding light acts on a person |
| Leil Hoshana Rabbah | The seventh day; the surrounding light enters Malchut |
| Simchat Torah | "The joy of the light": the light can enter the Kli, filling it in VAK |
| Purim | Keter, Hochma, Bina are joined |

- Tomorrow evening we leave the Sukkah, and by law can't return to it: "there's nothing to regret, no reason to go back, and only forward and upward."

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **The common Kli is new.** For the first time the group has a shared ego and shared Hisaron, and must now feel the despair of not yet being connected enough.
2. **The light doesn't change; our desire does.** AB-SAG is revealed only in the quality the Kli wants, and it is sensed only on the aspirations between us.
3. **Resemble Bina by acting.** We can't yet renounce the "I," but we can play at it, and that draws the light that pulls us through the Machsom.
4. **Intention is everything.** Lectures, flyers, and organization without intention for the common group have no spiritual effect.
5. **Study the originals, together.** Move from abridged texts toward the sources, and attend the virtual lesson as a group, side by side.
6. **Dissemination and Maaser bind us.** Every member needs a task, and the tenth is given back to the Creator for correction.
7. **No looking back.** Every state is optimal and leads higher; bring the Creator into a bad state and it is sweetened.
