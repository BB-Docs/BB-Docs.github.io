### Opening

With the Congress five days away, the lesson asks why we need to gather at all. If the Creator can be revealed anywhere, what does the Congress add? Rav Laitman's answer is that the upper light is revealed only in the connection between us. The Congress is a framework that presses us together, so that we care for one another and attach ourselves to the same system. From there he explains why every day must feel new, why our deficiencies come from the lack of connection, and what it means to build the **Shechina** out of the broken pieces each of us carries.

## Part 1 — Why the Congress

- "Only through connection between us do we reach a state where the upper light can be revealed." This is where the revelation of the Creator and of the upper system takes place.
- It is not that we can't reach this outside the Congress. But the Congress means that "we connect together. We're more pressed together, we care for each other, we make sure that we're all connected to the same system."
- The purpose of our corrections is that the Creator "will be revealed in us and will rejoice from us."

## Part 2 — Every Day Is New

- Don't be disappointed if what we reached disappears again. As it is written, they shall be "as new in your eyes."
- Every day a person has to start anew, and it seems to him that everything he did yesterday is gone. That's because it really is new: new discernments, new **Reshimot** from the shattering. The society also seems new to him.
- "Get up in the morning with a new song in your heart."
- So each morning we need to renew the questions: What is a Congress? What connection should we have every day, in the lessons and outside them? Does our gathering influence the correction of the general vessel, "our general soul that for the time being is broken"?

## Part 3 — Equivalence of Form and the Records of the Path

#### Why bestow upon the Creator

- Commenting on the first excerpt ("to establish a society where each of us follows the spirit of bestowing upon the Creator"): we need to reach **equivalence of form** with the Creator.
- First, because that is the purpose of creation, to reach **adhesion** (Dvekut) with Him.
- The Creator wants to benefit His created beings. "The greatest and really the only benefit that exists in reality" is to bring them to His own degree, His state, "and His state is absolute bestowal."
- So we need to start taking a few steps toward it.

#### Records of the complete connection

- The Creator created us and broke the connection between us. But in that breaking we have Reshimot (records) of the good, correct state, the complete connection.
- We already carry records of the whole path that each of us, and all of us together, must go through until the end of correction.
- Our work is to awaken these records and try to make them real. "The Creator does it, but according to our deficiency."

#### Where the deficiencies come from

- The Creator performs the corrections. **We discover the deficiencies for those corrections from the lack of connection between us.**
- The Creator also shows us every condition in which we don't want to connect. On the other hand, if we want to achieve the goal we have to connect, "and only in that is where we stand."

## Part 4 — Building the Shechina from the Broken Pieces

- A person has to have a family, work, take care of his health, and all of that is true. "But what is all of this for?" So that he gradually understands that he must be truly connected to everyone in a single desire.
- That collective desire, which the friends put together between them, is called the **Shechina**: the vessel in which the upper force, the Creator, is revealed.
- Each of us holds one part of it. A person who tries to build the Shechina by putting its parts together, gathering the broken pieces by connecting with the friends, comes closer to a state where he receives help.
- Over time the friends feel that it isn't a physical connection but an **internal** one: in their desires, their passions, their intentions. The desires shift from egoistic ones to desires of the upper world, of bestowal, love and connection.

> To the extent that they want more internal connection, to that extent they feel that the Creator is with them.

- In this way, starting from the deficiencies, they gradually reach connection with the Creator.

## Part 5 — Questions

#### How does connection between us bring connection with the Creator?

- If we really try to read what the Kabbalists wrote, see their texts as new every day, and reach a state where we want to make them real, "it happens."
- "A few days, a few years, but eventually it happens."

#### What can we add besides annulment?

- **Q (Tel Aviv): What can we add from ourselves other than annulment?** "There is no more."
- Only annulment toward the friends, toward our teachers Rabash and Baal HaSulam, and toward all the laws they tell us about. On these the wisdom of Kabbalah stands: the system of the upper world, "of bestowal and love emerging out of oneself towards the others."
- "This is what we need. Besides that, nothing."

#### What does it mean for the Shechina to dwell between us?

- **Q: What does "dwell" mean?** We are parts that came from the shattering of the Shechina. "The Shechina is the field in which the Creator..." (the recording ends here).

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **The light is revealed only in connection.** The Congress is a framework that presses us together, so that we care for one another and attach to one system.
2. **Every day is new.** When yesterday's state disappears, it's because new Reshimot and new discernments have come. Get up with a new song in your heart.
3. **Equivalence of form is the only real benefit.** The Creator wants to bring us to His own degree, absolute bestowal, and we start with a few steps toward it.
4. **Deficiencies come from the lack of connection.** The Creator makes the corrections, but we find what needs correcting where we fail to connect.
5. **Each of us holds a piece of the Shechina.** Gathering the broken pieces through the friends builds the collective desire where the Creator is revealed.
6. **The connection is internal.** It lives in desires, passions and intentions, and the Creator is felt to the extent that we want it.
7. **Nothing to add but annulment.** Annulment toward the friends, the teachers and the laws of bestowal and love is everything we need.

## Key Terms & Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Congress (Convention)** | A gathering of the world Kabbalah group, a framework that presses the friends together so that the upper light can be revealed in their connection. |
| **Equivalence of Form** | Becoming similar to the Creator in bestowal; His state is absolute bestowal, and bringing us to it is the only real benefit. |
| **Dvekut (Adhesion)** | The purpose of creation: adhesion with the Creator, reached through equivalence of form. |
| **Reshimot (Records)** | Records of the complete connection and of the whole path to the end of correction, left in us by the shattering. They come anew every day. |
| **Shattering** | The Creator breaking the connection between the souls, which leaves each one holding a broken piece of the whole. |
| **Deficiency (Chisaron)** | The lack that the Creator answers with correction, discovered from the lack of connection between us. |
| **Shechina** | The collective desire that the friends build between them, in which the Creator is revealed. We are its broken parts. |
| **General soul / general vessel** | The single broken soul that our connection gradually corrects. |
| **Bitul (Annulment)** | Annulling oneself toward the friends, the teachers and the laws of bestowal and love; "there is no more" than this to add. |
