### Opening

Rabash writes that a person feels his lowliness because he feels the Creator's greatness. When we want to bestow upon the Creator, the evil inclination depicts the Shechina in the dust: whatever we give is enough. One who works for reward, by contrast, easily praises the Giver. On the path of Baal HaSulam, where all one's thoughts and desires are to be for the Creator's benefit, the descent comes at once, and we must not be impressed by it, since "many pennies join into a great amount." The advice is not to wait to be lowered: "Before I lose, I search." The keeping is mainly during the ascent. Rav Laitman builds the whole lesson on this: why the Creator's importance must become fuel to resemble Him rather than a filling, why every transgression begins in the ascent, why only a society can give us a lack that nature can't, and how the society itself rises through the levels of the soul of Adam HaRishon.

## Part 1 — Importance as Fuel, Not Filling

- "Ultimately what we need to attain through our work is the importance of the Creator," so that He is more important to us than our will to receive, "and then giving to Him will serve for us as reception."
- Why isn't He revealed as important now? If we saw Him openly we'd yearn to cling to Him "because of the will to receive. So the will to receive enjoys it."
- Because of concealment, the importance of the Creator can become "fuel for us to come to be like Him." Not bestowing on Him because He is important and that pays off, "but rather my bestowal to Him will be because I want to resemble Him, which is called adhesion," because His quality of bestowal is higher than my quality of reception.

#### Which lack moves me?

- If we yearn for the Creator because we're in a descent, where He awakens an empty space in us, "then we act due to the lack in the will to receive," and look for His importance to cover that lack.
- "The right correction is during the ascent," when a person feels no lack in the will to receive and still longs to come close, "because the quality of bestowal is truly important to him." He watches not the departure of his feeling but the departure of the importance of the Giver, "and he starts seeing himself as always being in a descent, because you can always add to the importance of the Creator."
- "That's what we actually need to distinguish in our work, to see exactly what makes us work." If the empty vessels remind us, the Creator is doing it. If from good states we yearn for Him, "it means that we ourselves are looking for the lack."

## Part 2 — Every Transgression Begins in the Ascent

- "All of our mistakes are of ascent." When I have some importance of the Creator and some filling in my vessels, "but I'm not looking for an addition, that is then called the sin. That is the beginning of the descent."
- We come to the lesson, we feel good, we reach a good state in the group, "and we begin to become comfortable in that state, to settle for it." A week later the state has deteriorated "and we don't know where it came from." A week or two ago everyone sat yearning before the lesson, and today they barely arrive on time.
- "Satisfaction and lack have to always exist together in our work, in parallel," because we are made of the will to receive and He is bestowal. If we just cling to an inspiration, a good feeling, without understanding what it rests on, "then we begin to fall back. The search for the Creator's importance is never-ending."

#### Adding without a descent

- An ascent means a good feeling of belonging to spirituality together with some recognition of the Creator's greatness. "If I yearn from a good state, then it's me yearning." If I yearn because I feel empty and bad, "I'm searching because I feel bad... The vessels of reception shouldn't be what determines what I yearn for."
- By magnifying the importance of bestowal within the state I'm in, "it's as if I increase the Galgalta ve Eynaim and then I can also add to the AHP, more and more, without a descent," climbing to a higher GE and a greater AHP.

## Part 3 — Above Nature, and Only Through the Society

- **Q: If I feel good, why would I look for deficiencies? It's above nature.** "You are correct... this search from the good state, searching for the importance of bestowal, is a search that happens above nature." Here I truly demand that the Creator reveal Himself and that the study shine on me, "that this concept of the Bestower will be so important to me that it really dominates my field of vision."
- From a descent I demand because I feel bad, "and it's not Him who's important then." I look to belong to the Great One so I'll feel safer. "That's what religions sell to people... You're just given this psychological method for settling for little." "Spirituality is the Bestower, the Creator is the Bestower; don't translate it into any other word."

#### The only place of free choice

- **Q: There's no alarm clock that rings when I'm in an ascent.** "This is the only place in which a person has free choice to make, and that's done through the group, through the environment. When I feel bad I don't need the environment; I run either way."
- For what exists in nature I don't need a society; "maybe they can sell me Coca-Cola." "I need a society where something above nature exists. And that's why without the group it's impossible to advance."
- "I need the group only for one thing, the importance of the Creator, the importance of the Bestower," not because He fills me but because "I want to be like Him." "The society actually needs to cause me an ascent from my state of ascent." "To invent a lack which is not a lack, only a unique society can do that for me... And that is actually my only choice."

## Part 4 — Supporting the Ascent in the Group

- **Q: We said to support those in a descent; now it seems we must support those in an ascent?** We must help people out of a descent as much as we can, but one who can't come or sleeps through the lesson is "a hospital already." "We're talking about how we need to advance from one healthy state to a more healthy state."
- "In each state of ascent, we need to see ourselves as already lacking the greatness of the Creator, and in that to help each other." There must be "a burning fire" of the Creator's greatness in the society all the time, and if a society can't reach it, "simply run away from such societies" and build one where this is the only goal.
- Everything, films, books, lectures, must lead to this. For beginners it opens slowly, since a person can't yet yearn for something above nature. But among advanced students "it needs to be only that." One who draws attention to himself and hinders the friends from connecting through the greatness of the Creator "is dangerous," and must be dealt with, even to the point of removing him.

#### What it means to yearn to be the Bestower

- **Q: It seems abstract. How can I check myself without seeing the Creator?** "Love your friend as yourself gives you the ability to check and see to what extent you are in bestowal. Full stop." "Don't add more words."
- I can measure ascent and descent by how full I feel, or measure myself against the Creator: not as the source of my good, nor as one who needs me to do His commandments, but "as an example for me," to belong to the upper one above the Parsa. "I cannot actually do such a thing to myself," but if like a little child I make an attempt, as in a game, the force from above does it. "This is called I have labored and I have found."

## Part 5 — Ride Above the Will to Receive

- **Q: What do we gain each time?** A more serious game, though still "a game below the barrier." Each time a greater will to receive is added, with ascents and descents in good and bad and in true and false. Above the barrier one receives something of the scrutiny of Adam HaRishon, "for whom bitter and sweet and true or false were as one thing."
- The ascents and descents differ by how much will to receive is added, dressed in money, honor, knowledge, sex, food or rest. We never work with the bare will to receive and "we can never measure this, nor do we need to." Even "when the Temple was destroyed, the taste of intercourse remained": it's confusing, and follows the relation of branch and root.
- "Our work needs to be above this, in the importance of godliness." "Someone who begins to deal with his will to receive receives a doubled will to receive," divided into more and more forms that bury him. "All of our filth, the entire will to receive with all of its fulfillments, we should simply ride above it."

#### The flashlight

- The will to receive is cunning, "and it constantly catches you and twists you around however it wants, the moment you pay attention to it." Work with it, but "internally your inner point needs to be connected to the Creator... If you don't do that, then you're lost. You're under Pharaoh's control."
- "Position a kind of flashlight before you, so that the One who bestows is what's important... and only increase the importance of this flashlight so that it occupies my entire field of vision."
- Once you grasp the Creator, you see the will to receive helps you: "Pharaoh brings you closer to the Creator, to redemption... there is nothing bad in reality." So our attitude to the friends and the study must begin with "my determination that the Creator is the goal," and then the society becomes a means, "rather than me building a closed community." "Only one point. The importance of godliness, that's it."

## Part 6 — Building a Correct Society

- **Q: How can I tell I'm not deluding myself?** "Only in the society can I check this. I have no other lab." Forcefully instill the importance of the Creator into the society, "so that it truly becomes the message of the society," and draw from the collective force as your fuel. "We need to magnify the Creator in the society."
- "Love your neighbor as yourself, which you practice in the society, is a means of seeing the Creator in the society. It turns out that you, the society and the Creator become one, under one authority."
- **Q: Couldn't a group just feed its ego on this?** A group that studies the correct books and thinks about the Bestower will reach what is correct, even if at first "whoever gives to me is the one who bestows." Gradually, through the surrounding light, the inversion happens: "being one who bestows is important" in itself. Such a group "won't be able to deceive itself" and will see its deficiencies in bestowal. Only the upper force oversees us, so keep depicting it as precisely as possible. "To love Him means... not like I love fish," but to want His quality.

#### Like a sun

- There are thousands upon thousands of states; the pace depends on how much this burns in us through the society. "Unfortunately, the society does not take sufficient care to be truly united around this... That's called the health of the society."
- Every time we reach some yearning to be like the Bestower, the will to receive renews itself, giving room to decide again. To speed things up, each one must make the society "like a bomb that sends out waves," like the sun "constantly exploding and burning" so its radiation reaches everyone.
- "Out of all your activities in life, only in this point is it worthwhile for you to invest, because here you truly are the one acting." The rest "has already been signed and written in advance." A few properly used years are enough: "There is no person who cannot reach the end of correction in a single incarnation."

#### Quality of effort

- "Effort operates through its quality": narrowing your field of vision to this one point, and how strongly you press on it.
- **Q: If during the lesson I see a friend who looks detached, do I ask for lights or go help him?** Pray for him as you demand forces for yourself and the society. Being physically close does help, "that's why we are sitting here together, not studying virtually." External acts of bestowal and organizing are worthwhile up to a limit, "as long as you don't become immersed in them and abandon the quality of effort," writing new regulations and systems and drowning your state in them.

## Part 7 — The Society on Every Level

- **Q: How do we make our advertising more real?** It doesn't need to be: "this is already implanted in our nature. We listen to advertising." A strong society that cultivates the importance of the Creator's quality, "not to receive from Him, rather to be like Him," will influence us.
- We are all parts of Adam HaRishon, connected in one system, and "the light can't be revealed by each individual separately, but only by the whole system." "The system itself was not shattered. Only our feeling that we are connected to each other was shattered." In truth we are already in the corrected form, in Ein Sof.
- **Q: Is annulment bestowal upon the society?** It depends what concept in the society you annul yourself before. "It's not simply choosing and annulling yourself. It's building it, and building an attentive ear toward it so that you are impressed only by what you truly choose."
- "Society exists on all levels up to Ein Sof." When you want spirituality from it, you raise it and yourself to a higher level of connection. "We're not building anything new through this. We're only raising ourselves to a different level of connection between us," closer to the soul of Adam HaRishon. That level depends on how strong my love of the friends, my annulment and my desire for this quality become. "So it's possible to be in the youngest possible society and to get from it much more than they themselves even think."

#### From bitter and sweet to true and false

- **Q: How do we move from bitter and sweet to true and false, when we don't feel truth?** "That's all our work." Beasts have only bitter and sweet, "therefore they don't mistake." Some people feel no truth at all; others will die for their opinion, a kind of truth relative to bitter and sweet. All education develops this on the human level.
- On the spiritual degree, truth is bestowal and falsehood is reception, "and you don't feel it... and you have no supporters towards holding on to Him. And this is all so that you will demand the Creator." This is called the seal of the Creator, who stamps His form upon you.
- Take the right line, the truth, and as much as you can, join your bitter and sweet to it, imprinting the Creator's quality on your matter more and more, "until... the name of the Lord will be upon you."
- A caution: none of this is for young groups on the outside. "We have to be careful to work according to the plan and not according to what you heard today." As the Rambam writes, beginners receive Lo Lishma in small measure, and only gradually is it opened to them what the Bestower is and how far it is from our nature.

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **The Creator's importance is fuel, not filling.** Concealment lets His greatness become a reason to resemble Him rather than a pleasure for the will to receive.
2. **"Before I lose, I search."** The keeping is in the ascent; the true correction is yearning from a good state, because bestowal is important, not because I feel empty.
3. **Settling into a good state is the sin.** Every descent begins in an ascent that stopped looking for more importance of the Creator; satisfaction and lack must always go together.
4. **Free choice is only in the environment.** Only a society can invent a lack that isn't a lack, giving an ascent from the ascent; that is a person's one choice.
5. **Ride above the will to receive.** Don't deal with its forms; keep the "flashlight" of the Bestower filling the whole field of vision, and even Pharaoh becomes a help.
6. **Magnify the Creator in the society.** Love of friends is the only lab to check bestowal; make the group a sun of radiation, and invest only there, with quality of effort.
7. **The society exists on every level.** The system was never shattered; by choosing a higher level of connection, one rises toward the soul of Adam HaRishon and from bitter and sweet toward true and false.

## Key Terms & Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Shechina in the dust** | The evil inclination's depiction of Divinity as lowly when one wants to bestow, so that whatever one gives seems enough. |
| **"Before I lose, I search"** | (Shabbat 152) Searching for more importance of the Creator while still in an ascent, before the state is lost. |
| **"I awaken the dawn"** | King David's way: initiating the work oneself instead of waiting to be awakened by a descent. |
| **No absence in spirituality** | A departed state has only withdrawn to make room for work; whatever was sorted remains in holiness. |
| **Ascent / descent** | Measured either by one's own filling or against the Creator's importance; in the latter, a person always sees himself as lacking. |
| **Importance of the Creator** | His being more important than one's will to receive, so that giving to Him serves as reception; the fuel to resemble Him. |
| **Free choice** | The single point of choosing and building an environment that gives the importance of bestowal, a lack beyond nature. |
| **Quality of effort** | Narrowing one's attention to the one point of the Creator's importance and pressing on it as hard as possible. |
| **Soul of Adam HaRishon** | The single system connecting all souls; it never broke, only our sense of connection did, and society exists in it on every level up to Ein Sof. |
| **Bitter and sweet / true and false** | The beastly scale of pleasure and pain versus the spiritual scale where bestowal is truth and reception falsehood. |
| **Seal of the Creator** | His form of truth stamped upon a person's matter as he joins his bitter and sweet to the right line. |
