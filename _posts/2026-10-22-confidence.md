### Opening

What is confidence, and what is its absence? Rav Laitman begins by admitting that confidence is "a very complicated matter," hard even to define. His answer is that confidence cannot come from a person's own sense of being covered. It comes as a consequence of the light of faith received from above, and it frees a person from thinking about himself. From there the lesson turns to the group: how the relations between friends must copy the qualities of the Creator, how to handle friction, how to use the right and left lines, and how a person enters the group as an embryo.

## Part 1 — What Is a Lack of Confidence?

- A lack of confidence is a vessel that feels itself as empty. Or a vessel that feels it could be full, but filled with pleasure rather than with the feeling of the presence of the **source** of the pleasure.
- All our feelings come from the light: whether it is in us or not, whether we feel it as pleasure or as the feeling of the Creator.

#### First restrict, then use it to bestow

- The first correction of any deficiency, including a lack of confidence, is to rise above it and not feel it.
- No inclination was created in us just so we would erase it, "but rather for us to use it" as we adapt to the Creator.
- So a person first makes a **restriction** (Tzimtzum) on the lack of confidence until, from the side of his will to receive, he no longer feels any deficiency in it. Then "he takes out of his sack this lack of confidence that remains there but restricted," and begins to use it in order to bestow.
- "This is what we need to do with each and everything that's in us." We can't imagine how even inclinations to kill or steal could be used to bestow, but when we restrict and study them, "we'll see that there's a very great and special use of them."
- So the first correction for confidence is the general correction of the desire to receive: a restriction, reaching the feeling that "he will never lack anything."
- **How can I be sure I'll never lack anything, when there are millions of deficiencies I haven't felt yet?** The general feeling of the Creator is enough. It covers all the deficiencies even before they awaken. Each soul has its own personal work in this.

## Part 2 — Confidence Comes from the Light

- If a person still thinks about himself, about what he lacks, or is sorry about his state, "it's a sign that he doesn't have the feeling of confidence."
- **Confidence is a consequence of the light of faith** that a person receives from above: the **Kli** of **Hassadim** filled with the light of Hassadim, which doesn't need anything else.
- Then a person doesn't even think whether the Creator is doing something for his benefit. There is no thought of "his benefit" at all. All his thoughts become directed outward.
- "And then he becomes free of himself and can work in order to bestow."

#### Confidence and the friends

- In the article on **Arvut** (mutual guarantee), Baal HaSulam describes something similar: a person feels that 600,000 faithful lovers stand around him and care for him, so he won't lack anything. Rav Laitman said this belongs "to the same correction."
- But even this doesn't come from the whole world. "Never can one person supply anything to another. It always has to come through the Creator."
- For real corrections between us, "we need to include the third partner, which is the Creator," and connect only on His conditions and by His laws.
- The Creator supplies confidence according to my relations with the friend, according to the deficiency I bring to it.

## Part 3 — We Advance Only from Deficiencies

- People write that they can't get along in the group, or that they get along today and not tomorrow. "They think that we're advancing from good things. We advance only from deficiencies."
- "The calm needs to be from the fact that there is no calm." Without new problems each time, there is no advancement.
- "The biggest problem is indifference. Not caring. That's the biggest trouble in the world."

#### Copying the Creator onto the society

- Always check whether what happens between us is connected to the Creator at all, or whether we are completely disconnected from Him.
- "Instead of turning to Him, I turn to the group, to the friends. But with that, I must include Him." He is the goal and the one who sets the conditions.
- His qualities are the stature I want to reach, so they are the conditions by which I must shape my society: its boundaries, framework and relationships. "I need to take the qualities of the Creator and gradually print them upon my society."
- Wherever things don't yet match, I should enlarge that gap in my eyes. Then my prayer brings correction, and a greater gap is revealed, between me and the society and between us and the Creator.
- "The laws in the society between the friends are the same laws that need to be in the Creator," because the ideal law in the society is to bestow.
- "The group isn't just the means to reach the Creator. The group is that general vessel in which you will see the revelation of the Creator."

## Part 4 — Handling Friction: Restriction First

- **Q: If friction is revealed between people, what do I do?** The first correction is to "turn it off," which "does not mean to erase. It means to control it," like a restriction: forcefully act as if there is no friction.
- Today's friction may come from nerves or circumstances. So first act as though it isn't there, until everything is "rosy." Only then open it up gradually and learn from it "from the side," as if it doesn't belong to us, to see what it was showing us and what needs correcting.
- "We need to accept the law of unity above all things."
- Someone who deliberately makes "a hole in the side of our ship," refusing to subjugate himself to the society, has no place in it, except perhaps as an observer. But with temporary problems, "we must first forcefully enter the unity."
- You can be angry at each other for a few minutes, but the same day it must be closed "in a nice way." Not a compromise, but in order to reach the correct scrutiny of what was revealed.
- Each person's force of restriction is different. Rav Laitman didn't want to give the groups much advice on this: "Each group will find the correct way."

## Part 5 — The Right Line and the Left Line

#### Twenty-three and a half hours

- Baal HaSulam writes that a person should be on the **right line** twenty-three and a half hours a day, and spend half an hour in **critique** on the left line.
- The critique must be purposeful: at the end of it, it must cause an ascent, not a descent. "We have no forces for more than that."
- The right line isn't about watching the clock. It means solving all problems, thoughts and issues by adhering them to the right line.
- The right line is **Katnut** (smallness). From there I go to the left and critique my vessels of reception, taking as much as I can from the left and attaching it to the right. "And this will truly be the AHP that returned to the GE."
- I may open the left line only when I'm confident I have the strength to stay at my degree or rise above it, and not fall in my attitude to the friends or the Creator.
- The half hour is not permission "to corrupt the friends and curse the Creator and spit on them." The right line keeps the happiness, the joy.

#### What the right line is

- "The right line doesn't mean to be foolishness." It means opening all your reason and intellect, but through the Creator, not through egoistic impulses. Through egoism you can't see anything good.
- After the vessels of reception are corrected by the right line, the **middle line** is built from them, and then a person begins "to think like an Adam and not like a beast." Only after all the corrections is there correct thought in the vessels of reception.
- **Q: What does it mean to open the mind through the Creator?** Standing very firmly on the right, I deliberately open the left and examine: "Let's hear the donkey, the horse there."
- Before that, on the right line, "I don't even hear myself." I hear the great ones who tell me who is above me arranging all of life: "There is none else besides Him. That's the right."
- The right line is like the **embryo**: everything from the upper one is good for it. It has no intellect of its own and uses the upper one's. The **Rosh** of the embryo's **Partzuf** is the upper one; it is itself just a **Guf**.

## Part 6 — Measuring the Greatness of the Creator

- **Q: With what vessels do I discover the greatness of the Creator, quantity or quality?** "We can measure only against our vessel."
- He is great when He is stronger than me: He presses me from all sides, "putting His hand upon me from above," and I have no choice. Afterward I understand that this is good.
- The Creator created in me the specific discernments by which I feel Him, the nine qualities. "He could have entered into me a million other discernments." How much more He has, "how can you even say?" "According to my vessel I choose and determine."

## Part 7 — The Group as a Copy of the Creator

- "The society needs to be a copy of the Creator." The friends are egoists without corrections, but they must understand that they are building a machine between them whose structure resembles the **ten Sefirot**, where the relationship between the parts is "as pure bestowal."
- Why "as"? Because we are still egoists. "We're seemingly playing at this still," wanting these actions to bring that form.
- **Q: If I tell a friend what I think of him, won't it disturb our connection?** Each of us should know more about the other: his qualities, his conditions at work and at home. Some intimate things no one discloses, but there are things the other should know about me because it helps him connect to me.
- Learn one another, sit together and talk. But always against the background of connecting to the Creator. "Otherwise... what is it? A group of psychotherapists?"

## Part 8 — Entering the Group as an Embryo

- **Q: How does conception (Ibur) work in the group?** I annul myself before the group's laws and want them to rule me.
- When I feel resistance and no desire, and still subjugate myself to the group's law, "in this measure, I became an embryo." This is "a huge effort": upon his entire desire to receive, the embryo takes on the upper one's reason and lets it influence him 100%.
- Rather than wanting to be the head, a person says: "I want to be the smallest of the whole society. Whatever's in the society I accept. Whatever they think, I'll think." The fact that I disagree means I can be greater later. "But for now... the group is like a mother."
- Like a child, you learn by copying. "There's no other example in all of nature that one can learn in a different way."

#### Playing the example

- "Do I love you? No. If I were to love you, I wouldn't need a group." But we must show each other good behavior. You are impressed as if I'm already at a higher degree, you copy it, "and you already by this ascend."
- Even if everyone is a "cheater" inside, each presents a nice role to the others, and the "beast" in the other receives it as real.
- The same is true of raising children: "The only real true education is for you to give examples." The Soviet attempt to educate without example "is not sustainable."
- If a person comes to believe his own example, good: it becomes his second nature.

#### The game is not the point

- The games are fine, but what obliges you must come first: "There is none else besides Him."
- "You can't start any action if you don't feel that you are at peace with the Creator," that His presence and control of reality compel you to act. This is the preparation, and the society should help you hold on to it.
- Reminding one another to say "with the help of God" is not a simple custom. It means joining Him in each thing and going according to His way.

#### Nine months of conception

- **Q: When complaints come up, how do I connect them to the group?** Don't criticize; always subdue yourself to the society. Each time more will to receive is revealed, you annul it.
- All the discernments of the **coarseness of the root**, everything from now until **Ein Sof** that belongs to you, enter during conception and you restrict them. Each detail adds up to the "nine months of pregnancy." "So you went through another moment of life as an embryo."
- **Q: Should the group's reason be fixed or dynamic?** What needs to be known is the first nine Sefirot of the Creator that **Malchut** must copy onto itself, and to copy them onto the group. The example is Rabbi Shimon's group of ten students, each with a specific quality of the nine Sefirot, with **Rabbi Shimon** gathering them.

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **Confidence comes from above.** It is the consequence of the light of faith filling the Kli of Hassadim, not of feeling covered by the world or the friends.
2. **Confidence frees a person from himself.** As long as he thinks about his own lacks or his state, he has no confidence; with it, all his thoughts turn outward to bestowal.
3. **Restrict first, then use.** Every inclination, including a lack of confidence, is first restricted and then used in order to bestow. Nothing in us was created to be erased.
4. **The Creator is the third partner.** Nothing passes between friends except through the Creator, and the society must be built by printing His qualities onto it.
5. **We advance only from deficiencies.** Indifference is the greatest danger. Friction is first suppressed for the sake of unity, then studied from the side.
6. **Right line most of the day, purposeful left line briefly.** Twenty-three and a half hours adhered to the right, half an hour of critique that must end in an ascent.
7. **Enter the group as an embryo.** Annul yourself before the group's laws, copy the good examples of the friends, and begin every action from "there is none else besides Him."
