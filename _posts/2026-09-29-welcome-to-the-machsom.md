### Opening

We naturally feel either this world or, before birth and after death, the upper world. To feel both at once has to be earned. How do we move toward a degree we don't have yet? Rav Laitman describes two kinds of work. The first draws the surrounding light through study and shared actions. The second is bolder: we **play the higher degree**, modeling among the friends the relationships that exist above the **Machsom**. The key feeling to cultivate is **shame**: "I'm ashamed to look my friends in the eye, because I want to use them for myself."

## Part 1 — One Point, Two Worlds

- Our sensation is "a point that senses either the Creator or His opposite, the complete consumer." Before birth the soul is like a point within the Creator; then that sensation vanishes and it finds itself in this world with no memory.
- Sensing both worlds at once isn't given; it's attained through our own effort and free will. This world is the "atmosphere" specifically set up around that point, the optimal place to build from it a **Partzuf**, a Kli equal to the Creator.
- We attune ourselves to the upper world before we're part of it: through study, the group, and shared actions.

## Part 2 — Two Kinds of Work

| First kind of work | Second kind of work |
|---|---|
| Study, meals, songs, shared tasks, dissemination, done together | Picturing ourselves on the higher degree and modeling its relationships in the group |
| Draws the **Ohr Makif** from our present level | Pulls ourselves toward the higher degree: **equivalence** |
| Done even mechanically, "because he was told to" | Only together with real actions toward the friends, never as private fantasy |
| "A little bit underwater" | "A little bit above water" |

- In the first kind, ask why we gather: "for the sake of it being pleasant for us, or for the sake of an action that raises us?"
- Kabbalists rarely write about the second kind, because people fly off into illusions of "already soaring somewhere up there." It was done only between teacher and students, where the teacher could keep them grounded. It's desirable to do both, so you don't "float away."

#### The exercise: exit your "I"

- Baal HaSulam writes (in the speech for the completion of the Zohar) that the spiritual is a complete exit from yourself, like the soul leaving the body. Just as the Creator doesn't ask whether He exists, "my I does not exist."
- The first exercise: imagine you're in the thoughts and desires of others, and act only according to them.

## Part 3 — What Is Felt at the Machsom

- **One field.** Someone who has exited himself senses one force moving everyone: villains and righteous, wise and foolish. Each person is a mechanism seeking maximum pleasure at minimum cost, automatically taking its place in "a field" of one thought. That field is "a manifestation of His governance," not yet the Creator Himself.
- He sees everything turn toward the good. Nothing outside changes. "Olam ke-minhago noheg," the world goes on as always; only perception changes.
- **Oscillation.** Crossing wavers above and below the ego, like the ten plagues before **Yam Suf**, "the end sea." Once crossed, there's no going back, but the swings process all the ego across.
- **Like a swimmer.** Below the surface, even group work is for "so that I would advance." Above it, he sees everyone's ego as children's, created that way and not to blame, and wants only good for them.
- **Justifying the Creator.** Past the Machsom a person asks to see how the Creator relates to humanity, not to enjoy the view, but to justify Him. Then comes **Sinai**, a "mountain of hatred" and of doubts (*hirhurim*); only one point of him rises above it.
- **Commandments.** Humanity is "one clay" of desires. Taking each person's desire as my own and wishing its fulfillment is keeping a commandment. Through this, the great Kli called **Adam** is formed.

## Part 4 — Shame as the Driving Force

> I'm ashamed to look my friends in the eye because I want to use them for myself. This should already be our motto, our key rule.

- In the border layer of the Machsom a person feels shame: he doesn't judge others at all (they carry out the Creator's will automatically, "like little children"), but he sees he uses them.
- It's like a mother who catches herself being egoistic toward her children and reproaches herself, without demanding that they be altruists.
- True shame comes from the light itself. Like the first phase of direct light, which felt the giver and wished to become a giver, revealing the giver evokes a sense of oppositeness.
- So we evoke it artificially: imagine the light already acting on you, and feel yourself opposite. "The most effective action for drawing the surrounding light upon me."

#### Q&A: Shame about what?

- **I'm created this way, so why be ashamed?** Not for how the Creator made you, but "that I have not yet corrected myself, that I haven't persuaded Him, demanded of Him to correct me." That part depends only on me.
- **Does shame stop me acting?** No. Keep acting and let egoism drive you, "some rewards, some badges," while knowing you're an egoist.
- **Is asking to be relieved of shame a correct prayer?** It depends on the intention: to escape an unpleasant feeling, or to become like the Creator and justify what He made in me.

## Part 5 — Play Is Not a Lie

**Question:** What do we do with the shame of seeing that it's all artificial, "played"?

- "It doesn't matter to me that it's played." These are drills. To the extent we do them, the surrounding light comes and we acquire the qualities for real. "It is not simply a game. It's not a lie. It is a special type of training."
- This is **raising MAN**: even in the upper worlds, the next degree feels like its **Achoraim** (backside) until you rise to it, so there too "I must play." That is **faith above reason**: modeling the higher degree against all my present qualities.
- **The child with a doll.** His young daughter wouldn't play with a real baby, "a doll is my baby, that's real for me." Each level of growth has its own objects to grow on. Our "toys" are how we adapt to the higher degree.
- Light shines from afar (surrounding) while I'm unlike it, and enters (inner) once I resemble it. We draw it only by our desire to be similar.
- Athletes and armies train the same way. It comes from the spiritual root: every success comes through such rehearsal and the upper light.

## Part 6 — Q&A: The Kabbalist Looks Ordinary

**Question:** How do actions change toward the group after crossing?

- Inside, love arises toward all creations, like our attitude to a falling stone: no anger at it. You see people moved by one field, as "corrected" in their root state, and ask where *your* "I" is: the new one you must now build.
- Outside, the group "sees nothing." People around Rabash said "there was nothing special," a stocky little man who talked fast and got irritated. "Why should anything be visible?" A higher degree lowers itself to be in contact, like a mother babbling with a child. Only magicians show off miracles.
- A higher degree even turns its backside toward the lower. The one who can help you may make things harder, so you don't run off the path.
- **How to include myself in a friend's desires:** follow Rabash and lift his mood. That is what a friend is always missing. Don't interrogate him about what he needs.
- The Kotzk Hasidim mocked each other outwardly to hide love inside. "We're not that strong." Today we must relate to each other openly as on a higher degree. Take a duty: half an hour in the kitchen and "you feel all that they feel." Anyone without a shift is "very unfortunate" and should ask for one.

## Part 7 — Q&A: The Group, the Future, and Premature Action

- **No individual crossing.** "Not one Kabbalistic method provides for an individual crossing of the Machsom," except a soul sent on a mission and dragged through great suffering. Use the group as an **amplifier** of whatever quality you want, including shame and the wish to stand out.
- **Does the ego come along?** The one who crosses starts as "a naked altruist," a point. The will to enjoy never vanishes; it comes back on the left line in exactly the measure needed for the next degree, and the intention to bestow is built on it.
- **Pioneers.** "We only know how individuals cross the Machsom, not groups." No one, not even the greatest Kabbalist, can predict what has never happened. Kabbalah deals only with matter and form clothed in matter, not abstract form.
- **What's coming.** He expects the group to grow to perhaps tens of thousands, becoming, per "The Last Generation," a small model society whose main work is education. He guessed it might begin around 2007–2008 and says more than once that he can't know.
- **Israel's future.** Asked about the threat of destruction, he says it concerns the Jewish people in Israel, not the Kabbalistic group, and that he sees no solution yet. "Israel" means those who strive toward the Creator.
- **The Third Temple.** It is the image of the corrected soul of Adam expressed in matter. Built before inner correction, "it would be a mockery." Every mistake since Adam's "unripe fruit" is **acting prematurely**, before being spiritually ready. Our artificial likeness is different: we know we aren't there yet and ask to be raised.
- **From above, all is correction.** Below the Machsom we feel what comes from above through Achoraim, as suffering. After crossing, "no one is fighting with anyone."

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **Two kinds of work.** Draw surrounding light through shared actions, and model the higher degree in our relationships. Do both.
2. **Exit your "I".** The first exercise is to exist in the thoughts and desires of others.
3. **Past the Machsom, one field moves everyone.** Nothing outside changes; perception does.
4. **Shame is the engine.** Not for how I was created, but for not yet having demanded my correction.
5. **Play is training, not a lie.** Like a child's doll, the "artificial" group is how we grow into the next degree.
6. **The higher one looks ordinary.** A higher degree lowers itself, even shows its backside, to stay in contact.
7. **Nothing prematurely.** Every mistake since Adam comes from acting before inner readiness.

## Key Terms & Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Machsom** | The barrier to the spiritual. Crossing it means sensing one field that moves everyone, and exiting the "I." |
| **Surrounding Light (Ohr Makif)** | Light that shines "from afar" while we're unlike it; drawn by our desire to be similar. |
| **Equivalence** | The second kind of work: pulling oneself toward the higher degree by modeling it. |
| **Shame** | The sense of oppositeness evoked by the revealed giver: "I'm ashamed to look my friends in the eye." |
| **Playing the Higher Degree** | Deliberately acting in the group as on the next degree; training, not deception. |
| **Raising MAN (Aliyat MAN)** | Raising the desire toward a degree one hasn't reached, which draws its correction. |
| **Achoraim** | The backside a higher degree shows the lower; perceived as unpleasant or as suffering. |
| **Faith Above Reason** | Modeling the higher degree against all my present qualities and understanding. |
| **Sinai (Mountain of Hatred)** | The doubts (*hirhurim*) after the crossing; only one point rises above them. |
| **Adam (the Common Kli)** | The great vessel of all souls, formed as I take others' desires as my own. |
| **Unripe Fruit** | Acting prematurely, before being prepared; the root of all mistakes since Adam HaRishon. |
