### Opening

The goal of creation is adhesion with the Creator, yet the one law we are given points us at other people: "love thy friend as thyself." Why should correcting my relations with others be the same as correcting my relation with the Creator? And if it is, why does it turn out to be impossible, even toward one person? Rav Laitman uses that impossibility to explain what raising MAN really means. He then takes on why the method was given to Israel, and ends with **shame**, which he calls "a creation in its own right" and the root of all human development.

## Part 1 — Love of Others Is Love of the Creator

- Why weren't we commanded to correct ourselves toward the Creator? The answer: "Don't start correcting your relations with Him... You seemingly can't do anything towards Him, but rather you have others."
- Love of others isn't a step toward the Creator. "It doesn't resemble it, it is actually one and the same." Reaching it, "I don't need any addition in order to reach congruence with the Creator."
- It seems surprisingly simple: all the worlds and infinite distances "converge, shrink inside this small group," right in front of me, where I can check myself and the connections.
- **Hillel's version.** "What is hateful to you, do not do to your friend" sounds passive, far from love. Rav Laitman agrees they are not the same statement and leaves it to be clarified later.

#### Layers of life

- What about the 8–10 hours outside the group? At work, behave by that place's rules. You don't kiss your manager.
- The group is "more internal even than the family." The family is an external society.
- Live in several layers at once. The innermost is the Creator, then the group, then the family, then work and the world. Gradually a person discovers "that in truth, it is one... one force unites all of this."

## Part 2 — The Impossible Commandment and Raising MAN

- "As thyself" means caring for the other no less than for myself. But I can't even care for myself properly, so how can I fill even one other person?
- "The Creator seemingly says, jump to the next degree, but that next degree is... just painted on the wall."
- **Who is "every person of Israel"?** Not everyone who studies Kabbalah, and not humanity. It's those in my group who yearn for the same goal. Choose a limited circle, a handful or a few dozen. Feeling the whole world group will come, but more through the group's combined force than through each one's effort.

#### Why give a law we can't keep?

- People think they must achieve some spiritual action with all their strength. "We need to walk on an entirely different path." We don't carry out the action; we reach the deficiency for it.
- Know the present state, picture the next state correctly, know you can't reach it, and want it so much that "my present state is completely hated by me."

> This tension of being in that state that I'm incapable of being in, it is called raising of MAN. And then it happens from above.

- "The lights correct the vessels, they fill the vessels, only the lights." Our only obligation is that gap, which is called a prayer, and "this desire alone is sufficient."

## Part 3 — The Sixth Sense and Changing the Shirt

- The Torah speaks only about spirituality. "Spiritual" doesn't mean far away; it means hidden from our ordinary senses. That's why it's called **Kodesh**, special, needing a special sense: the **Neshama**.
- We must reach adhesion together, carrying the still, vegetative, and animate along with us.
- **Rabash's answer.** Rav Laitman once asked how a person who has crossed the barrier feels his body. Rabash said it's like coming home, showering, and putting on a new shirt. When the body can no longer carry out the remaining records, a person "rises to the degree of Bina," is immersed in the upper waters, and returns in a new body. Some come back even after completing their correction, to carry out a task.
- A person lives at the highest level he feels. We don't feel cutting our nails or hair. Rise to the degree of **Adam**, and the beastly life feels like that too.

#### The embryo of humanity

- Like an embryo (three days of absorbing the seed, 40 days of formation, three trimesters), humanity is one soul whose parts develop at different times, not uniformly. That is the question behind why one group received the method first.

## Part 4 — Why the Method Went to Israel

- The sages' story of the Creator offering the Torah to every nation is "good to tell to children," and too many tell it to adults. Baal HaSulam says only a fool would see a choice of nationality here.
- **The point in the heart** is a "godly part from above." It isn't our nature; it's a piece of the broken screen, a part of Bina, planted through the breaking of the vessels. That point in a person is called **Israel**. It connects the will to receive below with the Creator above. Later it is called NHY of Ima, studied "yesterday" for Hoshana Rabbah.
- The same structure appears in humanity. When **Adam HaRishon** broke, the pieces fell according to their coarseness. Those with the most Bina (HGT, the "ancestral merit") stayed nearest the barrier: the coarseness of the root.

| Layer | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Coarseness of the root (Israel) | The most refined records, closest to the barrier. Both their Bina and their Malchut show up first. |
| Coarseness 1–4 (the nations) | Lower layers whose will to receive hasn't been activated yet by the surrounding light. |
| Every layer | Contains all of them, so every nation has people yearning for spirituality, and Israel has many who don't. |

- This is a role, not a privilege. "Uniqueness, it's not uniqueness, there's nothing special about it." Those records must correct first and pass on the method.
- Bina used incorrectly produces "revolutions and scientific discoveries" nobody needs. Malchut revealed early means the race for money and honor. Both show up first.
- Here are people from 25 countries who all belong to Israel, "Yashar El, longing for the Creator," while many Israelis by nationality "have no interest."

#### The blows and the role

- Because Israel doesn't correct itself or pass on the method, "the stick first comes to us." Unrealized coarseness rises above the point in the heart as added concealment, felt as disaster and disease.
- Kabbalists in the 1920s and 1930s, Baal HaSulam among them, warned of coming disasters if the land was built on a beastly level, "and it happened."
- **Why is the closest layer the most concealed?** Its will to receive is simply the first one activated. When the nations' larger vessels are revealed, the explosions would be bigger, unless the root layer is prepared first. Israel is "the adapter between the Creator and the rest of the nations."
- **How do we awaken the nation?** Only through the surrounding light we draw. Citing Isaiah (Introduction to the Book of Zohar, item 66), the nations will carry Israel "on their shoulders." The spiritual Israel from all nations must awaken the corporeal Israel, and from there the rest of humanity. Bnei Baruch Israel declared this "the year of dissemination in Hebrew."

## Part 5 — Shame: A Creation in Its Own Right

- Rav Laitman says he condensed Baal HaSulam's sentences: the Creator reveals Himself through equivalence of form. Desires aren't destroyed; we correct how we use them.
- The Creator created two things: the will to receive, and **shame**, the feeling of disparity of form between receiver and giver. Without shame "we would never want to change, just like beasts."
- **The calf and the child.** A calf is born knowing how to stand and eat, and never changes. A human needs 20 years to manage, and even then needs education. The difference comes from the point of shame.
- True shame, and its correction, exist only above the barrier. Below it we have "a copy."
- If the Creator had filled Phase One without a sense of the giver behind it, development would have stopped there. All development comes from the lower one wanting to resemble the upper one.

#### Why shame is so strong

- The still, vegetative, and animate feel only good or bad from the presence of the Creator. The degree of **Adam** feels a further parameter: *who* is giving. That brings shame.
- Shame comes from a higher degree than the vessel, which is why it outweighs all the pleasures. Malchut of Ein Sof, filled with all the light, restricted herself because of it. Correcting shame lifts a person "from the created being's degree to the Creator's degree."

#### Q&A on shame

- **Is wanting to resemble the giver the same as escaping shame?** At first, yes: I just want to get rid of the burning. That allows the first restriction. Then Malchut and Bina begin to need each other, and "completely new thoughts... that enable the fulfillment through bestowal" appear. "In place of shame comes love."
- **Free choice.** The concealed king (from Baal HaSulam's letter): a person can choose to be like the Creator, or to receive everything like Malchut of Ein Sof and erase the shame. That point of choice is the middle third of Tifferet, Klipat Noga.
- **Shame needs a giver.** A child doesn't feel he's receiving from his parents. Once he marries and becomes independent, he "goes to visit" them, and feels ashamed. Independence brings the ability to feel shame.
- **Shame over a lack of yearning** can come only from society, not from the person himself. That's "a topic on its own."
- **"He has everything, why should I be ashamed?"** That kind of cleverness only works until you actually feel the giver. Everything human in us, beyond the beast and the baby, comes from developing shame: toward a wife, parents, the world, oneself. Education should mean "more sensitivity to shame."

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **One law at every degree.** Love of others is not a step toward love of the Creator; it is the same thing.
2. **The group is the laboratory.** A small, chosen circle of people with the same goal is where the law is practiced, and where its impossibility is discovered.
3. **Impossibility creates MAN.** Clearly seeing the next state, knowing I can't reach it, and hating my present state is the prayer. The light does the rest.
4. **Israel is a role, not a nationality.** The point in the heart is Israel within each person; the root layer must correct first and pass the method on.
5. **Unrealized correction returns as blows.** Coarseness that isn't turned into purity becomes added concealment.
6. **Shame is a separate creation.** It comes from sensing the giver and drives all human development.
7. **Shame turns into love.** It starts as escaping discomfort and becomes longing to resemble the giver.

| **Point in the heart** | A "godly part from above," a spark of the broken screen (Bina) in a person; called Israel. |

| **Ancestral merit** | The Bina qualities of HGT in the first part of Adam HaRishon, which fell nearest the barrier. |
| **Shame (Busha)** | The feeling of disparity of form from the giver; "a creation in its own right," and the root of development. |
| **Bread of shame** | Receiving unearned gifts, which brings shame; why perfection must be reached through our own effort. |
| **Neshama** | The additional (sixth) sense in which the spiritual world is felt. |
