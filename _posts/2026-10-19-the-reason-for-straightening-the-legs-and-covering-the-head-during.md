### Opening

In our world, sin is a bad act, punishment is suffering, reward is what I gain, and faith means going forward with my eyes shut. Rav Laitman says that in spirituality every one of these definitions is turned around. Above all, faith is not walking in the dark. It is the **revelation of the Creator**, and that revelation is what corrects a person. Why don't we want it, and what does it take before we do?

## Part 1 — A Different Dictionary

#### Why the articles are so long

- Rabash wrote one article each week for the assembly of the society. He typed it on an old typewriter, usually at midday, over the course of the week. As thoughts changed and students asked questions, the articles grew longer.
- So it is worthwhile to divide an article into parts. When students asked Rabash what to do at home in their free time, he recommended taking the articles, shortening and summarizing them, breaking them into pieces, and adding short questions and answers.

#### Sin, punishment, and reward

- **Sin:** in corporeality, sin means I did something wrong. In spirituality, sin means a **state**. The fact that something bad happened is called a sin, and "of course it happens from above." It is not a matter of guilt.
- **Punishment:** whatever a person suffers comes from above, so in spirituality it is not called punishment. "The punishment one sees in his eyes is considered **correction**," even though it comes through unpleasant events.
- **Reward:** what I gain and feel in my own vessels as earned is, in spirituality, typically considered punishment.

> What we interpret so easily in corporeality according to what the eyes see looks different once we follow the inner vision, "where all the reasons are related to the upper force."

## Part 2 — Faith Is the Revelation of the Creator

- In this world, faith means going with shut eyes, and the greater the darkness and danger, the greater the believer. "Faith in spirituality means the opposite, that everything opens up." I feel that the Creator is great and omnipotent. "For us this is called knowing, not believing."
- This is why Kabbalah is a wisdom opposite to our "beastly logic."

#### Everything is done by the light

- From the Preface (**Pticha**) and the study of the Ten Sefirot: the light shines to the vessel, gives birth to it, corrects it, and gives it its qualities, according to how far the vessel has prepared to absorb and copy those qualities.
- "Without light, there's no **Kli**. There's nothing, there's no reality." So faith means **feeling the Creator**, the way we feel this world through our senses, mostly sight and hearing.
- Before the light appears in the Kli, the Kli cannot be corrected. "You can cry, it's not gonna help." We can only want to fulfill our desires. The revelation of the Creator becomes the reason for all the later corrections.

#### Our nature hides the Creator

> "Not that the Creator is hidden, rather our nature hides the Creator."

- We don't have a desire for the revelation of the Creator, because it brings correction, which turns our nature toward bestowal. The will to receive resists it.
- So a **time of preparation** is needed, and it takes years. A person gathers impressions from the lack of revelation, through **double and single concealment**, and feels how burdensome the concealment is.
- Bit by bit a strong desire forms: nothing is lacking except the revelation of the Creator. This is called **"I am lovesick."** "If He won't be revealed, I can't bear it."
- When a person comes to such a desire, even egoistically for his own needs but wanting nothing else, he has a Kli for revelation, "and then the Creator is revealed."

## Part 3 — The Gentile and the Jew Within Us

- Gentile and Jew, the nations of the world and Israel, exist both in spirituality and in corporeality. Because we all come from the shattering of **Adam HaRishon**, these qualities are mixed in each of us.
- **Exile** means the desires of the nations rule over Israel inside a person: "our Gentile rules our Jew."

#### What the inner Gentile tells the inner Jew

- It doesn't say "don't engage." It says: keep **Torah and Mitzvot** only because the Creator commanded it. You don't need to reveal His greatness or long for His revelation. Remain as a slave, not a son.
- **Nature** in **Gematria** equals *Elohim* (God). All of nature already keeps the Creator's commands, and every law of physics, chemistry, biology, or psychology is a particular rule within that general law. So the Gentile says: keep the laws of nature, "remain within nature, like the rest of nature," in the beastly degree.

#### What makes a Jew

- The **Yehudi** is one who performs his actions **due to the greatness of the Creator**. He goes beyond keeping nature's laws and becomes a servant of the Creator because of His greatness.
- To the extent he feels that greatness, the human, **Adam**, is born in him: "I will resemble the Creator."
- Without the revelation of the Creator, a vessel without light cannot change. "To be Jewish, a person can only be that after having been awarded with the revelation of the Creator."
- So the wisdom of Kabbalah is by definition "the revelation of His godliness to His created beings in this world," a method for not remaining within nature but reaching the purpose of creation. "Faith is the revelation of godliness."

## Part 4 — Q&A: Rewards, Illusions, and Degrees

#### Reward that is really punishment

- **Q: Does spiritual reward come as punishment?** "God forbid." What appears to us as reward is *usually* punishment, because we are still in egoistic desires.
- If you won the lottery today, would you come on Sunday to study Kabbalah? "I wouldn't have come. For certain." You would be "bribed by the pleasure of that gift, imprisoned by that pleasure," and would not reach the true goal. "So that is called a punishment, although to you it seems like a reward."
- We can't change this by force, since by nature we operate according to these rules. It is only to recognize that spirituality doesn't work according to our logic.

#### The illusions keep growing

- **Q: Every time I see my current state as an illusion, I'm given more desire and greater confusion. Can this go on forever?** You've understood the state correctly.
- Even in spirituality, toward each higher degree "you never understand anything... you fail again." Everything attained today cannot even serve as a foundation for recognizing the higher degree.
- Example: **AB** has only a tiny illumination that **Galgalta** exists, with nothing of its fourth degree of coarseness. It knows what it means to be **Hochma**, not **Keter**, and these are adjacent degrees.
- Each new degree is "a completely new nature," the opposite of the previous one, a difference greater than between a person and the fish in an aquarium, or between an adult and a newborn.
- We never come to rule over these degrees. Once you truly acquire a degree, you have exhausted it and immediately continue higher. "It's only in order to elevate us to adhesion to the Creator, not towards mastery over ourselves."

#### A predefined measure

- These states are predefined and come to an end, each according to the structure of his soul. As Baal HaSulam writes in his letters, each person must complete his **Seah**, his full measure, before entering spirituality, and there too he has a set number of states until the **end of correction**.
- A student put it as chasing one's tail: in corporeality I suffer from the chase, in spirituality I enjoy it. Rav Laitman accepted the simple picture but said there are no words for what it is to live in a different dimension.

## Part 5 — Faith and Knowledge

- **Q: In spirituality, what is the difference between knowledge and faith?** "Faith is the revelation of godliness," and this revelation gives the forces to resemble Him.
- By correcting myself through His revelation and becoming similar to Him, I come to know who He is, and the holy names are revealed in me. **AB, SAG**, Moses, Jacob are created beings with special screens that perform acts of bestowal toward the Creator.
- From that act he can say, "I know the Creator," maybe a million times greater than me, but to that extent I know Him. This is **From Your Actions We Know You**.
- The light revealed, the **light of Hassadim** with the illumination of Hochma, is knowledge, "the light of adhesion," **Dvekut**.

> "The easiest way for us for the time being [is] to define faith as the force of correction. Knowledge is the result of it, when the vessel is already corrected and is performing an action similar to that which the Creator does."

- "And Adam knew his wife Eve": knowledge is adhesion, knowledge is connection.
- When I discover how great He is, I want to resemble Him, subdue myself, and receive screens from Him. "First faith and then knowledge."

## Part 6 — The Thin Light and the Sparks of Bestowal

- A student asked about page 11, where Rabash says we learn the work in order to bestow on corporeal matters, where only small pleasures exist, called by the Zohar a **thin light** (**Ner Dakik**).
- After **Malchut de Ein Sof** restricted herself and said, "I'm not receiving anything ever," the vessels later broke. The **Klipot** were made from that, and very small sparks of light entered them. "That's why the shells are attractive."

#### The map of the worlds

| Level | Its role, as Rav Laitman described it |
|---|---|
| **Ein Sof** | Where everything was decided |
| **Adam Kadmon** | Translates Ein Sof into five vessels and five lights (Keter through Malchut; **NRNHY**) |
| **Atzilut** | Controls and governs everything below; carries out the plan |
| **BYA of Kedusha / BYA of Tuma'a** | Holiness on the "right," impurity on the "left"; each soul stands between them and rises toward Atzilut as it corrects itself |
| **Machsom** | The barrier, "seemingly a wall," between BYA and this world |
| **This world** | Below BYA of impurity, which nourish and sustain us with only the thin light |

- The thin light is what lets people, and the still, vegetative, and animate, exist. It is called **imaginary life** because it is nothing like the true action of the light.

#### Bestowing in order to receive

- Here we learn receiving in order to receive and bestowing in order to receive: giving something so as to enjoy as a result. Babies don't know this; a growing person learns it from society, and with it how to deceive others. That is why it is called a lie.
- The sophistication comes from the shattering: sparks of bestowal fell into the vessels of reception. Animals and the rest of nature have no such sparks because they don't need to correct themselves.
- The more a person can use bestowal in order to receive, the greater he becomes. The rich man gives work to a million people and receives from them; the laborer gives a little and receives a little.

#### Using the sparks correctly

- In our society we learn to use these same sparks purposefully: to connect with others and, through those connections, gain spirituality, until "I'll resemble the Creator."
- **Corporeality:** the sparks of reception rule over me, and I use even the sparks of bestowal for reception. **Spirituality:** the Creator is revealed and gives me the strength to rule with the sparks of bestowal over reception. "The measure seemingly flips."
- The scale depends on the revelation of godliness. Even the minimal revelation of double or single concealment already lets me work with these sparks.
- "A person is the desire, just like all of nature." While he calculates for vessels of reception, he is a beast like the rest of nature. When he moves to calculating for vessels of bestowal, he is called **Adam**, "the true opposite. And no one can understand what happened to him."
- **Q: Adhesion, bestowal, faith, knowledge, attainment: how do I find my way among all these words?** Today we received such a dictionary, punishment is this, reward is that. Start writing a booklet for yourself. "This is something that each has to write for himself in his heart."

## Part 7 — One System from Here to Ein Sof

- **Q: We make our discernments in our beastly vessels. How does it reach the soul?** There aren't several systems. "The structure is one unified one."
- Even though I am below all the worlds, all my states lie between me and Ein Sof "like spheres one within the other, like the onion layers." To the extent I act from my heart, "it reaches Ein Sof in the end."
- The difference is only like that between a baby and an adult acting toward the society. Even Baal HaSulam's example of kibbutz members who want rain for their farming, with no thought of the Creator at all, works through that same system.
- Our prayers are received above differently from how we cry them out. "Please let me win the lottery" may return as the opposite, as a mother gives a child what he needs, not what he wants.
- "I am the Lord who dwells with them in the midst of their impurity." This gives importance to every state a person is in, and to how much effort he needs to come closer.

#### Past, present, and future

- **Q: In spirituality past, present, and future unite. What does that feel like?** There is no such thing as thinking something and it happening. When you unite with the Creator, He is the cause of what you will think, of the **MAN** that rises from you and what descends to you.
- To the extent you unite with Him, you reach the root, "the drop of unification," and cause and consequence disappear. To the extent you haven't reached that wholeness, you still have cause and consequence. "This is the matter of the degrees."

#### What holds a person in the hardest states

- **Q: People fall into darkness, drugs, madness. What keeps the vessel from falling apart?** A person in spirituality goes through even more, fully conscious, thrown from side to side. This is called **the slingshot**.
- Rav Laitman gave his own example: when he needed lung surgery, he told the surgeon, "Just give me the anesthesia and start cutting." He had no fear, "because this thing saves my life."
- "We think that we are the owners of our lives." That is the lack of faith we talked about, the lack of feeling the Creator.
- Through the blows, a person begins to relate to these states with understanding and then with love, and to feel how the Creator works on him. "There's a reason why it's called the Creator's work and not a person's work." Learning His actions, he takes on His nature and truly rules over himself. "Right now it's a complete 100% illusion."

## Part 8 — Who Can Hear Kabbalah?

- **Q: How do I explain Kabbalah to religious people?** You can't. Without the sparks calling him from above, a person can't understand, whether religious or secular. Whoever is satisfied with the question of the meaning of life doesn't have it.
- "I understand their state. I love them and I live amongst them," but there is no connection. Kabbalah is only for those who already have a preparation for changing their nature, "and this has nothing to do with great rabbis or small ones."
- **Rabbi Yosef Karo**, author of the **Shulchan Aruch**, would fall asleep in the Ari's lessons: he understood they were very important but "there wasn't quite a burning point in him." The sages of **Tzfat** tested the Ari on how much **Gemara** he knew.
- His recommendation: don't talk to anyone about it unless his heart beats for it.

#### Impressions from the friends

- The friends shared what they took from the lesson: faith as the force of correction and knowledge as its result; the new definitions of sin, punishment, and reward; that our nature, not the Creator, conceals; one system before Ein Sof; and the Jew as one who acts due to the greatness of the Creator. Several called it "a lesson for beginners" in which Rav gives himself completely.

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **Spirituality has its own dictionary.** Sin is a state, suffering is correction, and what looks like reward is usually punishment.
2. **Faith is the revelation of the Creator,** not walking blind. Only the light corrects the vessel, so everything depends on that revelation.
3. **Our nature hides the Creator.** Years of preparation through concealment build the one desire, "I am lovesick," that makes a Kli for revelation.
4. **The inner Gentile keeps us within nature (Elohim);** the inner Jew acts due to the greatness of the Creator and grows the Adam who resembles Him.
5. **First faith, then knowledge.** Faith is the force of correction; knowledge is the corrected vessel acting like the Creator: "From Your Actions We Know You."
6. **The sparks of bestowal that fell in the shattering** make us more than animals; used for connection in the society, they lead to resembling the Creator.
7. **There is one system from this world to Ein Sof,** so every state and every effort matters, even when our prayers are answered differently than we asked.
