### Opening

As with a corporeal building, the deeper the foundation is dug, the taller the building can rise; in spirituality, the digging is a deficiency dug in the heart. The article says that all a person needs is the desire to bestow in mind and heart. When he wants to work in order to bestow but cannot, the Creator gives him the light that corrects the vessel, and that vessel of bestowal is the foundation on which a whole building can rise. Abraham's servants, those who walk in the quality of Chesed, keep digging for this deficiency, and each time the Philistines seal the wells and they must dig again. Rav Laitman explains the two points a person must reach before the foundation appears, why we work only on the good and never go looking for the bad, why a person is shown the truth when he is still too weak to act on it, how the light acts on us in time, and then turns at length to the first condition of any group: absolute equality among the friends.

## Part 1 — The Two Points Before the Foundation

- After all the work a person is able to do, "whatever your ability allows you to do, you shall do," he thinks he is advancing toward Kedusha, "but he's shown each time that he advances in the opposite way, seeing himself as opposite to Kedusha."
- Ultimately he needs to come to two points: "the maximum yearning for Kedusha, holiness, and knowing his own nature," that it is completely opposite to spirituality.
- From these two points comes such disappointment that he sees "he himself cannot achieve the thing that's most important to him in life. On the one hand it's most important to him in life, on the other hand, he's the farthest he can be from it."

#### The cry that opens the door

- Between these two polarities a tension forms in the heart, "such a claim to the Creator to help him." Before that, "he can cry and yell, it's like babies. No one really hears." The true demand appears only at the end of the path, where the new foundation shows itself.
- The cry opens an opening for the light that reforms. "I created the evil inclination, I created for it the Torah as a spice."
- The light "doesn't change the desires, it rather changes the direction of how to use them, from in order to receive to in order to bestow." It adds a screen, and the person begins to enter the holy ladder.

#### After entering

- Each time he advances he is shown a bit more to correct, "but he no longer needs to reach such a point of crisis," because he already has Kedusha.
- He builds himself on the earlier basis of despair of his own strength. "He already knows it's not in his own strength to attain anything," and now he works with both forces, left and right, the good inclination and the bad, receiving opportunities to see what else he can add to Kedusha.

## Part 2 — Work Only on the Good

- **Q: Does a person work only on his yearning, and the other point appears by itself?** "One never needs to work on the bad, only on the good." Our goal is the Creator. "Our eyes should only be aimed at one place": our heart, our deeds, our thoughts.
- Other thoughts will fall into your mind; the article calls them rocks. They come and show how opposite your interior is. The bad is allowed to be revealed "only on the condition that you'll be able to pick them up" and continue yearning forward.
- "There is no commandment of entering thoughts that don't belong to Kedusha. On the contrary, try to not leave the authority of Kedusha."
- There are methods in Kabbalah where a person leaves Kedusha to work with his vessels of reception and attach them to bestowal. "This is the work of the weekdays. The work of Shabbat is only to maintain the presence of the person in Kedusha." For now, that is enough. "In short, don't look for bad things. We're still far from being able to go with our own strength and awaken the evil within us."

#### What a beginner does

- This isn't about left and right lines; those come once a person feels the Creator. A simple beginner starts to ask: what is godliness? "Huge, grand, great, beautiful, strong?" Later he interprets Him through qualities: God means Bina, "that He only wants to bestow, only wants to do good."
- Then: do I see good or not? Do I want to bestow or not? "You don't need to deliberately go and pick into whether I'm good or bad. Don't think about that. By yearning for the Creator, immediately the qualities within you awaken correspondingly."

## Part 3 — Above Reason for a Beginner

- **Q: Where does the method of above reason start?** "After all the tests, whatever happens, I need to remain in wholeness, in the governance of the Creator."
- Not in an imaginary way, "that I am now in Ein Sof," or that someone hits me on the head and I say it isn't true.
- It means "not to escape the governance of the Creator": everything that happens to me, with my qualities and with everything around me, "I need to accept it as direct providence towards me." Whether I agree with it "is a different question." The first thing is to accept that through all the different garments it all comes from the Creator.

## Part 4 — Why a Person Is Shown the Truth

- **Q: If a person is shown the truth, why does he prefer to sustain himself from falsehood?** Because "he's still incapable of getting something from truth."
- If I give things to everyone, that's truth, but I won't feel any benefit. If I'm respected for it, "to the extent that I'm respected I'm willing to pay," because self-respect matters more to me than effort or money. Uncorrected, "I need something in exchange." The only exchange that works is "the greatness of the goal, that the goal shines and I awaken myself to the goal."
- **Q: But if he's too weak, why is he shown the truth at all?** "This is the best possible state." He sees the work being done on him from above: he's given an opportunity to bestow and a desire to receive is awakened in him, so he sees he must bestow and that he's incapable. "This is a diagnosis that is worth a lot."
- A person exists between the two "like between the grinding stones," and every grain of the discernment is felt. "It's the recognition of the reality the Creator created." With both in mind and heart, bit by bit understanding and willingness form in him.

#### What is hidden from us

- Some of our intentions to receive are shown to us. Others are not shown, "and we think that's Kedusha." So "our own falsehood is hidden from us so that we think it's truth."
- Once a person is shown that his intention is in order to receive and that he can't get rid of it, "he's already being taken care of." Otherwise it would stay closed, and he'd think he's all in bestowal and ready to enter Kedusha.
- The hidden area of our thoughts, desires and intentions has two parts. One "we are yet forbidden to reveal, otherwise we just won't be able to exist," because revelation happens by the light that is opposite to it. The other belongs to "a different kind of revelation, a different kind of concealment," in which we think certain actions are in order to bestow; this is still the inner structure of the beastly soul.

## Part 5 — The Light Grows in Due Time

- "Don't try to link things." We think if I do this, that will happen, if I do good I'll get better things. "But you don't understand the system... there's almost no connection between one thing to the other."
- "I can only say one thing. From above, what comes is always an addition of light." When it comes constantly without stop, "that's spirituality." When it grows gradually, "that's a matter of time. This is called in due time": the light grows to draw all of reality to its goal.
- "If reality in accordance with this light doesn't grow, doesn't move forward, meaning it feels more of a gap, then it is felt as suffering." So the same adding light sometimes appears good and sometimes bad, and life doesn't simply get worse from day to day.
- "We need deficiencies, not forces. One cannot give forces; the forces come from the light." You can't work on one part of the soul and forget the others, so to advance them equally you're given "a little bit of bad and a little bit of good," because "everything is felt only through the clash between opposite things."

## Part 6 — So Simple, and Still So Many Pages

- "All we need to do is to yearn for the Creator, and He will show us how incapable we are, and in truth everything is very simple." Rabash writes that a person invests in the study, the light that reforms comes, he enters truth and begins to work.
- "In spirituality, there's no problem. It's all above time." Why then does it take years? "Because there are many discernments inside the soul."
- "One page that you can read all the time and delve into could be enough for you," if it speaks of the roots from which you draw the surrounding light through your yearning. But the soul becomes refined, so the page must keep adapting to each one's mind and mentality. "So it turns out that it is worthwhile to write as much as possible," and as current as possible, for the souls that exist right now.

#### Scrutiny only from the side of the light

- **Q: When a person sees he's immersed in the will to receive and has no desire to bestow, how does he advance fastest?** "Constantly perform maximal scrutiny," in whatever state you're in. But "scrutiny can only take place via the light. The light shining upon the will to receive scrutinizes the will to receive."
- First attach yourself to holiness, then gradually awaken the problem and see it "from the perspective of holiness." If you just descend into the problem, "you begin to eat yourself up... and you're a beast."
- "Scrutiny means that you hold very tightly to the side of the light and as much as possible scrutinize drop by drop, touching on these problems, but never to leave the side of Kedusha. If you left it, there's no longer any scrutiny."

## Part 7 — The First Condition of a Group: Equality

- **Q: What is a complete degree in the group?** A group of friends means "they're connected together without any difference, without differentiating between one and the other." Before entering spirituality, I must at least yearn that all the friends in my group "will be felt by me as if there's no difference between them."
- Because the general and the particular are equal, to be included in all of them "there has to be a feeling of absolute equality between me and everyone else." "That is the first condition, and this is what everyone should aspire to."

#### A spiritual leader?

- Asked how to choose a group leader, he answered: first yearn for there to be a group. If you connect to the Creator each time together, "He will become your leader."
- Someone is needed to take care of meals and drinks; such burdens are divided among everyone. "But a spiritual leader, if they're all equal, how can there be a spiritual leader?" With a spiritual leader, "it's no longer a group. It goes against the very concept of the group."
- **Q: And when the group is bored and doesn't know how to advance?** "Did you come to a point where there is a group? So first have a group and then we'll talk about how to advance."

#### Even by chance

- A group can be made of random people who don't know each other at all, "and it doesn't matter." Whether I'm in this group by chance or not, "in any state of the group, we have to work only on one thing": to relate to everyone as absolute equals. "If I remember one more than another, it's a sign that I'm still not incorporated with everyone."
- Equal in concern, in thought, in all things. Working on some project with a friend "has nothing to do with the group"; here "it's only spiritual connection."

## Part 8 — Resembling the Creator Through Equality

- **Q: Where does the deficiency for this come from?** From the light. "The light needs to come to something that is similar to it." If each friend's will to receive bends the structure toward himself, "it becomes all messy."
- Just as the Creator relates equally to all created beings, "the group needs to have a similar structure," a circle, a sphere. "Because we still don't have the structure of the three lines from Keter through Malchut, we simply have to be equal," so that the beastly nature won't act out toward each friend.
- When you think of the group, "you don't think about anyone in specific, but everyone equally." You don't measure the friend; "you measure your attitude towards everyone."

#### Not by merit

- A newcomer who "just came here off the street" with only his yearning is related to the same as everyone, "because he's one of the group." Not by his past, his merits or transgressions, tall or short. Otherwise "you begin to relate to the group with the will to receive" and judge him.
- **Q: Isn't one who studied longer more important?** "He's important because he's in the group. That's it." With the intention to bestow you won't see any difference, "that's how it appears to the Creator, everyone as equals": the Good Who Does Good to the wicked and to the good.
- Each one feels this providence as better or worse "according to his own measure of corruption," like a child who feels the father's education as blows. This stops "when you can make the first screen," come to equivalence of form, and see His true bestowal. "Anyone who sees flaws is seeing his own flaws."

#### In practice

- **Q: What if I have a closer friend?** That closeness comes from beastly things that came before. "Every day, every moment, you want to determine a new attitude towards the group... Start from now." "Without that there is no group. Without that, there is no spiritual structure."
- "The Creator who is one, He treats everyone equally, and so I'm also compelled to treat everyone equally as He does. Otherwise, I'm not building a spiritual body." "You want to resemble Him? Go ahead. You have the first exercise already." Later, more stages will come.
- **Q: Does equal mean I don't care?** "If I don't care about them, it's not equal." "As one is dear to me, the other is just the same as dear to me. Without even one it would not be the group."
- **Q: What do I demand from the friends?** "You simply need to show everyone your example, how much you relate to everyone equally and in the best possible way." Pressure and shouting won't help: "Believe me, I'm good at shouting, and there's no results from that."
- Never forget this unity. "If you start working on that, you'll see how much this thing is disconnected from you, how much it's helping you to come out of yourself... to be in each and every one and not inside himself."

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **The foundation is born of two points.** Maximum yearning for holiness and the recognition of one's opposite nature create the real cry, and the light answers by turning the desire's direction, not changing it.
2. **Work only on the good.** Aim at the Creator alone; the "rocks" will reveal the bad by themselves, so don't go looking for it or leave the authority of Kedusha.
3. **Above reason begins simply.** Remain in wholeness under the Creator's governance and accept everything as His direct providence.
4. **Seeing the truth while weak is a gift.** Feeling both "I must bestow" and "I can't" is a diagnosis worth a lot, and from it understanding and willingness grow.
5. **The light only adds.** What we feel as suffering is the gap between the growing light and a reality that doesn't keep pace; we need deficiencies, and the forces come from the light.
6. **Scrutinize from the side of the light.** Hold tightly to holiness and examine the will to receive drop by drop, never descending into it.
7. **Equality is the first condition of a group.** Relating equally to every friend, like the Creator does, with personal example instead of pressure, is the first exercise in resembling Him.

| **In due time** | The gradual growth of the light, drawing reality to its goal; a lag behind it is felt as suffering. |
| **Scrutiny** | Examining the will to receive by the light, while holding fast to the side of holiness. |
