### Opening

The Creator wants to raise the created being to His own degree, not just to fill it with pleasures. To get there, the created being must feel the gap between itself and the Creator, and that gap is felt as **shame**. But the Creator can't simply be revealed, because we would immediately use Him for ourselves. So where does a person actually meet the Creator? Rav Laitman's answer runs through the whole lesson: **never directly**. "I have no other place for a rendezvous with Him" except inside the desires of the created beings, in my attitude to the friends.

## Part 1 — The Whole Round of Creation in Brief

- The Creator, the light, created **one vessel**, a will to receive, and filled it. That state is **Ein Sof**, and "there is nothing besides that."
- The Thought of Creation, "to do good to His creations," means bringing the created being to the Creator's degree: "Not to make him a good child and fill him with pleasures, but to enlarge him up to the degree of truly being the King's son."
- To be equal, the created being must build both the desire and the feeling by itself. To want that, it must feel the gap between itself and the Creator, and "this gap... we call it the feeling of shame." Until we feel the Creator, "we have no shame and we have no desire at all to be similar to Him."
- **Why the Creator is hidden.** If He were revealed now, "we would want Him for ourselves," just as we use every pleasure and every person. So we advance in concealment, through the **surrounding light**.
- Whatever the Creator gives from above, the created being doesn't feel. What it hasn't built itself as a deficiency and filled with feeling "with respect to me... does not exist." This is called still.

#### The shattering, and why

- The created being is only a will to receive. How can it bestow, and to whom? So the Creator **breaks the vessel**: He injects the quality of **Bina** into **Malchut** and brings it to the shattering of **Adam HaRishon**.
- Now the one vessel appears as many unconnected points, "each one in his cell," not feeling the others' deficiency, or feeling it only in order to exploit them.
- Adam HaRishon's soul divided into 600,000 parts, each divided further. If each part takes the quality of Bina, bestowal, and uses it toward all the other parts, it relates to them "as the Creator" and reaches **equivalence of form**.

> This here is the whole thing: from the beginning, to the shattering, to its descent to this world, and its ascent back to the degree of the Creator, to the world of Ein Sof.

## Part 2 — "As Yourself" Is the Measure

- The created being must relate to all created beings as the Creator relates to them. That is **"love your friend as yourself,"** the great rule of the Torah.
- "As yourself" is the scale. "Without knowing how I love myself, I won't know how to love others." So my will to receive is opened bit by bit, and each time I see how much I love myself ("I don't care if everyone outside will burn... so long as I'll receive some smallest pleasure") and correct it into the same love toward others.
- That process, repeated, "is called an ascent on the spiritual degrees."
- **How can a will to receive bestow like the Creator?** The measure of our will to receive is exactly the measure of the Creator's desire to bestow, since He created the vessel with His light. "If I will use the vessel as light for bestowal, then I'll resemble the light precisely. I don't need anything from the outside."

#### Climbing the worlds

- Each of us carries the will to receive of Ein Sof, but feels only its smallest part. The picture felt in that small part "is called this world." It is given only so we have somewhere to start. "With respect to spirituality, we're dead. We don't have a screen."
- After effort in the group draws the surrounding light, a layer of coarseness of the root opens. I feel a great descent, then work to use it in order to bestow. When it's corrected, that is **Ibur** (conception).
- Then coarseness 1, 2, 3, 4 are added. Coarseness 1 is **Katnut** / **Yenika**.

| Corrected coarseness | Felt as |
|---|---|
| Root | Ibur, the world of Assiya |
| 1 | Katnut / Yenika, the world of Yetzira |
| 2–4 | The higher worlds, up to Adam Kadmon and Ein Sof |

- All the worlds and every revelation of the Creator are inside the created being, inside the corrected will to receive.

## Part 3 — Never Opposite the Creator

- "I can acquire a screen only through my effort towards the other created beings... I'm never in a state where I can acquire a screen towards the Creator," even in Yetzira, Beria, or Atzilut.
- As Baal HaSulam writes in the Introduction to the Study of the Ten Sefirot: one who shares in the sorrow of the public merits the consolation of the public. "I never work opposite the Creator Himself until the end of correction. Forget about that."
- Now the souls are my small group, which seems to be my choice, though "actually the Creator brings me into this group."
- One who crosses the barrier stops seeing faces and bodies and begins seeing the desires inside them, the souls. The work stays the same: relate to them as the Creator relates to the created beings.
- My work is always to reattach the broken vessel. Each of the 600,000 attaches the whole vessel to himself, and then a single operation joins all 600,000 corrected vessels: the general end of correction.

> I have no other place for a rendezvous with Him, except inside the desire of the created beings.

- **What is the revelation of the Creator?** The revelation of the upper light inside the corrected vessels, in the Toch of the Partzuf, from Peh to Tabur, to the extent it is corrected.
- **Can I reach correction while others aren't corrected?** I never relate to how corrected others are. Whoever enters spirituality sees all other souls as corrected and himself as uncorrected. Each one starts from zero and goes the whole path, even if he were the last soul in history.
- The generations do change, as surrounding lights accumulate and egoism grows, but that happens through those who have already attained. For each individual, the picture is the same.
- **Body.** In Kabbalah, Guf means the sum of all desires, not the biological body. "If you take my body away from me, my desires remain."

## Part 4 — Love Your Friend, Egoistically and Truly

- Humanity senses this rule is right, and many religions and methods sprang from it. But an egoistic person turns it into a deal: if I'm good to others, I'll gain something in this world or the next.
- "It never happens." Running to hospitals, giving food, donating to funds, "and I get one big zero... because in order to receive is in order to receive." Everything is measured by the screen.
- We can't truly bestow before the upper light, the **light of AB-SAG**, works on us and gives a screen on each degree of coarseness. "Only the light builds the vessel and the light corrects the vessel."

#### Not the whole world, but the group

- If I try to love the whole world, "nothing will come out of it." Baal HaSulam explains in "The Giving of the Torah," "The Arvut," and "Peace" that I can't fully fill even my own needs working 24 hours a day. As a commandment toward everyone, it can't be implemented.
- "First of all, we don't do anything. We don't run around and we don't try to do good to anyone." Humanity tries, and nothing but bad comes of it.
- The intention must come first: to resemble the Creator. Loving the other just because you feel like it "is called a Klipa." Loving the other because you want to approach and resemble the Creator "is already called Kedusha."
- So take a group with one head, one goal: to find the Creator and adhere to Him. Give up our desires of reception toward one another so that He will bestow correction on us. Between us, that is **love of friends**.
- **Acting it out.** "If I act it out... as if I love the friends, as if I want to exit myself and enter into them," I simulate the higher degree. Its light acts on me from afar as surrounding light, gradually gives a screen, and enters as inner light: the sensation of the Creator within the created being.
- Giving away all our abundance, even among the friends, is "a very common mistake in the world."

## Part 5 — The Sixth Sense and the Field

- The screen is an additional sense, "the sixth sense," called the **soul**. It works coherently with the other senses. "You don't start differentiating between this world and the spiritual world."
- You see a deeper layer: the one force, the field, the thought called the Creator, operating every body. With these eyes you see people walking around; with the inner sense you see why.
- **Everyone is righteous.** All of them keep the commands of the Creator "without knowing why," like puppets. Seeing that, you begin to feel yourself wicked: you have an inner area of **free choice**, to join the Thought of Creation or not. That brings shame and effort.
- Joining by your own will makes you, to some degree, free. "From before and from behind, You have besieged me." The horse begins to walk together with the rider.
- **The first revelation**, even approaching the barrier, is seeing that one thought fills and operates everyone, and that "my instincts, movements, everything" will happen exactly as this field changes toward me.
- **How to feel it?** By exerting to exit yourself into the yearnings of the society. Exiting 20% is called the world of Assiya; 40%, Yetzira. People operate automatically because they are inside themselves.
- When you see it, "oh how beautiful," a new will to receive arrives and it all disappears. Work again, until enough force accumulates to move to "a different dimension of existence," where the life and death of the body and distances don't matter.

## Part 6 — The Group as a Concept

- **Does it matter which friend I incorporate with?** "I don't know what type of souls exist in all my friends." The group is the group, called **Yashar-El**, "straight towards the Creator."
- "That is my boat... that I go into and fly towards the goal." It amplifies my yearning.
- The group isn't faces, "the one with the bald head and the one with the beard." It's "our collective yearning to the Creator... not in each one of us, but between us."
- **Short path and long path.** Taking on love of friends in the group to resemble the Creator is the short path, "really the only field of work that we have." Witchcraft, Gematriot, and other studies turn it into a long path of ten or twenty years or more. Baal HaSulam made "The Giving of the Torah," "The Arvut," and "Peace" his most important articles and published them as a newspaper.
- **Keeping the law within** means starting from the **importance of the Creator**, so much that I accept loving others as the means to approach Him. "Always start with the Creator and always end with the Creator. The group is in the middle as a means."
- **Why "a game"?** The Creator, the group, and the yearning feel virtual and keep vanishing, while food, money, and family feel real. Forcing myself above that reality is **faith above reason**, and it's "loose... like a game." When you enter, you see only that is real and the rest is the imaginary world. Everything below the final state of adhesion is a game: "the Creator plays with the Leviathan that He created."

## Part 7 — Actions, Shame, and the Body's Desires

- **Do only intentions matter?** "It cannot be that in our world actions have no importance." A person never knows his true intentions, only in retrospect, from the reaction. Before the barrier we see bodies, not thoughts, so the friends' external actions inspire us.
- Don't act badly outwardly with supposedly good intentions; it makes friends fail. "Towards the society, I have to appear as a good boy, a good friend externally," and advertise good deeds, "because that multiplies love."
- **Stages:** first I see the friend as an egoist, then myself as the egoist and him not. Then shame, "where I can't look at him in the eye," and after that, correction.
- **Beastly desires** (food, sex, family) don't need special attention. There is no coercion in spirituality. "If your head is in the right place, then the heart is drawn after it, and the actions are drawn after the heart." No monastery, no diets.
- If you fall into something, "the mother will come and clean her son." Never regret the past: "The fool sits with his arms folded and eats his own flesh." Connect with the group to the Creator, "forget about yourself... You don't even know who you are."
- Scrutiny of past acts, if needed, is done at a set time, coldly, with the mind. Rabash told how his grandmother would say only "Baruch, watch it," and days later call him in for the consequence.

## Part 8 — Inspiration and the Society

- **How do I stay inspired daily?** "There is no such question." Just as tons of advertising pour onto me and I can't resist, because I'm a will to receive scanning like a radar for the greatest pleasure for the least effort, a society "on fire" inspires me whether I want it or not.
- If it doesn't happen, "it's an indication that you are in the wrong society," or the society is in a descent.
- The society is "the storage for my energies. When I am capable, I invest into the society, and when I can't, the society has to give back to me."
- **Why this Congress succeeded.** People from 25 countries, one mind, regardless of race, color, or mentality. "In the soul there are no such differences," only more or less will to receive, like organs in one body.
- Rav Laitman calls it "a success that is beyond words," because the group not only ascended but also descended together, received the descent "as a common Kli," and began to exit it. "Today no one kissed and no one cried... they left as if they didn't leave."
- The next work is strengthening the connection between groups. That is "already called one nation, one group," what Baal HaSulam describes in "The Giving of the Torah": the Torah could come only to a nation where each supports the other in adhering to the Creator.
- **The next degree:** a stronger incorporation, "despite the distances, despite the differences, despite all the obstacles." If nothing can disturb our connection, "we will receive the screen."

#### Practical answers

- Enemies outside the group, too, are observing the Thought of Creation, "in order to hasten us in our correct development." Not that one rejoices in suffering, but "everything needs to be measured in relation to the goal."
- When artificial shame seems to cancel artificial love: "It's not allowed to be too smart." Like children who play and develop, follow the advice of those who crossed the barrier: **group, study, and dissemination**.
- Daily shared exercises (a motto for the day) help, but the work inside is each person's own, and each reaches personal adhesion.
- Don't disseminate Kabbalah at work. Within the group, the best is no connections besides love of friends: not relatives, not business, not working together.
- On new friends arriving: help them connect, "give them a little to drink, bring them into the song and dance circles," from externality toward internal connection. Alcohol, in moderation and for a purpose, disconnects you from the intellect so "you stop thinking about yourself a little." Drugs "disconnect you from the goal." Lose your "I," "not your human image."

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **The gap is felt as shame.** The Creator hides so we won't use Him; revealed, He awakens the shame that drives correction.
2. **"As yourself" is the measure.** The will to receive is opened step by step so I learn how I love myself and correct it into love of others.
3. **Never opposite the Creator.** The screen is acquired only toward the created beings; the only rendezvous with Him is inside their desires.
4. **Intention first, then the group.** Doing good without the aim of resembling the Creator is a Klipa; the group is the means between the Creator at the start and the end.
5. **Play the higher degree.** Acting as if we love the friends draws the surrounding light that builds the screen.
6. **Never look back.** Put the head in the right place; the heart and actions follow, and the past is corrected.
7. **The society is my storage of energy.** A society "on fire" inspires me whether I want it or not; I invest when I can, and it gives back when I can't.

## Key Terms & Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Shame (Busha)** | The feeling of the gap between the created being and the Creator, felt only when the Creator is revealed; the drive for correction. |
| **Shattering of Adam HaRishon** | The breaking of the one vessel by injecting Bina into Malchut, dividing it into 600,000 souls so each can bestow toward the others. |
| **Equivalence of Form** | Relating to all parts of the vessel as the Creator relates to creation; the created being's way to His degree. |
| **Sorrow / Consolation of the Public** | Baal HaSulam's principle that one who joins the public's sorrow merits its consolation; the screen is gained only toward others. |
| **Guf (Body)** | In Kabbalah, the sum of a person's desires, not the biological body. |
| **Sixth Sense (Screen / Soul)** | The additional sense acquired with the screen, perceiving the one force that operates all of reality. |
| **Yashar-El** | "Straight to the Creator": the group as the collective yearning for Him, existing between the friends. |
| **Faith Above Reason** | Entering, by force, a state above tangible reality that feels like a game until it proves to be the only reality. |
| **Short Path / Long Path** | Love of friends in the group for the Creator's sake, versus other studies that stretch correction over decades. |
