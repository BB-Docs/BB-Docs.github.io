### Opening

The article promises that all of Israel have a part in the next world, but only those who keep the covenant. In the Q&A Rav Laitman removes the idea of a reward after death: there isn't one word written for souls separated from the body. The next world is the next state, attained in this life. From there he takes up why the work is hard if the Creator does it all, what prayer really is, why Kabbalists write texts we can't understand, how the covenant must be renewed every moment, and why there is no button to press, only the request for correction.

## Part 1 — The Next World Is a State, Not After Death

- "We're not talking about after the death." The future, when you are accountable to the Creator, "could be today also. It's a long day."
- "There is not even one word that is written for the souls that are already separated from the body... not even hints." Everything is written for a person who attains now, while alive in this world, in this body. After the body is gone, "by which eyes will you read it? By which ears will you hear it?"
- Baal HaSulam defines the wisdom of Kabbalah as "the revelation of His godliness to His creations in this world." So this world and the next world are simply states: what I feel now is this world, the next state is the next world.
- More precisely, the next world is **Bina**. If I attain the quality of **Israel**, the qualities of Bina, what I feel is called the next world, "felt in one who is in this world just like you and like me."
- Don't depict or hope for anything after death. "Whatever you did not earn, why would you eat?" So we must always search within ourselves for the vessel called the soul, see by which laws it's managed, and correct it. What we will feel in it will truly be the next world.

## Part 2 — Why the Work Is Hard If the Creator Does It

- **Q: Why do we say the work is difficult, yet the whole work is done by the Creator?** The Creator does all the work, "but He does the work by changing us," according to what we want Him to do. "Nothing is done from above coercively." You must prepare a vessel, a need.
- If your desire is correct toward the goal, the light from above carries it out. If it's according to the will to receive's own wishes, the same force of correction is felt as **suffering**, so you will examine more precisely: why is this happening to me, what exactly should I want?
- "Nothing bad comes from the Creator." The good that comes down is received as bad because our vessels are opposite to it. It appears according to the vessel's qualities: "we get sick or we get blows from this or that."
- Until your desire matches the correction the light wants to make for the next degree, the suffering grows more precise and subtle. "This is considered that suffering cleanses or refines the body, the vessel."

> Everything exists in the same light. Only according to your state you either feel suffering, or the force of correction, or the abundance of fulfillment.

- It is the same **Thought of Creation**, to benefit His created beings, received in different variations as we pass through different states.

## Part 3 — The Eve of Shabbat, and What Prayer Is

#### Toiling on the eve of Shabbat

- **Q: What is the toil on the eve of Shabbat?** "The eve of Shabbat is an entire week," the state in which we make corrections and prepare. As in the **Shulchan Aruch**: as soon as Shabbat ends, start preparing for the next one, and keep anything good you see, a nice fruit or garment, for Shabbat.
- Shabbat is the abundance from above, an **awakening from above** we don't build. "The awakening from below is the preparation of the entire week." Without it, "what will you eat on Shabbat? You won't have a vessel." Shabbat is the end of correction.

#### He hears the prayer of every mouth with love

- **Q: If abundance can't be received in vessels of reception, how can we pray for a living or for health?** He "doesn't just hear, He hears the prayer of every mouth of His people Israel with love." His giving and our feeling "are at the same place, at the same point," with no transition between them.
- "If I now feel bad, then whether I want it or not, understand it or not, I feel bad from Him." I can't feel bad and say a blessing; "it's just a lie." But "my feeling itself is the response."
- **Prayer** is that inner response, "with no involvement of the words," even when I'm not aware of exactly what I feel. It is what rises above as my feedback to Him.

#### Correcting the box, not the response

- I can't change the response itself, only ask for a correction of the vessel that responds: the will to receive, so that it receives the abundance correctly.
- "Either I care about how I will feel... Or I care about how He feels my response. I don't want Him to feel a bad response from me. This is called **in order to bestow**. That's all." This exists even between human beings, so it's not completely inexplicable.
- **Q: Does prayer come from egoism?** No. "The prayer is a natural response of mine before I even inspect that response." I have no control over it; I can only prepare by asking Him to correct my vessel.
- Rav Laitman's image: a box receives from above and responds, and the response returns above. "I stand on the side and I can only ask, make it so that this box will behave differently." Then there will be different prayers. "My requests can only be about correction, nothing else," even when things are unclear.

## Part 4 — From Female to Man of War: The Light Does It

- **Q: How do we make the transition from the quality of female to the quality of a man of war?** "Suddenly you're capable of it." Kabbalists describe stages of development, but we never understand them properly. Can I make myself into that character? Did I act correctly?
- So why do they write? To give us "a bait of sorts," like people at the lesson who chew a dry cracker so as not to fall asleep, and meanwhile hear something. Much of the Kabbalistic material is written so that a person engages while "under the influence of the upper light," even through tales.
- "It doesn't matter what a person reads... Maybe even in another language." Understanding is "completely irrelevant." Studying helps you connect, demand and be occupied with it, "but the essence of the action is the light that operates."
- From experience, did I know anything in advance or do anything deliberately? "A person kind of moves forward, he doesn't even know how. He's being led." So: do whatever your ability allows. "Push as much as you can and connect as much as you can."
- There is no beastly quality, simplicity or cunning, that helps a person advance in spirituality, except simply doing what you can.

#### Interpreting articles in the group

- **Q: Should we interpret the articles we read in the society?** Yes, because from there we extract actions worth doing. Otherwise "I'll just shout in the streets, Kabbalah, Kabbalah, or just sit quietly at home." Working in the society brings far greater progress than sitting alone with the book of Zohar.
- "What the mind doesn't do, time does." A person who stays put like a donkey being pushed, though he doesn't want to, still succeeds if he continues. "Just stubbornness and that's it. Each and every day."

## Part 5 — Renewing the Covenant Every Moment

- **Q: Is the covenant a permanent state, or does it change all the time?** "The state of the covenant is called connection. It's an agreement of connection."
- But "I am a new man every day, new desires, new thoughts." What I felt yesterday I may not feel now, whether from external forces or from within. So each moment we must monitor this covenant; perhaps "I don't want it anymore... I hate it, and then I have to go back and sign it again."
- The Creator doesn't change and I do, so I must keep checking how my change fits what I signed with Him. Keeping myself in the state of the covenant while changing "is called repenting," **Teshuva**.
- **Q: What's the point of making the covenant?** Every disturbance, thought or desire is a sign of added **coarseness**, the left line. Aware of it, I immediately turn to the **right line**: restriction, justification, "delighted in mercy," by which I gradually correct it. "That's already a step in making the covenant... I'm going to unify with the Creator."
- Signing means "making the action itself, which is the correction that comes to Malchut."

## Part 6 — Reading to Reach the Root

- **Q: It's not enough to follow the words with my finger, I have to understand. What should we do?** "While we're reading in the book, we need to yearn to reach what's written in the book," to find in me all these discernments and stroll where he strolls.
- A Kabbalist can write only what he feels from within: he is in a certain degree in **Atzilut**, descends to **BYA**, raises the **AHP**; serpents, scorpions, angels, the fathers, the nations of the world are all discernments within him, which he collects into one Kli and aims to the goal, to adhere.
- The particular and the general are equal; all souls share the same structure. "If he's writing about a certain state, my soul is also in that state now as well," though concealed from me.
- "There's no time in spirituality... All the degrees are there, they all operate. I, Michael, am in all the degrees." He describes degree one thousand while I feel degree one. By searching and wanting to experience it, "I am inviting upon myself the **surrounding light** from that high degree."
- This is the whole matter of **branch and root**: if within the branch I want to exist within my root, I draw the surrounding lights from it. "I need to believe them that this is how it is," because surrounding light needs external vessels I don't yet have. "This is the only action that we can do for our benefit," and for it we need a thousand and one actions in the society.
- **Q: Is my root the root of my soul?** Root and branch are not Creator and created being. A root is a spiritual force whose result is a corporeal phenomenon; only the root of all creation can be called the Creator.

## Part 7 — Our Language Is Imprecise

- **Q: The three discernments in the work end in Shabbat; isn't there a contradiction?** "We have a problem with the communication between us," because spoken language is very general. That's why Kabbalists set the language of Kabbalah: discernments, Partzufim, Gematria, a scientific language where it's hard to mistake, though also difficult for us.
- Example: "You are called Adam and not the nations of the world. So what are they? Beasts? Go tell them." In spirituality, one without a screen, below the **barrier**, is zero, not yet a spiritual object; yet in this world he is a human being.
- "The language uses one word for all kinds of different cases," each a different essence. Understanding gradually comes from the inner connection between people, not from precise definitions, "because they're simply not there."

## Part 8 — No Button to Press: Ask for Correction

- **Q: What action of mine is most effective and shortest to reach the corrected state?** "Anything that's apparent to a person at any given moment as the best thing for correction is the action." You can't turn a switch and jump to the goal.
- Each vessel is infinite, **Ein Sof**, with thousands of discernments, corrected one by one. Correct the smallest desire and "I'm already on a level a millimeter higher," with all the other discernments shifted and a new one to correct, according to the root of my soul.
- I don't know which quality or percentage I'm correcting now or next month. Biological systems like proteins and genes "are nothing compared to how the soul is built," and "I don't need to get involved in that. I simply need to push."

> The maturity, the ripeness, is where a person understands that a person is not capable of touching anything, but rather he truly just needs to ask for correction.

- From asking, he gradually becomes incorporated in the correction, and the picture opens before him as before a child, "a wondrous picture." This is called the illumination of the **Shechina**, seeing the whole structure and how it operates.

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **The next world is attained in this life.** Nothing is written about souls without a body; the next world is the next state, the quality of Bina.
2. **One light, three sensations.** Depending on the vessel, the same light is felt as suffering, as a correcting force, or as fulfillment.
3. **Prayer is my response, not my words.** I can't control the response; I can only ask that the vessel be corrected so He won't feel a bad response from me, which is in order to bestow.
4. **The light does the work.** Texts are bait to keep us under the upper light; push and connect as much as you can, and stubbornness over time succeeds.
5. **The covenant is renewed every moment.** Each added coarseness calls for turning to the right line and signing again, which is Teshuva.
6. **Yearn for the degree you read about.** All degrees are in us, and wanting to feel them draws surrounding light from the root.
7. **There's no button.** The vessel is infinite and corrected millimeter by millimeter; maturity is knowing we can only ask for correction.

## Key Terms & Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Next world** | Not life after death but the next state, the quality of Bina, attained in this world and in this body. |
| **Israel** | The quality of Bina, aiming straight to the Creator, by which one attains the next world. |
| **Suffering** | The light of correction felt in a vessel whose desire is opposite to what the light wants; it refines the vessel. |
| **Thought of Creation** | The single intention to benefit His created beings, received in different impressions in different states. |
| **Eve of Shabbat** | The whole week of correction, the awakening from below that prepares a vessel for Shabbat's abundance from above. |
| **Prayer** | A person's natural inner response to what the Creator gives, before any words or awareness. |
| **In order to bestow** | Caring how the Creator feels my response rather than how I feel. |
| **Man of war (male) / female** | One with the strength to overcome and go above reason, versus one who feels his lack but is too weak to overcome. |
| **Covenant (Brit)** | An agreement of connection with the Creator that must be re-signed every moment as a person changes. |
| **Teshuva** | Keeping oneself in the state of the covenant despite one's constant changes. |
| **Left line / right line** | Added coarseness and disturbance, versus restriction, justification and mercy by which it's corrected. |
| **Surrounding light (Ohr Makif)** | The illumination drawn from a higher degree by yearning for it, which improves a person though he doesn't feel it. |
| **Root and branch** | A spiritual force and its corporeal result; not the same as Creator and created being. |
| **Language of Kabbalah** | The precise technical language Kabbalists set because spoken language is general and imprecise. |
| **Shechina** | The illumination in which a person sees the whole structure of the soul and how it operates. |
