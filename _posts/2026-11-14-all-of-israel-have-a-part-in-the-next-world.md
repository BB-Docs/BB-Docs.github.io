### Opening

The Zohar says "all of Israel have a part in the next world," and then seems to add a condition: only those who keep the covenant. Rav Laitman starts from the inner meaning. Israel is the point in the heart, sent from above, and everything else a person must add through labor. The "next world" isn't something waiting after death. It is the state a soul reaches once it has corrected itself to be Israel, "straight to the Creator." From there the lesson takes up what the next world really is, why the work is hard if the Creator does everything, what prayer truly is, the covenant as a connection that must be renewed every moment, and how texts we can't understand still draw the light on us.

## Part 1 — Israel Is the Point in the Heart

- Israel is the point in the heart. When it begins to be felt in a person, "it is a sign that he is invited to develop it." Only this point is sent from above; "the rest, the person must add," and the addition is through labor.
- A person can't create vessels or screens for his desires. The desires, the screens and their right order "are all sent from above." All that's required is the desire and willingness to accept these corrections, even when they feel to the will to receive like being "under the unpleasant steamroller of development."
- "Nothing is done from above without the request of the person." To reach that request, a person can look for the right desire in the society, as Baal HaSulam explains in "The Freedom." Otherwise the desire comes from the Creator in the form of suffering. From the society it can come in a good form or a bad one.
- One who develops this part called Israel begins to feel the next world inside that very desire: "his share in the next world."

#### The map inside

- Everything the texts describe, "Israel and the nations and Pharaoh and the Egyptians and Moses and the Creator," is inside the person. We only have to search for where each of them is within me.
- A person who begins building this inner map of reality adds discernments to it bit by bit. Gradually he begins to feel in the whole picture the parts of creation: "Where is the Creator with all of his qualities and him with all of his qualities and the connection between them."
- A student's note before the lesson: take every article and look for where we are in it. Where is the work of the ten, where are our efforts, which of Rabash's stages am I in, and with each action, "who's receiving the pleasure" and what adds to our connection?

## Part 2 — What Is the Next World?

- **Q: The verse seems to give hope: all of Israel have a part, so if we've made the covenant or circumcision, we already stand a chance. Why break our heads?** "In my eyes this verse doesn't give any hope." If you interpret it in this world, those called Israel here will have a share after they die. But why would you be Israel there? If you interpret it in spirituality, interpret all of it in spirituality.
- "The Torah doesn't promise one anything." As the article says, one who did not toil on the eve of Shabbat, what will he eat on Shabbat? The next world is called Shabbat. "There is hope for one who works," and one who works is called Israel.
- Nor do you deserve anything for a circumcision: "you didn't even do the circumcision. Your father and mother did that."
- It's the same rule as making a living. If you want to live well in this world, you work at your profession. "If you want to earn and live in the next world, please go ahead and work."

#### A state of the soul

- **Q: So do I have the next world now or not? Someone up there decides?** "There is no up there." If I reach a state where I'm called Israel, the state I'm in is called the next world. If I'm now called one of the nations, my state is called this world.
- "There is a soul and the state that it is in." A soul in the form of in order to receive, its world is called this world. A soul in the form of Israel, its state is called the next world. "That is all."
- **Q: Then what is "you are destined to give an account"?** The future is when you reach a state of being accountable to Him. "It could be today also. Look, it's a long day, just beginning."
- "There is not even one word that is written for the souls that are already separated from the body." Not in any book, not even a hint. It's all written for a person who attains now, alive in this body. After that, "by which eyes will you read it? By which ears will you hear it?"
- Baal HaSulam defines the wisdom of Kabbalah as the revelation of His Godliness to His creations in this world. This world and the next are simply states, what I feel now and the next state. More precisely, the next world is **Bina**: attaining the quality of Israel, "Yashar El," I feel the qualities of Bina. "This is also felt in one who is in this world just like you, one like me."

> Therefore, we should always poke within ourselves, search for this vessel called soul. See how it is built, according to which laws it is now managed.

## Part 3 — If the Creator Does Everything, Why Is the Work Hard?

- **Q: We say the work is difficult, and also that the Creator does all the work.** The Creator does the work, but "through changing us." He does it according to what we want Him to do. "Nothing is done from above coercively." You must prepare a vessel, a need.
- If your desire is correct toward the goal, the light carries it out from above. If it follows the will to receive and the changes it wants, then the force of correction "is felt in you as suffering instead of pleasure."
- The suffering brings us to examine more precisely why this is happening and what I should want, until "a little bit more clearly" we see what we truly should want.

#### One light, three impressions

- "Nothing bad comes from the Creator." The good that comes down is received as bad because our vessels are opposite to it. To the extent we correct them, we feel pleasure.
- The suffering takes whatever form fits the vessel, illness or blows in this or that, "it doesn't matter." It turns a person to begin wanting what the light is inviting him to. When the light's desire for the next degree matches his desire, the correction is made. "This is considered that suffering cleanses or refines the body, the vessel."
- Everything exists in the same light. "According to your state you either feel suffering or the force of correction or the abundance of fulfillment." Uncorrected, you feel suffering. Wanting correction, you feel a force giving you screens. With screens, you feel abundance.
- "It's the same light, the same thought of creation to benefit the created beings," received in different impressions as we go through different states.

## Part 4 — Toiling on the Eve of Shabbat

- **Q: What is the toil on the eve of Shabbat?** The eve of Shabbat "is an entire week," a state where we make corrections and prepare ourselves for Shabbat.
- The **Shulchan Aruch** says that as soon as Shabbat ends, you start preparing for the next one. If you see a nice fruit or a beautiful garment, you keep it for Shabbat. The preparations for Shabbat are **vessels**.
- Shabbat is the abundance from above, "and that abundance, we don't build it." It's an awakening from above. The awakening from below is the preparation of the entire week, and without it, "what will you eat on Shabbat? You won't have a vessel." Shabbat is the end of correction.

## Part 5 — What Is Prayer?

- **Q: If abundance can't be received in vessels of reception, how can we pray for a living or for health?** The text says the Creator hears the prayer of every mouth of His people Israel **with love**. "Let's not shorten it." We've seen what Israel means; every mouth means only about the corrections, and with love.
- Whatever I feel, I receive from the Creator, "whether I want it or not, understand it or not." And by that feeling itself I answer Him. "My feeling itself is the response."
- So prayer is "my response with no involvement of the words, even without my awareness of what exactly I feel." That inner response is what rises above.

#### Correcting the box

- I can't change the response itself, only the vessel that responds this way, the will to receive. If it is corrected to receive the abundance in equivalence of qualities, "certainly my response will be good and correct."
- Either I care about how I will feel and yell at Him to make me feel good, or "I care about how He feels my response. I don't want Him to feel a bad response from me. This is called in order to bestow."
- **Q: Does prayer come from egoism?** No. "The prayer is a natural response of mine before I even inspect that response," and I have no control over it.
- Picture a simple mechanism: something enters from above into a box that responds in a certain way, and the response goes back above. "I stand on the side," and can only ask that the box will behave and feel differently. "My requests can only be about correction, nothing else," even when things are unclear.
- **Q: Must I know precisely what I lack?** To ask for the correction of the soul so it responds correctly to the Creator, I really have to study how it behaves, "what is it, who is it."

## Part 6 — Texts That Wash Us in Light

- **Q: How do we move instantly from the quality of female to the quality of man of war?** Kabbalists describe all the stages of our development, but "we never understand these stages properly." Can I make myself into the character he describes? Did I understand or act correctly?
- They write it to give us "a bait of sorts." Like people chewing a dry cracker to keep from falling asleep while they listen, much of Kabbalah is written so a person stays engaged under the influence of the upper light.
- "It doesn't matter what a person reads." It could be an unclear text or another language. Understanding "is completely irrelevant." It helps you connect, demand and stay occupied, but "the essence of the action is the light that operates."
- From experience, too: did I do anything deliberately? "A person kind of moves forward, he doesn't even know how he's being led." So do whatever your ability allows: "Push as much as you can and connect as much as you can." No beastly quality of this world, simple or shrewd, helps a person advance.

#### Why interpret at all

- **Q: Should we interpret an article in the society, or is it redundant?** We should, because from it we extract actions worth doing. Otherwise I'd just shout "Kabbalah" in the streets or sit quietly at home. Working in the society brings greater progress than sitting at home with the Book of Zohar.
- "And what the mind doesn't do, time does." Like a donkey that's pushed and thrown around but keeps going, a person who stays put day after day succeeds. "There's no intellect here," no discount from above, "just stubbornness, and that's it."

## Part 7 — The Covenant, Renewed Every Moment

- **Q: Is the state of the covenant permanent, or does it change all the time?** The covenant is a **connection**, an agreement. If I'm a new man every day, with new desires and thoughts, then in a moment I no longer feel so connected to what I signed yesterday, whether from outside forces or from changes within.
- The Creator doesn't change; I do. So each moment I must check how my change fits what I signed with Him and correct it, "and then I have to go back and sign it again." This is called **repentance**.
- **Q: Then what's the point of making a covenant?** Any thought, desire or disturbance is a sign that coarseness was added: the **left line**. Being aware of it and wanting to correct it, I turn at once to the **right line**: restriction, justification, mitigating judgment with mercy. Gradually correcting that addition "is already a step in making the covenant."
- Signing is the action itself, the correction that comes to **Malchut**. "The signatures are like the letters."

## Part 8 — Drawing Light from the Degree the Kabbalist Describes

- **Q: Is it enough to follow the words with my finger, or must I understand?** While reading, yearn to reach what's written: to find all these discernments within me "and stroll exactly where he's strolling."
- A Kabbalist writes only what he feels now. He describes a degree in **Atzilut**, descending to BYA and raising the **AHP**, serpents and scorpions and angels, the fathers, the sons, the nations. He arranges all these discernments, good and bad, into one desire and aims them to the goal.
- The particular and the general are equal, the same structure in a great Kabbalist and in me. If he writes about a state, "my soul is also in that state now as well," perhaps a thousand degrees above me and concealed. "There's no time in spirituality." All the degrees exist and operate.
- In degree one I can't distinguish those actions. But by searching and wanting to experience them, "I am inviting upon myself the **surrounding light** from that high degree." Whether it's written as a fable, a Torah story or the study of Sefirot and Partzufim, it describes how I act in spirituality. This is the matter of **branch and root**.
- I don't feel the surrounding light; that needs external vessels I don't have yet. "I need to believe them that this is how it is." It is "the only action that we can do for our benefit," and for it we need a thousand and one actions around the society.
- **Root and branch** aren't the Creator and the created being. The root is a spiritual force, and its result in our world is a corporeal phenomenon. Only the root of all of creation can be called the Creator.

#### Language that isn't precise

- **Q: Doesn't the article contradict itself, calling one who doesn't feel a lack "not yet a man" and then describing his stages of work?** That's a problem of communication between us. Kabbalists chose the language of Kabbalah, Partzufim and gematria, a scientific language where it's hard to err. But it's still a language of this world.
- It's written "you are called Adam, and not the nations of the world." So are they beasts? "Go tell them that they're beasts." One without a screen is zero in spirituality, below the barrier, not yet a spiritual object. Yet we can't read the branches of this world that way.
- "The language is short." One word covers truly different essences, so each time we have to see what we attribute it to. Understanding comes out of the inner connection between people.

## Part 9 — No Button to Jump to the Goal

- **Q: If I feel really bad, what is the shortest, most effective action to reach the corrected state?** "Anything that's apparent to a person at any given moment" is the best action for correction. Don't search for a switch "to jump and reach right into the goal."
- Each vessel is infinite, **Ein Sof**: thousands upon thousands of discernments, corrected one by one. Correct the smallest desire and "I'm already on a level a millimeter higher," with all the other discernments shifted, and then another discernment comes up to correct according to the root of my soul.
- Biology shows how changing one small gene changes everything, "and those systems are nothing compared to how the soul is built." "I don't need to get involved in that. I simply need to push."
- "The ripeness is where a person understands that a person is not capable of touching anything, but rather he truly just needs to ask for correction." From asking, he becomes incorporated in it, and like a child he's shown a wondrous picture, the whole structure and how it operates: "To see the **Shechina**."

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **Israel is the point in the heart.** It alone is sent from above; through labor and the request for correction, a person develops it until he feels his share in the next world inside it.
2. **The next world is a state, not an afterlife.** A soul in order to receive lives in this world; a soul corrected to Israel, "straight to the Creator," feels Bina. Not a word of Kabbalah is written for souls without a body.
3. **There is hope only for one who works.** No verse promises anything; the whole week is the eve of Shabbat, the preparation of vessels for the abundance from above.
4. **One light, felt by the state of the vessel.** The same thought of creation is felt as suffering, as a correcting force, or as abundance, and suffering refines the vessel until its desire matches the light.
5. **Prayer is my response before I inspect it.** I can't change it, only ask that the vessel responding be corrected, caring how the Creator feels my response rather than how I feel.
6. **The covenant is a connection to renew every moment.** Each added coarseness is the left line; turning to the right line and correcting it is signing the covenant again.
7. **Push and ask; the light does the rest.** Understanding is secondary: yearning to be where the Kabbalist describes draws surrounding light, and maturity is knowing we can only ask for correction.

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Israel (Yashar El)** | The point in the heart, the desire aimed straight to the Creator; one whose actions are all for the Creator. |
| **Next world (Olam HaBa)** | The state a soul feels once corrected to Israel; the quality of Bina, attainable in this life. |
| **This world** | The state of a soul in the form of in order to receive. |
| **Covenant (Brit)** | A connection and agreement with the Creator that all actions are for His benefit; renewed each moment as one changes. |
| **Eve of Shabbat** | The whole week of labor and correction that prepares vessels; without it, "what will you eat on Shabbat?" |
| **Shabbat** | The abundance from above that fills prepared vessels; the end of correction. |
| **Prayer (Tefila)** | The natural inner response to what the Creator gives, before any inspection; it rises above as my answer to Him. |
| **Correction (Tikkun)** | The only legitimate request: that the vessel responding to the Creator be changed to respond in bestowal. |
| **Suffering (Yisurim)** | The same light felt in an opposite vessel; it refines the vessel until its desire matches the light. |
| **Surrounding light (Or Makif)** | Light drawn from the degree a Kabbalist describes when one yearns to be there; it corrects without being felt. |
| **Branch and root** | A spiritual force and its corporeal result; texts in any form describe roots so the branch can draw from them. |
| **Left and right lines** | Added coarseness and disturbance, versus restriction, justification and mercy that correct it. |
| **Ein Sof (infinite vessel)** | Each vessel holds thousands of discernments, corrected one by one, each opening a new level. |
| **Shechina** | The whole wondrous structure revealed to one who asks for correction: "to see the Shechina." |
