### Opening

Why would anyone need a covenant? If I'm strong, I do what I want; if I'm weak, I don't want to be bound. Rav Laitman explains that a covenant comes from mutual dependence, and in the spiritual work it comes at a precise moment: when a person has attained a degree, checked it to its full depth, and recognizes that falling below it would be the worst thing possible. Then, besides an agreement for now, he asks the Creator to keep it for him forever. The lesson asks what that covenant is, what it requires of us, and how it works with the group, with the Rav, and before the barrier.

## Part 1 — Agreement and Covenant

- A covenant means "from here on we have a connection, under certain conditions." On every degree of the spiritual ladder, from the first step to the end of correction, there's an agreement between us and the Creator, each time in a different form and measure.
- From the Creator's side, He is good and does good, with no limitations; He agrees to anything a person wants in this connection. **The conditions, and the desire for the agreement, depend entirely on the person.**
- **Agreements come from weakness.** A strong person doesn't need one, and a weak one doesn't want one. We make an agreement when each depends on the other and makes concessions to the other.
- **The Creator shows Himself as weak.** He needs our love and attention one hundred percent. "The Shechina is in exile; she cries that we don't pay attention to her," like a mother who needs her child whether good or bad.
- **His one condition:** the connection must be in what we share. I can't ask Him to help me steal or murder. I can only ask for "the things that are equal in Him and in me," which means a degree where we are already in order to bestow.

#### From agreement to covenant

- Once we've reached a certain mutual understanding, love, and fear, I realize that confusions and unjustified reasons could someday make me want to break it. (If the reasons are justified, the agreement is cancelled anyway.)
- But if I recognize that breaking it would lead to the worst possible relationship, "worse than death," then I make a **covenant**: whatever disruptions come, the state we have now will be kept, never less.

> An agreement is what I write for now. A covenant means forever, that we promise each other that we will hold on to each other on the same level as now.

- We check the agreement in every state our degree can bring until it proves sustainable. Then the next degree comes, with hatred (left and right lines in the created being), connection, a middle line, coupling by striking, a new agreement, and again a covenant that it never be less. This happens "each time we determine our relation to the Creator."

## Part 2 — Before the Barrier

- Even before the barrier we have all these discernments, "just in a form called ordinary concealment," not concrete enough to work with, like children playing with dolls and cars. "A game is a serious thing, because it builds the person."
- So search within: if right now I feel good about the Creator in even one channel, one out of the 620 channels of the soul (He is great in my eyes, I fear Him, I'm grateful), then in that channel I can ask that it stay forever and that He remind me of it and renew it even in my worst state.
- I can't guarantee the future, "however, my desire to guarantee myself for the future already gives me the next vessel." I seemingly insert it as **MAN** into the higher degree, which keeps it and prepares its filling.

#### What makes the request real

- From His side He always keeps it. The only thing I can do is raise MAN: a fear for the future. Where does it come from? From having fallen many times and seen how much I suffered when I was disconnected from Him.
- That earlier suffering prepares a mature request, not crying "like a little child": **"Do not cast me away from You, and do not take Your holy spirit from me."**
- **Gather the suffering, don't lose it.** Previous degrees vanish unless we reach their final discernment; "only the final discernment, registered in the Malchut of each state, remains." In our world we sign agreements out of lack of choice, after fighting and secret talks. With the Creator I must sign not from lack of choice, but because "I have no better state than that, whatever may come."

## Part 3 — Covenant in the Group, and With the Rav

- **The group** is well suited for this: friends can watch over each other and help each other keep the covenant. A covenant among friends that is connected to the covenant with the Creator will hold in both, "and one will support the other."
- Without the Creator it fails. Russia tried to build a covenant of love among people with no connection to the Creator, and "they ended up destroying themselves." But a covenant whose purpose is the Creator "is a tremendous force. There is nothing like that in advancing."
- **Rav and group** are two degrees, the Rav above and the group below. Neither side is corrected, and both change constantly, so there can't be an absolute covenant between them. The Creator is the standard: the connection exists only in what each has that resembles Him.

> The presence of the Creator in the covenant between us is the first and last condition. No more, no less.

- If He disappears from the connection, "there's no connection, there's nothing between us," and that's how it should be. Any other condition between a student and a Rav makes it "not a pure connection."
- **Where covenants begin:** with a baby who delivers himself into the hands of the upper one, Abba and Ima. It starts with the covenant of circumcision; later a person makes a covenant with the Creator himself, then with others.

## Part 4 — Covenant and Faith

- **Isn't fear of the future a lack of faith?** A covenant is precisely a request that He will look after me at the worst moments, made after I've checked that this state is the best I've had. "We don't sign a covenant for today, we sign it for the future."
- **Faith** is believing He can keep it: in the covenant are my recognition and His ability, His good will, His loyalty to me. "To that extent I connect with Him in a covenant for the future, and I deliver myself to Him, not only now but in the future. This is called true faith."
- **How do I know He accepted?** First I must feel Him as a partner: "know the God of your father and serve Him" is the first condition. A covenant is a sign for the future, built on a present degree whose vessels, lights, and circumstances I've fully checked and attained. Without that, "there are no conditions for the covenant."
- **The minimum state** I want to keep is my *Katnut*, my smallness on that degree. *Gadlut* will come, but "at least to remain in this degree," like Atzilut's permanent state. And there are many covenants in each degree, one for every discernment: "I felt You were great... I want this discernment to remain with me forever. Don't take it away from me."
- A person doesn't sign by remembering a lesson. Cause and consequence bring him to the state called signing a covenant. And the next degree can come "after a second." In spirituality there's no difference between the next second and the next hundred years.

## Part 5 — The Covenant in the Language of the Sefirot

- **The covenant is the final condition in each degree**, the last correction on the Kli, after which comes **coupling by striking** and attainment of the degree. Without it there's no coupling and no degree.
- Rabash's example: telling your wife "I love you until the end of my life, but every five minutes I may stop" isn't a covenant; it erases the whole connection. Spiritual means eternal.
- This is what's called "until He who knows all mysteries will testify that he will not return to his folly." Each degree is infinite in its measure, a part of Ein Sof.
- **What do we strike?** Not the Creator or the light. "I'm striking my will to receive," my intention to receive, which wants to let the light in unconditionally. Then I couple with the intention to bestow.
- **What do we share with the Creator to sign with Him?** Only mutual bestowal: "from His side it's only bestowal, so also from us."

#### After the covenant

- After adhesion on the current degree comes a new state. "I'm not me and You're not You": a different Creator, a different created being, entirely new conditions, from zero to one hundred percent. Only the cause of the next state remains from the previous one.
- The previous covenant isn't broken; it's preserved for eternity, since in spirituality whatever exists, exists, and the upper light sustains it. Falling deeper into the left of the next degree isn't called breaking the covenant. Here Rav Laitman stopped: "I have no more words... we lack a vessel of reception."

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **The conditions are ours.** The Creator agrees to everything; the depth of the covenant depends only on what we're ready to keep.
2. **An agreement is for now, a covenant is forever.** It comes after a degree is checked to its depth and anything less is seen as the worst state.
3. **Past suffering is the material for the request.** Fear of losing the connection, built from earlier falls, is the MAN of the covenant.
4. **The Creator must be inside every covenant.** Among friends or between Rav and group, His presence is the first and last condition.
5. **A covenant is faith for the future.** I deliver myself to Him not only now but in every state to come.
6. **The covenant is the last correction before coupling.** Without it, no coupling by striking and no attainment of the degree.
7. **Practice it before the barrier.** Even in one channel of connection, ask that it never be less.

## Key Terms & Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Brit (Covenant)** | A commitment for the future, after a degree is fully checked, that the connection with the Creator will never be less than it is now. |
| **Agreement** | The mutual terms of connection on a given degree, made out of mutual dependence and concession. |
| **Shechina in Exile** | The Creator's "weakness": His need for our love and attention, like a mother's need for her child. |
| **MAN** | The request raised to the upper degree; here, a mature fear for the future built from past suffering. |
| **Final Discernment** | The completed scrutiny registered in the Malchut of a state; only it remains, while incomplete discernments are erased. |
| **Katnut** | The minimal, permanent state on a degree that one asks never to fall below. |
| **Coupling by Striking (Zivug de Hakaa)** | Striking one's own will to receive, then coupling with the light through the intention to bestow; it follows the covenant. |
| **Faith** | Believing in the Creator's ability and loyalty to keep the covenant, and delivering oneself to Him for the future. |
