### Opening

The whole of reality is the Creator and the created being, and the perfect, eternal state already exists. We are already in it. So why don't we feel it? Rav Laitman recalls Baal HaSulam's image of a person in a dark basement full of treasures, with no light to see them. What's missing is a **vessel**, and a vessel appears only through a deficiency for that exact state. The lesson asks how a person gathers all his desires into one desire, the "synagogue," and where the strength and the importance for that come from.

## Part 1 — Gathering the Desires into a Synagogue

#### "Does not walk in the counsel of the wicked"

- A person who comes to equivalence of form is called one who "does not walk in the counsel of the wicked." The **wicked** are his own uncorrected desires, which keep awakening in him.
- Each time, he scrutinizes them and doesn't follow their counsel. His ego burns in him constantly, and precisely through this **help against him** he keeps updating his position toward the Creator, and grows.

#### The synagogue (Beit Knesset)

- When he gathers all his desires into "one great big desire in one direction," toward bestowal, he reaches a genuine prayer from the depth of the heart.
- That gathered desire is called the **synagogue**, Beit Knesset, a house of assembly, because "there he has already gathered all his desires together as one, toward one goal."
- The desires can be animate, emotional, human. "It doesn't matter. All of them are concentrated within him toward one goal."
- Then the Creator "sees that there is a person in the synagogue," someone ready for prayer who demands corrections, and he receives corrections for all his desires.

#### Why ten

- "No congregation less than ten" means not until all the qualities he has acquired are also examined, taken to their depths, and aimed at bestowal, "like the original ten Sefirot."

#### Strength from the light, importance from society

| What is needed | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| The power to bring all desires to the intention to bestow | The **surrounding light** shining on those desires |
| The **importance** of doing it | The **society** |

> To the extent that the wicked desires rise in him, he must strengthen himself against them through the society, receiving from it the importance of the goal. Then he is assured that the surrounding light will bring all of them to bestowal. "That is essentially the key subject of the article."

## Part 2 — A Place or a Person?

- **Q: Why is desire sometimes called a place (a synagogue) and sometimes a person filling that place?**
- Rav Laitman answers in terms of internality and externality. Sometimes the **point in the heart** is spoken of as the center, with all other desires gathering around it: animate, human, and later even the desires of the **Klipot**, all becoming one collective toward the same goal.
- That point is "the point of Moses, which truly leads all the other desires."
- The terms come from the verses the author chose to build on. Without them he would have used other words. "He can express it in any language, at any scale." Don't make the terms formal; the internal discernment is what matters.

## Part 3 — Attracting the Surrounding Light

- **Q: Can we invite the surrounding light only while studying?**
- The surrounding light is the light destined to clothe in the vessel once it has a **screen**, the intention to bestow. "I currently have no intention to bestow. So the light is outside of me and shines upon me from that same place where I'm destined to be."
- Every person has a place reserved above the **Parsa**, beside the Creator. There, I am connected with the light, the source I study, the other souls, and the Creator: "Israel, the Torah and the Creator are one."
- To draw that light down, I need to make something below the Parsa "similar to that state," according to effort and desire.

#### Two ways to make a likeness

- **Study:** as the Introduction to TES says, one who studies and yearns to know, even without understanding, attracts surrounding light. If through study I want adhesion with the Creator, "it doesn't matter that I still imagine adhesion in my own terms."
- **Friends:** above, all souls are one body, "one man with one heart." Below, I must do this with several friends, and that too becomes a correspondence.

#### What society actually gives

- Society doesn't mean sitting together and saying "let's think about that state." It must give me the **importance** of being there.
- "First I need to hear from them that this is the most important thing... that there is nothing else in life besides it," instead of hearing about fashion, football, and a thousand other things.
- "The importance I give it myself will never be enough." There are special people who can do it alone, "but we're not among them."

## Part 4 — One Desire in the Lesson

- The study is meant to be a state where all the friends gather "with all their thoughts, with all their desires, with all their prayers" into one desire toward the Creator. Instead of 70 or 80 people, "one desire from this whole building."
- During study there must already be "a decision and a clear understanding" that we are one desire asking for correction, and certainty that it's coming, because the Creator is waiting and we've done the preparation.
- "Everything depends on preparation." If we just sit and open the book, the benefit "might also come after several more incarnations."
- A **decision** is not a note stuck on the wall. "Write it on the tablet of your heart."

#### Different people, one desire

- **Q: Each one has his own character and imagines something different. How can it be one desire?** Eighty people building a house: it doesn't matter that one pictures a palace and another a little house. "We are all concentrated on one goal."
- Each one contributes according to what he was given. One can think about it only a moment a day; another is fully immersed after a month.
- **The effort is what counts.** If Erez sits for 10 minutes listening and that's a great effort for him, while Gilad studies as usual, "Erez will contribute more than Gilad. We cannot examine these things."
- "We are forbidden to speak with one another about our personal states."
- What we should talk about: "not about how I relate to the Creator, but... about Him." The importance of the goal, not what I get from it.

#### Buy yourself a friend

- How important a friend's words are depends on how important the friend is to you. A child's words matter to parents simply because it's their child.
- "Invest in him as you invest in your own child and you will see how important it will become for you. Buy yourself a friend."
- If the goal were really to be in the lesson as one group with one intention after maximal preparation, "you would organize your society completely differently."

## Part 5 — What Exertion Really Is

- **Q: How should I relate to pleasure that comes from exertion?** Rav Laitman questions the premise: "It seems to you that you're making exertion." Kabbalah's terms don't mean what they mean on the street.

#### Faith, as a parallel case

- On the street, faith is believing there is a Creator who rewards and punishes.
- In Kabbalah, faith is "a feeling of the light of Chassadim" that lets me become **Chafetz Chesed** above my egoism, "a tremendous force" received from the revelation of divinity. **Reason** is what is revealed to my eyes now; faith is above it.

#### Exertion (Yegia)

- On the street, exertion is sweating, praying, studying a lot. A student thinks that's what he's doing.
- In truth, exertion is a **determination of the state**: the discovery that by my own powers I can never reach the goal. "Effort above human capability."
- I reach the end of my forces and see that nothing of mine can do it. Then comes the true shattering, an outcry, the true prayer.
- He demanded something of me "that isn't in me" so I'll discover the gap between us. That gap appears only at the end of all the scrutinies, "an infinite cut-off point." Then it's clear that only His blessing, the letter **Bet** of Bereshit, can bring salvation.
- **Q: So exertion is the prayer raised once he sees there's nothing he can do?** Yes. Exertion is the result of many actions, but the concept itself is "a revelation that is above my powers."

## Part 6 — Advancing While He Is Hidden

- **Q: The Creator is concealed. How can we be sure He exists, rules, and helps?** The Creator is revealed to the extent that leaves us **free choice**. Until a person crosses the **barrier** (Machsom), he is in darkness, yet as he advances he receives more surrounding lights.
- **Q: Is it a set sequence, double concealment, then single concealment, then reward and punishment?** "No." There are many aspects we can't arrange in a clear sequence.
- Laying out a fixed map ("this is phase one, this is phase two") "is not good. By that, you steal from the person his uncertainty. The feeling of uncertainty is very beneficial."

#### Laziness and the social effort

- Beyond the barrier the work with friends is different. "All the work is here, all the exertion is here," in double and single concealment.
- "In the main thing, which is the social effort, we are lax." A person waits until the state explodes before acting. Laziness protects him from doing empty things, but also stops him from doing anything.
- "We think that we need to improve the situation in others, not always in myself." So put yourself in the friends' hands so they improve you, and do the same for them. "If you can give your friend your life, your fate, everything. Go ahead."
- Without the one shared desire, "the entire learning is not worth anything." The society must constantly remind me of the greatness of the goal and of the Creator.

#### Closing sharing

- Friends highlighted "buy yourself a friend," feeling new sensitivity even to friends outside their ten ahead of the regional Congress, preparing with the ten before the lesson, and the value of uncertainty.

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **The perfect state already exists;** we lack only a vessel, a deficiency aimed exactly at it.
2. **The synagogue is all my desires gathered into one** toward bestowal; the uncorrected desires are "help against me" that keep updating my direction.
3. **Strength comes from the surrounding light; importance comes from the society.** My own sense of importance will never be enough.
4. **Make a likeness of the state above:** study with yearning, and connect with friends as one body.
5. **The lesson must be one desire,** built through preparation and a decision in the heart; the effort, not the person's state, is what counts.
6. **Exertion is discovering that the goal is beyond my powers,** which gives birth to the true prayer.
7. **Uncertainty is beneficial,** and the main work before the barrier is the social effort: "Buy yourself a friend."

## Key Terms & Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Congregation of Ten** | All of a person's acquired qualities examined and directed to bestowal, like the ten Sefirot; also the ten friends as one body. |
| **Synagogue (Beit Knesset)** | The state in which all of a person's desires are gathered into one desire toward one goal. |
| **The Wicked** | A person's own uncorrected desires, which keep awakening; he scrutinizes them and doesn't follow their counsel. |
| **Help Against Him** | The ego that burns in a person, through which he keeps renewing his position toward the Creator. |
| **Surrounding Light (Ohr Makif)** | The light destined to clothe in a vessel with a screen, shining from outside; drawn by making a likeness of the upper state. |
| **Parsa** | The boundary below which we are; above it, the souls, the Torah, and the Creator are one. |
| **Importance of the Goal** | What a person receives from the society, without which he can't overcome his desires. |
| **One Desire** | The goal of the lesson: all friends' thoughts, desires, and prayers united toward the Creator. |
| **Decision in the Heart** | Not a written resolution but an inner commitment, "the tablet of your heart." |
| **Buy Yourself a Friend** | Investing in a friend until his words become important to you. |
| **Exertion (Yegia)** | The revelation that the goal is above my powers; the point from which the true prayer comes. |
| **Faith** | The force of the light of Chassadim, received from revealed divinity, that lifts a person above egoism. |
| **Uncertainty** | Not knowing the sequence of one's path; beneficial because it keeps the person's scrutiny alive. |
