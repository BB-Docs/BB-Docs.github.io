### Opening

Halfway through the Congress, the group is tired, confused, and "heavy." Rav Laitman calls this the best state. The lesson turns on one idea from Baal HaSulam's "The Last Generation": the source of all our mistakes is that **we eat unripe fruit**, taking on actions we haven't yet grown into. How do we avoid both leaping too far ahead and sitting and waiting for history's blows? And what do we do with the new, heavier ego that has just been added to us?

## Part 1 — Work With the Text

- A lesson "is good, but it passes." Even if replayed on video, it's better to work with the text itself.
- Print it, work on it with a pen as if editing it, copy out parts for yourself. Then "each of us becomes not a reader, but a writer or an editor."
- If he hadn't read Baal HaSulam's article some two hundred times, "it would amount to not reading it at all." Only when a text becomes completely familiar do you begin to see what's in it, and each time at a different level.
- This isn't only for teachers; it's for everyone. The articles being studied (The Giving of the Torah, The Arvut, and others) were being prepared for publication as booklets.

## Part 2 — Is Kabbalah the Only Path?

- For a person standing "small, naked, like a child in the middle of the world," all methods look equal; each is drawn by his inner structure, toward the scientific, the emotional, or something in between.
- Kabbalah says clearly, as in Baal HaSulam's poem: you will see, and no one else will see it for you. Only once you cross the **Machsom** does the vision of the upper world open, and it is vivid, measurable, repeatable, transmissible. Compared to it, our present world is "apparent," and its connections dissolve, though you keep living and moving among people.
- Until then there are "greater and greater doubts." Mount Sinai is named for the mountain of doubts under which a person stands, unable to imagine overcoming it. Without overcoming them, he doesn't receive the light that corrects them.
- **"Go and check."** His Rav's answer to this question was the same one he now gives: we don't hold anyone. He himself had visited Israeli Kabbalists, the Kabbalah Centre, Baba Sali. At his Rav's instruction he went to hear a young Kabbalist in Holon who "spoke correct things," yet his Rav showed him this was an instinctive sense from a small ego, not attainment through a growing ego.

#### Two kinds of method

| Methods that negate the ego | Kabbalah |
|---|---|
| Move less, consume less, think less, isolate yourself | Uses the ego as it grows, with the correct intention |
| Sense the upper worlds "externally," as fluids or movements | The worlds and the Creator are revealed *inside* you |
| Suited to small ego (even a large earthly ego is "almost zero" spiritually) | For one whose spiritual desire has ripened |
| Remnants of ancient methods preserved in the East | Built on including the Creator in all your interferences |

- People who come and leave need to keep ripening, "if not in this life then in the next." There's no need to regret it.
- The Ari says an unripe person who is included in a strong Kabbalistic group, even mechanically "like a child," develops much faster. But until his true **Reshimo** emerges, he isn't really included; he acts because others do. That's why there's no coercion: the group's shared efforts draw **Ohr Makif** on such people so their Reshimot ripen faster.

## Part 3 — The Unripe Fruit

> The source of all our mistakes is that we eat unripe fruit, meaning we perform actions we're not yet able to perform.

- **The root in Adam.** The breaking happened on purpose, so we could attain the Creator's level ourselves. Had Adam eaten the fruit "ripe," with a screen, there would have been Gmar Tikkun, but an unconscious one. The full light and the full Kli touched, and the desire lost its screen because it wasn't prepared.
- **Russia and the kibbutzim.** Baal HaSulam uses Russia as an example: altruistic laws imposed by force on an unripe society break it. The collapse doesn't prove the idea false; it proves the society was unfit for it.
- **Bnei Baruch's own experiments.** An anonymous donation box collected more at first, then declined as ego grew and no return was felt. The group prepared to settle in a kibbutz, took courses, and even passed the kibbutz movement's exams. "We were ready for anything."
- **Neither extreme.** If I sit and wait, "the steamroller of history" crushes me into crying for help: the path of suffering. If I leap too far, I imagine I'm ready for Gmar Tikkun and wonder why I'm not there. The method exists so we take on exactly the next small step.
- **Borrowed euphoria.** If we tried to live now as the society of "The Last Generation," we might feel days of euphoria, from a strong surrounding light several degrees above us. "It will not be ours. It won't correct us." Only light from the **AHP of the upper Partzuf**, one step above, can correct us.
- The effort must be "coercive and conscious": against the ego, but sized to our present qualities. We can speed up passing through the Reshimot, "but not by jumping over" them.

#### Q&A: Where does the correcting light come from?

- Baal HaSulam wrote from the level of Gmar Tikkun. The light of Rabbi Shimon, the Ari, Baal HaSulam, and Rabash is "one and the same soul" descending into our world. But it reaches us passing through every degree, through the AHP of the one just above.
- "In principle the AHP of the upper one is myself": what I prepare for you. Whatever you imagine of the spiritual is "some billionth part" of a tiny upper degree connected to you.
- **Learn from our past.** He holds nothing back; the group went through "absolutely everything," and providence kept it from entering states that would have destroyed it. By connecting to Bnei Baruch, a new group can get in half a year to where another took ten, "like a child in the arms of an adult." Consult on every small step.

## Part 4 — The Giving of the Torah: Why a Group

- The method of correction wasn't given to one person, such as Abraham, but only when a nation arose: 600,000 people who had suffered Egyptian (egoistic) slavery and each agreed to satisfy the neighbor's desires. The number describes "a whole structure," a group, not a count; ten, twenty, fifty, or a hundred can be it.
- This happened then unconsciously, the upper root clothing its lowest branch. Now, from the Reshimot of the latest stages of **Aviut**, we must reach the same conditions from below, by our own desire.
- **Naaseh veNishma.** On an egoistic level we can't see the altruistic level in advance; we have no Kli for it. "First the screen is acquired, and then the light is received into the screen."
- **Spiritual, not physical, communal living.** We take the law of mutual concern only in spiritual form; adopting shared property now would be an unripe fruit. The group is an invisible "spiritual object" that an outsider can't see. When it reaches a **critical mass**, the Creator is revealed and raises it to the next degree, and the process repeats.
- **Arvut.** Rabbi Elazar, son of Rabbi Shimon, says the world is judged by its majority: one who corrects even one desire tips himself and the whole world to merit. All 613 corrections reduce to "love your neighbor as yourself."

## Part 5 — Tired and Confused Is the Best State

- He's "very happy" to see the group tired and muddled. These are new Kelim forming; the next degree is being built. Don't drive the thoughts away, or you won't use the Aviut now manifesting.
- **There's no such thing as tiredness.** "There is nothing aside from these two parameters": the thickness of the ego and the intensity of the light. Aviut has been added with no light facing it yet. Waited for passively, the light may take years or a generation; drawn by effort, it's "earned," the path of light rather than suffering.
- The first days ran on what was built in preparation. Now "there was evening and there was morning, one day." The new day begins with the evening.
- **Clothe the spiritual in life.** It's easy to float into the clouds; the work is to come down. Two hours of study a day count only by how they're pushed into the other twenty-two: work, wife, children, mother-in-law. Kabbalists obligate a person to take part in life, not withdraw from it.
- **The exit is from despair.** "There's no greater darkness than before exiting into the upper world." Don't wait for someone to start singing to cheer you up. Begin working, "with a little smile."
- **The feeling "it's all a game."** The urge to walk out ("what's real is my job, my family, a beer with friends") is itself the Creator's gift: the same force that makes life seem hopeless is what gathered us.

#### Examples of thanking the blows

- **Rabash** would lock himself in a room in a terrible state and dance, bringing himself out on every level toward the next spiritual state.
- A Kabbalist asked how he could live with a shrewish wife answered that thanks to her he had attained everything.
- On Rabash's first day at the *Cheder*, the teacher struck him with a ruler, then held out his hand: "kiss the hand that holds the ruler."
- The blows "are his inner flesh, his ego, in which he can feel the Creator. How can I ascend if I don't step on it?"

#### Why the group must remind you

- New Aviut comes as **Malchut without Bina**: "the point in the heart is given to you only once." You may not remember who you are for a week.
- So the group must hold each one "in full combat readiness." You can't do it alone; you must invest in advance, "like an insurance fund," and get back your own deposit.
- **The Ari** told his students a leap to Jerusalem that Friday could bring Gmar Tikkun; each sent an excuse. With **Baal HaSulam**, a group advancing toward a state of utmost tension lost it when one student sighed.
- Sitting dejected, "I am simply cutting off oxygen to the rest of my friends," drilling a hole in the common boat. "That's what I should be ashamed of before them."

## Part 6 — Q&A: States, Rest, and Connection

- **Is Gmar Tikkun constant excitement?** No: "a blessed state of rest," balance between you and the Creator. Each time ego grows, correction grows to meet it, and when they coincide you feel peace.
- **How to handle shifting moods?** States come "completely arbitrarily." Control only your intention toward each. Invest today, in yourself and the group, so that in any next state you can restore the direction toward the Creator at once. "The future is today." The left line is not bad but effective.
- **Critical mass and the world Kli.** Every group in the world, near or far, determines my next state; "like someone gravely ill, I am in their hands."
- **How to be included in the Rav?** The same way as in the Creator: by similarity. Take his thoughts and desires and work in them. Mechanical closeness is unnecessary; someone far away and rarely in contact can be truly close.
- **Reshimot.** We were once one Kli filled with light. Falling to our degree left Reshimot, now awakened in reverse order; working them through restores the connection. "There's the Creator, there's the Kli, there's the light."
- **Only trial and error?** "There is no righteous man who has not sinned." But included in the main group, you can pass through such states in moments rather than years. When the group proposes ideas that bring it closer, his answer is "try." "We are the first, we must try everything," just not utopian leaps.
- **What does the group do together?** Only external expressions of spiritual actions: building the Sukkah, dissemination, a common center. "We're just creating a spiritual community, invisible."
- **Going home with a heavy suitcase?** It's "a purely psychological barrier." A second inside the group is enough to make a lifelong connection.

## Part 7 — Pharaoh Is the Creator's Back

- Pharaoh asks: "Who is the Creator? Why should I obey him?" Tiredness, detachment, every thought against the goal is called **Pharaoh**. Watch how he works inside you and look for methods against him; that is **recognition of evil**.
- Or picture the Creator in his place. Inside this "evil angel," this Aviut, sits the Creator, adding it on purpose so we learn to resemble Him.
- **The king and the slave** (from Baal HaSulam's letter): the king staged attacks on himself and thefts from his treasury; the slave could have taken the throne or the treasure but defended the king, and so became equal to him.
- Our interferences are the **Achoraim**, the reverse side of the next degree, "like a seal and an imprint." If the Creator showed His face (**Panim**), we'd act automatically from ego and never grow. The light is a formless force; all the form comes from our properties. "If we turn them inside out, the face of the Creator appears."
- **The daily motto.** The group continues its practice of a *mesima*, a task of the day, to think about as much as possible. Today's: what to do with the Aviut that has grown within us.

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **Don't eat unripe fruit.** Every mistake, from Adam to Russia to the kibbutzim, comes from acting before inner readiness. Take only the next small step.
2. **Correcting light comes from one step above.** It reaches us through the AHP of the upper degree; borrowed euphoria from higher degrees doesn't correct.
3. **The group is spiritual, not communal.** Mutual concern in intention, with joint actions limited to spiritual expressions, until a critical mass forms.
4. **Tiredness is added Aviut.** There is only ego and light; a heavy state is the evening of a new day and the most fruitful place to work.
5. **Bring the spiritual into all of life.** Two hours of study count by how they enter the other twenty-two.
6. **Invest in the group in advance.** New ego comes without the point in the heart, so only the group can remind you; letting yourself sink cuts off your friends' oxygen.
7. **Interferences are the Creator's back.** Pharaoh is the reverse imprint of the next degree; turned inside out, it shows the Creator's face.

## Key Terms & Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Unripe Fruit** | Taking on an action or correction before one is inwardly ready; per "The Last Generation," the source of all mistakes. |
| **Machsom** | The barrier to the spiritual; only after crossing it does the vision of the upper world become clear. |
| **Aviut** | The thickness of the will to receive; added in each new state, felt as tiredness and confusion until light is drawn upon it. |
| **Surrounding Light (Ohr Makif)** | Light shining from outside the Kli, drawn by effort; it becomes Inner Light as the Kli is corrected. |
| **AHP of the Upper Partzuf** | The part of the degree just above that connects to us; the only source of light that can correct us now. |
| **Reshimot** | Records of the states we passed through in falling; awakened in reverse order and worked through to return. |
| **Naaseh veNishma** | "We shall do and we shall hear": the action (screen) comes first, then the light and understanding. |
| **Critical Mass** | The measure of shared desire in a group at which the Creator is revealed and raises it to the next degree. |
| **Arvut** | Mutual guarantee; each one's actions tip himself and the whole world toward merit or guilt. |
| **Pharaoh** | All the inner interferences against the goal; in truth the Creator acting from behind. |
| **Achoraim / Panim** | The reverse side of the Creator felt as interference, versus His face, revealed when those properties are turned inside out. |
| **Mesima** | The group's task or motto of the day, kept in mind throughout the day. |
