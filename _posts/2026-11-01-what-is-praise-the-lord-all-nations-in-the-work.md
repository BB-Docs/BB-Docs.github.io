### Opening

Rav Laitman says the whole article is about one "very subtle matter": the **uniqueness of the Creator**. Man was created corrupted so that by correcting himself he would recognize that the Creator is "one, unique and unified," that there is none else besides Him, and that recognition is what gives the Creator contentment. But how can a person discover oneness? Only from its opposite. The lesson explains why we need the darkness, why the attainment must be felt as our own in the fourth phase, how the gap between opposites becomes our spiritual degree, and why the only place to work on all this is with the friends.

## Part 1 — The Whole Work: Recognizing That the Creator Is One

- "What a person has to discover, and his exertion, is the uniqueness of the Creator." The Creator created man "because He wants for man to acknowledge that the Creator is one, unique and unified... This is why He created man corrupted."
- The degrees of that recognition are the degrees of man's correction, of equivalence of form. Someone "from the outside" has to acknowledge there is nothing besides the Creator, and by that man gives Him contentment.
- All the disturbances, even "if I am not for me, who is for me?", exist "only in order to reveal the uniqueness of the Creator."
- In this recognition we feel two things: that we feel good and that He feels good. But "our entire work is only in relation to us." We don't feel the Creator; we feel our equivalence of form with Him. Rav Laitman notes that the **Ramchal** emphasizes this beautifully, and that Rabash loved the Ramchal.

> Besides the Creator, there's a man that discovered that there is nothing besides the Creator. That's it.

## Part 2 — Discovering Oneness from Its Opposite

#### White only from black

- "You can't discover anything but from something that is the complete opposite." White is revealed only against black, not against green, which may contain some white.
- So to reveal the Creator as one, unique and unified, everything opposite to that must appear in us. In the chain of feelings we discover the opposite, and "from the oppositeness, from the empty vessel, from the record, we reveal the uniqueness."
- It is not infinite: the **125 degrees** are 125 actions that bring us to complete recognition, and that limit is the **end of correction**. Each action includes the **posterior**, the darkness, the feeling opposite to what we must attain.

#### It must be felt as mine

- It isn't enough that the Creator shows us opposite forms and then corrected ones. We must be "complete partners," feeling that both the opposite qualities and the corrected ones are **ours**.
- That is why attainment happens in the **fourth phase**. In the first three phases (the first nine Sefirot) I understand qualities I received from Him; in the fourth phase "I feel that it's my own quality, both corrupted and also corrected."
- The worlds, like the still, vegetative and animate in nature, have feeling but no independent self, so they aren't called a created being. "The created being is actually called outside of the Creator." All the souls of **Adam HaRishon** come from phase four, which can feel this oppositeness.

#### Pharaoh and recognition of evil

- **Q: How can one fall into idol worship?** By feeling that he is like the Creator: that is "the measure of Pharaoh," "Who is the Lord that I should obey Him?" Through blows a person begins to understand that "it's not I who's in control, but He." Only at the tenth plague does he recognize that it's his own ego in disparity of form.
- What is revealed first is always the posterior, the uncorrected form, and it must be felt to its end: that is **recognition of evil**. But we also need to see how the evil turns into good, going through each phase "from Dalet to Gimel to Bet to Aleph to Shoresh."
- "The more you approach the correction, the more you discover how uncorrected we were previously." At the start of the way, telling someone he's the opposite of the Creator means nothing to him. Like Rabbi Shimon who felt like "Shimon from the market": the greater one is, the greater his inclination.

## Part 3 — Bread: The Connection of Bina and Malchut

- In the article, a person's desires are called **earth**. Rav Laitman connects this to the blessing over bread.
- Animals eat what is ready-made. Man must prepare his food: plowing, sowing, harvesting, separating, until it becomes bread. "By your own efforts will you eat bread."
- Bread is "the fruit of faith," the result of the actions between **Bina and Malchut**, by which Malchut becomes food for man. Wine and oil are other such branches of the connection between Bina and Malchut, and so are the seven special fruits of the land of Israel.

## Part 4 — The Border Between Opposites Is My Degree

- **Q: Is oneness attained on each degree?** Yes. Each degree is corrected only when a person recognizes the one, unique and unified on that level. As in *Inner Observation*, "the Creator includes all deeds" before, during and after I discover them. "He himself does not change." Only my recognition of Him changes.
- "We don't talk about Him at all. We talk about how we feel Him." All of reality, people, myself and everything, is part of the feeling of the Creator through which I discover His oneness, and when I discover it "all these things vanish" into the feeling of **Ein Sof**.

#### Contrast and coupling by striking

- "I can't discover what white is and I can't discover what black is. I can only discover the difference between them." Without a border between opposites "it's invisible to me."
- On the first degree the contrast between me and the Creator's quality is very small. I equalize the two forms, then attain an even greater oppositeness, and grow. The same ten Sefirot exist on the smallest and the largest degree, "but the distance between them... is called my level."
- From that collision comes **Zivug de Hakaa**, coupling by striking, and in the body of the Partzuf we attain unity. Choice and independence are the conditions for it: "I need to feel that this is my me."

#### Where does the first spark come from?

- From the higher degree. The **AHP of the upper** is in each degree of the lower and shows him, in its vessels of reception, how to perform unification, "like the adult teaching the young," as in Abba ve Ima and Keter of the world of Nikudim. "If I don't follow an example, I fail."
- Like starting an old car by pumping the gas, the initial awakening comes from the light illuminating the **silent Reshimot** of the broken vessels. How deep the light shines determines whether I want money, honor, knowledge or spirituality.
- "We're never in control of the vessel." But we can draw the light more strongly, so we reach the "speaking" degree in the Reshimot quickly instead of over many years. "It's only from above that we can change our situation."

## Part 5 — Vessels Before the Light: Turning Suffering into Sweetness

- **Q: How do I know I'm advancing?** Baal HaSulam (Letters, p. 17): "If I'm more and more lovesick, I am advancing. That's all."
- **Q: Is there suffering on every degree?** What matches my desire I feel as pleasure; what doesn't, I feel as suffering. His wife likes double the salt: "She feels pleasure from that. When I feel that, I feel suffering." There is no suffering or pleasure outside the vessel.
- So "bring the vessels before the light." The Reshimot are predetermined, but if I bring a desire before entering the state, even something unbearable becomes "eminently desirable." This is how the path of suffering turns into the **path of Torah**, and how the **posterior becomes the front**.
- "Our vessels are called yearning, and not the desire itself, because the desire itself I have." The will is His creation; the **yearning** is mine to generate: "an attraction to the thing that is called love."

## Part 6 — Q&A: Oneness Through the Friends

- **Q: Why do I need a society? Why not a book or an alarm clock?** We are alone inside the will to receive. Emerging from it happens by working on the friends so they work on me: "I attain my desires which are in them," and I must suppress my will to receive to connect with them.
- **Q: Why not work with the Creator the same way?** With the Creator we can work only once we've reached true, eternal desires, even a little. With the society we can work while still rising, falling and forgetting, in states "before consciousness."
- **Q: How do I see the friends and myself as one?** Take their desires and suffering as yours, and their good qualities too. "You can't unite with the Creator if not through the friends." Your attitude to the friends is "an indication of your measure of annulment towards the Creator... It's the same Creator," only here it is revealed to you while He is concealed.
- **Q: They seem to have bad qualities, and I won't gain anything.** Both doubts show you are "completely outside" of the next degree: you don't yet understand what the proper vessel is or what the outcome of unity should be.
- **Q: How do I cancel my resistance to connection?** "I, by myself, cannot perform corrections. This I need to ask for from above." Trying to get along with the friends without the Creator is artificial and fails, like the kibbutzim or Russia. That's why "the commandments between man and man precede the ones between man and the Place": there you see everything in the field.
- The chain: I need the Creator to connect with the friends; I connect with the friends to break my ego and acquire a screen; I acquire a screen to connect to the Creator. "If someone just listens from the side... he'll say, what dummies, kindergarten."

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **The whole work is recognizing oneness.** Man was created corrupted so that by correcting himself he recognizes the Creator as one, unique and unified, and that gives Him contentment.
2. **Oneness is revealed only from its opposite.** Every degree begins with the posterior, the darkness, which must be felt in full.
3. **Attainment must be mine.** Only in the fourth phase are both the corrupted and the corrected qualities felt as my own.
4. **The gap is the degree.** We feel only the border between opposites; the greater the contrast we unite through coupling by striking, the higher the level.
5. **The light awakens the Reshimot.** We control nothing in the vessel, but we can draw the light so it awakens us more deeply and quickly.
6. **Yearning is our part.** Bringing the desire before the state turns suffering into sweetness and the posterior into the front.
7. **The friends are where the Creator is tested.** Our attitude to them shows our annulment toward Him, and connecting with them requires asking Him for help.

## Key Terms & Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **One, unique and unified** | The recognition that there is none else besides the Creator, attained degree by degree until the end of correction. |
| **Posterior (Achoraim)** | The dark, opposite form that appears first in every degree and from which oneness is revealed. |
| **125 degrees** | The 125 actions leading to complete recognition of oneness; their limit is the end of correction. |
| **Fourth phase** | The phase in which a person feels the qualities, corrupted and corrected, as his own: the created being. |
| **Recognition of evil** | Feeling the uncorrected form to its end, then seeing how it turns into good. |
| **Bread** | The fruit of faith: Malchut turned into food for man through the actions of Bina. |
| **Coupling by striking (Zivug de Hakaa)** | Uniting opposite forms through their collision; its outcome is unity in the body of the Partzuf. |
| **AHP of the upper** | The higher degree's vessels within the lower, showing it by example how to perform unification. |
| **Silent Reshimot** | Records of the broken vessels that awaken only as the light illuminates them. |
| **Vessels before the light** | Bringing yearning before a state so it is felt as pleasure rather than suffering. |
| **Lovesick** | Baal HaSulam's measure of advancement: growing longing. |
| **Between man and man / man and the Place** | Work with the friends comes first because there the work toward the Creator becomes visible. |
