### Opening

Rav Laitman starts with the title: "Are there any other kings in reality besides the Creator?" There is none else besides Him, so why say we choose Him? His answer is that turning to the Creator, making real contact with Him, is possible only through **equivalence of form**. A request that isn't aimed at His qualities doesn't reach Him. The correction that makes a true request possible is **faith**: a force from above that lifts a person above the will to receive. The rest of the lesson asks what faith is, how we get it, and what the group has to do with it.

## Part 1 — Calling Upon Him in Truth

- Contact with the Creator is possible "only according to equivalence of form." When a person acquires even one of the Creator's qualities, he is "already connected with the Creator through this quality." That measure is called **truth**.
- So "a person should know to whom he's calling": I need to know, in some form, what qualities the Creator has, and yearn for them, starting with those easiest to build on top of my will to receive and moving to harder ones, "until I will be completely in my qualities as the Creator."

#### What "MAN" means

- **MAN** is "a lack for correction, so that my quality will be exactly like His quality. Make your will as His will."
- If my desire to be like Him isn't corrected, "I'm still wrong about who He is," and I'm not calling Him in truth. That deficiency "is not called MAN," and the upper one doesn't feel it.
- **The sick child:** the doctor tells the mother what she may and may not give. The child screams for everything; the mother gives only what the doctor allowed. "It is the same with our MAN": there's an answer "only on the condition that my desire equalizes with His desire."

#### Faith as the general correction

- A request for self-benefit can't be a correct request. "It has to be for His benefit, in order to bestow," above my will to receive and above thoughts about myself.
- The quality that lets us think outside ourselves, "meaning ultimately about the Creator," is called **faith**. It too comes from above.
- Before a person has it, "he's not turning to the Creator, he's seemingly only turning to himself."

## Part 2 — Why Study Alone Didn't Help

- Rav Laitman points to the **Introduction to TES** (around items 12-15): Baal HaSulam saw students "to whom the engagement in Torah did not help," so they never reached Lishma through the light in it.
- These were people who "truly invest... 24 hours a day truly from the bottom of their heart, but the method is incorrect."
- Baal HaSulam's conclusion: no engagement in Torah helps a person who doesn't acquire faith, meaning a state where he rises above the will to receive. "And this is what he should demand."

> Demanding faith is actually the essence of our engagement. While we study, while we read our books, we must yearn only to receive from above a special power that will help us disconnect from our will to receive.

#### The middle line is a soul you can't know in advance

- Rabash says the right and left lines come as two angels, "operated from above." The **middle line** is the Creator giving the soul, "a part of God from above."
- "Before we receive this part of God from above, how can we know what it is?" Each new soul adds a middle line, and the person doesn't know which part he'll receive.
- Right and left are "only the conditions for receiving the soul. They are not the soul in itself." This is **"I labored and I found"**: work between the two lines, and the result comes from above with no visible link "besides cause and consequence."

## Part 3 — Raising MAN Correctly

- **Q: Is knowing the Creator's qualities the first stage?** "How can you start with knowing the qualities of the Creator? It's impossible." What he said was to raise the correct request.
- As in this world, you need conditions: the one you turn to has what you want and wants to give it, and you know how to ask. "If they exist altogether on your side, this is called raising MAN."
- From above, the answer is certain. "The Good that does good" is operating, with a single condition: "Ask for what I have, and ask in such a way that it will be for your benefit."
- Our work is **preparing the vessels**: having a deficiency for what He wants to give, according to the Reshimo of clothing and the Reshimo of coarseness. Yelling and studying are also counted; they don't activate the upper one directly, but "bit by bit these things are accumulated."

#### The store, the boss, and the shirt

- To get a new shirt, I go to the store and pay. For money I go to the boss, who wants work. For work I need fuel: understanding that the shirt is important enough to make me exert.
- "There has to be some equivalence between the desires and the understanding of the connection. It's the same everywhere. There's no innovation in spirituality."
- **Item 17** (Introduction to TES): there is no greater commandment than acquiring faith, the vessels and qualities of **Bina**. When Malchut acquires Bina's qualities, it rises to Bina, "and then everything is open."

#### Faith and knowing

- **Knowing** is what I feel and want inside the will to receive. **Faith** is wanting to work above that, "in spite of whatever I feel within the will to receive."
- That is for the correction of bestowing in order to bestow. Later, in receiving in order to bestow, one works with knowledge and feeling inside the will to receive too, "something that we can't quite imagine."

#### One quality or all of them?

- **Q: How can we measure qualities in percentages?** Holding one of the 620 qualities means holding them all; "it appears to me that it's only one, because that's how it gets clarified. It's not a quantitative thing."
- A drop of seed, an embryo, and a newborn all have every organ. "The only question is, are they activated, are they felt, are they under his control in order to bestow."
- Phrases like "the light of Nefesh in the vessel of Keter" are expressions of the degree of work. "Don't relate to the language formally."

## Part 4 — What Faith Is

- Faith is "a force that a person acquires that helps him be above his beastly nature": desires for food, rest, and sex, and then for money, honor, and knowledge. All of these "are called our heart."
- Inside the heart is a desire for the Creator, the **point in the heart**. The work is for that point to stand out and have "greater determining power than the rest of the heart."
- To yearn for the Creator "until He doesn't let me sleep," as written in Pri Chacham (p. 70): then one moves from Lo Lishma to Lishma. Having risen above beastly and human lusts, "his heart doesn't attract him," and the point in the heart itself is corrected into **Chafetz Chesed**, "delighting in mercy."

#### Faith is not a feeling

- "Faith is the feeling of the Creator. I said it many times, and it's not precisely so."
- Faith is a **result**: a person who feels the Creator acquires a power that makes him independent of his will to receive. "His will to receive surrenders."

#### Before the barrier

- **Q: How can a person learn the Creator's qualities before the barrier?** He can't. "We never know ahead of time." The work is in stages: I labor, gradually understand a little of what bestowal is, and yearn for it to that degree.
- "I cry like a baby who's crying. He doesn't know what he's crying about, but he feels bad."
- **Item 18:** there's no point thinking you'll develop until you first acquire this disconnection from the will to receive. As Baal HaSulam writes in the Speech for the Completion of the Zohar, if a person thinks of himself, "there's no greater separation between him and the Creator." Faith is skipping over that cut and beginning to think outside oneself.

#### "One should say" — acting on a desire you can't command

- On p. 262 Rabash writes that a person sees he is far, yet "should say" the Creator is close and showing him his deficiencies. How can you tell a desire what to feel? "The desire feels the filling," and words from the side don't reach it.
- So "we have to bring about and cause a state where I'll feel."
- **Jacob:** I can't reach directly into someone's desire. I perform actions on him, and as a result the desire forms in him. "The same thing relative to myself," because my will to receive "is not under my control." I can change it only through the Creator.

## Part 5 — Faith as the First Step

- **Q: Is faith a condition or a path?** "The attainment of faith is the first step on our path," the first stage from which we can speak of spiritual actions, meaning in order to bestow.
- After the shattering, even Bina's vessels are used to receive, because they sit inside Malchut. The correction begins with a restriction on Malchut's vessels; then Bina's vessels start to be corrected, working only with **Galgalta veEinayim** and not with the **AHP**.

#### The role of the intellect

- The intellect "is an external thing," but it's all I have besides uncorrected vessels. I use it to decide that engaging in Torah to correct myself is worthwhile, even for reward. "From Lo Lishma we come to Lishma."

#### Egypt is inside us

- All of it is in us: Egypt, Pharaoh, the Egyptians, Moses, Aaron, and the people of Israel in exile.
- The Exodus means one point called Moshe, "only one intention, one small desire," escapes. "Pharaoh and all those things remain in us" for now.

## Part 6 — Faith in the Sages

- **What is faith in the sages?** It's what makes you go to the doctor. You know you're sick, and someone understands your state better than you: where it was born from and how to reach a better state from it.
- To come to such a sage means to believe him "against my intellect," even when what he says contradicts what I think.
- **Hand in the fire:** if the doctor said that to be healthy you must put your hand in the fire, your beast wouldn't let you. You'd need "a force from above" greater than the natural force that keeps your hand out. "That's called a new creation." In faith in the sages, "you're acquiring his intellect, his wisdom."

#### Is suicide also going against nature?

- **Q: Isn't that like someone who commits suicide?** No. That is a calculation within nature, "his beastly calculation," to escape suffering. Animals make such calculations too. Rav Laitman adds, "Don't judge your friend until you reach his place."
- **Q: Can a regular person be in faith?** "Of course he's not capable. One who has acquired faith is no longer a regular person."

## Part 7 — How to Study

- **Preparation** comes before the lesson: what a person wants "as a consequence of the study." "I don't care too much what I'm reading, I care about the results."
- Why this book? Because of faith in the sages, who said there's a **virtue** in it, like a medicine. On a 15-hour flight he and a student took sleeping pills: "We didn't know how the pill works, we just knew that it's a virtue."
- What should the virtue work on? Acquiring faith, "not to be immersed in myself, not to constantly think about my vessels, my desires, and how I will fill them."
- If I don't yet feel how harmful that immersion is, I can demand that too: that the book show me how far I am from spirituality. That distance felt as a disease "is called the **recognition of evil**." Then I'll want the medicine, the **Reforming Light**.

#### Where the first desire comes from

- "From the book I don't have, from the Creator I didn't receive." What remains? "The group of friends. That's what you're forgetting."
- Before the lesson, friends need to think together about how and why to study, and "inflate each other's minds." Seeing them burn, I start to envy; my pride is hurt. Envy, lust, and honor take a person out of this world to the upper world, and "we need to use this, and then we come to the study."

#### Exertion is toward the society

- **Q: Where is the exertion?** "The exertion is towards the society." The rest is "a desire that begets another desire." You can't exert toward above; you exert with your beastly qualities toward the friends, feeling smaller than them and their message important.
- "Leave the Creator. You can't turn to Him directly. Through the society you'll receive a desire towards above."

#### Showing why you work

- **Q: How do I receive a desire for the Creator from friends?** He gives the video department as an example: a hired expert at $2,000 a month might work better. What matters is showing that "spirituality is more important than two thousand dollars a month," so people around you are impressed.
- Working humbly so no one is impressed "is a loss." It's easiest to be impressed by work similar to your own, because you understand what it takes. "I'm impressed not by him making a lot of noise," but by what's produced.

## Part 8 — Signs of Progress

- **Q: What signs show we're going the right way?** They differ and even contradict, because we're made of vessel and light. As I advance, my deficiency grows and I can feel worse, or I can feel better.
- Better to measure by the quality of the deficiency and filling: is the deficiency precisely toward the Creator, and is the filling "because I'm connected with Him"?
- The clearest sign: **the number of changes** in a period of time. After a trip to America, so much happened in two days "that we barely remember." Being thrown this way and that in the inner work "is a sign that you're advancing."
- One can even be happy digging the hole deeper, since "until I reach a certain depth of this hole... I won't reach spirituality."
- In the closing sharing, the ten's role was framed as giving every friend "the place, the infrastructure" to make these changes, keeping them in the importance of the goal.

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **There is only one King, but contact with Him requires equivalence of form.** A request is "true" only when it aims at His qualities.
2. **MAN is a request to be like Him:** "Make your will as His will." Other demands aren't felt above.
3. **Faith is the force to rise above the will to receive.** It comes from above, it's the first step of the path, and demanding it is the essence of study.
4. **Faith isn't a feeling;** it results from feeling the Creator, which makes the will to receive surrender.
5. **We can't know in advance** what soul the middle line brings: "I labored and I found."
6. **Faith in the sages** is following the doctor's wisdom above my own reason, with a force greater than nature.
7. **The first desire comes from the group.** Exertion is toward the friends, and the sign of progress is the number of inner changes.

## Key Terms & Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **"We Have No Other King But You"** | Only the Creator can give the power to serve Him for His greatness; the person learns this after doing all he can. |
| **Calling in Truth** | Turning to the Creator through a quality shared with Him (equivalence of form); otherwise the call is "false." |
| **MAN (Raising MAN)** | A request for correction, to make my will as His will; only this activates the upper one. |
| **Faith** | A force from above that lifts a person above the will to receive and lets him think outside himself; the qualities of Bina. |
| **Faith vs. Knowing** | Knowing is what I feel in the will to receive; faith is working above it. |
| **Middle Line** | The soul the Creator gives after work in right and left lines; unknown until received. |
| **"I Labored and I Found"** | The work is done without knowing the result, which comes from above as its consequence. |
| **Point in the Heart** | The desire for the Creator inside all other desires; the work is to give it decisive weight. |
| **Chafetz Chesed** | "Delighting in mercy": the corrected state of not thinking of oneself, rising to Bina. |
| **Faith in the Sages** | Going by the wisdom of one who sees the cause and cure of my state, above my own reason. |
| **Virtue of the Book (Reforming Light)** | A remedy that works without our understanding; we must know what we want it to change. |
| **Recognition of Evil** | Feeling my distance from spirituality as a disease, which creates the need for the remedy. |
| **Exertion Toward the Society** | The only place a person can exert; through the friends comes a desire toward the Creator. |
