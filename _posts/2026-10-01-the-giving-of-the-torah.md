### Opening

The Congress is ending, and the question on everyone's mind is "what do I do next?" Rav Laitman's answer: now the real, conscious work begins, the work of mastering your own **spiritual independence**. But independence from what? From the Creator Himself, who lifts us up and drops us like a yo-yo. And the only place a person can build that independence is the group. The lesson moves through why the group is the only path, how to handle the descent that is about to come, and a long round of practical questions, ending with the meaning of Purim.

## Part 1 — Why Only Through the Group

- Baal HaSulam's "The Giving of the Torah" (item 13) describes two kinds of actions: between man and the Creator, and between man and man. Rav Laitman says the actions toward the friends are closer and more important, because toward the Creator "I can't relate to Him, He doesn't react to me."
- Toward the Creator everything is absolute: "If you made a screen, you're with Him. If you haven't, you're not, and there's no intermediate state." With the Creator a person can imagine anything, and over the years he has seen people convince themselves they are "somewhere between the spiritual worlds" without having started work on their qualities. People invent customs, rituals, even cults.
- In the group, every state can be checked. Friends criticize us, we criticize them, we invest in them, and in hard moments they come back to us. Our relationships are "criticizable and controllable" by me and by others at once.
- This is why the common soul was broken on purpose: so that we could find the Creator's qualities in our relationships with other souls and merge with Him "only there, in that common Kli."

> Either you are in the group or you are spiritually dead. There is no third option.

- He has seen "smart ones" refuse the group for five, ten, fifteen years and then realize they wasted those years because they could not overcome a "petty little egoism." You have to "step on yourself," even when every second you want to run away.
- What is "a little higher than us"? The articles: the twenty or thirty main articles Rabash wrote for us, together with Baal HaSulam's, contain everything. Only then will we start to feel what is really in Talmud Eser Sefirot.

## Part 2 — Spiritual Independence

- **Don't waste the good states.** When you wake up inspired and everything shines, "that's not me. That's how I was woken up." It is light descending from above, not your own work on the **Reshimot**. Use it: open difficult passages, relate clearly to the friends, write down your states, and later look at what happens when it is taken away.
- When the light leaves, a person can become completely lifeless, unable to "move a finger." Record these states and ask: who am I, and why am I being played with this way?
- **The yo-yo.** The Creator pulls us up and down, giving euphoric states and then draining all our life force, to show how dependent we are, "hanging on a little thread." With this He shows us the boundaries between our upper and lower states, within which we must learn to exist on our own.
- **Freedom from whom?** From the Creator, the only one who controls everything. The difference between a small spiritual degree and a larger one is the **degree of independence from the Creator**. Even if He drains all my energy, I can still rise on my own to the level set for me, and at the next degree the range grows.
- So there is no room for melancholy or depression. The group must demand that each member get out of such states quickly, "because otherwise they're holding the entire group back." It must cultivate such a fire that no one can sit "moping around somewhere down below."

#### The triangle: the Creator, me, and the group

- A person who becomes independent can generate his own desires and moods. The states he has processed become his "own internal power plant."
- He has invested so much in the group that when the Creator adds ego and he falls a little below it, he rises at the group's expense. When he rises, "according to the law of connected vessels," he overflows and gives his accumulation back to the group. "He already has a reserve in the group," so he isn't afraid of the next fall.
- The left line is what the Creator does to me; the right line turns out to be the group. Both the Kli and the light come from the Creator. What depends on the person is how to combine them.
- Independence only truly begins at the **Machsom**, when a person starts to build a screen: Ibur, Yenika, Mochin and onward. Below the Machsom we are a hundred percent dependent, and "nothing will be asked of you." Even the greatest sinners and the greatest righteous ones, if they haven't crossed it, are spiritually "inanimate, non-existent."

## Part 3 — Silence, Reshimot, and Disturbances

- **Silence is inaction.** The Creator is in absolute rest. He doesn't need to do anything to awaken the next Reshimo; "as soon as you process the previous Reshimo, the next one immediately emerges." If states change slowly, that's how you are working with them.
- Changes of state can reach several hundred per minute, until the sense of time disappears. Kabbalists switch back and forth hundreds of times a minute.
- **How do I know I'm processing my Reshimot?** If new disturbances arise every minute and they push you, despite themselves, to look for connection with the Creator and the friends. Inner silence is "a terrible thing"; it means you've stalled.
- **There are no disturbing thoughts.** What we call disturbances is additional Aviut, given so we can make a greater ascent.
- **"I've become cynical like a surgeon."** That is because ego was added. Before, you were like a small child who laughs and cries at anything. Now you must add **Zakut** (refinement) to the new **Aviut**, a screen at its level. Holding on to unity with the Creator and the friends with "this harshness, cynicism, cruelty" is very difficult, and it is the work.

## Part 4 — The Descent That Is Coming

> A good effort always ends with a great descent, and then a very slow, independent ascent.

- The Congress made an enormous leap. Over these days the group joined under one idea and created its "first common Kli," and now, like the vessel the Creator built through phases zero to four and then broke, it must undergo a breaking: decline, rejection, hopelessness, disappointment, "felt by everyone."
- Until now every spiritual state was given from above, "like a mother, a father showing a child how to act." If we now restore our best state together from inside this shared ego, it will be a state **we** achieved, not one given to us. This will be "our first truly independent action, a joint one, with a great Kli and great Aviut."
- **Don't wait for things to get worse.** Don't think "when my full Aviut comes, then I'll make the leap." At the slightest disconnection, cry out for help, demand an ascent, rally the group, and do something. Otherwise you keep falling, "not by your own will."
- **Can I ascend without falling?** Yes, if you search from your present level how to rise. Like the old man who walks bent over as if he lost something, look for more opportunities to ascend. Then the same light that adds Aviut will give you the forces to correct it, and push you forward.
- **How do I notice a fall beginning?** If you are always with the friends on the same topic, same desire, same goal, then the slightest separation from this common effort is a fall.
- **Two dimensions at once.** On one hand, through the group I aim at the Creator; on the other, I work, talk, and deal with life's problems. Live in both freely, without discomfort, until they merge into one when the Creator is fully revealed.

## Part 5 — No Individual Revelation

- "You have nothing that's individual." The Creator is revealed in the Kli you have glued together with your friends, and ideally with all of humanity, never in the "I." The "I" is what the **Tzimtzum** was made on; the light does not enter it.
- Humanity's biggest mistake is trying to fill its own "I," chasing a pleasure that always leaves. Light exists only in the nine upper Sefirot above Malchut. Think how those nine Sefirot can become our common Kli. This is the general rule of the Torah: **love your friend as yourself**.
- "If you can't convince yourself of this, then wait until life convinces you, and a couple more reincarnations."
- **Mass crossing of the Machsom.** There has been no such case yet. In our time a solo crossing is practically impossible; it can only happen as a group. Baal HaSulam writes about this in nearly all his articles: "The Giving of the Torah," "The Arvut," "Peace," "The Last Generation." "That's what I understood from my Rav," and from his own attainment.
- **The Arvut.** Each one in the group guarantees the others' spiritual ascent: to the extent he invests in the group, the group holds him and won't let him fall. That's why the Torah was given to a nation, not to Abraham. The Kabbalists before us were individuals with a mission, but correction of the world happens only through a group. The fear of going home and "falling out of the common Kli" means you don't yet feel that "we're going to hold you."

## Part 6 — Seeing the Friends Correctly

- **The group is not the people.** "The group is that spiritual idea that guides all of us." Don't look at faces, bodies, or animal character, because those have nothing to do with the soul. "You will be great Kabbalists and your characters will remain the same," with all the irritability, impatience, and laziness.
- See only how each one yearns for the Creator; **that is the soul**. Merge with that idea, and gradually you'll stop being put off by one's face or another's belly.
- **Irritation at long toasts.** Neither you nor your friends are corrected yet. The golden rule for every irritation and bad relationship: **love covers all transgressions**.
- **Above, below, or equal.** If I'm above my friend, I'm his teacher; below, his student; equal, we're friends. If he falls, I pull him up; if I fall, he pulls me up.
- **A strong group** is one with strong connections to other groups, not a group strong by itself. Don't brag about being strong "in Canada or in Volgograd." Without seeking connection with everyone, "you're just simply egoists." A weak but loyal group must be strengthened, never abandoned.
- **Arguing over articles.** Don't try to prove who's right. Read together, agree in advance that you're yearning toward the Creator through the text, and draw the **Surrounding Light**. Discussing texts is fine as long as it doesn't feed the ego.
- **Self-criticism as Klipa.** There is no commandment to immerse in your ego or eat yourself up. Strive upward the moment a little ego appears: "where you're yearning toward is where you are." Suffering you stay in and enjoy is not real suffering. "It's truly a Klipa," which also feeds pride: "oh, how I suffer."
- **"Everything comes from the Creator, so why argue?"** Then stay home. A person comes to the group to realize his free will, which exists only in the relationship through the group to the Creator.

## Part 7 — Practical Questions

#### Tithe (Maaser)

- Every person, even a beggar, must give a tenth of net income to the dissemination of Kabbalah. Less is "your problem"; more is allowed. It must be given per person, not taken from a weak group's general funds.
- **Why a tenth?** It relates to Malchut, which only the Creator corrects. We correct the nine Sefirot, so we keep ninety percent. Giving the tenth as root and branch involves even a person who doesn't work spiritually; under-giving it is "robbing himself spiritually."
- It's only on new income: once paid, savings aren't tithed again. Kabbalah does not allow earning interest that way.
- **Maaser vs. Mikveh.** Mikveh is "purely ritualistic"; Maaser is spiritual, because taking it out of your pocket goes against your ego, while bathing can be for the ego's sake.

#### Rituals and the screen

- Everything below the Machsom, without a screen, has no effect on the upper world. "With the screen it's a spiritual action. Without a screen it's an animal action." The same applies to Mikveh and the Sabbath.
- The root of the Mikveh: the Kli (Malchut) immersing in Bina, purified by the light of Hassadim. If it truly inspires you to yearn for the Creator, go; there are "no hidden secrets, no higher powers" in it.

#### Other answers

- **Going to check other teachers.** When he asked Rabash the same, Rabash said: go, check, ask; maybe it'll be more comfortable there. A person must be where his heart says is right.
- **Where are Rabash's other students?** Split into several separate groups studying from once a week to only on Rosh Chodesh, still opposed to dissemination and accepting outsiders only on strict conditions.
- **Getting out of passive states.** Only through the group. Rabash writes: start dancing, awaken the friends, work in the kitchen or in dissemination. The best way out is dissemination; teaching others evokes an additional Kli that immediately receives light.
- **Sleeping at the lesson.** He never gets angry: if that's the most he can give now, he crawled there at three in the morning hoping the group will revive him. Only if it lasts six months or a year is it a problem.
- **"I'm powerless."** There's no such thing. There is Aviut from below, light from above, and "we are a point that senses the interaction between them."
- **Shame for one's ego** arises only relative to the light, as we reveal ourselves before the Creator.
- **Free will as we become like the Creator.** Free will grows: each time more ego accumulates, I again decide above it to merge with Him. Like the slave in the king's parable, everything is placed in our hands, and the decision must be absolutely free.
- **Gmar Tikkun and the stony heart.** A person's own Gmar Tikkun corrects his **Lev HaEven**, but not the inclusion of everyone else's, which is why the world is not yet corrected.
- **Reading the Torah itself in simple language.** "I'm not ready yet." The prerequisites don't exist; what's missing is the students' sharper demand (*Hisaron*) of him.
- **Ten-year veterans vs. newcomers.** Don't calculate. "We need you and you need us." The lower rung supplies the higher with its deficiency; the hierarchy is only experience.
- **Missing the Congress.** Pictures and videos won't revive those states, and don't try. "A person cannot return to the past." The best states are still ahead.
- **Recommended reading.** King David's Psalms, which hold all 150 aspects of Malchut and every state of every soul, and any of Baal HaSulam's articles and letters. Summarize articles yourself until the whole method becomes clear from within: why Adam broke, why we glue the pieces, why in a group.

## Part 8 — Purim at the Congress

- **Purim** is the state relative to Gmar Tikkun: all of Malchut corrected, the nine Sefirot filled, all of NRNHY filled with the upper light. Creator and creation give to each other and are indistinguishable.
- In our world, Ohr Hassadim is expressed as food and Ohr Hochma as strong drink. Hence the commandment to eat and drink "without restraint," until you "can't even remember where you are, where your friend is, where is the Creator."
- When a person discovers the highest source of pleasure, every other need disappears. The point is to dance and sing "to the point where we will forget our own I," and leap out of it.

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **There is no third option.** Either you are in the group or you are spiritually dead; only there can relationships be checked and corrected.
2. **Spiritual growth is independence from the Creator.** He swings us up and down to show the range in which we must learn to hold ourselves.
3. **The group is your reserve.** Invest in it when you're up, and draw from it when you fall; the Creator gives the left line, the group becomes the right.
4. **Don't wait for the fall to deepen.** At the slightest disconnection, cry out and rally the friends; better yet, search for ascent before you fall.
5. **There is no individual revelation.** The Creator is revealed only in the common Kli glued together with friends, never in the "I."
6. **See yearnings, not faces.** The group is a spiritual idea; the soul is each friend's aspiration toward the Creator, and love covers all irritation.
7. **A strong group connects to other groups.** Strength by itself is just collective egoism.

## Key Terms & Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Spiritual Independence** | The measure of a person's ability to hold or raise his state on his own, despite the Creator's swings; the measure of spiritual growth. |
| **Machsom** | The barrier to the spiritual; independence begins with the first screen built after crossing it, and in our time it can only be crossed as a group. |
| **Common Kli** | The vessel the friends glue together from the shattered soul; the only place the Creator is revealed. |
| **Arvut** | Mutual guarantee: each member guarantees the others' ascent, and the group holds whoever invests in it. |
| **Aviut / Zakut** | The thickness of the ego added in each new state, and the refinement (screen) a person must add to it. |
| **Reshimot** | Records awakened one after another; processing them quickly brings rapid changes of state, and silence means stalling. |
| **Maaser** | The tithe of net income to dissemination; corresponds to Malchut, which only the Creator corrects. |
| **Klipa** | The shell that lets a person enjoy his own suffering and self-criticism, feeding pride. |
| **Purim** | The state of complete correction, when all desires are filled with light and the "I" is forgotten. |
