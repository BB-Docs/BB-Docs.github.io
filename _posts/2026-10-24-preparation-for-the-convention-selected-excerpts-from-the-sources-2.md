### Opening

With the Congress five days away, the lesson asks why we need to gather at all. If the Creator can be revealed anywhere, what does the Congress add? Rav Laitman's answer is that the upper light is revealed only in the connection between us. The Congress is a framework that presses us together, so that we care for one another and attach ourselves to the same system. From there he explains why every day must feel new, what it means to build the **Shechina** out of the broken pieces each of us carries, and how a Ten bestows upon the Creator: not by adding up ten deficiencies, but by each friend caring that the others turn to the Creator.

## Part 1 — Why the Congress

- "Only through connection between us do we reach a state where the upper light can be revealed." This is where the revelation of the Creator and of the upper system takes place.
- It is not that we can't reach this outside the Congress. But the Congress means that "we connect together. We're more pressed together, we care for each other, we make sure that we're all connected to the same system."
- The purpose of our corrections is that the Creator "will be revealed in us and will rejoice from us."

## Part 2 — Every Day Is New

- Don't be disappointed if what we reached disappears again. As it is written, they shall be "as new in your eyes."
- Every day a person has to start anew, and it seems to him that everything he did yesterday is gone. That's because it really is new: new discernments, new **Reshimot** from the shattering. The society also seems new to him.
- "Get up in the morning with a new song in your heart."
- So each morning we need to renew the questions: What is a Congress? What connection should we have every day, in the lessons and outside them? Does our gathering influence the correction of the general vessel, "our general soul that for the time being is broken"?

## Part 3 — Equivalence of Form and the Records of the Path

#### Why bestow upon the Creator

- Commenting on the first excerpt ("to establish a society where each of us follows the spirit of bestowing upon the Creator"): we need to reach **equivalence of form** with the Creator.
- First, because that is the purpose of creation, to reach **adhesion** (Dvekut) with Him.
- The Creator wants to benefit His created beings. "The greatest and really the only benefit that exists in reality" is to bring them to His own degree, His state, "and His state is absolute bestowal."

#### Records of the complete connection

- The Creator created us and broke the connection between us. But in that breaking we have Reshimot (records) of the good, correct state, the complete connection.
- We already carry records of the whole path that each of us, and all of us together, must go through until the end of correction.
- Our work is to awaken these records and try to make them real. "The Creator does it, but according to our deficiency."
- The Creator performs the corrections. **We discover the deficiencies for those corrections from the lack of connection between us.**

## Part 4 — Building the Shechina from the Broken Pieces

- A person has to have a family, work, take care of his health, and all of that is true. "But what is all of this for?" So that he gradually understands that he must be truly connected to everyone in a single desire.
- That collective desire, which the friends put together between them, is called the **Shechina**: the vessel in which the upper force, the Creator, is revealed.
- Each of us holds one part of it. A person who gathers the broken pieces by connecting with the friends comes closer to a state where he receives help.
- Over time the friends feel that it isn't a physical connection but an **internal** one: in their desires, their passions, their intentions. The desires shift from egoistic ones to desires of the upper world, of bestowal, love and connection.

> To the extent that they want more internal connection, to that extent they feel that the Creator is with them.

- **Q: How does connection between us bring connection with the Creator?** If we really try to read what the Kabbalists wrote, see their texts as new every day, and want to make them real, "it happens. A few days, a few years, but eventually it happens."
- **Q: What can we add besides annulment?** "There is no more." Only annulment toward the friends, toward Rabash and Baal HaSulam, and toward the laws of the upper world, "of bestowal and love emerging out of oneself towards the others."
- **Q: What does it mean for the Shechina to dwell between us?** We are parts that came from the shattering of the Shechina, the field in which the Creator is revealed. *(The first recording ends here, and a few minutes are missing.)*

#### From the missing section

- One friend's summary at the end recalls an answer from the gap: asked how to rise to the next degree, Rav Laitman said much is written about it, but the main thing is to enter the **first degree**. Rising to a degree means the extent to which my will to receive is revealed, and the extent to which I am in the will to bestow above it.

## Part 5 — How a Ten Bestows upon the Creator

- **Q: If we bestow to the friends, what can we add to bestow to the Creator?** We gather all our acts of bestowal between us, "which is already called a Ten." If each one just wants to bestow to the others, "that is not a Ten yet. These are just individuals."
- I bestow to the Creator first by helping the friends I love turn to Him: I care that they will be free to turn to Him, concerned about the connection with Him, asking of Him, together as much as possible.
- "It's not that we are joining the ten deficiencies together." Each one cares that the others connect and come closer to the Creator. "This is an entirely different connection than in some ordinary group."
- Through this mutual concern, as Rabash writes in his social articles, we build our common desire, common deficiency and common hope, until it becomes one, "and at that moment when we connect as one, then the Creator comes and He's revealed between us."
- A person must be "truly sick" about this. Then the desires reach "a single heart," and the Creator says, "Where are you? I was waiting for you for such a long time."

#### One concern, one point

- **Q: How does each friend's concern become one thing?** When I care for all the friends "like a father towards his children," I come out of myself and find that I'm concerned with the essence of the Ten. "Everyone connects in the center of the Ten." In the transition to the next degree it will be felt as one.
- **Q: What does it mean to care that the friends turn to the Creator?** Like a kindergarten teacher caring for the little children: you only want the connection between them. "Concern in a man's heart" is what sustains it.

#### The first spiritual degree

- The condition for the first spiritual degree is mutual bestowal and mutual help, so that the force of bestowal, "or at least the deficiency for that," dwells among us.
- Then the upper light acts on us according to our deficiency and connects us. "This is called 'the Lord will finish for me.'" He establishes the vessel and then is revealed in it.
- Revelation, from the side of the vessel, means discovering a connection and mutual help that already have the quality of the Creator, "to the smallest extent, but still it's something."

## Part 6 — Why We Are Born Disconnected

- **Q: Are we born egoists, or do family and culture disconnect us?** Everything is arranged so that all the forces of attraction and rejection help us build the spiritual vessel.
- If we were in a Garden of Eden with nothing hindering us, "that would not be good." The Creator created only the will to receive. Everything that awakens in us is for our benefit, because without disturbances "we will be lacking the intensity of the desire, the intensity of the connection."
- **Q: How do we know when the Ten is ready for the Congress?** If you discover you're ready, the next moment you discover something is still missing, "until the end of correction." There is no "ten kilograms of common deficiency" to reach. In spirituality you always stand opposite **Ein Sof**. There is no limit to bestowal, so each one bestows as much as he can.
- **Q: Does our connection help all of humanity?** "Of course." We are in a single vessel with all the souls, so every action of ours influences all of humanity.
- **Q: Which connections matter, internal or external?** "All connections are connections, let's not start sorting them now." All of them are in order to discover a single desire, and in it the Creator, who is one.

## Part 7 — Concessions and the Spiritual Vessel

#### Excerpt 2: As their faces differ

- People's views differ as their faces do, yet those who unite in love of friends find "there isn't such a great distance between them," and each can make concessions for the others.
- Only on condition that each one diminishes and annuls himself before the others does he prepare himself for spirituality: willing to be "a carpet spread before them," letting them step on him so long as they rise.
- Bestowing to the friends above his force of reception is the work of **restriction, screen and reflected light** (Tzimtzum, Masach, Ohr Hozer). This is his spiritual effort, by which he builds the spiritual vessel.
- Each time greater disturbances come, to build a greater inclination above them, until he receives a certain "quota of disturbances," overcomes them, and prefers bestowal above reception. Then "the entrance to the upper world opens up for him."

#### Questions on the vessel

- **Q: What desire is in the center of the Ten?** All the desires: each one's egoistic desires, rejection and connection, calculations. We sort them by a "ruler of values," always preferring the force of bestowal to the force of reception.
- **Q: Is there a difference between voluntary annulment and trying to annul against my desire?** You can only try, and the Creator does it, "but nevertheless the work is your work, your effort." In spirituality we remain a point, with the will to receive on one side and the desire to bestow on the other, built through the right combination of the two.
- **Q: Does the spiritual vessel include the egoistic desire?** Yes, including the rejection between us. "I'm specifically standing upon the coarseness," and from it make the restriction and rise to the desire to bestow.
- **Q: How is the Creator revealed in the vessel? What is inner light?** "If you do it, you'll find out. I don't like to engage in fantasies." We speak only from the side of the vessel: what we must do to reach revelation.

## Part 8 — Annulling Self-Love in the Society

#### Excerpt 3

- Without annulling self-love, there is no adhesion (equivalence of form). Because this is against our nature, we need a society of people who unanimously agree on the goal, so that "all the individuals become one great force" and each one can fight against himself.
- Each person annuls himself before the others by seeing the friends' merits and not their faults. "One who thinks that he is a little higher than his friends can no longer unite with them."
- Seeing friends scattered across computer screens is "given to us" to make it easier: without corporeal closeness, we can aim more at the inner connection, "from heart to heart."

#### What connects us

- **Q: What exactly connects us heart to heart?** The **Reshimot** from the broken vessel called **Adam HaRishon**. Returned into a single system, these broken, connected desires are called the Shechina.
- The upper light is revealed according to the gap between the shattering and the correction. "The more resistance we have for connection, and as much as there is connection above that resistance... this tension produces the revelation of the Creator." It works by laws, like electricity.

#### Helping a friend

- **Q: How can the Ten help a friend who sees himself as higher?** We know these egoistic forms from ourselves. Look at how they hinder our connection, and help "in the form of an example without much talk," each showing how much he invests in the Ten.
- **Q: How do we reach critical mass when friends are at very different places?** "You can't choose anyone or anything... It's forbidden." The Creator brings a person and says, "Take it." As long as one doesn't interfere or corrupt, give everyone the chance to advance.
- Great souls often advance very slowly. "I saw such cases where a person took twenty and thirty years," because of the many heavy layers of desire he has to sort. "Beware of the children of the poor, because Torah will come out of them."
- You feel different from each other because you're not yet connected into one vessel. Once you connect, each one's nature stands out even more, as children differ more as they grow. "The main thing is not to enter sorting and correction in place of the Creator."
- **Q: What does "beware of the poor" mean?** It refers to great desires that feel they have nothing. Specifically through them the Torah comes out, the upper light and the revelation of the Creator. "He loves them, those poor people."
- **Q: How do we take back a friend who left and wants to return?** If he has inner swings but doesn't interfere, take him back. If he stirs up turmoil in the Ten, "we shouldn't forgive everything." Decide between you and tell him: he must connect to what the society is going through.

#### Annulment and equivalence

- **Q: Is true annulment a gift from the Creator?** "It's truly a spiritual action," and like all spiritual actions it needs help from above. If we think we can do it alone, it's still egoistic. We have to discover this and demand the forces from Him.
- **Q: Does the created being have more experience than the Creator?** No. The Creator manages both the will to bestow and the will to receive. He "moved aside" and gave us a restricted area where we exist through our ego, like parents watching children who think they're alone.
- Our work is to draw the Creator's force into our dark state by imagining that we already do His actions: helping a friend, "love your friend as yourself," connection in the Ten. This draws the **reforming light**, the surrounding light working on us from afar.
- **Q: What is the inner action of equivalence of form?** "Only connection. Only connection and love." There was a complete vessel, it shattered, and our task is to connect its parts. The best "spiritual glue": "Concessions. First of all, concession."

## Part 9 — Each One Becomes Like Adam HaRishon

#### Excerpt 4

- Through the adhesion of the friends, views and thoughts pass from one to another, so each one has the power of the entire society. "By connecting, each one is incorporated in the other."
- This is the reason for the shattering of **Adam HaRishon**: when we connect above our egos, each broken part acquires the shattering and the vessel together, and becomes like the entire corrected vessel, "like the general soul." Each one receives the ability to discover the Creator entirely.
- **Q: How do we see that everything is connected in the spiritual world?** We don't need to do anything but remove the ego that prevents us from seeing that we are already connected. "I don't think there ever was a broken state." The ego was created only so we understand and feel, "as light excels from darkness," the perfect state we're in.
- **Q: How important are visible, physical actions in the Ten?** As important as the friends can be impressed by them. "Physical actions, the visible actions, have a great deal of influence on us."

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **The light is revealed only in connection.** The Congress presses us together so we care for one another and attach to one system, and every day the connection must be renewed with new Reshimot.
2. **Each of us holds a piece of the Shechina.** The broken desires, reconnected above the shattering, form the Shechina in which the Creator is revealed.
3. **A Ten bestows to the Creator through care for the friends.** Not by adding up ten deficiencies, but by each one caring that the others turn to the Creator, until all desires meet in the center of the Ten as one heart.
4. **The first spiritual degree is mutual bestowal.** When the force of bestowal, or at least the deficiency for it, dwells among us, the upper light completes the vessel and the Creator is revealed in it.
5. **Disturbances are needed.** The will to receive, the rejection between us and our differences are the material of the spiritual vessel, built above them by restriction, screen and reflected light.
6. **Don't sort the friends.** No one can judge another soul's path; help by example, not talk, and leave correction to the Creator.
7. **Only connection and love, held together by concessions.** True annulment is a spiritual action we must ask the Creator for, and through it each one becomes like Adam HaRishon.

## Key Terms & Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Congress (Convention)** | A gathering of the world Kabbalah group, a framework that presses the friends together so that the upper light can be revealed in their connection. |
| **Equivalence of Form** | Becoming similar to the Creator in bestowal; in practice, "only connection and love." |
| **Reshimot (Records)** | Records of the complete connection from the broken vessel of Adam HaRishon. They connect us and come anew every day. |
| **Shechina** | The broken desires reconnected into one, in which the Creator is revealed. We are its broken parts. |
| **Ten** | Not individuals bestowing to each other, but friends each caring that the others turn to the Creator, connecting in the center of the Ten. |
| **Single heart** | The state where all the friends' deficiencies, directed to the Creator, become one; then He is revealed. |
| **First spiritual degree** | Mutual bestowal and help among the friends, where the will to bestow stands above the revealed will to receive. |
| **Tzimtzum, Masach, Ohr Hozer** | Restriction, screen and reflected light: bestowing to the friends above one's force of reception, which builds the spiritual vessel. |
| **Bitul (Annulment)** | Annulling oneself before the friends by seeing their merits; a spiritual action that needs help from above. |
| **Adam HaRishon** | The general soul that shattered; through connection each part becomes like the whole. |
| **Reforming light** | The surrounding light drawn by acting as if we already do the Creator's actions. |
| **Concession** | The best "spiritual glue" for connecting the broken parts. |
