### Opening

If the goal is to reveal that there is "none else besides Him," why must the path pass through the revelation of evil, and why is that evil shown to some people and hidden from others? Rav Laitman frames the whole article around one goal: revealing the **singular authority**, the Creator alone ruling in our vessel. From there he explains the pyramid of souls, why the collective and the individual are taught in opposite ways, why critique may only be given to someone who can still correct, and why, from the twentieth century on, no one can reach spirituality without pulling the whole world along.

## Part 1 — The Goal: Revealing the Singular Authority

- Rav Laitman praises how Rabash builds "a bridge" between the difficult language of the Kabbalists and the way the Torah, Mishnah and **Gemara** explain the work of the Creator, of the individual and of the public.
- The goal of our work is **the revelation of the Creator inside our vessel**, called the revelation of the Singular One: "He and His name are one," "there is none else besides Him," the **singular authority** (Reshut HaYachid).
- We begin from the opposite state, because the vessel for this revelation is something we must build ourselves, "by our choice, by our labor," resembling the Creator.

#### How the question awakens

- At first a person doesn't ask about it at all, like the still, vegetative and animate levels.
- Then he asks, "What is the point in life?" This is a question about the **source**: who gives me life, from whom do I receive? It is like phase one in the Four Phases, where the light reveals that it comes from the root, "not just pleasure, but the giver of the pleasure."
- The question is "no longer about the taste... it's about the source from which come either suffering or pleasure."
- Life events keep awakening the question, but in this world he doesn't reveal the singular force.

#### Three authorities along the path

| Stage | What a person feels |
|---|---|
| **His own authority** | Through the method of Kabbalah he draws **surrounding light** and feels a force that awakens him, and he demands from it: "Why don't I have what I deserve?" A little of the degree of **Pharaoh**: "Who is the Lord that I shall listen to His voice?" |
| **Partnered authority** | "Me and Him." Then he wants to be *like* Him. |
| **The Creator's authority** | Only the Creator rules in his vessels: "That will be the end of my correction." |

## Part 2 — The Pyramid of Souls

- Baal HaSulam explains in *The Last Generation* that although the collective soul, **Adam HaRishon**, is one uniform system in which every element matters, once divided "there are souls that are more important and less important in terms of the order of the correction."
- Like a body, it can't be whole without the last soul, but its structure is a **pyramid**: the public in great quantity at the bottom, the individual at the top.
- Hence "the view of Torah is opposite to the view of landlords" (**Baalei Batim**). This holds only during correction, not at the end of correction.
- The singular authority must be attained first in the individual and then in the public, and the guidance for each is different. Yet "it is forbidden to leave the public." We must disseminate as broadly as possible.
- Example: prayer for the collective is simply a prayer "to have it good," as **Rambam** says of teaching "children, women and little ones," revealing gradually what spirituality is. Each of us, too, has a collective and an individual within, and the soul ascends from collective to individual.
- Even in the last generation the pyramid remains: some are included simply by agreeing to belong, while others work individually and independently, longing for the **inner light**.

## Part 3 — Two Kinds of Guidance

- "We always need to tell a person what he wants to hear."
- **The individual**, who wants spirituality at any price, is told more and more correctly what spirituality is and helped to critique his state. "He's happy from the revelation of evil... because it helps him" go forward.
- **The collective** is shown nothing bad in itself, "always joy without any criticism," in wholeness. They are not capable of corrections and have no deficiency for it, and "it's prohibited from the outside to show or reveal a deficiency to someone who's not capable of understanding it" and of correcting it.

#### Before and after the purchase

- Rabash's example: before someone buys, you can tell him whether it's worthwhile. After he has bought, "tell him that it's good."
- Critique must be brought "in a very cautious manner and only to people that are capable of correcting." Otherwise, "don't put obstacles in his life."
- As in the Scroll of Esther, "according to the desire of each person": whether he wants truth or only to feel good.

#### What we bring the public

- To the collective: general knowledge, without individual deficiencies, but certainly the deficiencies of the nation, why we all suffer, "the sorrow of many is half a comfort."
- Individual critique belongs only within a group, and even there not as critique: "by us building such an environment where a person will feel... what he's certainly missing in his personal correction."

## Part 4 — Joining the Singular Authority Above Reason

- The whole path leads to revealing the singular authority, and then to how much I'm incapable of being in it, and then to incorporating in it "as an active partner," degree by degree. This gradual work is the ladder of degrees.
- It begins with "that first drop of unification called there's none else besides Him."
- A person uses his two authorities actively: the desire to receive on the left and the force of correction on the right, to join **Zeir Anpin and Malchut of Atzilut** "on one equal level in a never-ending coupling."

> It's not that we annul ourselves there. It's not that we disappear there, are swallowed there, but rather we join to it with our own forces, with our own abilities and our awareness.

- Each discovery of the greatness of the Creator and the goal brings willingness for incorporation, and with it joy and upliftment.
- When difficulty comes, it means new desires that weren't yet included in the unification have been added. We work immediately to reach the same state with them too, "not to erase them, but rather understand that it's with them together." That is the next degree.
- "This work is considered the work above reason, where these disturbances are our current reason and faith is the incorporation in the quality of in order to bestow despite every disturbance."

## Part 5 — Q&A: The Collective and the Individual Among Us

- **Q: Our lessons are for everyone, so where is the difference expressed?** The individual is the inner group, where I invest in the friends and they in me, "on one commando team," drawn to the goal. The collective is where we spread knowledge about the purpose of creation and the causes of our troubles.
- To the public: "the knowledge of life... There's no secrets here. There's no personal criticisms." The world is conducted by one force, one law, toward one goal. If it is within our awareness we feel life as good; if not, its bestowal feels bad.
- Present it "in a scientific way," like a third party: this is reality, this force operates on us, this is what it demands. Obey and we feel good; don't prepare and we feel bad.
- The general force demands according to development. The more developed a nation, the more it suffers, not necessarily in hunger but "in the clarity of the question, what is the meaning of our lives?"

#### The pyramid of suffering and of development

- Some suffer terribly yet aren't willing to hear; others have everything arranged, and that small lack of meaning already makes them say "my death is better than my life."
- Explanation must be gradual. "If you start explaining in first grade what you explained in sixth or seventh grade, they'll think that you're crazy." "Don't put an obstacle before a blind man is truly the very correct approach to education."
- Books are open to everyone, "but each understands as much as he's capable of seeing."
- Some people at forty or fifty are emotionally like children, though smart; others are mature but not wise. Some must be reached through the heart, some through the mind, and it must be "the collaboration of mind and heart."
- Periods also differ: in the time of the **Ari** it was a period of mind; in the time of the **Baal Shem Tov**, of the heart. "With us, I would say both."

## Part 6 — Why Care About the Whole World?

- If I am incorporated in everyone and dependent on everyone, and I received knowledge of the correct reality, I am obligated to pass it on, according to the structure of the general vessel and our **Arvut** (mutual guarantee).
- "All the laws are absolute, meaning they don't change through my desire to participate with them or not. But rather I will feel different with them if I participate or not."

#### The train

- I'm on a train going from point A to point B, but I want to stay at A. The farther it travels, the more I feel the pull back, and "this distancing is felt in me as suffering."
- Eventually the suffering becomes so great that escaping it becomes fuel, and "I'll come at three AM and participate in all the actions just to exit the suffering." That is the path of suffering.
- Instead, the Kabbalists say: accept the process upon yourself and you'll discover good and endless pleasures in it. For that there are means: the group, the books, the guide.
- "The audience's reason and opinion convinces me as well. I need to convince the audience, otherwise I won't be convinced." And "I can't reach the good if the whole world is in bad." So dissemination must be very broad.
- Some find this easy and have talents for it; others are not built for it and must find their own way.

## Part 7 — Q&A: Pyramid, Greatness, and Dissemination

- **Q: Why didn't a pyramid structure work in our group?** "We can't sort it out. Because we don't really know according to what." Every spiritual structure is built by degrees of importance, and "the difference is what builds the wholeness," but in the group "we relate to each other equally." Perhaps one day, like Rabbi Shimon's society, where each of the ten friends was one of the ten **Sefirot**.
- **Q: If we weren't ready, you wouldn't tell us to publicize the greatness of the Creator; what's missing?** We are in "the generation of the Messiah, the last generation," which must reveal the general force to everybody. The suffering in the world "testifies that the world must be on a different degree."

> Let's say I'm on degree number five and have to be on degree number ten. The gap between these five degrees that I'm behind, that's what presses on me as suffering.

- The surrounding light that should already be entering as inner light comes as suffering. "If you close the gaps... you will always be in happiness... Every time that the evil is revealed in you, you see it as something beneficial."
- The method is simple: "It's not correct your soul, something abstract. It's correct your life." The world is ready from the side of suffering but not from recognition, and that is what we must help with.
- **Q: What about our inner dissemination?** As a group, "pretty successful," but we must work more on unity to bring more **Reforming Light**. Outside, in the nation, "we're really negligent," and must do beyond our possibilities.
- The world's level holds the group "on a lower degree than what we could have been." It's all one system.

#### A truth from an unexpected source

- Rav Laitman cites a statement then in the news from Malaysia that the Jews control the world in their thoughts: "I agree with him a hundred percent... We in our thoughts are controlling the whole world." He sees it as the Creator revealing an important truth through a messenger. "My enemy is inside, in me."
- The sources say the same: calamity comes to the world only because of Israel. "Putting your head in the sand" doesn't help.

## Part 8 — After the Congress: One World

- **Q: After the Congress, what should we concentrate on?** Organize our work together with the world group so that we take care of the whole world, "and in that way we'll unify between us."
- Kabbalists of the past worked alone because they had a different role. From the middle of the twentieth century, "nobody could reach spirituality and nobody will be able unless performing the general role to pull the whole world behind them."
- The descent of the sparks has ended. As Baal HaSulam wrote, the light of the **Zohar** has been revealed from above to the lowest degree; "now we have to start rising. We are the first to do it." This is the general correction of the whole soul.
- To relate to the Creator means relating to His vessel, "this entire pyramid, the whole world, all the souls together." That is where you meet Him. "The world has become one."

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **The goal is the singular authority.** From my own authority, to partnership ("me and Him"), to the Creator alone ruling in my vessels: that is the end of correction.
2. **Souls form a pyramid.** The collective and the individual are guided in opposite ways during correction, but no one may abandon the public.
3. **Critique only where correction is possible.** Before the purchase, tell the truth; after it, praise. The collective receives wholeness and general knowledge; the individual welcomes the revelation of evil.
4. **Join, don't disappear.** We incorporate in the Creator's authority actively, with our own forces and awareness; each new disturbance is new desire to include, above reason.
5. **Suffering is the gap between degrees.** Closing the gap voluntarily turns every revelation of evil into joy instead of blows.
6. **The whole world is the vessel.** Since the twentieth century, personal spiritual advancement depends on pulling the whole world along; broad dissemination is not optional.

## Key Terms & Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Singular authority (Reshut HaYachid)** | The revelation that only the Creator rules in our vessel: "He and His name are one." |
| **Partnered authority** | The middle stage, "me and Him," before wanting to be wholly like Him. |
| **Pyramid of souls** | The structure of the divided soul of Adam HaRishon: the many at the base, the individual at the apex. |
| **View of Torah / View of landlords** | The individual's aim to cancel his own authority versus the public's work for its own reward. |
| **Collective guidance** | Wholeness and general knowledge, without personal critique. |
| **Individual guidance** | Teaching to critique one's state, so the revelation of evil becomes a help. |
| **Recognition of evil** | Seeing one's distance from the Creator, given only to those able to correct it. |
| **Above reason** | Incorporating in bestowal despite disturbances, which are our "current reason." |
| **Surrounding light / Inner light** | The light that awakens from outside, and that comes as suffering when it should already be inside. |
| **Path of suffering** | Being pushed by growing pain, like a passenger resisting a moving train. |
| **Arvut** | Mutual guarantee: the obligation to pass knowledge on because all are one system. |
| **Last generation** | The generation that must reveal the general force to the whole world. |
