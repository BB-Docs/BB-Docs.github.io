### Opening

If the world we see is only an inner picture, and that picture keeps changing, is the change happening inside me or outside me? Rav Laitman says this question interests only scientists. The real question is: **can I change anything at all?** The lesson walks from the four states of the Creator's concealment and revelation to a sharp conclusion. A person cannot correct himself. Only the revealed Creator draws him to become similar. The single place where a person has real free will is in **choosing and building his environment**.

## Part 1 — The World as an Inner Picture

- Everything a person senses happens inside him. It only *appears* to be outside, like a projection on an internal screen. With different senses we would see different colors and tastes, "pass through walls," and feel air as solid.
- The inanimate, vegetative, and animate levels that seem to surround me, and the states they seem to go through, all change **because I change**, according to rules I don't control. My mood shifts, and it feels as if the world moved.
- Whether it's "really" inside or outside only matters to scientists. "What will change ultimately if I know it or don't? The main thing is, can I actually change it?" That is exactly what Baal HaSulam's article on free will takes up.

#### Three articles for the masses

Rav Laitman describes Baal HaSulam's newspaper *HaUma* ("The Nation"), which Baal HaSulam published because newsprint was the cheapest way to reach the world when "he had no money." In it he printed **"The Giving of the Torah," "The Arvut (Mutual Guarantee)," and "Peace."**

- These articles set out a person's right relationship with the world. They are "the shortest path for the masses, not for individuals."
- Baal HaSulam placed "such strong forces" in them that a person who "tries to feel them on his flesh" receives **Surrounding Light** in small, gradual portions that "take him by the hand and lead him through the Machsom."
- His academic, sage-like language is hard, and that is on purpose. Struggling with it is like **kneading dough**: a person reshapes himself from within until the Ohr Makif can become Ohr Pnimi.
- For now they'll study Rav Laitman's short summaries. Later, everyone should go back to the full original texts.

## Part 2 — Kabbalah Is a Means (a Segula)

- The goal isn't the science. It's entering the spiritual world by the shortest route. The wisdom of Kabbalah is "a helpful, complementary tool," a **Segula**, a special power in these books that draws the Surrounding Light that corrects.
- Throughout history there were people who never studied the science. They worked directly on correcting their ego by helping others, and came to sense the upper world.
- After the Machsom, Kabbalah stops being a science. A person sees behind everyone **one force** acting in perfect harmony, "like a particle in a common field," with the field telling each particle what to do.

## Part 3 — The Four States of Concealment and Revelation

Everyone who studies Kabbalah seriously goes through four states:

| State | What the person feels |
|---|---|
| **Double concealment** ("concealment within concealment") | He doesn't even sense that the Creator is hidden. Once he felt something behind events; now he feels nothing. |
| **Single concealment** | He feels the Creator is hidden but still arranging things behind events, so some connection remains. |
| **Achoraim (the Creator's back)** | One force fills the world. It sends blows and suffering, but with a purpose: to raise him to a perfect, good state. |
| **Panim (the Creator's face)** | He feels the force that governs everything as absolute goodness, "the good who does good," love for all creation without limit. |

Rav Laitman says the text on double and single concealment comes from Baal HaSulam's personal notes, "a manuscript torn from two pages," not a finished article.

#### The Creator never turns His back

> In reality the Creator doesn't turn His face or His back. His attitude is absolutely constant.

What changes are **my 620 qualities**. Where they match the Creator, I am "face to face" and see Him as He is. Where they are opposite, working only for myself, I feel concealment or nothing at all. When all are corrected, that is **adhesion, Gmar Tikkun, the world of Ein Sof**, with no limit.

## Part 4 — 620 Qualities Like an Equalizer

- Picture a sound equalizer. Each of the 620 qualities stands at its own level of correction. Together they "paint the image of the world."
- The corrected part is the **measure of the Creator's revelation**. The uncorrected part is the measure of His **concealment**. The Hebrew word for world, *Olam*, comes from *He'elem*, concealment. Our world is total concealment. The spiritual worlds (Assiya, Yetzira, Beria, Atzilut) are levels of revelation.
- Desires can't all be corrected in one go because they differ in quality and **Aviut** (coarseness). Nor do they rise evenly: going up a degree might improve one desire by 1% and another by 8%. The Creator is felt more strongly, and also differently in character.

## Part 5 — Q&A: 620 Desires and "Love Your Neighbor as Yourself"

**Question:** What is the connection between our 620 egoistic desires and the rule of love your neighbor as yourself?

- Love your neighbor as yourself is **the only condition** for becoming similar to the Creator, who reveals Himself as the One who loves. Crossing the Machsom is a change in *our* qualities. "Nothing else changes."
- We never grasp the Light itself, only what the **Kli** feels. The Kli is our sense organ. It is everything apart from the Creator, who fills it and gives it life.
- To make similarity possible, the Creator created a Kli, **broke it**, and put into each part His quality of **Bina** together with the Kli's own quality, **Malchut**. A person can then stand, as it were, beside the Creator and relate to all of creation the way He does. Only humans have this ability.
- **Left line and right line:** my 620 egoistic qualities show up on the "left"; the Creator is on the "right." Comparing the two, I correct my attitude toward creation step by step, and that is my closeness to Him.
- **The soul grows like an embryo:** it starts from a single egoistic point and develops Rosh, Toch, Sof and all 620 qualities to their full size.
- **Why Judaism forbade images:** so as not to imagine anything existing outside a person's own senses. Whatever language we read (Torah, Talmud, Kabbalah), we translate it into our **inner, sensory language**. Past the Machsom there is no external language, only the matching of corrected desires.

## Part 6 — Q&A: Can We Overcome Our Nature?

A participant asks how to overcome our nature. Rav Laitman notes that people hide much of themselves from themselves, because full self-knowledge would bring unbearable suffering.

He quotes an old prayer: *give me strength to change what I can, courage to accept what I can't, and wisdom to know the difference*. That is fine for ordinary life, but "we're talking about correcting ourselves completely." So how?

> Until a person begins to feel the Creator, there is no hope, no possibility of anything changing within him. How can it change if it is 100% a consequence of the breaking of the Kli?

- Even Ohr Makif doesn't yet show us how completely opposite to the Creator we are. If we had even one corrected quality out of 620, we would sense Him clearly in it. "It's not something achieved in meditation."
- **Conclusion:** "Under no circumstances should a person think that he should correct himself." It is impossible, like pulling yourself out of a swamp by your own hair. Only the Creator's revelation draws us to Him, and to that extent we correct.
- So strive for correction, but "not for what will be good for me." Strive because you want to be like Him in how He relates to creation. In our state, that means **how we relate to the group**.

#### The small society ("The Last Generation")

- Baal HaSulam writes of first building **a small society**, like a small state. It works fully on the principle of bestowal, and differs from communism and the kibbutzim in one thing: **its goal is adhesion with the Creator**. It will draw "the cream," the most refined people from around the world.
- Those who strive for the Creator are called **Israel** (*Yashar-El*, "straight to the Creator"). This is not tied to any nation or state.
- When a person feels how the Creator relates to all creation, he relates the same way to everything, "cockroaches and rats" included.

#### Playing like children

We can't yet relate to all humanity that way, so we are given a group. There, even knowing we're egoists, we **imitate**, like a child copying an adult or "like a monkey." We pretend to be above our ego. This is **faith above reason**. Mechanical imitation from this side of the Machsom draws Ohr Makif, which cleanses us and pulls us across. "Never think that we can correct ourselves by ourselves."

## Part 7 — "The Freedom": Choosing the Environment

Rav Laitman reads the key line from Baal HaSulam:

> A person cannot change his essence, nor the laws by which his inner properties change under external influences, but he can completely change the environment on which he is completely dependent. This is where free will lies.

- Our Reshimot, the "spiritual gene," unfold from within, and we cannot change them. What we *can* do is choose what influences them.
- We all come from one common Kli. So no matter how big an egoist a person is, he stays dependent on **public opinion**. Society can flip all his values and "convince him it's better to die or better to live."
- **Use this.** Build an artificial environment against the natural egoistic one, a group governed "by the law of the higher world," that keeps reminding each member of spiritual values. Then a person comes to want the Creator even before he reaches the Machsom.
- **"Change the group so it changes you."** I can't change myself, but I'm glad to change others. So shape the group, and it will shape me back. That is why Baal HaSulam speaks of always seeking a new environment: not a new group every day, but constantly renewing the one you're in.
- A group that doesn't give its members an example of this "is not a group."

## Part 8 — Q&A: Can One Quality Be Fully Corrected?

**Question** (from a scientist friend, a professor in Holland): If I've corrected some of the 620 qualities, must I wait for the rest?

- No single quality can be fully corrected while the others aren't. All of them approach 100% together, at different rates. Their "average" is a person's actual degree, from 1 to 125.
- The distance between a person and the Creator is divided into **five worlds** (Assiya, Yetzira, Beria, Atzilut, Adam Kadmon), then Ein Sof. These measure how close he is, which means how much he relates to all humanity, "even the little beetles," the way the Creator does.
- Today we can do this only **artificially**, in a group. The small society then grows, as in "The Last Generation," until it takes in all of humanity.

#### What the group really is

> The group is something spiritual that exists between us, a shared plan or idea. It's what will be revealed within us as the Creator if we treat each other this way.

A person discovers that he doesn't need to copy a Creator standing "next to him." He finds the Creator *within the connections between the friends*. Taking part in them, he loses his "I." **That is crossing the Machsom**: "as if you jump there, and your ego stays outside."

## Part 9 — Outer Actions and the Sukkah

- All the work is inner. Outer actions exist only because we need a **framework** to be together, unite more, and express the inner work outward. Without shared tasks, "we'll just stand there like a conveyor belt peeling potatoes, and for what?"
- **The sukkah:** we sit under its covering (a symbol of the Masach) while the upper light shines in "through the cracks," as Surrounding Light. "In principle we could do without the sukkah," but we use shared symbols to join our efforts, so that the yearning for the Creator is revealed between us.
- **Without connection, outer forms empty out.** When Rabbi Akiva's 24,000 students "began to hate each other," the Temple fell. Otherwise "what would remain of these bricks? Some palace to take tourists to for money."
- So take on as many shared duties as possible (kitchen, broadcasting, printing, translating), "only to unite and be connected with the members of the group."

## Part 10 — The Law Is the Law: The Clock Is Ticking

> The world is not judged by intentions, but by the inner degrees of our correction, our qualities.

- A person is judged by who he really is, "by his present state," not by what he'd like to be.
- The upper light comes down constantly from the state of **Gmar Tikkun** and reveals new **Reshimot** in us, like a spiral unwinding. Everything we feel as changes in the world is just these Reshimot opening.
- Every Reshimo that meets the light has to be realized right away by building a **Masach** for it. If not, it slips by, the next one can't be handled either (there's no earlier screen), and the whole chain slows.
- **That delay is the only cause of suffering**, both personal and global. "No one is asking about your intentions. The clock is ticking."

## Part 11 — Q&A: Does a Person Stop Feeling Himself?

Rav Laitman says he doesn't want to say much about what lies past the Machsom, but explains:

- The egoistic "I" that separates me from others is gradually lost, all the way to Gmar Tikkun, and **another "I" appears**. The first steps past the Machsom strongly suppress it. That is why this stage is called **Ibur** (embryo): the person is included in the upper light, fully nourished at the Upper One's expense.
- That doesn't erase the person. A **spiritual body** starts to form from a point, and he feels himself "in a sea of higher light, of thought, intention, love, which is called the Creator." In it he sees everyone else as already corrected, each unknowingly carrying out the Creator's plan.
- **Stages as studied in TES:** Part 10 covers Ibur, Part 11 Yenika, Part 12 Gadlut, Part 13 how the Partzuf receives from Arich Anpin, and Parts 14–16 the ascent to Gmar Tikkun. Like a baby born with legs it can't use yet, a person can use a quality only once he has the **Masach** (the "mind") to govern it.
- **Uneven development:** it depends on each Sefira's nature, not only its Aviut. **Bina**, closest to the light, grows fastest. **Hod**, a small-scale Malchut within Zeir Anpin, grows slowest. In the breaking, Bina fell but stayed higher than the rest, while Malchut, with the most Aviut, fell lowest.
- The chain of **Gilgulim** is shaped by this spiritual information too. "We're not historians or archaeologists. We need to only look forward."

## Part 12 — Q&A: How to Raise My Attitude Toward the Group

**Question:** How can a person, in a given state, strengthen his attitude toward the group?

- Only **necessity** brought us to Kabbalah, and only necessity changes us. Don't ask how the change will happen. Ask how to build an urgent **need** for it. "Everything depends on the preparation of the Kli."
- That need comes from the environment. If the group won't let a person accept a beastly life, if it makes "the slightest delay" feel intolerable, then real change happens. It is a mutual effort between the individual and the group.
- A person can only push artificially. But if the group, under his artificial pressure, raises *him*, the change is genuine, "like values we receive from outside, which become our own opinions."

> There is no other way to change ourselves except through the society. Therefore, this is our only free will.

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **Only I change; the Creator's attitude is constant.** Double concealment, single concealment, Achoraim, and Panim describe how my 620 qualities perceive a Creator who doesn't change.
2. **Don't try to correct yourself.** It's impossible. Only the Creator's revelation draws a person to become like Him, so aim for that revelation, not for self-improvement.
3. **Free will is in the environment.** A person can't change his Reshimot or his nature, but he can choose and shape the society that shapes him. "Change the group so it changes you."
4. **Imitate like children.** Acting above reason from this side of the Machsom draws the Surrounding Light that pulls a person across it.
5. **The group is a spiritual thing between us.** The Creator is found in the connections between friends, and joining in them, leaving the ego outside, is the crossing of the Machsom.
6. **Outer forms serve connection.** The sukkah, the kitchen, and shared duties have value only as frameworks for joined effort. Without connection they empty out, as with the Temple.
7. **The clock is ticking.** Every Reshimo has to be met with a Masach at once. Delay is the only cause of suffering, and we are judged by our real state, not our intentions.
