### Opening

The plan of creation is fixed: its beginning, its end and its stages were set as reality descended from Ein Sof to this world. What's left to us is the **time**, meaning how fast we pass through those stages. Rav Laitman explains how to hasten development: by how often a person returns to the thought of the purpose, by using every ascent for correction rather than enjoyment, and above all by working in a group, where the friends' ascents and descents are one's own and the vessel is measured by its weakest member. Since it is just before Passover, he also explains what it means to leave Egypt.

## Part 1 — Hastening the Stages

- "Time is called 'stages.'" Like a computer program that runs at a certain frequency, the question is how often a person returns to the thought: "What about me, what of the purpose, what of the Creator, where am I in my direction towards Him?"
- This is done mainly in the **ascent**. In a descent a person is "a Golem, a beast"; the failures and hardships come from above, so what he does then "is not considered to be an offense." You can hasten the time of descent, but you can't truly demand advancement in it.
- In the ascent comes the real test: the force from above is given to be used for correction. "If a person uses this strength for enjoyment... then he immediately starts to descend."
- So most of our effort is "not to go down, but to rise, and rise, and rise." Ascent and descent should be judged by my turning to the Creator, my attitude toward Him, "not from the feeling that I'll feel bad."
- Whoever surrounds himself with reminders of the purpose (friends, books, "all kinds of symbols") so that all his actions are done together with the Creator, "he hastens the time of development."

## Part 2 — Preparing for Passover

- **Pesach** is the exodus: leaving this world for spirituality, the state of being together with the Creator, with some connection, understanding and union.
- As with a friend, "hold only the part where you agree, and the part that you don't agree, you should leave it. The correction will come afterwards."
- Even the mechanical preparations (food, equipment, cleaning) should carry this intention: "that each and every action is for me and the Creator to live together."
- **Redemption (Geula)** "is connection with the Creator, that's all." Picture these things as simply and as close to your feeling as possible, not as distant theory.
- When working physically, add the thought "I want to do it," and then aim that desire at feeling the Creator: "I want Him to move my hand." That is the horse and the rider going together, as in "Behind and before You have formed me."

## Part 3 — The Friend in Descent and the General Vessel

- **Q: If a friend is in descent, can that give me fuel?** "If I see that a friend is in a descent, I don't need to think about myself that I'm in a better state. I need to at least elevate him to my level."
- If he has descended, "it means that I've already descended." It is forbidden to think about my own state then; "I need to save him."

> The general vessel cannot be higher than the level of the lowest friend.

- **Why the weakest?** Like a group running ten kilometers to a goal: "We don't receive the payment until the last one arrives." Spirituality requires the full measure. Help him, "like you take someone on the gurney. He reaches the goal although he is on the gurney."
- Invest in everyone, not in one friend, and don't cling to a stronger friend: "how to lift the weak ones."

#### Why are we divided?

- **Q: What is the difference between ups and downs over time and division into separate bodies?** Both result from the **shattering**: a great screen was broken into ever smaller pieces down to the most elementary desire, which is the desire in this world. Each feels separate and must reach its particular correction, then connect for the general one.
- "In spirituality, the particular and the general correction are the same correction." We can't rise a centimeter unless we connect "in width" with all the souls. Whoever starts feeling this link "really started to feel spiritual processes."
- The more a person advances, the more he cares for his environment, until entering spirituality "he stops feeling his own existence."
- In preparation we deliberately build a society with **spiritual laws imposed by force** and see how much our inner desire resists them. That reveals our disparity of form, gives everyone fuel, and hastens the time. Whoever takes on "the burden of the group" only partially advances in that measure.

## Part 4 — Q&A: Ego, Recognition of Evil and the Creator

- **Q: Are my ups and downs only for the group?** In preparation a person still advances himself. Only after crossing does he discover he was serving the collective all along. This is hidden so we have fuel to work.
- **Suffering is the gap.** "I'm supposed to be on level number 10 in spirituality now, and I'm only on level 3." The seven missing degrees are felt as pain, blows and wars: how far I've fallen behind, or how much I can't yet bear the degree I should already be on. "It's actually the same."
- **Q: If I enjoy working in the group, is that a Klipa?** "It's very good." We are in Lo Lishma and must reach "the highest Lo Lishma." Suppressing the will to receive like a yogi may give some feeling, "but this feeling is without development... I cannot bloom from it." Take the respect, the thanks and the good results. "First of all, the action."
- **Q: Where does recognition of evil come from?** Not from your work but as its payment: after efforts, a light comes from above and you recognize the distance between you and it. "You used to work hard, and after that you received a hit, you walked here like a poor Golem... it's spiritual payment for the effort."
- **Q: Feeling the Creator as a friend?** Not exactly. But "with the same emotions where you feel the friends... there you should also place the Creator, let Him enter, because these are true feelings."
- **Q: How do I measure ascent and descent in preparation?** "Only by as much as a person can be abstract from his personal gain."

#### Day and night

- "Day to day pours forth speech, and night to night reveals knowledge." In the **descent**, if I keep fighting to be connected to the hidden Creator, the darkness lets me discern all the points of friction and connection between us, in the mind. In the **ascent** I fill those connections "with love, with emotion," in the heart (*Liba*).

#### Pharaoh or Moses

- **Q: How do I fight the evil inclination together with the Creator instead of against Him?** You can't pull yourself out by your own hair; "you have to have someone external," a friend on a higher degree or help from above. In the ego I am Pharaoh; suddenly I may cross to the side of Moses.
- Don't complicate it with how the will to receive imitates bestowal. "So what if it's egoistic?" In the end comes the greatest Lo Lishma, a yearning "that won't let you sleep. What could be more egoistic?"
- As Baal HaSulam writes in his letters, the main thing is to tie three together, **Oraita, Israel and the Creator** (the light, me, and the One I want to discover), above whatever thoughts arise. "Some things you don't need to talk about... you just need to do it."

## Part 5 — Crossing the Barrier as a Group

- The group crosses together, each in his own measure. **Crossing the barrier** means that one's desires, thoughts and intentions gain the ability to connect with others, a screen of **coarseness of the root** (Aviut Shoresh).
- Coarseness of the root is being an **embryo** toward the group, the friend or the Creator: annulled, passive, "only because of them, only for them. They can use me as much as they want."
- One can't cross alone while the group stays behind. Veterans and newcomers "will be equal in the end, in a very short time." Knowledge "will never determine your spiritual degree."
- "The opening of the eyes is in a group... It's one body." Just as in this world you buy your clothes from others, in spirituality you acquire vessels from the other souls by paying in spiritual effort. "The revelation of spirituality is not in the soul itself but in what the soul acquires from the other souls."

#### About Rabash's groups

- Rav Laitman says the groups in Rabash's time never reached this kind of work; "it was very superficial," the time wasn't right, and the Hasidic and family environment held them back.
- Rabash wrote the articles for the friends, but "he wrote it for generations," as a written method, being "the last one from all the generations of the Kabbalists." They are a stepping stone for preparation before the barrier. After the barrier come the work in three lines, the Sulam commentary and the *Study of the Ten Sefirot*.

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **Hastening is about frequency.** The stages are fixed; how often you return to the thought of the purpose and the Creator determines your pace.
2. **Use the ascent for correction.** Enjoying the ascent brings the descent; the effort is to keep rising.
3. **Every act before Pesach can be with the Creator.** Redemption is simply connection with Him.
4. **The vessel equals its weakest friend.** A friend's descent is my descent; lift everyone, carrying the weakest "on the gurney."
5. **Particular and general correction are one.** You can't rise without connecting in width; imposed spiritual laws in the group reveal the evil and give fuel.
6. **Don't suppress the ego; develop it.** Enjoying the group is good Lo Lishma; recognition of evil comes as payment from the light.
7. **The barrier is crossed as an embryo in the group.** Coarseness of the root is passive annulment to the friends, and spiritual vessels come from the other souls.

## Key Terms & Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Time as stages** | The fixed stages of development; hastening means passing them faster by returning often to the purpose. |
| **Ascent / descent** | Ascent: force from above to be used for correction. Descent: a "Golem" state in which failures come from above. |
| **Pesach / Geula** | The exodus from this world into connection with the Creator; redemption is that connection. |
| **General vessel** | The group as one vessel, whose level is set by its lowest member. |
| **Shattering** | The breaking of the great screen into the smallest desires, making souls feel separate and states alternate. |
| **Highest Lo Lishma** | Yearning for the Creator so strongly it won't let one sleep: the peak of egoistic desire before the change. |
| **Recognition of evil** | Seeing the distance between oneself and the light, which comes from above as payment for effort. |
| **Day and night** | Descent gives discernment in the mind (Mocha); ascent fills it with love in the heart (Liba). |
| **Oraita, Israel and the Creator** | The light, the person and the Creator, held together as one point in the work. |
| **Coarseness of the root (Aviut Shoresh)** | The first screen: annulment to others like an embryo. Gaining it is crossing the barrier. |
| **Embryo (Ibur)** | Being passively present for the group, letting them use one's forces without self-calculation. |
| **Horse and rider** | Doing physical actions while wanting the Creator to move one's hand. |
