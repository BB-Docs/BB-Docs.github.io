### Opening

The Zohar says the Creator is near "to all who call upon Him in truth," and that one who calls without knowing Whom he calls calls in falsehood. Rav Laitman begins with what a name is: recognition, the way the vessel names what it feels. The names we give the Creator measure our correction, from concealment up to the full revelation of "the Good Who Does Good." From there the lesson explains how to rise above time by bringing the future into the present, why the fuel for that can come only from the society, how prayer is born only from necessity, where free choice really lies before and after the barrier, and what "from Your actions we know You" means for us now.

## Part 1 — A Name Is Recognition

- The Creator has many names. "A name is recognition, acknowledgement. Meaning according to what I feel." The vessel names things according to what it feels, a person or a phenomenon: moist, cold, good, bad.
- If the Creator is **the Good Who Does Good**, then any name other than 100% good "attests to a measure of the corruption of the vessel." If I tell Him He does good forty percent of the time, "then it means that I'm on, let's say, degree forty out of a hundred."
- So "the names we give the Creator express the measure of our correction," the measure of revelation or concealment. Recognizing all of Him as the Good Who Does Good means we are corrected.

#### Names of concealment and of revelation

- If we turn to something other than the Creator, we are under **concealment**. In revelation we would see "that it's all one force at work and we deal only with Him."
- In concealment I name phenomena within me and around me good or bad without attributing them to the Creator. To the extent I attribute the concealment I feel to Him, these are degrees of concealment, single and double. These are "names of concealment for the time being."
- "When we cross the barrier, we begin giving these phenomena names of revelation." Whatever exists, "it's always the feeling of the Creator."

#### To know the names of the Creator

- **Q: What does it mean to know the names of the Creator?** It means to attain all the degrees, "from the lowest degree in concealment up to the greatest degree of revelation."
- No one reaches the greatest degree without climbing through all of them, discovering every name until the inclusive general name, the Good Who Does Good. That final name "includes all the names, all the vessels in all their states, the good and the bad, with all the feelings that are revealed in them."

## Part 2 — Rising Above Time

- Because we still live in time, place and motion, a person thinks that "in the future he will be awarded with the Good Who Does Good," and then he'll get this and that.
- "But we learn that there is no such thing as time." It must exist in the state we're in now. "If a person waits for the end, it won't come. Time will make no difference."
- If a person works in the present for the Good Who Does Good to be revealed in his current state, "he is working to bring the future to him." Past, present and future are only the revelation of God in the state a person is in.
- "Nothing will ever change" by itself. Only if the person changes the vessel will the state change. "And so the concept of time depends on us." Bringing the revelation close in our current state "means that we rise above time."

## Part 3 — The Fuel Comes Only from the Society

- Where do we take the forces for this? "Only from the recognition of greatness": of the mission, the Creator, the goal. But how can I recognize the greatness of the goal before attaining it? "How can I yearn for the good if I don't feel it's good yet?"
- "For that we have the help of others." Just as society in this world gives us the importance of becoming rich, important, learned or respected, the society must give me the importance of adhesion with the Creator "at least to the same degree," before I have attained it.
- All our work revolves around improving this in the society. Examine it by one criterion only: "Can it provide me every day with the importance of the goal, with more power, more confidence, more upliftment?" If so, it's a proper environment. If not, "I won't have any existence, any advancement in spirituality."
- If it gives everyone fuel, then "in the same place, same time, in the same state we are in," we bring the end closer to here and now. "This is the meaning of rising above time."

#### Means, not goal

- **Q: How do we keep the society from becoming the goal instead of the means?** By working each time on the importance of the Creator. "There's me, there is society, and there is the Creator. Three things. That's it."
- "The society is not important if it cannot give me every day greater upliftment towards the goal." Then it stops me, confuses me, buries me, "and I should escape this society. Escape means to improve it or to actually leave it." Said seriously: "We are that much dependent on society."

## Part 4 — Calling in Truth from Within Concealment

- **Q: If under concealment we don't call Him by His true name, doesn't every prayer move Him away?** No. There are states where we lie to ourselves, and states where we're true to ourselves "but objectively it's a lie in relation to the Creator."
- I feel bad. Perhaps through this the Creator wants to show me corrections I lack, "and so His attitude towards me is good, but in my vessels it is depicted as bad." So what name do I give Him? "Thank you so much for beating me up"?
- Saying it with the mouth isn't the intention. Telling myself "something good will come of it" is to live in an illusion instead of peeling it away.
- The state can be good only when I bring the future close to the present. "The sum total of my current present feeling with my future feeling" must come out positive, "a plus instead of a minus." Then I can justify the Creator, "then I can say the truth."
- This isn't done intellectually. "I need to feel it." We must feel the present and future as one so the calculation comes out with a surplus, "and the magnitude of that surplus is my fuel." Then "I don't feel bad. I feel good."

## Part 5 — Prayer Comes Only from Necessity

- **Q: What is the order of actions before any address to the Creator?** "Only if there is a need for it." There are people very close to me whom I look right through, and others I must have near me. It's all "according to the measure of my need for them, according to the measure of my recognition of their importance."
- He is everywhere: "I'm immersed in Him. He's all around me. And He's also inside of me," yet I can't feel Him. He presents this whole world and my inner self to me instead of Him. My work is the opposite: to recognize "that it's Him and that nothing else exists here. Not me and not anything around me."
- He doesn't stand over us with a whip; if He did, we'd run to reveal Him. "He only awakens us to allow us free choice," through little stings and hints. Otherwise "I will always find something more important," and that's what we call life.
- "It's a wonder": we exist within Him, He fills us, nothing exists besides Him, "and I don't want it." So "the urgency is something we need to attain on our own. This is the only thing that's really up to us." And alone we can't advance even one millimeter; for this we need society.

#### The click

- **"There can't be any prayer initiated by me, only due to necessity."** Well said. Otherwise it isn't the raising of the deficiency, of **MAN**.
- A deficiency must be one where you know what it is and whom you're turning to, and that this place can accept it and is willing to give. "If all those conditions are there and there's a click, then you get a response. Otherwise you don't."
- To feel that your life depends on the friends: "Imagine that you are standing on a barrel with a noose around your neck," and the judge turns to the whole group and asks what to do with you.

## Part 6 — Which Fear Brings You to Him

- **Q: Can a person deliberately enter a state of fear so that he'll need to pray?** When you feel fear and anxiety about what will happen, the question is whether that fear "will be effective for the goal."
- Suppose I stole something a year ago and now I'm afraid I'll be caught, so I need the Creator. Is that effective? Only if I'm now thankful to Him that He arranged it, because "without that hit you wouldn't yearn for Him." Then "you don't care what the future will be; what's important for you is that you are now thinking of Him."
- **Q: And when life is good and I don't need Him?** "Why do you need to look for a bad life?" Baal HaSulam writes that a person should start from the question "what is the point in life?" every moment. Is that about corporeal blows and prison, or about having no connection with Him?
- Does your body give you deficiencies, that you'll suffer blows? Or do the society, the books and the authors give you the deficiency that "if you don't attain spirituality, it's worse than being in prison and suffering blows"? "It all depends on what you are afraid of. What are your values? That's what you should work on."

## Part 7 — Where Free Choice Lies

- **Q: Before the barrier we're like beasts or dreamers, with no screen. How can there be free choice there?** After the barrier, too, there's a question of where choice exists.
- Under the good inclination or the bad, **Kedusha** or **Klipa**, "both are forces of the Creator and in both we are not free." We are free only in **Klipat Noga**, the middle third of Tiferet, "in the choice of what do we belong to."
- When I acquire the screen I'm not free: "I acquired His nature, so I follow what He's doing." Now I have the opposite nature and follow that. "My work in the middle line, my choice, is the only I."
- "We're not the owners of the actions, only the owners of the choice." The good or bad action is already a result of the choice.
- Before the barrier, the choice is "how to choose the society that will attract me to choose the good, to choose the side of the Creator." After the barrier the choice is likewise of the society, but now among spiritual forces, the Klipot and Kedusha. Either way "it's only the choice."

#### "From Your actions we know You"

- **Q: Can we then say He is good?** That's already a result, already revelation.
- For us now, it means that after all the actions and means I try, "I see the results, and the results I see, they progress me, they advance me in all kinds of ways, even if they're felt in the body as bad."
- You can't discover the Creator through the eyes or touch. "The awareness of Him is an inner emotional awareness that comes as a result of the sum total of actions that we go through."

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **A name is what the vessel feels.** Every name we give the Creator measures our correction, until all names are included in the Good Who Does Good.
2. **There is no time to wait for.** Revelation comes only by working for it in the present state; changing the vessel brings the future close and lifts us above time.
3. **Greatness comes from the society.** Judge the environment by one criterion: does it give me daily more importance of the goal? If not, improve it or leave it.
4. **The society is the means, not the goal.** Me, the society and the Creator: three things, with the importance of the Creator always on top.
5. **Truth is a felt surplus.** Feeling present and future as one, with a positive sum, lets a person justify the Creator honestly; that surplus is the fuel.
6. **Prayer is born of necessity.** Knowing the lack and whom it's addressed to produces the "click" of a response; the urgency is the one thing up to us.
7. **We own only the choice.** Before and after the barrier, freedom is in choosing what we belong to, through the society; the action itself is a result.

## Key Terms & Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Name of the Creator** | The recognition of Him according to what the vessel feels; a measure of correction. |
| **Truth (Emet)** | Calling the Creator by what He truly is, the Good Who Does Good; the seal of the King's ring, the completeness of everything. |
| **The Good Who Does Good** | The inclusive general name, containing all names in all states, revealed at full correction. |
| **Concealment (single and double)** | States in which phenomena aren't attributed to the Creator, or are attributed only partly; their names are names of concealment. |
| **Above time** | Bringing the revelation of the end into the present state by changing the vessel. |
| **Greatness of the goal** | The importance of adhesion with the Creator, received from the society before it is attained; the source of all fuel. |
| **Surplus** | The positive sum of present and future felt as one, by which a person can justify the Creator. |
| **Prayer from necessity** | A raising of MAN in which one knows the lack and whom it's addressed to, yielding a response. |
| **Klipat Noga** | The middle third of Tiferet, the only place of free choice, between Kedusha and Klipa. |
| **Owners of the choice** | We choose what we belong to; the good or bad action that follows is not ours. |
| **"From Your actions we know You"** | Recognizing the Creator as an inner emotional awareness from the sum of what we pass through. |
