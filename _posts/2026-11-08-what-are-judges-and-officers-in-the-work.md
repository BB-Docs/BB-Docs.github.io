### Opening

"You shall appoint for yourself judges and officers in all your gates." Why are judges and officers so important? Rav Laitman explains that they are the two forces in a person that set his path to the goal: one that discerns where he stands, and one that carries the decision out. Without them a person doesn't know where he is, where he is going, or whether he is advancing at all. But the article doesn't say how we get them. The lesson answers that they come only through **faith**, the sensation of godliness that lifts a person above his nature. From there Rav Laitman explains what faith really is, how it frees us from the "program" we were born and raised with, and what the officers actually do once the judges have decided.

## Part 1 — Two Forces Along the Path

- **Judges** are the power of discernment. Like medical examinations, they determine "who he is, what he is, to what extent he is flawed, whether he has deviated from the path."
- **Officers** are the forces a person builds in himself to carry out what the judges decided, bringing him "precisely to that goal along the very path that he has determined to be correct."
- The two must always go together, one supporting the other: examination and assessment of the current state, determining the goal, and execution. "And again at the next degree, judges, officers, execution, and so on."
- **"In all your gates":** whenever a person opens for himself the entrance to the next degree, "that's called a gate."

## Part 2 — How Do We Get Judges and Officers?

- "It is true that they are necessary. We agree, we understand. But how do we attain them?" This is man's work.
- **Judges are the recognition of evil.** To the extent the Creator is revealed to a person through his exertion in the study, he sees how opposite he is to Him. "He starts to judge himself, and to judge himself correctly." Correctly means "he begins to see himself in the light of the Creator."
- Once he sees where he stands in that light, he also understands that his officers can be forces from above, which establish him and set his next step each time.
- So everything is attained "only through the recognition of godliness, through the sensation of spirituality, which is called faith."

> If he has not determined his true position, then he is without judges and without officers. He is simply at the mercy of his own nature, in which case the direction is always opposite to the goal.

## Part 3 — Faith and Faith in the Sages

- **Q: The article says judges and officers come through faith, or through faith in the sages. What's the difference?** Both are faith. Faith is "the sensation of godliness," which "gives a person the ability to rise above his nature." This is how the reforming light works.
- Rising above his nature, a person becomes free: he can see his state objectively and set goals truthfully, making decisions "in a truly neutral manner."
- There are degrees of faith: simple, natural faith and **complete faith**. Faith is the force of **Bina**, connected to the **screen**, which lets a person rise above his will to receive whatever its form or size. The size of the will to receive I rise above defines the force of faith I have.
- **Faith in the sages:** a person does not use a force of faith he acquired himself, but annuls himself before a higher degree and receives forces from it. "It's the transition." Later, faith in the sages is the light of Atik.
- Faith is the light of **Hassadim**, **Chafetz Chesed** (delighting in mercy), at each degree where one acquires only vessels of bestowal. A screen for receiving in order to bestow, Hassadim with the illumination of Hochma, "is already called complete faith."

## Part 4 — "Before the Barrier There Is No Faith"

- What we call faith in this world "is a kind of knowledge." A rumor, or what a thousand people tell me, gives my body the same confidence as if I knew it myself.
- From childhood we are filled with supposed facts we never checked, until "they've become principles inside me," and we judge, decide and live by them. "We are all filled with these things, with actual nonsense."
- Education shapes a person: compare someone raised in Africa, Australia, Israel or America. Until the end of his life he can't free himself from these stigmas and acts on them as his own knowledge.
- In spirituality, where definitions are genuine, faith is defined as **a force** through which "you can rise above your nature so that no one can influence you or your decisions."

#### Breaking out of the program

- **Q: Before acquiring that force, can you rely only on society?** "Before you acquire that force, you rely only on your will to receive."
- I am born with animal traits, then acquire human qualities from society through envy, lust and honor, plus all the knowledge of my environment. "My program is already within me, and this program determines my entire course in life."
- How can I ever rise above both the animal body's demands and society's stigmas? "The force that enables me to rise above this entire nature, both animalistic and human, is called faith."
- The first time I am free of it is **crossing the barrier**: "That's the exodus from Egypt... becoming free."

#### Born into the middle

- That freedom must be cultivated. It builds in the soul the **middle third of Tifferet**, a truly neutral discernment from which I begin to acquire both the Creator's qualities and the created being's.
- "My I is neither this nor that. It's right in the middle." We are born into spirituality as a part that belongs neither to the Creator nor to the created being, neither the good inclination nor the evil, but the **middle line**.
- Growing that free point between the two opposing forces into a full **Partzuf** called the soul is done "through the judges and the officers."

## Part 5 — What Do the Officers Actually Do?

- **Q: What is the role of an officer?** "The officer is the one who executes. He has no free decisions to make." Governance divides into degrees: judges decide, officers execute.
- **Q: The moment I scrutinize a state, it already changes from above. So what is execution?** "No, no, no. The man's work is not only that of scrutiny." After deciding what you want to be, you must actively build your future state, carrying out "the entire process of the coupling by striking."
- **Forces come from above, but the execution is yours.** Like police and judges, who receive their power from the state, both domains have no forces of their own. "You receive the forces. But the execution you have to do by yourself."
- **Q: I've reached the request. What else is there?** That is only a partial action, the recognition of evil. Then you ask for the power of faith and the power to execute. The upper one may give greatness, showing you the next degree, "however it may be, you have to perform all the actions slowly, gradually, by yourself."

#### Knowing the degree completely

- In **Ibur** (conception), the lower one asks each time for the force of the screen for self-annulment. After birth, in **Yenika** (suckling), he receives forces from the upper one and grows with the first level of coarseness, asking again.
- "Asking means that I perform some scrutiny regarding what I lack, what I truly need," and what next stage I want to reach.
- In this world a baby doesn't know what it means to be a month and a day old, and can exist without knowing how to get along. "In spirituality, there is no such thing." If you don't know the causes and consequences of the higher degree, "you will not be allowed to enter it." It's not enough to cry like a baby; you must know exactly what food the upper one is ready to give and how to ask for it.

#### The example from the upper one

- The **AHP of the upper one** inside the lower one gives him an example, "like a mother teaching the child, take this, eat that, sit like so."
- The lower one compares his own forces to what he sees. **That is the judge.** He works to agree with what the upper one recommends, decides it is truly his desire, asks for the force, and executes it with his Partzuf. "It's a long process."
- The upper one doesn't give the example before the lower one prepares, "otherwise he won't understand the example at all. It will be to his detriment to see it."
- "The light is abundant, complete rest, and everything depends on the vessel."

#### Officers as restrictions

- The officers are "a force of limitation," the **screens** that restrict and stabilize you so you actually become equivalent to the image of the upper one.
- **Q: Is the officers' work to awaken a feeling of guilt?** At the beginning of the path there is a kind of shame that isn't yet spiritual. But "humanity is not the same as spirituality. It's just psychology."
- Once you build a relationship with the Creator on a spiritual degree, "there is no issue of who is to blame there, me or him. It's entirely irrelevant." The feeling of His uniqueness immediately neutralizes it.

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **Judges and officers set the path.** Judges discern where I stand; officers carry out the decision. Without them a person follows his nature, always opposite to the goal.
2. **Judging correctly means seeing myself in the Creator's light.** The recognition of evil comes to the extent the Creator is revealed through exertion in the study.
3. **Both come only through faith.** Faith is the sensation of godliness, the force of Bina and the screen, that lifts a person above his nature.
4. **Faith in this world is just hearsay.** Our whole personality is a "program" built from nature and society; only the force of faith frees us from it, starting at the barrier.
5. **The soul is born in the middle.** It is the free, neutral point between the Creator's qualities and the created being's, grown into a Partzuf through judges and officers.
6. **The forces come from above, but the execution is ours.** Scrutiny and request are not enough; we must build the next state ourselves, step by step.
7. **Officers are screens, not guilt.** They restrict and stabilize us toward the example of the upper one; in spirituality the question of blame disappears.

## Key Terms & Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Judges (Shoftim)** | The power of discernment: the recognition of evil, seeing one's true state in the light of the Creator. |
| **Officers (Shotrim)** | The forces that execute the judges' decision; screens and limitations that stabilize a person on his path. |
| **Gates** | The entrance to each next degree, where judges and officers must be appointed anew. |
| **Faith (Emuna)** | The sensation of godliness; a force that lifts a person above his nature to decide freely and neutrally. |
| **Faith in the Sages** | Annulling oneself before a higher degree to receive its forces; a transition, later the light of Atik. |
| **Complete Faith** | A screen for receiving in order to bestow: Hassadim with the illumination of Hochma. |
| **Barrier (Machsom)** | The first crossing above one's whole nature; the exodus from Egypt; before it, there is no real faith. |
| **Middle line / middle third of Tifferet** | The neutral, free discernment between the Creator's and the created being's qualities, from which the soul is born. |
| **Ibur / Yenika** | Conception and suckling: stages in which the lower one asks the upper one for forces to grow. |
| **AHP of the upper one** | The example the upper one shows inside the lower one of what it needs to become. |
| **Coupling by striking (Zivug de Hakaa)** | The full action the lower one must carry out after scrutiny and request. |
