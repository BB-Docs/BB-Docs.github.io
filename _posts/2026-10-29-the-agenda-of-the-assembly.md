### Opening

The Congress opens with Rabash's condition for every assembly: "One should always praise the Creator and then pray," and so every gathering must begin with the **praise of the society**. Why start with the friends rather than with the Creator? Rav Laitman's answer: the Creator is hidden and we can't direct ourselves to Him, but He broke the vessel on purpose and stands behind every friend. What we can't do toward Him, we do toward the friends: annul ourselves, see their greatness, and receive their strength, and then cry out to Him to correct the connection, where He is revealed. This lesson was mostly workshops, so the guide is short and covers only Rav Laitman's own words.

## Part 1 — The Goal: One Man, One Heart (2023 Congress Excerpt)

- "Our goal is to discover the Creator." We gather to return to the system of **Adam HaRishon**, where all created beings connect into a single desire, "as one heart."
- **Adam** comes from *Domeh*, "similar" to the Creator. In that togetherness He is revealed: "He dresses into us, spreads into us, fills us," and we are in dialogue with Him "through our interior."
- Rav Laitman recalls 1984, when **Rabash** wrote the first article for the group: "We have gathered here to lay the foundation for a society for all those who are willing... to follow the method of Baal HaSulam," the way to rise in the degrees of man, becoming ever more similar to the Creator.
- "The interior of the world is the interior of our thought." Just as strongly as we felt detached before, we'll feel interconnected, until "the Creator envelops us, connecting us, fulfilling us, and we are becoming the system of Adam."
- From the article: the friends are "first-degree fighters" against the inclination, whose only aim is to win "for the sake of the Creator," meaning for Him to dwell among us and receive contentment.

## Part 2 — Setting the Goal: "Make for Yourself a Rav"

- Kabbalah is "a method for the revelation of the Creator to the created being in this world." The one force that bestows and guides everything: reach it, connect to it, become like it.
- **"Make for yourself a Rav"** means making this goal the deciding factor in everything. "The closer something is to the goal, the more important it is... if it's not in the direction of the goal at all, I just throw it away." Be as serious as possible and "lie to myself as little as possible."
- We don't attain the Creator Himself. "Creator" means *Bo-Re*, "come and see": the quality of **love and bestowal** appears in my vessel to the extent that I want to be in it. I must build a desire for that quality to fill me "as if it emptied me of everything else... a new program that entered my mind."

#### "Buy yourself a friend"

- You can't buy the Creator; the change of qualities comes from **the light's influence**. But you can buy a friend: invest in the connection and do for him what you can actually do. "Then the upper light appears on you and it shapes the desire of bestowal and love."
- "My friends are simply my springboard to the Creator. Through them I draw Him to me."

## Part 3 — Lesson with Rav Laitman: The Creator Behind the Friends

#### Why the friends and not the Creator directly

- After the breaking of the vessel, the Creator no longer develops us; "He waits for our own activity." We do our half (scrutiny, sorting, preparation) until we see we can't do more, then comes **the outcry**. "All He needs from us is the right request."
- But the Creator is hidden, without form. How do we aim at Him? "No problem": He broke the vessel on purpose. Treat every broken piece "as essential to you as is the Creator, because it's really so, it's a part of your soul."
- So instead of the greatness of the Creator we work on the **greatness of the friends**, and instead of connection with Him, connection with them. When we can't connect, we cry out, "and then He is revealed in the connection that He corrected."
- **Why does this work?** Toward the Creator "I'm willing to annul myself, to be small" because of the will to receive. Toward the friends "my ego doesn't let me." So by annulling myself before them I truly cancel the ego, "and I'm already by this ready to be similar to the Creator."

#### "There is none else besides Him"

- A person sees people's faces but should believe the Creator stands behind them and operates them. "They themselves don't exist, they are puppets, they are silhouettes."
- Everything in my heart and mind, "it's all the Creator cooking it there." He places people before me and moves how they relate to me and I to them.
- Paying attention to everything that happens, I begin to detect that "it is Him. And there's nothing else." These are all my own qualities that I see this way, and through them "by Your actions we know You," until adhesion.

#### Love of friends must point to love of the Creator

- From love of friends a person learns to leave self-love for love of others, and then he can be rewarded with love of the Creator. But "we cannot engage in love of friends unless we connect it to its final goal," or both loves go in wrong directions.

## Part 4 — Receiving the Strength of the Nine

- Alone we can't fight ourselves. Who has managed a diet or quitting smoking? "And it's certain that we can't. No one can. And I too would have been unable."
- The Creator deliberately gave each person only enough strength "to hang on to the society. The Creator pulls a person by the ear to this place and says, take this."
- "If I'm in a Ten, I get strength from nine people. I have enough strength to reach the end of correction." Wanting to receive their strength is our part; when we want it, we cry out and He makes it happen. "All that's needed from us is the question, the prayer, the deficiency."

#### Interconnected vessels

- To be incorporated with the nine, each must **annul himself** before the others "by each one seeing the merits of his friends and not their faults." Normally I don't see a friend as great, because then I'm small and it puts me in a bad mood.
- "One who thinks he is a little higher than the friends can no longer unite with them."

> The smaller he is, the more he can receive... If he is equal, he can't receive anything. If he is higher, then of course he can't.

- Like **interconnected vessels**: the lower one receives what is in the higher one. "I only need to lower myself a little bit. And immediately what is in them passes on to me."

#### Workshops

- After the lesson the friends asked: what actions must I take to receive the society's strength and desires, and the qualities I need to adhere to the Creator? Then each friend praised the friend on his right, describing how that friend's merits lead the Ten toward the goal. Gatherings around the world shared their impressions.

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **Begin with the praise of the society.** It is Rabash's condition for the assembly and the key that opens the heart to prayer.
2. **The goal decides everything.** "Make for yourself a Rav": weigh every thought and act by how close it brings you to revealing the Creator.
3. **The friends are the springboard.** We can't buy the Creator, but we can "buy" a friend, and that investment draws the light that builds bestowal in us.
4. **The Creator stands behind the friends.** He broke the vessel on purpose so that working on the friends' greatness becomes working on His.
5. **Annulment cancels the real ego.** Being small before the Creator is easy; before the friends it's real work, and it makes us similar to Him.
6. **Lower yourself and receive.** Like interconnected vessels, the smaller I am before the nine, the more of their strength flows into me.
7. **Ask for the connection.** We do our half until we can't, then cry out; He corrects the connection and is revealed in it.

## Key Terms & Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Praise of the society** | Rabash's opening condition for an assembly: first recount the friends' merit and importance, then pray. |
| **Adam (Domeh)** | Man as "similar" to the Creator; the single system of all souls connected as one heart. |
| **Make for yourself a Rav** | Make the goal (revealing the Creator) the great deciding factor in all of life. |
| **Bo-Re ("come and see")** | The Creator as the quality of love and bestowal appearing in one's vessel. |
| **Buy yourself a friend** | Investing in the connection with friends, which invites the light that changes one's qualities. |
| **Springboard** | The friends as the means through which a person draws the Creator to him. |
| **Ein Od Milvado** | "There is none else besides Him": the Creator operating behind every person and event. |
| **Annulment (Bitul)** | Seeing the friends' merits and making oneself small before them so their strength can pass to him. |
| **Interconnected vessels** | The lower receives from the higher; the smaller one makes himself, the more he receives. |
| **Strength of the nine** | In a Ten, the other nine give each friend enough strength to reach the end of correction. |
