### Opening

The article closes on "I labored and found." One who enjoys his labor is happy during the work itself, wants to serve the Rav not in order to be rewarded, and even agrees that the knowledge of the Torah revealed after his labor will go to his friend. So his labor wasn't preparation for an acquisition; the Torah came to him unexpectedly, and that is why it is called a finding. Like Abraham longing for guests after greeting the Shechina, and like Israel, who after they saw, craved faith, the advantage of the work over the reward is evident. Rav Laitman asks what work and reward really mean at each degree, why the only test of bestowal is toward the friends, why greatness is fuel and the Creator must stay concealed, and how the group prepares each person to make the study a prayer.

## Part 1 — What Is Work, and What Is Reward?

- We begin thinking that sitting and studying is labor. Later a person looks back and sees "it was not he who did it. He was given a desire, so he did it... It was like a machine carrying out the desire that was put into it from above."
- Real work is "that I choose in advance myself, that I carry it out against my desire," aiming at a result. And in the middle of the work, the reward I expected already changes, especially in quality.
- The true reward at the end: "although now I am unable to carry out the work without telling myself that I have a reward, in the end I will reach a state where I want to carry out this work without reward. And this result... will be my reward."

## Part 2 — The Only Test Is Toward the Friend

- How do I examine whether I'm advancing toward bestowal? "In the society. There's no other examination... It's impossible to carry out any other act of bestowal except toward one another."
- Toward the Creator we can't do any act; we don't see Him or understand what He needs. "Do what is good for you and do not lie to yourself that you're doing it for the sake of the Creator." Do it toward the friend, "then you will see whether you are even capable of it, whether you have something to talk about or should just keep silent."
- Every action has a will to receive behind it; perhaps 99% for myself and 1% for the friend. Learning to measure this takes "great work and a long path" of mutual attempts, until we discover that without the reforming light nothing can be done without return.

#### Labor, despair and the finding

- Saying in advance "I'm incapable of bestowing, so I'll sit and do nothing" means never reaching "the breaking of the heart and the cry." Without labor, no finding.
- From all the egoistic attempts with the friends, "I eventually come to a desire that I definitely want to have bestowal. This is called a finding." Then comes the desire for a desire to bestow, and a true cry that is answered.
- "The finding does not come for the abundance. The finding comes for the true desire. Abundance we want. Correction is the problem." When everyone works and despairs together and still wants to go on, "this will be a good group cry."

## Part 3 — Greatness Is Fuel

- Working for someone great isn't considered labor, "because greatness is fuel. And the work itself is then the reward." Rabash said: take someone you truly hate, but if he is great, you'll feel urges to serve him, devoted, without calculation. Even toward an evil tyrant whom everyone thinks is great, "the greatness simply breaks your eye, subdues it."
- "It is a law: annulment of the small before the great," because all of reality comes from the Creator to the created being. Evil or good doesn't matter; the greatness subdues us.
- "That's why the Creator is concealed. If He were revealed, we would annul ourselves, we would have no choice." So I want first to reach a free decision that I want Him and forces of bestowal, "after that let Him be revealed to me. Otherwise I'll be His slave, just as I'm now a slave of the other side."

#### The years of concealment

- "Labor is always in concealment," and only in concealment is there free choice, as the Study of the Ten Sefirot teaches. That's why concealment lasts years, ten or fifteen: "afterwards it serves you along the entire path in all the spiritual degrees, up to the end of correction."
- Toward the friends the concealment is the same: "You need to believe that the friend is great and that he is concealed from you." Bestowing on him brings truer discernments, and together with the friends we come to wanting the power of bestowal.

## Part 4 — Start Every Thought from the Creator

- **Q: Should we emphasize the Creator's concealed greatness or the friends?** "In each and every action we must put the Creator at the head. Every sentence, every thought must begin with Him," because in spirituality a thought is an action, and "goal-oriented means that it begins from the Creator. And after that, it continues to the friend."
- Carrying something out toward the friend is truth; there I discover whether I'm able or not. "If I tie these two things together, then it is truly a correct action."

#### Out of double concealment

- **Q: How should the society keep the concealment from slipping away?** The problem is double concealment: "concealment of the fact that I'm in concealment," when it seems "I'm simply in this world," with only some background knowledge that spirituality exists.
- There a person can't awaken himself. Only what he prepared in advance helps: "an alarm clock," commitments to actions for the society. The society can bring him out to ordinary concealment, where he feels he lacks spirituality.
- Better before it's done from above: "if it is done from above... you were not able to, and they came to help you again. This is already a loss, not a gain."

## Part 5 — The Group Prepares the Study

- **Q: How do we hasten the next state?** "Only through coming together." Like brainstorming: ten sit and throw out ideas, a word from one sparks something entirely different in another. What's found comes "by chance, not with respect to us, but from heaven," and we should accept it as directing us. "One who sits alone... will simply sit and eat himself up."
- The whole path: from opposite the Creator to equivalence, only in concealment and by free choice. The forces come from the reforming light, drawn when the study becomes a prayer, and the correct prayer comes from preparation before sitting down to the book.
- "What is the purpose of the group? To bring a person to the correct efficiency during the study. This is the main work in the group." It must bring "a very, very strong deficiency," through shame before the friends, obligation, envy, even hatred. "What do I care who's gonna influence me? The main thing is that they sit next to the book seriously and want to change."
- The same deficiency can be gathered through suffering over thousands of years of incarnations, "or I can gather it from all the friends... to really feel it within a few months or maybe years."

#### Don't reinvent the wheel

- The society can put "a new mind" into me, a different software, so that a person comes out of the room after ten minutes thinking only of spirituality.
- Why build bookkeeping from scratch on paper when you can buy software humanity developed over generations? "It's not advancement. It's stupidity." The same with the friends' thoughts: they've already been through it "in dozens of incarnations."
- Everything around you, home, furniture, clothes, food, comes from contributing a little to society and receiving. "And in spirituality we want to be alone... We don't understand how helpless we are if we're alone."

## Part 6 — Completing Each Other's Vessel

- **Q: Where do I look for my next step?** "Every time a person doesn't know what to do, he should search how to bestow to the friend, how to do good to the friend." Then an uplifting comes from above, and suddenly you have a good thought about the Creator, "because it's the same thing."
- **Q: Why does bestowal to others come before bestowal to the Creator?** After the shattering each one needs support from another, because what's lacking is the screen that glues all the parts into one vessel. As in this world society supplies your shoes and home, "it's actually a copy from spirituality": the Creator won't complete your missing parts; you receive them by interchange with the society, and the correction comes from the Creator.
- "It's not that I got his shoes. There's a part of me in him." Each gives the other what belongs to him, and by this exchange "we are connected together... and it's permanent," so the general screen spreads over all the souls. Even beyond the barrier, the sages rarely act alone; usually two or three.
- What we really give each other: "an impression from each one's inspiration from the goal." What can be lacking in the other except the importance of the goal?

## Part 7 — States, Waves and the Black Box

- **Q: How not to sink into satisfaction?** You can't run constantly like a mouse on a wheel. Some states have to be digested; dark times too, which we should try to shorten, "but it's impossible to avoid them completely." "There is no state in a person's life that is with no benefit"; the question is how much you activate yourself in it.
- Rabbi Akiva, the only survivor of a sunken ship, said he grabbed a plank, and "against every wave, I lowered my head. That's how I reached the shore."
- One who has worked on preparing a group "has nothing to fear." Not to retire and let it happen, but to advance at a reasonable pace. States differ by the structure of each soul: more inside or outside, in deeds or intentions, often accumulating unfelt.

#### Results we can't predict

- **Q: If I get the wrong result, is it the society or me?** The question may not be ripe, or the answer came and he doesn't hear it: you ask for shoes and are told to go make socks, "because I see that in the work of socks you'll come to a state that you will have shoes."
- "In our beastly egoistic mind we can never say what the result is from an action that is somewhat spiritual." Between us and spirituality there's a system, "truly like a black box." I do a good deed and feel bad: "that's the posterior, that's vessels. You'll later correct them and receive something really new." One who adds wisdom adds sorrow, a greater recognition of evil.
- **Q: What to do when I feel I lack connection with the Creator?** "Do something to have a connection." But who is the Creator? Not a kind old man who only makes you feel good, but the Partzuf of Zeir Anpin of Atzilut, holding all the deficiencies of the lower ones and all the force to bestow from the upper ones. "The Creator is the bestowing force. But how His form of bestowal is revealed is in the created being."

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **The finding is the true desire.** Labor brings not the abundance but a real desire to bestow, then a desire for that desire and a cry that is answered.
2. **The reward is to work without reward.** What we expect changes along the way, until the reward is the ability to bestow freely.
3. **Bestowal can only be tested toward the friend.** Toward the Creator we can't act; toward the friend we see whether we are capable.
4. **Greatness is fuel, so the Creator is concealed.** The small annuls before the great; concealment leaves room to choose Him freely, and the years of concealment serve every later degree.
5. **Start every thought from the Creator and carry it to the friend.** Tying the two keeps the action true; commitments to the society pull us out of double concealment.
6. **The group's main work is preparing the study.** It must generate a strong deficiency by any means, sparing us millennia of suffering and the folly of starting from scratch.
7. **Exchange completes the vessel.** Each holds parts of the other; giving them back connects the souls permanently, while results pass through a black box we can't predict.
