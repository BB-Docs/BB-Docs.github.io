### Opening

The four kinds of Sukkot are the citron, palm branch, myrtle, and willow. One has taste and smell, one has neither, and the other two have one each, yet the mitzva is to hold all four together. Rav Laitman reads this as the rule for all spiritual work. The Creator is revealed only when everything that seems unequal is bound into **one bundle**. From there the Q&A becomes a practical question: what are we missing to break through? His answers are **attack**, **loving the revealed flaws**, and **persistence**.

## Part 1 — The Four Kinds Are the Four Phases, and Society

- The four kinds match the **four phases of direct light**. The willow, with no taste and no smell, is Malchut feeling she has nothing and only wants to resemble the Creator. It builds up through the second and third phases to the fourth, the citron.
- All four phases gather in **Malchut**, which has its own four phases. It can't respond correctly to the upper nine until every discernment is included together: willow, palm, myrtle, and citron.
- The same holds in human society, across the inanimate, vegetative, animate, and human levels. "Until they all unite together, we won't be able to achieve the complete final correction."
- **The sukkah's covering** is made from the waste of the threshing floor and the winepress, meaning things unimportant to the will to receive. These are all "symbols of connecting in the will to bestow." Joined together in that form, we draw the upper light and correct all 613 desires of the soul within the corrected connection. "This is what the holiday symbolizes."

## Part 2 — Q&A: What Was Missing in Rabash's Sukkah? Attack

**Question:** You've told us about a Sukkot with Rabash, a time of uplifted spirits, when you asked what was missing to break through.

Rav Laitman remembers the year they built a new sukkah to Rabash's exacting standards. It was all wood, even the nails and screws had to be wooden, and the covering was so thick that barely any light came through. The shared effort "created a special atmosphere, everyone was on fire." On one of their daily walks he asked what was missing, and Rabash answered that it wasn't enough: **connection requires an attack**.

- **Exodus from Egypt:** they came out only when they connected at once, with Pharaoh's army behind them and the Red Sea in front. At Mount Sinai it was the mountain in front and, behind them, "the women." It is said they left Egypt thanks to the righteous women, inner and outer. The condition was "either you unite, or this will be your burial place."
- These are "not simple conditions. It's like labor pains. Only this allows being born into the new world."

#### The pressure is ours to create

> It all depends on the pressure that we generate ourselves.

- Some people in the world feel lost amid economic collapse; others don't feel anything is happening. "The world doesn't have a shape per se."
- We can stay calm ("whatever happens, happens"), or we can bring ourselves to feel that "it's enough, we must unite." A reality that feels calm to others can be **the darkness of Egypt** to us, with Pharaoh's army at our heels.

## Part 3 — The Torah Is About Degrees of Connection, Not Geography

- Every state in the Torah describes a **greater connection between souls**, in their inner desires. It isn't geography or the history of people wandering between Canaan and Egypt.
- **The map of degrees:**
  - **Babylon:** the desire to receive for oneself. "Go forth from your land" means move to a greater desire, connected with your group.
  - **Egypt:** the left line. The desires grow and the evil inclination stands out, which is called descending to Egypt.
  - **The Sinai desert:** rising above that state by uniting.
  - **The Land of Israel:** connecting above all the problems that appear.
- "We can take a bus tour from Babylon to Egypt and back. So what? It'll be earthly." Spiritual degrees are discovered sitting in one place, **to the extent of the connection between us**.
- Since the shattering, all desires are cut off from each other. "If a person doesn't make any connection, he doesn't correct any desire to receive."

## Part 4 — Q&A: How Was the First Environment Built? Abraham's House

**Question:** Do our sources show how to build an environment in this world?

- Abraham did it first. The "people of the house of Abraham" were those willing to leave **Babylon**, not the physical city but "the spiritual Babylon of each and every one," where everyone hated everyone.
- He joined them with his quality of **Hesed**: "love above hatred." He couldn't do the work for them. He only showed that by **faith above reason, love above hatred, connection above all contradictions**, they must stay connected.
- The Torah is "a story of transgressions and corrections." You correct something, think you're done, and a deeper layer of the will to receive comes up. "Every new degree appears because you made the previous correction," all the way to the 125th degree.
- So **public opinion** is needed, a shared thought and aim that spirituality is found in connection. "All the troubles happening are in order to aim us toward this connection." That is the world's problem now, and "we are the ones who possess the method of connection, and we must apply it first."

## Part 5 — Love the Flaws

> We should love the fact that the flaws are revealed. As Baal HaSulam says, specifically thanks to the discovery of the evils, you merit ascent and correction.

- A beginner feels his first descent after a few weeks "like someone hit him on the head." Everything goes tasteless, foggy, and discouraging. It passes, comes back, and "it takes a long time until he hears that these things are the path."
- **Like a yo-yo:** "the more you throw it downwards, the more it jumps higher." Later a person learns to go through the ups and downs knowingly, even controlling them.
- **Which flaws count:** not general disappointment with life, which "people on the street feel too." The only flaws that matter are **connection and disconnection**. "That's how we should observe and measure ourselves."

## Part 6 — Q&A: Loving Without Carrying Everyone's Pain

**Question** (Shlomo, Jerusalem): It's hard to love even my own child, because I feel his pain. How can I love the whole group and all of humanity?

- "I don't need to be connected to all their deficits. What for?"
- Picture them **already connected**, already at the end of correction, "the greatest of the generation," with only *my* connection missing to complete the picture.
- Then there's no criticism of them, only the question of "how do I mingle with them, get integrated with them, and truly lose myself there."

## Part 7 — Q&A: If Connection Is Simple, Why Can't We Find It?

**What connection is:**

- Connection isn't "okay, we're willing, we're moving toward each other." Our own desires "have no status in spirituality."
- When we make this "compression," the effort to unite, **the light comes and builds something new**. It resembles neither what each of us brought nor the sum of our desires. "It's literally part of the Shechina, of divinity," and there one finds eternity.
- "I labored and I found": what you find seems unrelated to what you came with. Like the children of Israel fleeing into the dark, into the sea, you don't see or understand anything. There is only pressure, and you demand "something about which you know nothing," as one man with one heart.

**What we're missing: persistence.**

> What you lack is persistence. As many people as possible connected to this idea, thinking about it all the time.

- The students of the Ari, and especially of the **Ramchal**, took shifts around the clock so that at least two were always reading the Zohar together. What mattered wasn't the reading but "keeping the intention of connection and keeping the others in that thought."
- "If you didn't labor, you won't find. If you did labor, you'll find": a new Kli you can't see in advance.

## Part 8 — Q&A: The People Watching from Afar

**Question:** More religious and secular viewers are watching our channel, but many can't tell this apart from any other rabbi's teaching. What's going on?

- Of those who left Egypt, 600,000 were men, alongside about three million women, children, and elderly. About one fifth "have the ability to overcome, to join together." The rest "follow along."
- Even so, they keep hearing words about unity and **Arvut**, and "because of the light in these articles, it works." Inner change takes a long time, "like a baby who keeps playing even with a 40-degree fever," and here the changes are far more basic.
- At the end of correction, even those who are completely detached today "will be just like Rabbi Shimon." It all depends on the light of Hochma, which will eventually fill everyone.

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **All four kinds in one bundle.** The Creator is revealed only when every level (the four phases, the four levels of society, every kind of friend) is bound together. None can be left out.
2. **Connection requires an attack.** Like the Exodus between Pharaoh and the sea, breaking through comes from concentrated pressure, and we have to create that pressure ourselves.
3. **The Torah maps degrees of connection.** Babylon, Egypt, the desert, and the Land of Israel are growing layers of ego with growing connection above them.
4. **Love the flaws.** Each revealed disconnection is fuel for the next degree, like a yo-yo thrown down that jumps higher. Measure yourself only by connection and disconnection.
5. **See the friends as already corrected.** Don't take on their deficits. Picture them whole, with only your own connection missing.
6. **Connection is a new creation.** The light builds a shared desire that doesn't resemble what we brought. "I labored and I found."
7. **What's missing is persistence.** Hold the intention of connection constantly, and keep others in it, like the Ramchal's students' round-the-clock shifts.

## Key Terms & Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Four Kinds (Arba'at HaMinim)** | Citron (taste and smell), palm (taste only), myrtle (smell only), willow (neither). They correspond to the four phases of direct light and to the kinds of people, and have to be held as one bundle. |
| **One Bundle (Agudah Achat)** | The Creator is reconciled only when all levels are bound together. |
| **Attack** | A concentrated shared push for connection, created by our own pressure, "like labor pains." |
| **Four Phases of Direct Light** | From the root to Malchut. All four gather in Malchut, which has to include every discernment. |
| **Babylon / Egypt / Desert / Land of Israel** | Degrees of connection above a growing will to receive, not geography. |
| **House of Abraham** | The first group: people who left "spiritual Babylon," joined by Abraham's Hesed, "love above hatred." |
| **Revelation of Flaws** | Discovering disconnection, which is the material for the next degree. It is to be loved, not feared. |
| **Persistence** | The missing ingredient: holding the thought of connection constantly and together. |
| **I Labored and I Found (Yagati uMatzati)** | Only effort brings the new shared Kli, which can't be foreseen. |
| **Arvut** | Mutual guarantee. The idea whose light works even on those who hear it from afar. |
