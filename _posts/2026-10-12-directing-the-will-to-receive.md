### Opening

Rav Laitman reads the article as saying that **anything we attain, we need a Kli for**. We perceive this world through five senses, but it is the will to receive behind them that actually feels. How, then, do we come to perceive spirituality, which we are already in but "just don't catch"? And can we speed this up by limiting the will to receive with diets, abstinence, and external restrictions? His answer is a firm no. The will to receive can't be cut down, only **aimed**: drawn with a group toward one goal, so that the reforming light can invert it.

*Context Rav Laitman gives:* Rabash had a seminary (kollel) in Kfar Saba named after him, and this article was written for that occasion. Yet in about 13 years of daily walks together, Rabash never wanted to visit it. Those studying there "didn't have any connection with him. They just sat and studied Gemara."

## Part 1 — Everything Depends on the Will to Receive

- Behind the five senses "stands the will to receive." If I'm tired, "they have eyes and see not": the senses work, but nothing enters me.
- The senses exist even in a dead person; without a will to receive behind them, "there's no one to perceive." Expertise is simply a developed will to receive using one sense in a special way.
- Each sense has a limited range. Instruments can widen the bandwidth (infra, ultra), but perceiving *directly* in the will to receive, without the senses, "is a specific, special method."
- Our problem: we have no **records** of the upper pleasures. "We don't feel them so as to teach ourselves... to want them. And if we don't want them, then we don't perceive them."

#### Krupnik's cat

> We wrote down the melodies of Baal HaSulam... We would cry and get excited... And a cat next to us, asleep, doesn't feel anything... He hears, but he doesn't feel.

- That's our situation: "Right now, I'm in spirituality, in Ein Sof. I just don't perceive."
- **The deficiency has to come first.** Developing a deficiency to perceive beyond the senses is possible "only by acquiring a special vessel called **faith**," because a sense works only when it has equivalence of form with what comes to it: in order to bestow.
- This is the difficulty of understanding **Purim**, how the light of Mordechai fills the vessels of Haman, and the article's distinction between a **gift** (no prior vessels needed) and **buying/obtaining**: "That's not how we achieve the goal."

## Part 2 — The Long Time of Preparation

- We come to Kabbalah because we were "somehow ready," having gone through part of the path unconsciously in previous life cycles. Now we start doing it consciously, but "we still have a lot to grow."
- **First stage, the recognition of evil:** recognizing that I perceive the world only "for my own good," in egoistic calculation, until the corporeal will to receive is fully processed. "This takes a long time."
- **Second stage, preparation under concealment:** a person starts to worry that he has no faith. Before, these ideas were "very abstract." Now he feels confined, "under such quarantine" in five senses, feels the pressure of the surrounding light, and a new desire opens: to be "detached from that calculation at last" (not from this world).
- This needs **habit**, as Baal HaSulam writes; "you can't absorb these things at once."

#### Don't trust your senses

- We can't shorten this time "in a controlled way where we know exactly what we do." Therefore, "whatever your ability allows you, you shall do."
- These stages are not lowly: "you'll be working with the vessels that you now open up" until the end of correction. "Don't disregard them."
- Progress may be fast "in a depth that we don't feel." Thousands of discernments a day may pass unnoticed while I count two or three. "So, you understand, don't trust your senses. We have no measurement here."
- Why? Because "you can't give the will to receive any reward," or you'd tie yourself to phenomena of the will to receive rather than of the vessel of faith.

## Part 3 — Bestowing to Bestow vs. Receiving to Bestow

- To bestow, I connect my will to receive to **someone else's lack**: a friend, the group, or the Creator, whom I imagine as having a will to receive so I can care about His pleasure. "I live within that external lack."
- The example is a mother with a baby: "She doesn't care about herself at all. Only for him."

| | Bestowing in order to bestow | Receiving in order to bestow |
|---|---|---|
| **Pleasure** | "I feel the pleasures in Him... I'm entirely in him" | "I can only give to the extent that I feel the pleasure" |
| **Vessels** | Coarseness levels root, one, two (vessels of bestowal) | Coarseness levels three and four (vessels of reception) |
| **Connection** | My will doesn't participate; I only care for him | "A doubled measure of connection": two desires joined as a single lack |
| **Limit** | Simple; no vessels or lacks revealed | "The vessel of reception is unlimited" |

- The degree of **Bina** depends on how much I discover the other as the object of my bestowal.
- Rabash describes a person "only within his wisdom" who doesn't eat, drink, or sleep. Not literally: necessities simply aren't part of his concerns. "Necessity is not to be praised nor denounced."

## Part 4 — No Diets, No Monks: Restriction Only Feeds the Ego

- **Q: Must the path to bestowal pass through abstinence, like a monk?** "We won't become confused with... fasts and all sorts of diets... or wearing only white." (He recalls a student in white garb, who then got a white coat for winter.)
- Behind all the electrochemistry of the body stands the desire, "a spiritual entity." Attaining bestowal "has nothing to do with" eating or sleeping less: "Eat double, sleep double, drink double." Kabbalists, by the way, often consume more.
- External refinement of the body may have worked "when the will to receive was only on the degrees of bare necessity," like a villager who can't imagine wanting more than a cup of rice. "In our times, it doesn't exist at all."
- **The sugar example:** Rav Laitman, being diabetic, got used to not eating sweets until sweetness felt bad. "Habit becomes a second nature. But how do I become more righteous by that?" Not eating the cracker feels like profit, "so it's the opposite, the will to receive enjoys it more."

> No direct limitation of the will to receive can ever be good for the actual correction of the desire. Because... if you limit it, explain to it that it profits more by that.

- "We're not capable of doing a restriction." If I shut my mouth, it's because I've found a greater compensation.
- Such methods are "actually harmful to a Kabbalist": he cuts off most of the desire, yearns for spirituality with a small part, and thinks he's righteous, "but he didn't correct the desire." Modesty and humility are fine "for the general folk... as a symbol," but "our correction must be internal only."
- A person who starts studying Kabbalah usually finds "all his inclinations, desires, even grow."

## Part 5 — The Only Method: Aim the Whole Desire and Draw the Light

- Telling myself Kabbalah will make me healthier, know the future, and alter my fate is fine for the will to receive. That's even how to speak at lectures: "This life is garbage. The life in the upper world is much better." But that alone is no correction.
- The correction "happens to us by itself without our intervention through the reforming light," which turns phase one of the four phases of direct light into phase two, its opposite. "It's only the light who does that... I only need to cause the light to come to me."
- **Surrounding light** is drawn not by a screen but "only by the desire." It doesn't enter me, but it refines my vessels. As the Introduction to the Study of the Ten Sefirot says, the light in the books of Kabbalah is greater.
- Why doesn't everyone who opens the book reach it? "It depends on the direction that we aim our hearts" toward the source of that light.
- So I organize a **group** that tells me this is "the greatest reward... the best lottery earning possible." Then the light corrects me with greater force: "I am lovesick," until it doesn't let me sleep (Baal HaSulam's letters, p. 70).
- **Don't disperse the desire**: not to sex, food, money, honor, or knowledge. "Knowledge is a problem" when one studies just to know. "Desire remains desire, pleasure remains pleasure. Only I replaced all the clothings of this world."
- Gradually the light brings a "grace of holiness," a split in the desire, and then "a miracle... the exodus from Egypt. An inversion." We need only be "ready in the middle of the night to run away from Egypt."

#### Love begins with two calculations

- "I love him because it's good for me with him" and "there's something in him that I love." In that second point the love of bestowal itself begins, "born in us" as a miracle: we cause it only indirectly.

#### Isn't the group a limitation too?

- When the society gives me different values and I have no time left for food or sleep, I am limiting myself. But "it's not that I'm working in a method that I'm preventing from myself something."

## Part 6 — Souls Like a Pyramid, and the Law of Passing It On

- **Q: Will the whole world need such labor?** Like science, a few souls toil "in a little laboratory of ten people" while 99% use the fruits. Souls are "like a pyramid... It's all one body. And no part is redundant."
- Whoever reaches the goal and fills his vessel discovers his **uniqueness**, because only he can connect to his root. Then all differences disappear, "like the light of Ein Sof in a circle."
- **"If I don't give, I can't receive."** Lights enter vessels in inverse order: Nefesh enters Keter; build another Kli and Nefesh descends to Chochma while Ruach enters Keter. You must be a **transition** onward, to dissemination, the friends, or the Creator.
- Without a screen, only "beastly pleasures" enter. Dissemination is the spiritual law: "I am the Creator's distributor... I'm besides him towards the whole world."
- We are "the first generation engaged in this." From above, the world is being arranged to feel "pathless," so people will want it before we arrive, "half a word."

## Part 7 — Q&A: Envying the Righteous

- **Q: Shouldn't I want spirituality instead of a bigger house and car, like righteous people who lived in a room and a half?** "You're choosing a greater pleasure. So what do you have to be sorry about?"
- **Football vs. professor:** if you'd rather be a professor, go into a society (the open university) that equips you with that desire, until "you'll throw your backgammon and your football" and sit on the books.
- **Weight Watchers:** "You can't do this on your own." So I pay to join a society that lectures me on why being fat is bad, and members call each other during the day. "It's not against the desire to receive... it's simply finding a greater pleasure."
- **Envying the righteous:** "What are you looking at the righteous and you're wanting to be in his place?" Take his room and a half, his robe, his corner. "He enjoys it, and you enjoy yours." A man devoted to pigeons, another to politics: "each and his engagements."
- A question from America: why do lessons make me eat more? Rav Laitman answers with humor. The desires grow.

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **Perception depends on the will to receive, not the senses.** We're already in Ein Sof but lack the desire and records to feel it, like the sleeping cat.
2. **The vessel for spirituality is faith,** equivalence of form in bestowal, and the deficiency for it has to come first.
3. **The time of preparation can't be measured or shortened.** Do whatever you can and "don't trust your senses."
4. **Restriction never corrects the desire.** Diets, abstinence, and external modesty only give the will to receive a better compensation and can harm a Kabbalist.
5. **Aim the whole desire at one goal** with a group's help; the surrounding light, not our efforts, inverts it.
6. **Receiving in order to bestow is the higher, unlimited vessel,** joining my pleasure and His as one lack.
7. **What we don't pass on, we can't receive.** Dissemination makes us the Creator's distributors.

## Key Terms & Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Kli (Vessel)** | The desire needed for any attainment; spirituality requires the vessel of faith. |
| **Records (Reshimot)** | Impressions of past states; without records of upper pleasures we can't want them. |
| **Faith** | A vessel of equivalence of form, in order to bestow, needed to perceive beyond the senses. |
| **Recognition of Evil** | The first stage: recognizing that I perceive everything only for my own good. |
| **Time of Preparation** | A long, unmeasurable period under concealment in which the need for faith ripens. |
| **Bestowing in Order to Bestow** | Living in another's lack, like a mother with a baby; works in coarseness root, one, two. |
| **Receiving in Order to Bestow** | Feeling pleasure so the Creator enjoys; coarseness three and four; an unlimited vessel. |
| **Reforming Light / Surrounding Light** | Light drawn by desire alone, not a screen, which refines the vessels and inverts them. |
| **Lovesick** | Aiming the whole will to receive at one goal "until it doesn't let me sleep." |
| **Exodus from Egypt** | The miracle of inversion, receiving a different nature, which doesn't depend on us. |
| **Inverse Order of Lights** | Nefesh first enters Keter, then descends as new vessels are built; we grow by passing light on. |
