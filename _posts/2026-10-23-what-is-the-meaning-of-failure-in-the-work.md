### Opening

If everything comes from the Creator, what is a "failure" at all? Rav Laitman opens with a striking answer: "The truth is that there are no failures at all." Every fall is a covering a person must pass through so that a revelation can appear in its place, and at the end he sees that the whole path, concealments included, came from eternal love. From there the lesson moves through the throne that is built out of coverings, the individual and general end of correction, why Adam HaRishon's "sin" was not really an error, why the shattering gives the creature its independence, and how a person keeps himself changing every moment through the society.

## Part 1 — There Are No Failures

- Everything "returns to its root," leaving only an **impression**, and that impression helps bring about correction, now or later.
- In the final state, when a person attains the Creator's eternal and perfect love for him, he sees that the Creator not only created him but planned his entire path, carried him through it, opened his eyes, and placed him in concealment along the way.
- Everything came from one point, "both the first and the last": **the good who does good** to the wicked and to the righteous, without any calculation concerning the person himself.

> There were never any failures, never any descents, never any blows of any kind. Everything was solely out of eternal love.

#### Failures are coverings

- "Failures are a matter of coverings that a person must pass through. Otherwise he would not feel the revelations."
- The Creator has to take a person through all these events, because the revelation is built on them.

## Part 2 — Two Kinds of Revelation

#### Individual revelation

- Revelation must be personal, so that each one can point with his finger and say, "Behold, this is our God."
- Each person reveals the Creator according to **his own vessels**, from the unique combination of desires and yearnings that exist only in him. No one else has the same vessels.

#### Up to the throne of glory

- The revelation must also reach "up to the throne of glory." **Kise** (throne) comes from **Kisui** (covering): all the coverings, all the concealments, are transformed into the glory of the Creator, the highest degree in the sensation of Him.
- **Q: What is the difference between the throne and the king's throne of glory?** The covering itself must be transformed into the throne of glory, "a covering that becomes glory until we come to recognize the King Himself."
- "Great is repentance, for it reaches up to the throne of glory" means the return must be so great that *all* the coverings become a recognition of the Creator's greatness.

#### Starting with the society

- While we don't yet feel the importance of the Creator, which would give us fuel, we create something like **publicity** about it for one another, "publicizing the miracle."
- "Even when we don't know what something is or what importance it has, if everyone says it's important, it becomes important in our eyes, and from this we receive fuel."
- Through this we also connect, join each person's desires with the others', and annul the ego. "Through a single action we receive many results."
- The beginning of the path: "we need to be impressed by the greatness of the Creator through one another," even though no one sees or knows.

## Part 3 — Individual and General End of Correction

| | Individual end of correction | General end of correction |
|---|---|---|
| **Who** | One person, through his own work over the 6,000 years | Every human being on earth, in full awareness |
| **The others** | Raised only to the extent they are incorporated in him; in themselves they may be at any level, even below the barrier | Each soul is corrected in practice |
| **Where** | Attained by Kabbalists; "the aspiration to reach that degree" | In souls still dressed in bodies: bringing the degree of **Atzilut** down to this world |

- In my individual end of correction, I reach connection with all the souls and pull them up "to the extent that they can be included in me through my strength," but "not that they have incorporated me in themselves."
- The general end of correction is "the glory of the Creator shall fill the earth." The still, vegetative and animate levels are included too, not consciously, since they have no screens or vessels, but through their inclusion in man.
- There are further distinctions at higher degrees, such as between **Rabbi Shimon** and his son **Rabbi Elazar**, "we'll talk about that another time."

## Part 4 — Recognition of Evil in Mind and Heart

- **Q: What is the recognition of evil in the mind and in the heart?** These are two kinds of spiritual vessels that open to a person, in revelation and also in the recognition of evil.
- "There's something that you feel, and in addition there's something through which you understand the reason for what you feel." Feeling is the **heart**; understanding the reason is the **mind**.
- Rav Laitman added: "It's not a perfect explanation."
- (In passing, he asked a student sitting far away to come closer: sitting off to the side and dozing "is not called sitting among the students.")

## Part 5 — Adam HaRishon as "Min"

- **Q: What does it mean that the sages called Adam HaRishon a "Min" (heretic)?** A Min pursues seemingly beastly lust, but "actually it's not beastly. It means in order to receive." The greatest of these desires is the sexual one.
- This exists only in human beings. Animals act "according to a program," mating at set times. At the speaking level it is "completely unrestrained," and this was made for a reason.
- Union in this world is not opposite to union with the Creator but "in resemblance," only at the lowest, and so the strongest, degree.
- Adam "received desires at the lowest degree." Corrections begin precisely there, after all the degrees descend through the shattering down to the desire to receive in order to receive.

#### Don't fight the desires

- In corporeality "we should not concern ourselves with it." Correction comes only through the **Reforming Light**: aspire to the force from above that will come and correct me, not to correcting the desires themselves.
- "Someone who has one desire and begins trying to break it ends up with two."
- "We should not struggle against the desires, but rather against the Creator's concealment." Where a person's thoughts are, there he is; fighting the desires keeps him in thoughts of sex, money and honor.
- "Let the light draw me out of them. That should be the intention, the direction, the desire, and the hope."

## Part 6 — Why the Shattering Gives Independence

- **Q: If the purpose is to reach the intention to bestow, why was the shattering specifically over it?** The desire to receive is "simply a sense that enjoys the Creator's presence." Good and bad come only from revelation or concealment.
- Such a desire is like a flower that opens to the sun or closes when there is no sun. For it to draw the sun toward itself and decide when it will shine, it must be given more: the ability to rise above the desire and become the one performing the action.
- Through the shattering it receives the Creator's quality, **bestowal**. Then, if it wants to bestow and opens itself, it feels the light "even if the light is absent," because "the desire to bestow itself becomes light."
- It is no longer dependent on what happens from the Creator's side: "**My sons have defeated me.**" It is free of the Creator and of its own desire to receive, "which is actually the same thing."
- The Creator instilled a desire to bestow equal in measure to the desire to receive. Using the desire to receive in order to give, it becomes a source of light like the Creator: "**the two great luminaries**."
- Both the desire to receive and the sparks of bestowal come from above, "but beyond that... all the work, so to speak, is its own."

## Part 7 — Adam's Sin Was Not an Error

- **Q: Didn't Adam have the intention to bestow?** "Adam HaRishon certainly had the intention to bestow." He was in the vessels of bestowal, **Galgalta veEinayim**, bestowing in order to bestow, "like an angel," with no evil inclination, in the Garden of Eden in **Atzilut**, feeling no shame though naked, because there were no vessels of reception.
- He used everything except one thing: the **tree of knowledge**, the vessels of reception (**AHP**) below the **Tabur**. And then he discovered that using precisely those vessels, in order to bestow, was the purpose of creation.

#### The serpent's argument

- The **serpent** is the inclination that tells him: use this and you will give the Creator the greatest pleasure, receiving in order to bestow with your whole vessel, adhering to Him completely. "What do you have now compared to this great thing? Nothing."
- "All these claims are one hundred percent correct. It's impossible to contest them at all." Yet there was a prohibition.
- It seems to the person that the Creator purposely forbids these vessels because He wants him to come to the decision to use them, but only when he feels his action will truly be in order to bestow. "This is full of subtleties."

#### The heart of stone

- From Adam's side "everything was one hundred percent correct." His mistake was that he could never have calculated that the **heart of stone** (Lev HaEven) cannot mix with Galgalta veEinayim. Before it is clarified, "it's impossible to calculate anything regarding it."
- As an outcome, he gets discernments between the heart of stone and all the other qualities, and scrutinizes it, "the main, the primary scrutiny," seeing how his whole nature is opposite to the Creator. For that he receives the end of correction.
- Rav Laitman closed: "I don't have the words for this yet. Perhaps we need to discuss it more."

## Part 8 — Can Unity Be Shown by Example?

- **Q: Could you give practical examples of how to perform a unity?** "I have no examples of spiritual actions." An example takes something that exists in reality and demonstrates it on a smaller scale. "We do not have the will to bestow, we do not have actions performed by a screen."
- A blessing before drinking, said exactly as in the **Shulchan Aruch**, is an action but not a spiritual one. A spiritual action would mean recognizing the pleasure in the drink and having, opposite it, a screen and reflected light, by which I receive adhesion to the Creator.
- Taking the **Lulav** corresponds to great things, Zeir Anpin of Atzilut in its Gadlut, at the time of unification. But to an onlooker it looks like an ancient people waving a plant in ritual. "You can explain it as much as you want, but nobody understands."
- The only way to learn: "If you with your will can connect to my will and be present passively within my will as I perform the action, from that you will learn." This is the upper one teaching the lower, the NHY of Ima spreading into Zeir Anpin.
- Learning with a teacher "is called connection." **Unity** is only with the Creator, when you do it on your own afterward. For a student who isn't ready, it isn't recommended.

## Part 9 — Turning to the Creator and the Force of the Light

- **Q: Can a person turn to the Creator too quickly, to escape suffering, without scrutinizing his state?** "When you have the opportunity to address Him, address Him." Don't wait to suffer more for a truer cry; "you won't have a truer cry."
- "It's the light that scrutinizes the vessel. And you yourself will only ever eat your own flesh." From the light come the vessel's growth, the recognition of evil, the appeal upward and the recognition of greatness.
- Not turning to the light is "sitting within the will," feeling what the will feels with no benefit. "Don't hold back from turning to the Creator... always pray."
- **Q: What does it mean that a student is not ready?** Turning comes when there is truly a great, complete need, and then the cry comes. That need depends on the extent to which the society, the friends and the books provide it. "Us ourselves on our own, this will never happen," only a collective attack against the disruptions.
- **Q: What does the force of light depend on?** "Only necessity": the measure to which a person feels a need for correction and how scrutinized the request is within him.
- **Q: What does "your eyes will open" mean?** It refers to Adam and Hava. Everything opened up for them, using both in order to bestow and in order to receive. So it opens for one who comes through the recognition of evil, and the qualities of the created being and the Creator connect.

## Part 10 — When Nothing Seems to Change

- **Q: If I feel no change for a week or two, what can I do?** A person should feel he is changing every moment. A moment passing is a sign of a change from above; if nothing changed below, "that is already a problem." "If the clock is ticking but nothing is ticking inside," a blow is coming: "the hammer is already starting to rise behind you."
- First, **raise the threshold of sensitivity**: how did a few moments pass without thinking of the goal?
- Writing it down won't help. "Write on the board of your heart... The paper doesn't need repentance. It doesn't care."
- Build "a circle, a mechanism around you" that wakes you to the thought every moment, like an alarm clock. He recalled writing a note on his hand while driving with **Rabash** and not seeing it for hours. "Without the desire you don't see."

#### The society instead of blows

- Awakening the desire is in the Creator's hands, or instead of the Creator, the society. "That is the only thing other than the Creator that can work."
- An awakening from above always comes in an unpleasant way, to leave you free choice, and "you're forced to pay for it later with a very high interest rate. So it's not worthwhile."
- Invest in the society and they will work on you in the same measure. You connect, absorb desire from them, subjugate yourself to them, and they awaken you. Several corrections happen at once.
- The friends "aren't others really. They become a part of your soul through this." My soul needs to be built of all the parts around me.

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **There are no failures.** Every fall is a covering a person must pass through; in the end he sees the whole path came from the Creator's eternal love.
2. **The covering becomes the throne.** Kise comes from Kisui: true repentance turns every concealment into a recognition of the Creator's glory.
3. **Two ends of correction.** The individual one raises others only as far as they are included in me; the general one brings Atzilut down into every person on earth.
4. **Don't fight the desires; ask for the light.** Breaking a desire doubles it. The struggle is against the Creator's concealment, and correction comes from the Reforming Light.
5. **The shattering grants independence.** By receiving the quality of bestowal, the creature can generate light itself: "My sons have defeated me."
6. **Adam's sin was a necessary mistake.** His intention was correct; the heart of stone could not be calculated in advance, and its scrutiny leads to the end of correction.
7. **Change every moment, through the society.** Turn to the Creator at every opportunity and build a mechanism of friends that awakens you, rather than waiting for blows from above.
