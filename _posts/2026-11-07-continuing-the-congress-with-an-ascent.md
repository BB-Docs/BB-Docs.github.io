### Opening

The Congress is over. We received an awakening from the many friends around us and, as Rav Laitman puts it, "we made a new beginning" in building the Kli of the end of correction. But a Congress is not just a Congress: it has to be continued anew each and every day, without the external awakening that carried us. How do we hold on to the connection we felt? Rav Laitman's answer runs through the whole lesson: by noticing our very first thought on waking, by being glad of every disturbance and disconnection, and by understanding that our connection with the ten sets both the pace and the shape of our advancement.

## Part 1 — Waking Up "As If from the Dead"

#### Rabash in the morning

- Rav Laitman describes how Rabash would get up "like someone who has no idea where he is." He would wash, sit at the table, barely sip the coffee Rav served him, and only after ten or fifteen minutes would "the Adam, the human, begin to awaken in him" and the spirit of life return.
- This happened every day, at home and in the hospital. "I was amazed at how a person can get up like a beast... disconnected from life, even corporeal life."
- From this he learned the meaning of "they should be new in your eyes each and every day":

> Each day you will start as if from dead. And that way you will awaken forcefully, and continue forcefully to awaken yourself. Why did I get up? For what purpose?

#### The first thought

- A mother sleeping next to her baby's cradle hears him, is concerned about him, and her first thought on waking is about him. This comes naturally, "otherwise we could not continue the generations."
- In the same way, we should check on waking: is my first thought about the Creator, or about me and what I need to do in life?
- "Thinking about giving contentment to the Creator is what wakes him up from his sleep." That is already a good sign: the thought is spiritual, about the will to bestow rather than the will to receive.
- **Practical guideline:** all of us, with friends all over the world, should monitor how we wake up and how we want to continue our day.

#### The thoughts we fall asleep with

- After Baal HaSulam's Letter 18 (sanctify the first moment with **Dvekut**), Rav Laitman adds that the thoughts we fall asleep with matter. They give us a better chance of going through the night with good thoughts subconsciously and waking with the correct thought about the Creator.
- **Q: Reading an excerpt at night doesn't guarantee waking with thoughts of the Creator.** Not necessarily, he agrees. But it is well known as "asking a question in the dream": think about something a long time, especially before sleep, because in sleep we are freed from many images and disturbances of this world and solutions often appear on waking.
- "It is simple psychology. There's nothing spiritual here." What matters is not whether the waking thought is good or not, but that a person belongs to this direction.

## Part 2 — Holding the New Kli "Forcefully"

- The Kli that began to grow between us at the Congress must not be lost; we must hold it "forcefully, constantly."
- Why forcefully? "Because when it grows, then it always grows through **Aviut**, coarseness. Always through alien thoughts, always through disturbances, always."
- So "if there are no disturbances we cannot advance." We should be happy about the disturbances and about states opposite to spirituality, and get used to the fact that "all of our work is under pressure."
- Each time new, corrupted desires awaken, we add them from the **Klipa** to **Kedusha**. Penny by penny it accumulates into a great sum, until we see we already have forces that hold us in Kedusha opposite the Klipa.
- "One who is greater than his friend, his inclination is greater": the descents get bigger, and so do the ascents.

> I'm happy about those disconnections from in order to bestow, because these are signs of progress... I was rewarded with the fact that now they're revealing to me another great part of the broken Kli, so that I will correct it.

#### Whether we succeed or not

- **Q: The conclusion of the Congress seems to be bringing contentment to the Creator, expanding the Kli through efforts, love of friends and dissemination, holding on to both the good and the bad. Is that right?** "That is true." Both where we overcome the descent and where we don't.
- Where we don't overcome, "we immediately turn to the Creator to help us." Either we overcame and ascribe it to the Creator, or we didn't and through prayer we ascribe it to Him.
- More correct still is to ask, "because by that we give contentment": pray that He arrange our thoughts and desires so they all aim toward connection, through the ten, toward Him.
- And if even that fails, the mind can't focus and the heart is impervious, "that's even better." It is a sign that the Creator wants me to turn to Him strongly. "We are never rejected." These are not obstructions but a way into "multifaceted, stronger contact" with the Creator. We need to become "masters of this work."

## Part 3 — Why Everything Is Built in the Ten

- Rav Laitman notes that Kabbalists usually don't talk about the ten, because it is obvious to them that the whole Torah exists only to create a connection, a Kli.
- "None of us is a Kli." Each one's desire is a single point. Many points must connect into a complex desire that requires correction, tied together like a body through love, through **Arvut**.
- Then it can resemble the Creator, be "Adam," and the Creator is revealed in the connection between two forces: the rejecting force of the ego between the desires, and the force of connection above it.
- In the wisdom of Kabbalah we gradually speak of desires, not people, "because our bodies are not in spirituality."
- Each one should pray for the ten, that it be built from opposite desires, more opposite each time, "a stronger Kli specifically," until the **Shechina** appears among us as the place for the revelation of the upper light, the force of bestowal and love.

## Part 4 — The Pace and Shape of Our Progress

- **Q: How do these awakenings determine the pace and shape of our progress?** "The upper light is constant. And its goal is to correct only what we raise to it. It all depends on us." This is "**My sons have defeated me**."
- The more we awaken each other, don't fall asleep, and raise demands each moment, the more we advance. "Whether we think in the right way or not... what's important is just that we keep the direction towards the Creator." To the extent we care about the pace of raising **MAN**, we advance.

#### The layer of new souls

- Everyone on this degree in this world is interconnected across the planet, and this layer is "most important in the eyes of the Creator."
- The new generation, "babies in spirituality," are the most important, because through them we push forward the great souls who have already corrected themselves and pass the lights down to the lower ones. "We actually raise MAN through them to the Creator."
- Above humanity's ordinary desires, if we connect and raise ourselves to the Creator, "we'll sanctify the whole planet."

#### In a bubble of upper lights

- Rav Laitman recounts asking the doctors how the Congress went medically: with some five thousand people there, there was "barely someone sneezing... nothing happened."
- "The main thing is to see how we're in a bubble of the upper lights, and it's holding us just like a mother is holding a baby." We should feel that the light constantly cares for us and nurtures us, wherever we are.
- He asks that all of Bnei Baruch act this way toward the Creator: raising the mind, giving gratitude, making requests, thinking of all the friends wherever they are. "This we've learned is called Arvut."

## Part 5 — Descents, Ascents and the Connection with the Ten

- After Rabash's article on beginning each day anew with the kingdom of heaven, Rav Laitman explains: "This is how we are being lowered into the broken Kli." We feel a part of it, then must raise it, "connect it to the ten and through it to the Creator. This is the correction and this is how all the corrections are."
- The Kelim differ in quantity, quality and degree, "but all in all, the process is the same": either a descent to absorb the broken Kli, or an ascent to correct it.
- **If we feel no change**, it is a sign we must integrate with the other Kelim. With a connection to them, we start to move; without it, "we can be in that state until there would be a connection."
- So the descents and ascents don't depend on us directly, "but only through the degree of my connection with the ten." By increasing it, "we can awaken the pace of our advancement."

> Therefore we should only think about that. How can I be with the ten in greater connection, and all the rest will happen accordingly. The upper light will influence me.

- **Workshop question:** How much does my connection with the ten determine the pace and manner of my advancement? (Friends answered: "100%." The ten is what holds a person, gives him thoughts about spirituality and the Creator, and brings him closer.)

## Part 6 — Workshops: Making Room to Overcome

After Rabash's excerpt on overcoming ("each penny joins into a great amount"), Rav Laitman posed two questions for the tens.

#### How are we happy to have room to overcome?

- Specifically when we don't want it and still invest effort, and that effort is "in the ten, to get into them and get from them the force of connection" and Arvut. That way, as we advance, we give contentment to the Creator.

#### How do we shape the place?

- So that in every state, without cessation, we are aimed at the ten and through the ten at the Creator, giving Him contentment through the friends, "and that way establish the Kli where gradually the entire light will reside."

#### Points raised by the friends

| Theme | Insight from the workshop |
|---|---|
| Balance in the ten | Each gives the maximum and takes only the minimum needed to stay awake; those with more strength give to those who need more. |
| Making room | First make room within me "not for myself but for the friends," and hold the three together: myself, the ten and the Creator. |
| Gratitude first | Begin every state with gratitude for having been brought to this at all, and always feel "I can add a bit more." |
| Greatness of the friends | Pray to see the friends as the greatest of the generation and myself as the smallest, since the ego can't see them that way. |
| Non-stop work | Like making matzah, work without pause; the moment we rest, the importance of externality rises and we fall. |
| After the Congress | The Congress gives new coarseness and new vessels; only the connection prepared in advance lets us rise and work with them. |

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **Every day is a new beginning.** The Congress must be continued anew each day, starting "as if from dead" and awakening forcefully.
2. **Check the first thought.** Like a mother whose first thought is her baby, notice whether we wake up thinking of the Creator; the thoughts we fall asleep with help.
3. **Disturbances are the material of the Kli.** It grows only through coarseness and foreign thoughts, so we should be glad of disconnections as signs of progress.
4. **Never rejected.** Whether we overcome or fail, turning to the Creator in prayer gives Him contentment; failure is a call to stronger contact.
5. **The light is constant; it all depends on us.** It corrects only what we raise, so the pace of raising MAN sets the pace of advancement.
6. **The ten sets the pace and shape.** Descents absorb the broken Kli and ascents correct it, and both move only to the degree of our connection with the ten.
7. **We work for the whole world.** Our layer of new souls raises MAN for all humanity and can sanctify the whole planet.

| **Aviut (Coarseness)** | The growing will to receive through which the Kli always grows, appearing as foreign thoughts and disturbances. |
| **Klipa / Kedusha** | Shell and holiness; each corrupted desire that awakens is added from the Klipa to Kedusha. |
| **Dvekut (Adhesion)** | Connection with the Creator; Baal HaSulam advises sanctifying the first waking moment with it. |
| **Arvut (Mutual Guarantee)** | The connection of all the friends worldwide acting together toward the Creator. |
| **Ten** | The basic unit where the broken Kli is raised and corrected; its connection determines the pace and manner of progress. |
| **MAN (Mayin Nukvin)** | The request raised from below; the light corrects only what we raise to it. |
| **My Sons Have Defeated Me** | The principle that the upper light is constant and advancement depends on the demands of the lower ones. |
| **Shechina (Divinity)** | The place among the friends where the Creator is revealed as the force of bestowal and love. |
| **Babies in spirituality** | The new generation of souls, most important to the Creator, through whom MAN rises to the great souls and to Him. |
| **Descent / Ascent** | Being lowered to absorb a part of the broken Kli, then raising it through the ten to the Creator. |
