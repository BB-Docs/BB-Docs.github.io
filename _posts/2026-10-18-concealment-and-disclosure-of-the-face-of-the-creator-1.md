### Opening

Baal HaSulam describes the same person seeing a completely different world depending on his degree: in concealment, the righteous seem miserable and the wicked seem to prosper; in disclosure, it is the reverse. Does the world itself change? Rav Laitman's answer is that nothing outside changes at all. "The world follows its customary course." What changes is the person's inner vessel of perception, and everything he reveals of the Creator is the measure of his own correction.

## Part 1 — The Same World, Seen from Inside

#### Actions are read by their inner content

- We never interpret the actions of our world by the actions themselves, but "according to the inner content within them."
- When an action reveals an inner content directed at the Creator, it looks completely different: "Instead of corruption, correction. Instead of bad, good. And instead of illness, we see health."
- "We are feeling beings." If a good reason is revealed in an action instead of a bad one, the whole act turns from bad to good.

#### Two history books

> Take a history textbook from each of two countries that once fought each other, about the same war. Each describes it in the complete opposite way, and "neither of them is lying." Each sees it one way and cannot understand how it could be seen otherwise.

- In the same house, the same job, the same people, a person can see that very same world as **Ein Sof**, "filled with abundance, spirituality, eternity and perfection."
- "That's why I sometimes emphasize that it's a psychological matter. Meaning that it depends on our inner perception."
- He limits the discussion to the attaining person during his lifetime in this world, not to how reality appears to someone who has left the body. That is "a somewhat different subject."

## Part 2 — The Law of Equivalence

- **Q: So the whole issue is discovering that the Creator has a purpose for me?** Rav Laitman: there is nothing to disclose in my state except the Creator's attitude toward me, and it is revealed in my corrected vessels "according to how much they resemble His vessels."
- This is the **law of similarity**, or equivalence, and we see it everywhere in this world: to feel a color, sound, heat or cold, I need sensors inside me that correspond to it.
- A thermometer must be built to perceive heat within a certain range. To understand another person's thoughts, "I need to go through the same things." To the extent I have the same part as him, I perceive him. "We have nothing besides this."

#### Building the Creator's model inside me

- Correcting the vessels means "to create within myself the same model as what is outside, as the Creator." That is **equivalence of form**.
- I reveal His providence because "I actually reveal the correction of my vessels that I've made."
- "I have built the Creator's part within me... You could say, perhaps in a certain sense... I have become the Creator," because the same laws and system are now inside me.

> "The whole difficulty in understanding the wisdom of Kabbalah lies precisely in its simplicity."

#### Inner work

- "I HaVaYaH have not changed." Nothing changes except the person's vessel of perception. "We do not need to change anything outside. We do not need to run anywhere. Concentrate inwardly."
- That is why it is called inner work, the **internality of the Torah**, and why Kabbalists seem to give no importance to externality, to external actions and commandments: the entire correction is the correction of the inner vessels "where I reveal godliness."

## Part 3 — Good to the Wicked and to the Good

- **Q: If the Creator is good to the wicked and to the good, why do those not on the path suffer?** "Good to the wicked and to the good" is what a person who has corrected himself reveals, according to the degree of his correction.
- One who is still in evil "feels the Creator as evil and cannot justify Him."
- Seeing the Creator as good to everyone alike "is a very high degree of the revelation of providence. It's the world of **Atzilut**, and also part of the world of **Briya**."

#### How a person comes to disclosure

- Strengthening in faith that the Creator watches over the world leads him to the books, and from there he receives the **light of correction**.
- When his efforts accumulate enough for the light to affect him, he becomes ready for providence in a state of revelation, and the Creator is revealed as the **Good Who Does Good**.

## Part 4 — Concealment and Disclosure Are the Degrees of the Worlds

- Rav Laitman points to the Introduction to TES, where Baal HaSulam connects double concealment, single concealment, revelation (reward and punishment), and eternal love with the four worlds **Asiya, Yetzira, Briya, and Atzilut**. There "it becomes very, very simple and clear what the worlds actually are."
- "All we do is correct within ourselves a little more, a more internal layer of our vessel." The deeper the correction, the greater our **reflected light**, and the greater our revelation of the Creator's actions upon us.
- "That is all. There is nothing else. This is called **From Your Actions We Know You**." All of it takes place within one person's corrected vessels.
- Every degree has two sides of the same vessel: the worlds "from the perspective of concealment," and, as I correct my vessel through the **screen**, revelation.

#### What are "good deeds"?

- **Q: If external actions have no meaning, what does it mean that his success grows when he performs many good deeds?** Since we evaluate all actions according to the goal, "good actions are those through which I want to reach equivalence of form with the Creator. And bad actions are all those that do not pertain to this."

#### The TES passage (items 149–150)

| Degree of providence | World | As described |
|---|---|---|
| First and double concealment | **Asiya** | Mostly bad; the little good is mixed in and unrecognizable |
| First revelation (conditional love, repentance from fear) | **Yetzira** | Half good, half bad |
| Second and third discernments of love | **Briya** | Mostly good; a minority of indiscernible bad |
| Fourth discernment: unconditional, eternal love | **Atzilut** | No evil whatsoever; absolute revelation of the face |

- Closing the reading, the group noted that this passage can be added to the article, since all the worlds "are revealed within the person... nothing more than the revelation of the Creator's attitude toward us according to our corrected internal vessels."

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **The world doesn't change; perception does.** "The world follows its customary course." Only the person's vessel of perception is corrected.
2. **We read every action by its inner content.** When the purpose toward the Creator is revealed, bad turns to good, illness to health.
3. **The law of equivalence:** I perceive only what I have built a likeness of inside me, like a sensor, a thermometer, or understanding another person.
4. **Revealing providence is revealing my own correction,** building the Creator's model within me.
5. **"Good to the wicked and to the good" is a high degree,** Atzilut and part of Briya, revealed only in corrected vessels.
6. **The worlds are degrees of concealment and disclosure inside a person:** Asiya, Yetzira, Briya, Atzilut.
7. **Good deeds are measured by the goal:** whatever moves me toward equivalence of form.

## Key Terms & Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Concealment of the Face** | Providence felt as not good; in single concealment the person still attributes it to the Creator, in double concealment he doesn't see even that. Both are the world of Asiya. |
| **Disclosure of the Face** | Providence revealed as the Good Who Does Good, reached through the light of correction drawn from the books. |
| **The World Follows Its Customary Course** | Nothing outside changes; only the person's vessel of perception changes. |
| **Inner Content** | The purpose behind an action, by which we actually interpret it. |
| **Law of Equivalence (Similarity)** | One perceives something only to the extent he has a corresponding part inside himself. |
| **Equivalence of Form** | Correcting the inner vessels until they resemble the Creator, building His "model" within me. |
| **Good Who Does Good** | The Creator as revealed to a corrected person: good to the wicked and to the good alike. |
| **Light of Correction** | The light received from the books that readies a person for providence in revelation. |
| **From Your Actions We Know You** | We know the Creator only through His actions as revealed in our corrected vessels. |
| **Four Worlds (ABYA)** | Atzilut, Briya, Yetzira, Asiya: the degrees of the Creator's revealed attitude, from mostly bad to no evil at all. |
| **Good Deeds** | Actions aimed at reaching equivalence of form with the Creator. |
| **Inner Work / Internality of the Torah** | Work done entirely on one's inner vessels of perception, not on external actions. |
