### Opening

Korah "chased what was not his; he lost what was his and did not win the other one." What, then, truly belongs to a person? Rav Laitman begins at the root: the Creator created the will to receive existence from absence, but that desire is not yet a created being. It becomes one only when it acquires independence, and true independence is attained, paradoxically, through adhesion. From there the lesson explains Zeir Anpin and Malchut as the general and the particular, what "faith above reason" really means, why a person must be broken before he reaches the degree of a servant, and how to work with the left line without either running from it or drowning in it.

## Part 1 — Independence Through Adhesion

- When the will to receive was created, "it wasn't called the created being yet." It is under the Creator's authority until it cascades down to this world, and even then it isn't yet ready for its role.
- Only after it reaches such great distance from the Creator are conditions, qualities and discernments created in it from which it can determine a point of independence and define its relation to the gap between itself and the Creator.
- To the extent this point, the root of the soul, receives independence, "she is called the created being and the soul." And "the independence and true separation is attained specifically by adhesion," a state called **Dvekut**.
- Going from this world toward the final correction, the created being receives more coarseness each time and inverts it to in order to bestow. By the coarseness he is distinguished from the Creator; by the action he is similar to Him. "Specifically in these two opposite things," he becomes both independent and similar.
- The **point in the heart** is the beginning of the created being. Each time he must examine the deficiency in him, the "hole" in him, and what it is meant for, and he examines it toward the Creator. "There's no other way to do so."

## Part 2 — Zeir Anpin and Malchut: The General and the Particular

- These two opposites are the connection of **ZON**, **Zeir Anpin** and **Malchut**. ZA is considered reason (**Daat**), the written Torah, judgment and sentencing; Malchut is above reason, the oral Torah, the law.
- Malchut wants to be independent and also similar to ZA. Her independence is always measured "exactly against the measure of her adhesion and adaptation to the Creator." By the extent to which she separates from Him, she becomes similar to Him.
- The created being takes upon itself "the only form that is possible in reality, which is the image of the Creator." There is no other example: "It's either Him or seemingly the opposite to Him."
- The created being is a point of desire, and the only forms it can take come from the first nine **Sefirot** within it, the qualities of the Creator. It can use them in order to receive or in order to bestow, through reflected light. "That's all of the created being's work."

#### Private possession is the general

- ZA is called the collective and Malchut the individual. As Malchut implements the forms she discovers in ZA, absorbs them and stabilizes herself toward them, she becomes the particular.
- "It's not a matter of being smaller or diminished." The particular is a form of independence, in which she receives upon herself all the forms of the general.
- All the actions of the giver pass through Malchut into the souls. Malchut is **Knesset Israel**, the assembly of Israel that includes all souls, and each soul can adapt itself exactly to the general.

> So it turns out eventually that the private property, the personal private property of a person, is the general that he acquires.

## Part 3 — What Is Faith Above Reason?

- **Q: On page 144 he says faith "must not be touched," while the light of Torah must be investigated within reason. What is that?** Faith is the force by which the created being corrects himself and chooses to be in order to bestow. "To the extent in which a person has faith, to that extent he can aim in order to bestow." Then he goes on to receive in order to bestow.
- This force comes from **Bina**. In the **four phases** of direct light, at the end of Chochma, Bina felt besides the pleasure the giver of the pleasure, His greatness, and wanted to be similar to Him. That desire is phase two, and it is called **Emuna**, faith.

#### Bina versus Malchut

- In phases one and two, the desire to receive and the desire to bestow came from the Creator; "there's no created being yet." Bina simply bestows, because the desire to receive "is not hers."
- Malchut, after phase three, chooses: "I want to be the receiver." Her own desire to receive, phase four, is far greater than phase one. "The desire to receive is truly her essence."
- So Malchut can do what Bina cannot: make that desire into the form of the bestower, to receive in order to bestow.

#### Reason and above reason

- **Q: What is reason?** It is the light of filling, called Daat: the light of Chochma that fills the vessels of perception and brings knowledge, taste and attainment, "doesn't matter what kind."
- If I'm in a certain degree, I acquired it, "it's mine, that's me." To rise to a higher degree I can't simply swallow the lights there, "because we're about to attain the bestower." I must acquire it in faith above reason.
- "We're not aware to what extent attaining spirituality is the opposite of what we think attaining is." In this world, to become greater, wiser or more professional, I open my vessels of reception. In spirituality, to catch a higher degree, "I need to open my vessels of bestowal more."
- If I have fifty kilos of faith and fifty kilos of knowledge and go to a degree of sixty, I need to make sure I have sixty kilos of faith, "and the knowledge will be revealed accordingly. Because the upper light is in absolute rest." We only need to work on the force of faith.

## Part 4 — Korah, and Being Broken

- **Q: A degree once reached remains. So how did Korah lose what was his?** Korah wanted to receive in vessels that were not his, vessels of reception, to grab a little more in order to receive. So what he had acquired in order to bestow was also lost to him.
- **Q: How do we attain the force of faith? Through negation, or by feeling bad in this degree?** "How do you know that someone is stronger than you?" You have a battle with him and see. A person still thinks he can win with his own "software," his own knowledge, until the Creator breaks him and he surrenders. Then he is willing to receive from Him the forces to study and be with everything He wants.
- This comes after many tests, blows, suffering and discernments, until one reaches the degree of a **servant**. There are also degrees of son and partner, but reaching the degree of a servant is necessary.
- "All of the degrees we attain in spirituality are degrees from within our desire." Imagine how many discernments we must go through until, from all possibilities, "this is the only thing I want. Give me everything else instead of the Creator? No. I want Him to exist and I will give my soul." These are not just beautiful words.
- **Q: What should a person do so that the Creator will break him?** He doesn't exactly know. "Exert as much as possible through all the opportunities," and increase the pace. Everyone goes through it.

#### Shame

- **Q: Does the degree of servant come from shame?** "Shame is a general discernment about the difference between the created being and the Creator." We can't yet see that difference, only the one between us and other created beings.
- When the light shines a bit more, it's revealed how bad I am, how egoistic and lustful, how cruel toward everyone, what thoughts I have about each one. It comes only intermittently, as much as we can tolerate.
- Feel that "a billion times more," and it becomes clear that it's not toward people but toward Him. "This feeling all in all with respect to the Creator is called **shame**."
- The result is either to run away and wish never to feel it again, or to want whatever comes, as long as it brings the correction. And the correction is "not because I will feel good, but that I will truly be the bestower." I can't yet know what a bestower is, but "the opposite forms must be revealed," and that is a beginning. We want to awaken these sensations through the group work, though everyone runs away from them.

## Part 5 — Neither Escaping Nor Drowning in the Left

- **Q: What is a deficiency that may not be filled?** A deficiency filled according to itself, as it is, and not in order to bestow.
- **Q: What does it mean to escape the scrutiny?** "The more I delve inside the will to receive" and work with it and with the friends, "the more I can receive discernments from above." The coarseness goes downward and the attainments grow upward.
- So when unpleasant discernments come, a person should detail them "in a creative manner," check them in his connection with the friends and everyone, and most importantly tie them to the Creator and to the goal. If he isn't willing to resolve the problems on a lower plane, he isn't rewarded with higher discernments.

#### Two dangers

- If a person doesn't come from the right line, with the purpose of creation in his head, his attitude to life is "just life. This is already a beast, it's not a man." But coming to your sensations with the goal that they give you discernments "is called the work of man, Adam."
- Take as many forces from the right as possible, and delve into the left only as far as you've equipped yourself with the right. "It is forbidden to enter the left more than you have the power of the right." Otherwise you dwell in the feeling alone, "eating yourself up: what is happening to me, why do I feel bad."
- There are two dangers: escaping the opportunity given to us to examine the desire to receive, or sinking into it and forgetting why we came. "We have to always have our hand on the pulse": why do I feel this, what do I want to scrutinize from it, how should I use it?

#### When you're dead inside

- The exception is when a person is "knocked out," with only dark feelings where there's truly nothing. "In such a case, go to sleep," or go to the kitchen: peel a few bags of potatoes, wash a few thousand dishes, fix something in the center with friends, or do dissemination.
- "Internally you're dead, but it still works. From externality to internality, that's how it works." If there's no one to work with, work with your body.
- Conversely, if you're on fire within, it's better to delve into the study and subtler inner searches and extract discernments from there.

## Part 6 — Everything Is Absorbed in the Emotion

- **Q: Does it matter if we remember these states afterward?** No. "The intellect won't help at all," and they'll erase it from you a thousand times. From one degree to the next, the intellect doesn't pass.
- If you carry something from one state to another, "it's because it was imprinted as a **Reshimo**, a record." We don't have RAM like a computer memory.
- "In a person, everything that is absorbed is absorbed in the emotion." The mind only deciphers the emotion and assigns it a cell, as if writing "two times two equals four" on top of it. Even a number gives you an impression.
- Knowledge, memory and even the wisdom of Kabbalah exist only "in order to know what to do in the emotion." This is why "there is none wise as the experienced," one who went through many blows and emotional discernments.

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **Independence comes through adhesion.** The created being is distinguished from the Creator by its coarseness and made similar to Him by inverting it to bestowal.
2. **A person's possession is the general.** Malchut becomes the individual not by being smaller but by receiving upon herself all the forms of Zeir Anpin.
3. **Faith is the force of Bina.** It is the desire to be like the giver, and only Malchut's own chosen desire to receive can be turned into receiving in order to bestow.
4. **Attainment is the opposite of grabbing.** To rise, add faith beyond your knowledge; the upper light fills the vessel of faith by itself.
5. **We must be broken into servants.** Exertion through every opportunity brings a person to want only the Creator, and shame reveals the opposite form needed for correction.
6. **Hold the right before the left.** Don't escape the scrutiny of the will to receive, and don't sink into it; keep a hand on the pulse of the goal, and when dead inside, work with the body.
7. **What passes between degrees is emotion.** Records, not intellectual memory, carry us forward; the mind only interprets what the heart absorbs.
