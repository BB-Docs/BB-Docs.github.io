### Opening

How do we awaken ourselves from below and break through the barrier between us and the upper light? Rav Laitman begins by asking everyone to hold one prayer for the whole lesson: "What do I want from this lesson? Hold on to it by the teeth." Then he takes up a puzzle. A prayer is just a request, so why call it a prayer? Because I have no desire at all for bestowal, and I have to build that request artificially. The lesson is about how to build it: by seeing the friends' sparks instead of their bodies, and by gathering those sparks into a lens through which the upper world appears.

## Part 1 — Reading at the Writer's Pace

- Before the lecture, Rav Laitman stops the reader: "We need to read at writing pace." The writer feels something within him and passes it onto the paper. The reader should take it in at that same speed, "through my sensation, not in my mind."
- The effort is to enter the same image the author was in: "What did he feel when he wrote this?" Through the author, I adhere to what *he* feels in the revelation of the Creator to him. "That is called to study from the books and authors."
- So the author must be great and unique, someone with contact with the Creator who can convey that impression, "regardless of how he writes it."
- It's enough to be dependent on him "like a baby towards her mother" in order to begin to receive.

## Part 2 — Why a "Prayer" and Not a Request

- The words of Kabbalah get confused with words from religions, philosophies, and "external wisdoms." A prayer is simply "a request that I feel in my heart."
- **So why call it a prayer?** "Because I establish this request artificially. I have no desire for spirituality, nothing. I don't want it. I want to enjoy." For enjoyment, "I always have strength, energy."
- **Spirituality is bestowal.** "Faith or bestowal is one and the same." **Love** means "I love the other's desires and I want to fulfill them as if it's my own baby."
- "I have no tendency toward this. I'm not structured that way."

#### A dictionary of the words

- Rav Laitman suggests making "a dictionary of the words, definitions of the wisdom of Kabbalah and the sacred books, so they won't confuse us." Words like faith or "the Creator's honor" otherwise pull us toward meanings we don't understand. "We understand them incorrectly. It's even worse."

## Part 3 — Sparks Instead of Bodies

- "It's very difficult for me to detach from their bodies when I look at their faces." The faces "only hide man's inner desire."
- **How can I reach a state where, instead of bodies, I see sparks in the heart of each one?** A heart is a desire, and that desire is entirely egoistic. But a person rises above it: "He's riding in the air above his own heart, and his entire essence is in the spark."
- If we all connect in these sparks, each one receives everyone's sparks. "Only then he has the strength to rise above his ego. Only then is he able to exit his will to receive, his Egypt."

#### The conditions, like physics

- These are conditions, "just like in physics." As Baal HaSulam says, prayer, the commandments, and the laws of nature "are one and the same."
- If I concentrate my whole mind and heart on the friends' sparks and absorb them, "it is a strength that's like a balloon. It raises me above corporeality." Then I'm "in the air," neutral, no longer calculating everything egoistically.

#### The lens

| Lens aimed at me | Lens aimed outward |
|---|---|
| I look at the world and at myself through my ego, "and there I have no choice." | Included in the friends' sparks, I look through that matrix and "see the next world, the upper world, the world of bestowal." |

- "I need such a lens that will direct me outwards. And I do it by gathering the sparks of the friends."
- Meeting, gathering, studying, working, eating together: "These are the external conditions for the connection among us. No more than that." Everything is organized only so there's a chance to connect, "because in connection we form that very same lens."

## Part 4 — Uniting the Sparks, and the Shattered Spark

- **Q: At a convention of thousands, what does it mean to unite all the sparks?** "In spirituality there are no numbers but qualities. I don't need to count how many sparks I connect to myself."
- I rise above my ego and enter "this general thought that all of my friends are in a tendency toward mutual bestowal," building a mutual Kli to fill with the upper one. To the extent everything else is cancelled except this spark, "I already come closer to the form of the upper one, the Creator."

#### The spark itself starts out egoistic

- "That spark is shattered. It comes from the shattering of the vessels, from the shattering of Adam HaRishon," so at first it's the opposite of what it should be.
- When I start yearning for spirituality, "I want to acquire it." We feel like "a special elite unit" that will do it all, and that is still the ego seeking fulfillment.
- This is the dog that barks "hav, hav" (give, give) twice: once for this world, once for the next world. "I put a lot of effort in the study, in dissemination, all in order to get a result. Where is it? I deserve it."
- After many years I find "nothing's changed really." I just found more sources of pleasure. "Put the Creator there as well, take the upper world, put them there as well. I have a big bag to fill up." I use the friends, the group, the books.

#### "I have created the evil inclination"

- My attitude toward spirituality, the Creator, and the group is the **evil inclination**: "the shattered person in me, the first broken man." Before the spark, "I was an animal." Using the spark for my own fulfillment is what develops the shattered man in me.
- When I sense that I'm going in completely the wrong direction, using the right means (the wisdom, the teacher, the group) the opposite way, "I feel the force of shattering within me." That is **attaining the evil inclination**.

## Part 5 — The Inversion

- It happens "after quite a few years, not immediately." If you're not patient, "you can go on vacation, or even go on a pension." The entrance is free and the exit is free: "There's no coercion in spirituality."
- The inversion doesn't happen until a person "is truly shattered" because he always remains in his ego and can't come out of it.
- **What the inversion is not:** escaping the environment, thinking he's wiser and deserves more than everyone.
- **What it is:** "He begins to understand that he is the worst." Then everyone in the society "seems to him as the greatest of the generation," and he's willing "to bow his head and to be connected and serve everyone," just to absorb some of what they have.
- It's gradual, with ups and downs. He doesn't take the quality of bestowal from them; "he's just impressed by what they have."

#### The stages that follow

1. He advances "not towards becoming better, only towards the clarity that he is not yet in bestowal," until he feels true bitterness about it.
2. Bestowal becomes important to him for its own sake, "not because he has some self-benefit he derives from it."
3. Next to bestowal he "can't stand himself in the quality of reception." He feels good only when he sometimes rises above it.
4. He sees there must be a solution and discovers the need for the **upper force**, "which we always forget." Like salt: you don't think about it until the food lacks it.
5. Even then the calculations are still egoistic: "I need the Creator in order to give me the quality of bestowal, to give me the connection with the friends." He hasn't yet crossed the barrier.
6. Then he begins to ask why he wants the group above the ego at all: to attain the upper force "that will dwell in us," and to see that He arranges everything so we can adhere to Him. "But this will come."

## Part 6 — Scrutinies at the Speed of Light

- "Everything is scrutinized in thought," and these scrutinies "can happen at the speed of light."
- Humanity counted with animal bones and bears' teeth, then mechanical machines, calculators, computers. Yet even the most complex machine is only ones and zeros: "There's nothing more than two forces in the world."
- "The more we consolidate ourselves together, we accelerate the calculation." If we are focused, this whole process "is a matter of seconds, literally."
- Of 6,000 years of the spark's development, about 200 remain, and the Kabbalists say even that can be shortened. Baal HaSulam: "The time is right, we just need to make the effort."
- "Everything depends on the connection between us, the power of connection between us. And this is what the intention of the prayer should be."

## Part 7 — Leaving This World Behind (Q&A)

#### Disconnecting, then the harder exercise

- **Q: How can we get rid of all our habits, everything that ties us to this world, and become like a spirit?** First, physically disconnect for a few days. That was the desert: "mountains, tents, and antelopes. That's all."
- Now a harder exercise: a gathering of thousands, many not devoted friends, "within the most egoistic city in Israel," a **mini-model of the world**.
- We must prepare to be the core. If we unite, each nullifies himself before the others, and we build **mutual guarantee (Arvut)**, a shared desire and thought emerge that surround everyone at the convention.
- Through the interconnected human network (research now says about four degrees of separation), this flows to all of humanity as a higher, more integral force. People begin to feel "something's wrong in my thinking, in my desire," and become confused. "This is called dissemination, not the flyers that we hand out on the streets."

#### Becoming the friends' slave

- **Q: I came with just a bag, but some habits I can't throw away, and the friends tell me to. How?** "This is called being a slave. Whatever they say I do, without a thought, without a desire. And then by being ruled by them, I receive a desire and a thought from them."
- "Where is the I?" "You absorb a different quality from them." The problem: "You don't want to get rid of what you have, and therefore you have no room" for their integral quality of oneness and mutual guarantee. Without it, you don't have "the lens that reverses your view from inside out."

#### Thought and intention

- **Q: What is the connection between thought and intention?** **Thought** comes from calculation: a program for clarifying, sorting, categorizing. After the calculations I have an exact goal. **Intention** is then "an operating system" that aims everything I have toward it.
- "A thought is a scrutiny, and after the scrutiny comes the intention and the execution."

## Part 8 — The Group, Not the Teacher

- **Q (Terence): The solution will come from us, not you. Why do we keep looking at you?** "I always spoke about the same thing. You are hearing things a little newer because you change each time."
- There is an inner force in his words, "but in the group there is a much bigger force, a billion times bigger than what I have."
- **Rabbi Yosi Ben Kisma** rose from degree to degree through his small students, because they hold the collective force. "There's no ascent, there's just greater compression, greater consolidation." The light grows from **Nefesh to Ruach, Neshama, Chaya, Yechida** only through connection.
- "I'm enlivening you individually. Spirituality you can attain only in the group." "My job is to instruct. That's why I call myself a teacher, a guide."

#### "What will happen when you're gone?"

- "Whatever you do is what will happen." In spirituality there's no need for an authoritative manager. After receiving the path, everything else happens "from the connection between everyone." "You already know all the sources."
- Asked to add an evening Zohar lesson: "I don't feel a need. I feel a need in working on the inner connection." First comes the shared desire.
- "If you hang your hopes on me, leave this alone right away." Appreciate him only so that you hear the words, not personally. This is the whole difference from Hasidism and religions: no kissing the rabbi's hand.
- A **spiritual directorate** of about ten people, as detached from self-benefit as possible and able to decide together from connection, "should replace me."
- For the men, every phase in spirituality is attained "only through the connection between you."
- When Rabash departed, "Did I stop?" There is the upper plan and the upper force, "and He is the one that manages according to equivalence of form."

#### Closing workshops

- The friends recounted the lesson and asked what has to break in us for the inversion to happen. They also went around the ten, each describing the special spark of the friend to his right, then joined in a silent prayer to become what Rav Laitman describes.

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **Read at the writer's pace,** through sensation, to adhere to the author and to what he felt of the Creator.
2. **It's called a prayer because the request is built artificially;** by nature I have no desire for bestowal.
3. **See sparks, not bodies.** Gathering the friends' sparks lifts me above corporeality and forms the lens that turns my view outward, toward the upper world.
4. **The spark starts out shattered and egoistic.** Discovering that I use even spirituality for myself is attaining the evil inclination.
5. **The inversion:** I see myself as the worst and the friends as the greatest of the generation, and I want to serve them to absorb what they have.
6. **Connection accelerates the scrutinies** to the speed of light; this is the intention of the prayer.
7. **The force is in the group, not the teacher.** The teacher instructs; the light grows from Nefesh to Yechida only through connection between the friends.
