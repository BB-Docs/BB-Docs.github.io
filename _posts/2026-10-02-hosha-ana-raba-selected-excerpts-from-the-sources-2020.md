### Opening

It is the night of Hoshana Rabbah, the last night of Sukkot, when an old custom says a person goes out under the moon to check whether his shadow is complete. Rav Laitman's question is what this shadow really is. To answer, he walks through the whole cycle of the year, from the exit from Egypt to Purim, as one sequence of corrections in a single soul. Then he explains why we read texts we don't understand, what the "seal" and the "shadow" mean as measures of the screen, and, in the Q&A, how a vessel can change if the light never changes.

## Part 1 — From Abraham to a Nation

- Abraham did not receive the whole Torah, only "the method for inner self-correction." His group was large, but its will to receive was small, so it advanced without needing a full method of correction.
- Only when they multiplied and entered Egypt did the will to receive grow enough. The seven years of plenty and seven of famine correspond to **Zeir Anpin**, first filled, then emptied. "Great possessions" means great vessels of the will to receive, which now needed the Torah.
- **Moses** received a method of correction for an entire nation. He gave external customs whose roots are spiritual. In themselves they are on the "still" level: "however you move the still, it remains still." But the fixed framework keeps the nation, "this flock," from running away.
- He organized the people as a precise pyramid (priests, Levites, Israel; tens, hundreds, thousands) mirroring the spiritual hierarchy. Still, "everything depends on the inner intention and the inner correction."

## Part 2 — The Year as One Path of Correction

#### From Pesach to Shavuot

- After the exit from Egypt, a person begins to attain the quality of **Bina** over the 49 days of the **Counting of the Omer**: seven Sefirot of Zeir Anpin, each containing seven.
- Each day begins with evening (the revelation of the vessels) and then morning (their correction): "There was evening and there was morning, one day."
- At **Lag BaOmer** (Hod in Hod, the 33rd Sefira) the corrections of Zeir Anpin are complete. At the 49th, we enter Malchut: **Shavuot**, the giving of the Torah.

#### The breaking and the second tablets

- We too must go through states corresponding to the **breaking of the vessels**. To acquire something fully, "this thing breaks in my hands."
- While Moses (the quality of Bina in a person, in adhesion with the Creator) is on the mountain, the other desires below build the **golden calf**, connected to the Klipot. The first tablets break: Shavuot to the 17th of Tammuz.
- From the 1st of Elul, connection with the upper light returns, and the second tablets come. The first breaking is over vessels of **Hochma**, the second over vessels of **Hassadim**, like the first Temple (Mohin de Haya) and the second (Mohin de Neshama).
- Forty days on the mountain is a sign of Bina, the letter **Mem**. Inside Mem or Samech, a person is surrounded on every side, protected by the light of Hassadim, "in a state where no will to receive can cause him to fail."

#### Yom Kippur, Sukkot, Simchat Torah, Purim

| Stage | What happens |
|---|---|
| Yom Kippur | The vessels of the end of correction are revealed, but not yet corrected. The five afflictions mirror the five levels (Mocha, bones, nerves, flesh, skin = Keter through Malchut). |
| Sukkot | The vessels of Zeir Anpin are corrected. The four species are Malchut (Etrog), Zeir Anpin (Lulav), and HGT NH (myrtles, willows). The Sukkah is the surrounding light, built from the waste of barn and winery: faith above reason. |
| Hoshana Rabbah | The seventh day: all the surrounding lights, gathered in Yesod, enter Malchut. |
| Simchat Torah | "The joy of the Torah": the light of Hochma in corrected vessels, but only in VAK. GAR is still missing. |
| Purim | GAR. The great vessels, the "ten sons of Haman," are corrected by not using them. Full Hassadim and full Hochma: the end of correction. |

- The Sukkah is "the right embrace" from the Song of Songs: "His left hand is under my head, and his right hand embraces me." The left is the vessels of reception revealed from Malchut; the right embrace lasts seven days.

#### The last generation

- In our world, every spiritual root has already touched its corporeal branch: Egypt, the exodus, the Temples, the four exiles. Only Purim, the end of correction, remains.
- So, as Baal HaSulam writes, we are "certainly in the last generation." Nothing external is left to do except the inner correction, "and we are the first to do it."

## Part 3 — Why Read What We Don't Understand

- Baal HaSulam (Introduction to TES, item 155): Kabbalists write for one another, because each knows only the part of the upper world his own soul reached. Then why should we, who have never been there, study at all?
- The answer is the **Segula**: through "the yearning and the great desire to understand," we awaken the **surrounding lights** that correct the soul. Understanding is not the point.
- The same soul returns in each generation to lead us: Rabbi Shimon, the Ari, Baal HaSulam. Studying where the Zohar, the Ari, and Baal HaSulam are together brings "a very great surrounding light."
- The Zohar is not a book of ethics, and Gematria games and skipping-letter codes "do nothing except confuse people." You understand the Zohar only when you reach the degrees it describes.

> Surrounding light comes to us according to our desire to reach something, and not according to our fantasies that we are already there.

#### Language doesn't matter

- His first lesson with Rabash: old men with loose dentures, reading Aramaic and explaining in Polish Yiddish, often in "a quarter of a word."
- Baal HaSulam had a Yemenite student and an Iraqi student (who later married his daughter) who barely knew the language. They became leaders of the group, learning only from the yearning to know.
- Rav Laitman says his own explanations are just "to give you a little bit of upliftment," and then "you can fall back asleep."

#### Hoshana Rabbah night with Rabash

- Three festival nights are kept awake: the seventh of Pesach, Shavuot, and Hoshana Rabbah. With Rabash they came around 11:30, held a meal with stories and songs, read a little Zohar, and most went home by 2 a.m.
- These customs exist for the general public, so that questions might arise: "why are we up this night, what is this night about?"
- The instruction for tonight: connect intentions, yearn together, "this inner kind of kvetch," and want someone to "take us and bring us up."

## Part 4 — The Seal: NHY of Ima as a Vaccine

- Zeir Anpin of Atzilut (the Holy One, Blessed Be He) has two Nukvot, Rachel and Leah. Above them is **Ima** (Bina), who lowers her legs, **NHY of Ima**, into Zeir Anpin.
- NHY of Ima is the covering of Hassadim. With it, "I'm vaccinated against any problem that may come." I still have the will to receive, but I don't want to use it.
- The will to receive never becomes good by itself. It becomes calm only when light fills it. A little spiritual illumination and "this world can all burn, I don't care"; the light withdraws a little and I want corporeal fulfillment again.
- So everything depends on "this game of the upper light," its nearing and distancing. Our work is to draw it close until NHY of Ima clothes in us, and then "no disruption can come to me and confuse me."
- The "seal" (Chotam) means the external forces cannot take hold. The soul descends into the will to receive clothed in that seal, and so is protected.

## Part 5 — Gematria and the Kabbalist Who Says "It Seems to Me"

- The letters are the vessels: Aleph to Tet for ZAT of Bina, Yud to Tzadik for Zeir Anpin, Kuf-Reish-Shin-Tav for Malchut.
- **Gematria** is the number that sums up the state of a vessel: its screen, coarseness, reflected light, and how much direct light clothes in it. It's "a very succinct way" to name a degree, like saying "a half-liter bottle."
- Every name in the Torah is such a code for a Partzuf, force, or action. Moshe, Aharon, Levi are levels. But we can't decode the Torah from the codes; first we attain, then we see what the text means.
- When Chaim Vital writes "it seems to me," it isn't weakness. A Kabbalist who hasn't reached the root of a phenomenon knows it only as it appears on his level. "Nobody can tell what happens on a higher spiritual degree from the lower one."

> It's an adventure without comparison... You can't guess at what they may be, they're so full of pleasant surprises!

## Part 6 — Tzelem and Tzel: What the Shadow Is

- The names for the five levels (Nefesh through Yechida, or Malchut through Keter, or the worlds) don't matter much; "the main thing is the difference between the degrees."
- Since the first restriction, the ratio between Malchut and the light is always preserved. To the extent Malchut hides herself (her screen), the Creator can be revealed.

| Malchut (the created being) | The Creator |
|---|---|
| 100% "shut" (full screen), as in Ein Sof | 100% revealed |
| Partly screened | Partly revealed: a degree in the worlds |
| No screen, as in our world | Completely concealed |

- That proportion of concealment to revelation is called **Tzel**, shadow. The whole body (Zeir Anpin) is complete when every desire has its own screen, and so casts a shadow from every part.
- All of Sukkot, the surrounding lights have been building in a person "the image of God," a screen sufficient for all his desires. Checking the shadow on Hoshana Rabbah means checking whether that screen is complete.
- With Rabash, they never actually went out to look at their shadows.

#### Righteous, wicked, intermediate

- A **complete righteous** has the covering of Bina over all his vessels of reception. A **complete wicked** has no such covering; "it's not that he made himself wicked." These are degrees above the barrier. "We're just below the line of life... What the Zohar writes, not a word speaks about below the barrier."
- The extremes come from above. What depends on a person is how he joins right and left, taking vessels from the left and qualities from the right.
- **Hoshana Rabbah** is the last night the vessels are corrected, when the clothing of Hochma in Hassadim is completed.
- Angels, demons, and spirits are just names for forces with no will of their own. "It's None Else Besides Him."

## Part 7 — Q&A: If the Light Never Changes, How Does the Vessel?

- A student from Petah Tikva asks what it means that the vessel is "revealed" on Yom Kippur, if the light is in complete rest. Rav Laitman presses the room: the Reshimot? The screen? Yearning? Each answer still requires some change in the light.
- The answer: "complete rest" means the light always, 100%, wants to benefit the vessel. In its actions toward the vessel it changes at "an infinite frequency," to bring the vessel to the goal.
- **The neighbor and his son.** One day he hugs his son and takes him to the zoo, another day he beats him. Does he love him? He loves him. He never changed toward the son, only externally. "I have to keep justifying the Creator that He's always good."
- **Yom Kippur** is not the result of your own labor. Like Shabbat, it is an awakening from above, and even higher: an ascent to the Rosh of Arich Anpin.

#### Correction is the fulfillment

- Is there a gap between correcting the vessel and filling it? "There is none." The reflected light I build is itself the revelation of the Creator's bestowal. "I don't need, beyond that, to go and hug Him."
- **The membrane.** To measure pressure from outside, you apply a counterforce to bring the membrane back to zero, and measure that. "I never actually know what there is outside." The screen works the same way.
- How do I know I've equalized with Him? "Only according to the sense of **shame**." When there is no more shame: "Mine is mine, yours is yours."

## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **The year is one soul's path.** Pesach to Purim is a single sequence: exit from egoism, Bina, the Torah, breaking, second tablets, Sukkot, and the end of correction.
2. **Only the inner correction is left.** Every root has reached its branch except Purim; we are the last generation.
3. **Yearning, not understanding.** The Segula of the study works through the desire to understand, which draws the surrounding light.
4. **NHY of Ima is the vaccine.** The will to receive is calmed only when Bina's Hassadim cover it.
5. **The shadow is the screen.** A complete shadow means every desire has a screen; that is what Hoshana Rabbah checks.
6. **The light never changes in its intention.** It acts on us in endless ways, always to benefit us.
7. **Correction is the filling.** The reflected light is the revelation, and the end of shame is the sign of equivalence.

| **First and second tablets** | Breakings over the vessels of Hochma and of Hassadim, respectively. |
| **Complete righteous / complete wicked** | Degrees above the barrier: with or without Bina's covering over all vessels of reception. |
